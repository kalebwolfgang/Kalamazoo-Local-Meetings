SUBJECT:       Approval of a contract with Elhorn Engineering Company for the purchase of
               Liquid Phosphate (Bid Ref# 19000-013.0)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission approve a one-year contract with the Elhorn
Engineering Company in the total amount of $517,503.84.

BACKGROUND:

The liquid phosphate contract was bid on September 10, 2025. Once the bids were received, we
reached out to the Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE), to
verify if the lowest bid product meets the specifications of the bid. EGLE’s response was that the
lowest bid product is not considered an equivalent to their preapproved product. Therefore, we
recommend Elhorn Engineering Company.

Phosphate has been added to the City water supply since 1956 primarily as a means of keeping
iron and other minerals that naturally occur in groundwater dissolved in the water. The addition
of phosphate results in a higher quality, more aesthetically pleasing water supply with a
reduction in both sediment buildup in water mains and private plumbing systems.

Secondarily, phosphate helps maintain the regulatory requirement to reduce copper and lead
releases into the City’s water distribution system. The phosphate helps to coat the inside of water
mains to prevent corrosion of metals. It is the main ingredient in the City’s regulated corrosion
control program agreed upon with the USEPA and EGLE.

The Optimized Corrosion Control Project is under construction. Completion milestone phases of
this project are needed to begin to incorporate Product No.2. Once specific milestones of the
Optimized Corrosion Control Project are completed, we can begin to order Product No.2. Future
recommendations will be made under this bid tabulation to order Product No.2. However, we are
only recommending procurement of Product No.1 at this time.
