RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with County Line Nurseries for
2025 Tree Planting in the amount of $115,250.00.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo was a recipient of the Inflation Reduction Act Community Forestry
Grant to plant trees within the right-of-way in disadvantaged census tracts. There will be 250
new trees planted this fall under this proposed contract. Protection of the natural environment is
a key goal in improving urban resiliency. Trees and improved tree canopy within urban
neighborhoods can help to reduce the heat island effect, can help to reduce stormwater runoff
from storms, can sequester carbon emissions, can save energy and can provide habitat and refuge
for wildlife.

Increasing tree canopy within neighborhoods that can support tree canopies and planting trees in
proper locations for the species are strategies outlined within the Community Sustainability Plan
adopted by the City Commission in 2022.

The contract was bid in 2025, with bids opened on August 26. One conforming bid was received
from County Line Nurseries in the amount of $115,250. The vendor meets all specifications and
schedule requirements.
