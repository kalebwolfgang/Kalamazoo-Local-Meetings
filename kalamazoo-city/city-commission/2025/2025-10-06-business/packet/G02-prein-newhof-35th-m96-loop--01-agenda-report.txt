RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a professional service agreement with
Prein & Newhof for the 35th Street and M96 Transmission Loop Project, in the amount of
$111,600.00.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo asset management plan identified the design of the 35th Street and M96
Transmission Loop Project as a priority for design in 2025. The scope of this project is the
design of approximately 1.3-miles of 16-inch water main on 35th Street from Miller Drive north
to M96 and thence west on M96 to the existing transmission main near Station No. 33 at North
33rd Street. This loop will improve system reliability and redundancy. It will allow the system
to complete future needed upgrades to Station No. 33 and maintain service to all customers
during that future work.
