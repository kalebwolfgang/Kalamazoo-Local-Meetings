SUBJECT:       Resolution Setting a Public Hearing for October 20, 2025 to Consider an
               Application from ACW 3 LLC for a Commercial Redevelopment Exemption
               Certificate at 227 West Michigan Avenue.

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a Resolution setting a public hearing for
October 20, 2025 to consider the application for a Commercial Redevelopment Exemption
Certificate for ACW 3 LLC at 227 West Michigan Avenue.

BACKGROUND:

The City Commission established the Kalamazoo Commercial Redevelopment District No. 201
on September 2, 2025, by Resolution No. 25-46, pursuant to Public Act 255 of 1978, as
amended. ACW 3, LLC has submitted an application for a Commercial Redevelopment
Exemption Certificate for property located at 277 W Michigan Avenue, more particularly
described in Exhibit A of the accompanying resolution. The applicant proposes a $55 million
investment to rehabilitate the historic courthouse, part of the Bronson Park Historic District, into
a 127-room boutique hotel with restaurant and commercial space encompassing approximately
100,000 square feet.

Before taking action, the Act requires the City Commission to hold a public hearing at which the
applicant, the assessor, affected taxing units, and members of the public will be provided an
opportunity to be heard. At the hearing, staff will present the details of the application and
outline the anticipated financial impacts of the proposed exemption as reviewed by the City
Assessor. This redevelopment is a component of the transformational brownfield project
currently underway in Kalamazoo, supported by a significant incentive package from the State of
Michigan to encourage investment, economic development, and historic preservation.
