RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a one-year contract with Rowell Chemical
to provide Sodium Hypochlorite for wastewater treatment in the amount of $693,875.00.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant (KWRP) continuously feeds Sodium Hypochlorite to
disinfect plant effluent prior to discharge into the Kalamazoo River. Disinfection of the plant
effluent is required by KWRP’s National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES)
permit promulgated by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. The KWRP has been using
Sodium Hypochlorite effectively since June of 2006.
