RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a one-year chemical contract with PVS
Chemical Solutions, Inc. of Detroit, MI, in the amount of $110,124.00. for Liquid Sodium
Bisulfite.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant (KWRP) continuously feeds Liquid Sodium Bisulfite
to remove total residual chlorine from plant effluent. The removal of total residual chlorine, to a
concentration below 0.038 mg/L, is required by KWRP’s National Pollutant Discharge
Elimination System (NPDES) permit promulgated by the U.S. Environmental Protection
Agency.
