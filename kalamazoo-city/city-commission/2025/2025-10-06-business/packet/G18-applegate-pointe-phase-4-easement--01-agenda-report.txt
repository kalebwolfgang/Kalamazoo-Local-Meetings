SUBJECT:       Applegate Pointe, Phase 4 Utility Easement

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve and accept public utility easements with
Michiana Land Development, LLC for water main installed as part of phase 4 of the Applegate
Point Development; and authorize the City Manager to sign all related and necessary documents
regarding the acquisition of the easements.

BACKGROUND:

Construction of new water main is underway for Phase 4 of the Applegate Pointe Development.
 As all public water mains and sanitary sewer are required to be in a dedicated easement or
public right of way, Michiana Land Development, LLC in collaboration with the City of
Kalamazoo Attorney has drafted these agreements for approval.
