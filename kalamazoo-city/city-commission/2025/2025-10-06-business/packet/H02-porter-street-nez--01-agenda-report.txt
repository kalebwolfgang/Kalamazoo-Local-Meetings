SUBJECT:        Resolution to Create the Porter Street NEZ

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission approve a resolution establishing the Porter Street NEZ.

BACKGROUND:

The Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) Act (Act 147 of 1992, as amended) (“Act”) provides
a tax incentive for a defined period of time for new construction or substantial rehabilitation of
residential structures. The stated purpose of the legislation is to: improve the housing stock in
distressed areas where little or no new construction is occurring and where housing is in need of
repair. The legislation is intended to spur residential investment where it might not otherwise
occur. Plazacorp is the owner of properties within the proposed NEZ and is seeking to develop a
property at 619 Porter with a mixed-use development with apartments and ground floor
commercial space. This development is part of a Transformational Brownfield Application in
support of three developments of existing spaces in the downtown and Northside Neighborhood.
The NEZ will assist in supporting the development through a series of local support of reduced
tax payments through the NEZ program.

Within this decade the State has expanded the types of NEZs and types of qualifying residential
developments which qualify for a certificate, which is the formal approval of the reduced taxes
for terms for up to 15 years. The intent of these expansions of qualifying residential and mixed
uses were to promote development of more residential units and varied housing types (e.g house,
duplex, apartments, mixed-use). In order for a project to take advantage of the tax benefits of
this tool, an NEZ district must first be established by the City. Once the boundaries of the district
are approved, individual projects may then request approval of the tax incentives for their project
within the boundaries. The 619 Porter development would qualify as a new NEZ and would be
eligible for a certificate to qualify for the tax reduction for 12 to 15 years. The additional land
area to the north is meant to incentivize additional opportunity in the vicinity where zoning and

reduction, up to 15 years. The City Commission NEZ policy approved in 2025 requires an
annual affordability documentation submission to the City each year of an issued Certificate.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Complete Neighborhoods - residential areas that support the full range of people's daily needs.
The second floor spaces are where residential units can be built, with existing infrastructure and
buildings in place.

The costs and construction of these types of units can be higher, the NEZ helps bridge the gap to
encourage construction of residential units where density can be supported.

Economic Vitality - A supportive infrastructure for growing businesses and stabilizing the local
economy for the benefit of all.

The additional residential units and potential for others to utilize this incentive will encourage
residences where they can be supported and help support the downtown spaces for residents and
visitors.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Appropriate Depth of Engagement

Inform (one-way conversation) – no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

Consult (two-way conversation) – the community will have a chance to react to the project
through two-way conversation.

The community is made aware of the project by the public hearing by the City Commission and
the taxing jurisdictions who would be impacted were sent notice of the public hearing and
consideration for the NEZ in letters sent on July 7, 2025. At the June 2nd, 2025 Committee of
the Whole meeting, CPED staff and PlazaCorp provided an introduction and overview of the
steps to come for the local incentive support and upcoming agenda items, including this NEZ.
The community will have an opportunity at the public hearing to voice any comments on the
NEZ and district boundary.

At the August 18th public hearing, a total of four residents spoke to the request with comments
on encouraging the City to explore options for tax relief for existing residents and homeowners,
encouraging the City to explore more relief options for below 80% ami residents, wrap around
services and investment in businesses owned by neighborhood residents in the Northside
neighborhood. The NEZ district, an incentive offered by the State for qualifying communities, is
a tax reduction for new developments and new property owners. An NEZ certificate is only
eligible to new developments and property owners who purchase residents. In this instance, an
existing homeowner would not qualify by the creation of the district, with the incentive only
available for the transfer of property or obtaining permits to build.

FISCAL IMPACT:

Projects within a NEZ District with approved certificates will receive a tax abatement of
approximately half of their full tax obligation for a period of up to 15 years, based on what the
project is bringing forward.

The fiscal impacts for the City would be:

1. Staff time for treasury, assessing and CPED staff time work with the certificates and
   applications.

2. Staff time for annual compliance follow up on the terms of the certificate, such as verifying
   affordability.

3. Staff time for building and housing inspection fees as the project develops and is occupied.

4. A new fee for certificates was established in 2024, which will recoup costs for staff time.
