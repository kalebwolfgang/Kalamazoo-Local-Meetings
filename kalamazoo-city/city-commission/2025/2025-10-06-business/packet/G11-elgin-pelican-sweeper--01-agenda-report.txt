RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a MiDEAL cooperative purchase with
MacQueen Equipment, LLC dba Bell Equipment Co for one (1) Elgin Pelican Sweeper in the
amount of $317,589.00.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo’s Harrison Facility manages a Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan
(SWPPP) as required within the City’s Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant’s (KWRP) National
Pollution Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) as issued by EGLE under the Clean Water
Act.

The functional nature of the Wastewater Treatment facilities at 1415 N Harrison has been
identified in the SWPPP as requiring regular impervious surface cleaning maintenance to occur.
 The purchase of the Sweeper will be used to facilitate the maintenance requirements identified
in the SWPPP.
