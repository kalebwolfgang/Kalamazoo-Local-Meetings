RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the contract extension with Mission North
LLC for the Parking and Mobility Consulting in the amount of $239,440.

BACKGROUND:

In November 2022, the City of Kalamazoo entered into a three-year agreement with Mission
North LLC to provide consulting services related to downtown parking and mobility. The
consultant has supported the City in overseeing the parking system, implementing elements of
the Downtown Parking and Mobility Strategic Blueprint, and advising on policy and operational
improvements.

The First Amendment to the Parking and Mobility Consulting Agreement will:

      1. Extend the contract for an additional three years, beginning upon the expiration of the
         initial term.

      2. Revise the scope of work to reflect priorities for the 2025–2028 period, including
         oversight of the parking system manager, development of a residential permit program,
         and strategic guidance during construction projects that affect downtown parking and
         mobility.
