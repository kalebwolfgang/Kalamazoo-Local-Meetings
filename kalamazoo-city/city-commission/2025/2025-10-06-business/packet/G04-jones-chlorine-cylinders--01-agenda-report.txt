RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission approve a one-year contract with Jones Chemical Inc in
the total amount of $151,000.00.

BACKGROUND:

The liquid chlorine cylinder contract was bid on September 9, 2025 and Jones Chemical Inc. was
the low bid.

The City of Kalamazoo has been adding chlorine to the public drinking water supply since 1941.
The decision to add chlorine was based on several studies throughout the 1900s to 1930s and the
recommendation of the State of Michigan Department of Public Health to provide disinfection.
Under current Michigan Law, public water supplies are required to maintain a chlorine residual
within the distribution system. The chlorine residual should be at least 0.2 mg/L and detectable at
the farthest sample tap in the distribution system.
