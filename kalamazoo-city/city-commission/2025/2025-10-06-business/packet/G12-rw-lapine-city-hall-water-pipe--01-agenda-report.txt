               pipe at Kalamazoo City Hall (Bid Reference: 91060-012.0)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with RW LaPine to replace the
galvanized domestic water pipe at Kalamazoo City Hall in the amount of $435,000.00.

BACKGROUND:

The galvanized domestic water pipes in the facility have been failing and are in need of full
replacement. Over the past year, the domestic water piping has broken three times. On one of
these occasions, significant damage occurred on both the first and third floors of the building.

Purchasing has completed the formal bid process for the replacement project. RW LaPine
submitted the lowest responsible bid in the amount of $435,000. Based on the repeated failures
and the potential for additional costly damage, it is recommended that we proceed with this
project to ensure the long-term safety and reliability of the facility’s domestic water system.
