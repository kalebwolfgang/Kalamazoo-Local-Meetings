RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
THE FOSTER GROUP to continue consulting services to finalize development of the 2025
Wastewater Rates, and to provide a platform for ongoing Wastewater rate development in the
future in the amount of $180,000 with $40,000 of this request is to extend the current contract
through December 2025.

BACKGROUND:

A professional service agreement for rate and management services with The Foster Group
provides continuity for projects already under consideration and retains the knowledge and
experience of a 25+ years professional relationship with the City and representatives of customer
communities. The Foster Group will conduct a wastewater cost of service and rate study
alternatives and recommend a schedule of Wastewater Rates to be implemented during 2025, and
a subsequent study for rates to be implemented in 2026.

The Foster Group will participate in stakeholder discussions regarding potential modifications to
the manner in which levels of service are measured for (and costs allocated to) and maintained
for the various customer classes of the wastewater utility. Additional work scope includes
supporting the development of a new Wastewater Services Agreement including negotiations
and work sessions with Township officials and consultants. The Foster Group will also be
tasked to provide technical support to Environmental Services staff working with Significant
Industrial Users.
