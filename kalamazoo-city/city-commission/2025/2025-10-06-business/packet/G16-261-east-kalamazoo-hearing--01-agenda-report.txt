RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission adopt a Resolution setting a public hearing for October
20, 2025 to consider the application for a commercial Rehabilitation exemption certificate for 19
Props LLC at 261 E Kalamazoo Avenue.

BACKGROUND:

The City Commission established the Kalamazoo Commercial Rehabilitation District No. 01 on
September 2, 2025, by Resolution No. 25-47, pursuant to Public Act 210 of 2005, as amended.
On October 20, 2025, the City Clerk’s Office received an application from 19 Props, LLC
requesting issuance of a Commercial Rehabilitation Exemption Certificate for property located at
261 E Kalamazoo Avenue, more particularly described in Exhibit A of the accompanying
resolution. The applicant proposes a capital investment of approximately $18 million to improve
more than 51,000 square feet of former office space in the historic Kalamazoo Paper Box
Factory and convert it into much-needed residential units in the downtown.

Before acting on the request, the Act requires the City Commission to hold a public hearing at
which the applicant, the assessor, affected taxing units, and members of the public may be heard.
At the hearing, staff will provide a summary of the application and outline the anticipated
financial impacts of the proposed exemption as reviewed by the City Assessor. The project is
also part of a larger transformational brownfield redevelopment initiative supported by the State
of Michigan, which the Michigan Economic Development Corporation is providing a significant
incentive package to advance redevelopment and housing opportunities in Kalamazoo.
