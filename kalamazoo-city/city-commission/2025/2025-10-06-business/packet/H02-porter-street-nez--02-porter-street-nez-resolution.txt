ABSENT, Commissioners:

        WHEREAS The Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) Program was
established by Public Act 147 of 1992, as amended (the “Act”) to allow local
governments to provide a tax incentive for the development and rehabilitation of
residential housing in underdeveloped or distressed areas where neighborhood
revitalization, and the development and rehabilitation of residential housing might not
otherwise occur.

         WHEREAS the City of Kalamazoo is a qualified local government authorized to
 establish one or more NEZ's.

         WHEREAS the City Commission has established a policy for NEZ’s through
 Resolution 23-47 and terms of establishment of a district and qualifying terms for
 certificates in an NEZ zone.

        WHEREAS the City Commission finds that the neighborhood in the City of
Kalamazoo bounded by E North Street, N Porter Street, E Ranson Street, and N
Pitcher Street; as shown on the map (Attachment A) and legal description (Attachment B)
attached to this Resolution, (To be known as the Porter Street NEZ) is an underdeveloped
area consisting of undeveloped property, property with surface parking lots, vacant
structures, and property in need of rehabilitation.

       WHEREAS the City Commission finds that the neighborhood revitalization,
and development and rehabilitation of residential housing in the Porter Street NEZ block
needed to spur economic growth may not otherwise occur without the incentive for
such that would be provided by the establishment as an NEZ.

       WHEREAS the City Commission finds that the Porter Street NEZ is compact and
contiguous, contains enough platted parcels and is less than the maximum
percentage of acreage required by the Act, otherwise meets the requirements of the Act for
designation as an NEZ.

         WHEREAS the City Commission finds that the zoning designation of the area
within the Porter Street NEZ, zoned Live-Work 2 per the City of 50-1.2 Zoning Map, is
considered a Qualified Downtown Revitalization District per the Act. The Live-Work
Zone per City of Kalamazoo Code 50-3.1 (b) and 50-3.1(c) demonstrate that these zone
district are within the boundaries of an area zoned and primarily used for business as
defined by the City of Kalamazoo.

        WHEREAS the City Commission finds that there is a need in the City of
Kalamazoo for affordable housing and additional housing units which designation of the
Porter Street NEZ would help address.

       WHEREAS the City Commission finds that its designation of the Porter Street NEZ
Block is consistent with the Master Plan, the Northside Neighborhood Plan, Plan Area 3
goals and the neighborhood preservation and economic development goals of the City of
Kalamazoo.
       WHEREAS City staff has recommended that the designation of the Porter Street
NEZ as an NEZ District remains in effect for a period of five years from the date of such
designation. Any Certificates approved within that five year period shall be valid for the
number of years approved by the City Commission and in compliance with the terms
approved by the City Commission Nez Policy 23-47.

        NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT the Porter Street NEZ is
designated as a Neighborhood Enterprise Zone pursuant to Act 147 of 1992, as amended for
a period of five years from the date of the adoption of this Resolution.
