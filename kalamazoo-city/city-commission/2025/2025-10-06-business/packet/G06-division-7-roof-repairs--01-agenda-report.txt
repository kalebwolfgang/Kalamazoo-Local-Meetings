RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Division 7 Building
Contractors for primary settling roof replacement, raw pump & instrument tech building roof
repairs for the Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant (WRP), building 15A, in the amount of
$185,978.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant (WRP), building 15A, the fine screens building, has a
failed roofing system and is in need of a replacement. This roof has been repaired multiple times
throughout its life cycle. At this time, the roofing system is recommended for replacement.
 Building envelope repairs and replacements, including roofing, are critical maintenance and
replacement strategies necessary to extend the asset lifecycle.
