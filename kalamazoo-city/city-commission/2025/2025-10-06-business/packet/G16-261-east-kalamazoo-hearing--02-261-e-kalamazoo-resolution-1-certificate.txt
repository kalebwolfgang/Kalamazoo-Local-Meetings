                           CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                    RESOLUTION NO. 25-

       A RESOLUTION TO SET A PUBLIC HEARING ON THE APPLICATION
             FOR A COMMERCIAL REHABILITATION EXEMPTION
       CERTIFICATE FOR 19 PROPS LLC AT 261 E KALAMAZOO AVENUE

        Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City held on ______________,
at 7:00 o'clock p.m., local time.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Public Act 210 of 2005, as amended (the “Act”), the City
Commission established the Kalamazoo Commercial Rehabilitation District No. 1 on September
2, 2025 by Resolution No. 25-47; and

        WHEREAS, 19 Props, LLC has filed an application for a Commercial Rehabilitation
Exemption Certificate with respect to property located at 261 E. Kalamazoo Avenue, more
particularly described in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference, which
lies within said district; and

        WHEREAS, before acting on said application, the City Commission is required by law to
hold a public hearing at which the applicant, the assessor, representatives of the affected taxing
units, and the public shall be afforded an opportunity to be heard;

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED:

       1. Said request shall remain on file in the Office of the City Clerk.

       2. The City Commission shall hold a public hearing on October 20, 2025, at or after 7:00
p.m. Easter Daylight Time, in the City Commission Chambers at 241 W. South Street, to consider
the application for a Commercial Rehabilitation Exemption Certificate at 261 E. Kalamazoo
Avenue; and

       3. The City Clerk shall give notice of the hearing in accordance with the requirements of
Public Act 210 of 2005.
