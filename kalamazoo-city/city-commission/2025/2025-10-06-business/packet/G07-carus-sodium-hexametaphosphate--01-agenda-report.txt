RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a one-year contract with Carus LLC in the
total amount $199,320.00.

BACKGROUND:

The Sodium Hexametaphosphate contract was bid on September 10, 2025 with Carus LLC being
the lowest bidder. Sodium Hexametaphosphate has been added to the City water supply since
1956 primarily as a means of keeping iron and other minerals that naturally occur in groundwater
dissolved in the water. The addition of phosphate results in a higher quality, more aesthetically
pleasing water supply with a reduction in both sediment buildup in water mains and private
plumbing systems.

Secondarily, Sodium Hexametaphosphate helps maintain the regulatory requirement to reduce
copper and lead releases into the City’s water distribution system. The phosphate helps to coat
the inside of water mains to prevent corrosion of metals. It is the main ingredient in the City’s
regulated corrosion control program agreed upon with the USEPA and EGLE.
