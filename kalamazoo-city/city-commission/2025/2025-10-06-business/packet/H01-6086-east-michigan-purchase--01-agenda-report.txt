RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Purchase Agreement with Scott Smelker
and Serih Smelker (Seller) for the purchase of 6086 East Michigan Avenue (Parcel ID(s): 3907-
20-105-030, 3907-20-105-040, 3907-20-105-050), in the amount of $249,300.00, and authorize
City Manager to sign all necessary documents.

BACKGROUND:

Public Services manages a regional water system that has 11 pressure districts due to the
topography of the area. Public Services has interconnect stations between the pressure districts
to move water utilizing pumps or pressure reducing valves to meet hydraulic demands of each
area. Due to increased demands in the east side of the water system, Public Services desires to
build a new station between the Low, East Side High, and Intermediate pressure districts. The
ideal location for this new station is near East Michigan Avenue and North 26th Street in
Comstock Township.

Public Services saw 6086 East Michigan Avenue for listed sale in Comstock Township in the
amount of $249,300. Public Services retained the professional appraisal services of Appraisal
Group II, LLC via Metro Consulting Associates to perform an appraisal. The appraisal came
back at $220,000. After two weeks of discussion with the Seller, it was determined that the
property would not be sold below list price. The seller has provided Public Services a signed
Purchase Agreement in the amount of $249,300. Public Services recommends the approval of
this offer and Purchase Agreement.
