SUBJECT:       Public hearing to discuss and approve an application for a Commercial
               Redevelopment Exemption Certificate at 227 W Michigan Avenue

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended City Commission adopt the Resolution approving the exemption certificate at
227 W Michigan Avenue for 12 years.

BACKGROUND:

The City Commission established a Commercial Redevelopment District at 227 W Michigan
Avenue on September 2, 2025. On September 25, 2025, the City received an application from
ACW 3, LLC for a Commercial Redevelopment Exemption Certificate under Public Act 255 of
1978. The project involves the approximately 100,000 square foot historic rehabilitation of the
former County Courthouse building to convert it to a boutique hotel and related commercial
amenities. The development is a major component, called the "MAC", of the broader
Transformational Brownfield Project presented to the Commission in June and October 2025.

The building is vacant with no active economic use. The proposed investment will return the
property to productive use at a key downtown intersection and is expected to generate new
commercial activity. The project will create an estimated 69 permanent full-time jobs and 340
temporary construction jobs.

Under Public Act 255, issuance of the exemption certificate freezes the taxable value of the
property at its current level for the duration of the certificate, allowing the developer to defer
taxes on the increased value created by the rehabilitation while still preserving the City’s ability
to capture the full taxable value once the certificate expires. This exemption is intended to
support redevelopment of otherwise obsolete commercial properties in designated districts while
protecting long-term tax base growth.
