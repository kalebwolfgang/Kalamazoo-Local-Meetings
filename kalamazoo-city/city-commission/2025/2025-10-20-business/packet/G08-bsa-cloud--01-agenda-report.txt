RECOMMENDATION:

Approval of sole source purchase with BS&A software to the Cloud in the amount of $358,565.

BACKGROUND:

BS&A Software provides the City’s key financial and operational modules, including building
permits, utility billing, assessing, tax, miscellaneous billing, and cash receipting. During the
recent Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system evaluation, the City determined it was in the
best interest to retain the BS&A software package due to the program’s seamless integration with
the State of Michigan’s assessing tables and its ability to link billing items through customer and
parcel-based records.

BS&A is transitioning its platform from a locally hosted .NET system to a cloud-based
environment. Moving to BS&A Cloud will enhance functionality, provide secure and flexible
remote access, reduce IT maintenance, and ensure more reliable system updates. Upgrading prior
to Kalamazoo County’s transition will also support a smoother annual exchange of tax data
between the City and County systems.

BS&A is currently scheduling clients out at least 12 months. Agreeing to the contract will allow
the City to start the project in January 2027, immediately following the transition of the
remaining ERP elements to Tyler Munis.
