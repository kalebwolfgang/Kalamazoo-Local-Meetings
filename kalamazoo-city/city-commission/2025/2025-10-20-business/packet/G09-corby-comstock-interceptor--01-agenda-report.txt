Sewer Rehabilitation of the Comstock Interceptor between King Highway and Kalamazoo River
in the amount of $443,274.00.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo County Board of Public Works constructed the Comstock Interceptor in 1970 as
part of the Kalamazoo County Sanitary Sewer District No. 3 expansion. The interceptor serves
several upstream communities including Significant Industrial Users. Cured in Place Pipe
(CIPP) lining is a trenchless technology that provides an additional 50 years of estimated useful
life without service interruptions, major construction to the roads, or traffic congestion and
detours. Engineering has identified the original specified C3 reinforced concrete pipe and
structures within the identified scope of work has degraded to a NASSCO PACP 5.0 condition
assessment rating.
