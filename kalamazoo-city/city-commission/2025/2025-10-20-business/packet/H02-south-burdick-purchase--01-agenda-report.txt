RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Purchase Agreement with Maria
Hernandez for the purchase of 1216 South Burdick Street, 1222 South Burdick Street, 1228
South Burdick Street, and 121 West Crosstown Parkway, in the amount of $310,000.00, and
authorize City Manager to sign all necessary documents.

BACKGROUND:

In 2022, the City of Kalamazoo completed a study to evaluate the consolidation of Water
Treatment Plants for a reduction of entry points to the Kalamazoo Regional Water System for the
Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE). Treatment Plant
consolidation will improve water quality and simplify regulatory requirements. Public Services
owns and operates Central Treatment Plant at 215 West Stockbridge Avenue and Water
Treatment Plant No. 2 at 112 Born Court. 1216 South Burdick Street, 1222 South Burdick Street,
1228 South Burdick Street, and 121 West Crosstown Parkway are adjacent to Water Treatment
Plant No. 2 and will provide better Well Head Protection isolation distance for the current well
and potential future well replacement. Acquiring these four properties will be beneficial for
future source water protection and beneficial for the consolidated Central and No. 2 Treatment
Plants.

After other nearby sales, Maria Hernandez reached out to Metro Consulting Associates to inquire
about Public Services’ potential interest in these properties. Public Services retained the
professional appraisal services of Appraisal Group II, LLC via Metro Consulting Associates to
perform an appraisal of 1216 South Burdick Street, 1222 South Burdick Street, 1228 South
Burdick Street, and 121 West Crosstown Parkway. The appraisal came back and documented
water damage at the property. Public Service offered $300,000.00 based on the appraisal report.
