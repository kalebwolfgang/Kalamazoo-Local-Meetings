                            CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                     RESOLUTION NO. 25-

        A RESOLUTION TO APPROVE A COMMERCIAL REHABILITATION
            EXEMPTION CERTIFICATE FOR 19 PROPS, LLC AT 261 E
                         KALAMAZOO AVENUE

        Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City held on ______________,
at 7:00 o'clock p.m., local time.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

      WHEREAS, pursuant to Public Act 210 of 2005, as amended (the “Act”), the City
Commission established a Commercial Rehabilitation District at 261 E Kalamazoo Avenue on
September 2, 2025; and

        WHEREAS, 19 Props, LLC has filed an application for a Commercial Rehabilitation
Exemption Certificate with respect to property located at 261 E Kalamazoo Avenue, more
particularly described in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference, which
lies within said district; and

       WHEREAS, the project consists of the rehabilitation of approximately 51,150 square feet
of vacant commercial space to create 57 residential rental units, as part of the broader
Transformational Brownfield Project, and meets the requirements of the Act to support the
development of multi-family housing through the adaptive re-use of vacant commercial property;
and

        WHEREAS, issuance of the exemption certificate will freeze the taxable value of the
property at its most recent taxable level for the duration of the certificate, allowing the developer
to defer taxes on the increased value while preserving the City’s ability to capture the full taxable
value once the certificate expires; and

        WHEREAS, staff has reviewed the application and recommends approval of a 10-year
exemption certificate based on the scale of investment and the associated economic and housing
benefits;

        1. The City Commission hereby approves the Commercial Rehabilitation Exemption
