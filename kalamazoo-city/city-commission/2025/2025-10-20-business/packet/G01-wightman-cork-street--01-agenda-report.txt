RECOMMENDATION:

Approval of a contract supplemental/change Order #1 with Wightman for Design Engineering of
the Cork St (Westnedge-Portage) Safety Project in the amount of $66,400 bringing the contract
total to $147,310.00.

BACKGROUND:

Wightman is currently under a Professional Services Contract with Public Works to design a
safety improvements project on Cork from Westnedge to Portage. This project features a
reconfiguration from 4 lanes to 3, with several new mid-block pedestrian islands and other
crossing features. This is a Federally funded MDOT Local Agency Project.

Part of Wightman’s scope of work is surveying to determine exact property and right-of-way
lines as relates to the proposed work. Before this contract, it was anticipated that all work would
neatly fit into existing ROW, but the survey has shown that will not be feasible for this project.
 Most of the easements needed are temporary grading easements, but there are also areas where
existing sidewalk, and even some of the roadway of Cork St, is on private property.

Public Works has reviewed Wightman’s proposal and recommends proceeding with the Change
Order.
