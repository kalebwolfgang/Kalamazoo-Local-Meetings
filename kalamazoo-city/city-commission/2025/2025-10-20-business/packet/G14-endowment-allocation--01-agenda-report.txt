SUBJECT:       Approve an Updated Retirement System Endowment Investment Allocation

RECOMMENDATION:

Approve an update to the investment allocation described in the background and attached table
of this packet item.

BACKGROUND:

The Retirement Investment Committee (RIC) is responsible for formulating and recommending
to the City Commission investment policies, strategies, and guidelines for the retirement
investment endowment. The Committee regularly reviews the portfolio to ensure alignment with
market trends and current research.

Over the past year, a subcommittee conducted a comprehensive review of the equity portion of
the portfolio with the assistance of Zhang Financial. At its August 27th meeting, the RIC
approved a recommendation to update the investment allocation.

The recommended changes are designed to enhance portfolio diversification, reduce expenses,
and improve long-term expected returns. The far right column of the attached document
represents the final recommended portfolio allocation.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
