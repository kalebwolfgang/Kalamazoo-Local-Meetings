                             CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                      RESOLUTION NO. 25-

        A RESOLUTION TO APPROVE A COMMERCIAL REDEVELOPMENT
        EXEMPTION CERTIFICATE FOR ACW 3, LLC AT 227 W MICHIGAN
                              AVENUE

        Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City held on ______________,
at 7:00 o'clock p.m., local time.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

      WHEREAS, pursuant to Public Act 255 of 1978, as amended (the “Act”), the City
Commission established the Kalamazoo Commercial Redevelopment District at 227 W Michigan
Avenue on September 2, 2025; and

        WHEREAS, ACW 3, LLC has filed an application for a Commercial Redevelopment
Exemption Certificate with respect to property located at 227 W Michigan Avenue, more
particularly described in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference, which
lies within said district; and

       WHEREAS, the project involves the 100,000 square foot historic rehabilitation of the
former County Courthouse building to convert it to a boutique hotel, is part of the broader
Transformational Brownfield Project, and meets the requirements of the Act to stimulate economic
redevelopment of a vacant and functionally obsolete property; and

        WHEREAS, issuance of the exemption certificate will freeze the taxable value of the
property at its current level for the duration of the certificate, allowing the developer to defer taxes
on the increased value while preserving the City’s ability to capture the full taxable value once the
certificate expires; and

      WHEREAS, staff has reviewed the application and recommends approval of a 12-year
exemption certificate based on the scale of investment and projected economic benefits;

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED:
