RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Anlaan Corporation for
Academy Street Sidewalk Improvement in the amount of $122,582.50.

BACKGROUND:

Public Works requests approval to award a one-time construction contract for the Academy
Street Sidewalk Project, which primarily consists of constructing new sidewalk segments and
ramps. The improvements will enhance pedestrian safety and accessibility, particularly for
families with children accessing the nearby play park, and for those accessing nearby churches
and Bronson Park. The project will also support safer pedestrian connections within the
downtown area, consistent with City standards and ADA requirements.

Bids were opened on September 16, 2025. The lowest bid was from Anlann Corporation in the
amount of $122,582.50. The vendor meets all requirements and has done satisfactory work with
Public Services in the past.
