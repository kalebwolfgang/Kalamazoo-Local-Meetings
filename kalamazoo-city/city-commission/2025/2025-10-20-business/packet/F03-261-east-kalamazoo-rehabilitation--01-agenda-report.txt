RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended City Commission adopt the Resolution approving the exemption certificate at
261 E Kalamazoo Avenue for 10 years.

BACKGROUND:

The City Commission established a Commercial Rehabilitation District at 261 E Kalamazoo
Avenue on September 2, 2025. On September 25, 2025, the City received an application from 19
Props, LLC for a Commercial Rehabilitation Exemption Certificate under Public Act 210 of
2005. The project involves a 51,150 square foot renovation of vacant commercial office space to
create 57 residential rental units. The development, referred to as "Spearflex" project, is part of
the broader Transformational Brownfield Project presented to the Commission in June and
October 2025.

The building is vacant commercial office space that will be converted into residential apartments.
The proposed investment will return the property to productive use and is expected to generate
new, vibrant activity along this important corridor across from Arcadia Creek Festival Place. The
purpose of Act 210 is to include multi-family is to encourage adaptive reuse of vacant
commercial space to help address housing needs, particularly downtown or in mixed-use areas.
The project will create 57 new residential apartment units, an estimated 93 temporary
construction jobs, and 1 permanent full-time job.

Under Public Act 210, issuance of the exemption certificate freezes the taxable value of the
property at its current level for the duration of the certificate, allowing the developer to defer
taxes on the increased value created by the rehabilitation while still preserving the City’s ability
to capture the full taxable value once the certificate expires. This structure is intended to support

FISCAL IMPACT:

The current taxable value of the property is $0. Per State law, the certificate will be set at the
value of the building if the property was not exempt. The frozen taxable value will be
$1,850,100. The estimated value of the abatement over the 10-year certificate is $462,546. Upon
completion of the certificate, the full value of the improved property will return to the tax roll.
