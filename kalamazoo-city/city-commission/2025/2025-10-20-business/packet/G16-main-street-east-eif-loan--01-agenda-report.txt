RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the Economic Development Corporation
(EDC) Economic Initiative Fund (EIF) Loan for Main Street East, LLC in the amount of
$96,000.

BACKGROUND:

On August 21, 2025, the Economic Development Corporation (EDC) Board voted ro recommend
to the City Commission the approval of the EDC Economic Initiative Fund (EIF) Loan for Main
Street East, LLC. The proposed “Main Street East” project will retrofit approximately 9,146 sq ft
of the historic 65,000-sq ft building at 229 East Michigan Avenue, which includes Crafted
Copper on the ground floor and a 4-story atrium. The 0.606-acre site, including additional suites
to the east, will be rehabilitated to support Kalamazoo’s entrepreneurial community. The project
is estimated to cost $960,000.

Source of Funds and Disbursement Procedure:
The Economic Initiative Fund (EIF) was established with funds from the City's General Fund.
The fund is also replenished with repayments of loans made from the fund. The Economic
Development Corporation (EDC) retains and exercises control over its own separate fund, which
is established from various sources, including fees generated from the issuance of tax-exempt
financing. Funds from the EIF must first be advanced to the EDC or Brownfield Redevelopment
Authority (BRA) Fund for final disbursement. The City Commission approves all fund advances
from the EIF. The EIF is not a source of grant funding to for-profit businesses or nonprofit
organizations.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The Economic Initiative Fund (272-000-00.000-194.244) will advance $96,000 to the Economic
Development Corporation, which will issue a 10-year loan repayable at a 2% interest rate.

The City's Economic Initiative Fund will be replenished with the scheduled loan payments as
outlined in the promissory note. The long-term advance to the Economic Development
Corporation will be released with the receipt of the underlying principal payments.

There is no direct budgetary impact as loan disbursements are held within the balance
sheet.Future interest receipts will provide additional funding to the city EIF fund for future loans.
