RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the Economic Development Corporation
(EDC) Economic Initiative Fund (EIF) Loan for Kzoo Cream, LLC in the amount of $20,000.

BACKGROUND:

On September 18, 2025, the Economic Development Corporation (EDC) Board voted to
recommend to the City Commission the approval of the EDC Economic Initiative Fund (EIF)
Loan for Kzoo Cream, LLC. The proposed project will include roof and exterior site
improvements at the 1502 S. Westnedge Avenue location, supporting continued investment in
the building and strengthening a locally owned cookie and soft-serve ice cream shop along a
visible neighborhood commercial corridor. The project is estimated to cost approximately
$200,000, with EIF assistance representing $20,000 of the total cost.

Source of Funds and Disbursement Procedure:
The Economic Initiative Fund (EIF) was established with funds from the City's General Fund
and is also replenished with repayments of loans made from the fund. The Economic
Development Corporation (EDC) retains control over its own separate fund, which is established
from various sources, including fees generated from the issuance of tax-exempt financing. Funds
from the EIF must first be advanced to the EDC for final disbursement. The City Commission
approves all fund advances from the EIF. The EIF is not a source of grant funding for for-profit
businesses or nonprofit organizations.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The Economic Initiative Fund (272-000-00.000-194.244) will advance $20,000 to the Economic
Development Corporation, which will issue a 10-year loan repayable at a 2% interest rate.

The City's Economic Initiative Fund will be replenished with the scheduled loan payments as
outlined in the promissory note. The long-term advance to the Economic Development
Corporation will be released with the receipt of the underlying principal payments.

There is no direct budgetary impact as loan disbursements are held within the balance sheet.
Future interest receipts will provide additional funding to the city EIF fund for future loans.
