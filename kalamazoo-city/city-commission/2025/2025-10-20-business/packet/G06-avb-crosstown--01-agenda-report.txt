RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
AVB Construction, LLC for Construction Management Services for the Crosstown Renovation
Project in the amount of $204,361 for Construction Management Services for the 150 East
Crosstown Renovation Project.

BACKGROUND:

In 2003, the Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety (KDPS) headquarters moved from 215 W.
Lovell to 150 E. Crosstown Pkwy., Ste. A. The west portion of the building (Ste. B) was leased
by Kalamazoo County and housed the 8th District Court until the completion of the new Charles
A. Pratt Justice Center in 2023. With the lease expiration, the City of Kalamazoo (COK)
contracted with Intersect Studio to conduct a design and cost impact study, with the goal of
relocating Community Planning and Economic Development from the leased space at 245 N.
Rose and relocating Information Technology staff from City Hall to the Crosstown Facility.

Staff from the COK issued Request for Proposals (RFP’s) from three (3) pre-qualified
construction management firms. After receiving the RFP’s, the Firms presented their proposals
to COK staff. Based on the presentations, the group unanimously recommended AVB
Construction, LLC.

The requested change order in the amount of $204,361 reflects an increase pre-construction,
project fees, and general conditions based on the scope and timeline of the project.
