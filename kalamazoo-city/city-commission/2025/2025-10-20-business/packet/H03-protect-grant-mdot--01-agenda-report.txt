RECOMMENDATION:

That the City Commission acceptance of a subrecipient agreement with the Michigan
Department of Transportation and authorize the City to complete preliminary engineering
activities as part of the PROTECT grant.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo received notification on April 11, 2024, that it had been selected as a
recipient of a $37,990,158 grant through the Promoting Resilient Operations for Transformative,
Efficient, and Cost-Saving Transportation (PROTECT) program. This PROTECT grant will
build transportation resilience by eliminating flooding and surcharges from Arcadia Creek and
protecting the redesigned Downtown Kalamazoo Transportation Network. The project includes
the following phases:

      • Preliminary engineering and final engineering phases for all work to be completed under
        the grant.
      • Streets Phase: Replace aging and undersized stormwater infrastructure under Lovell,
        South, Stadium, West Main, and Douglas as part of their conversion from one-way to
        two-way streets—this work is funded through another federal grant.
      • Arcadia Creek Phase Zero: This phase is a prerequisite for all remaining stormwater
        phases and is funded by a private developer. Phase Zero includes relocation of
        stormwater infrastructure to prepare for planned and future economic development,
        including the new Kalamazoo Event Center and ancillary businesses.
      • Arcadia Creek Phases One through Three: Address Arcadia Creek flooding due to aging
        and severely undersized stormwater infrastructure from Kalamazoo Ave to West Main

When complete, the City of Kalamazoo will have an efficient and effective transportation system
with sustainable and resilient assets to withstand natural disasters and hazards, which
incorporates natural infrastructure, supports future economic development and growth, supports
multi-modal transportation choices, and truly reconnects the community in all conditions.

The City of Kalamazoo chose to be a subrecipient for this funding, with the Michigan
Department of Transportation (MDOT) serving as the direct recipient. MDOT has approved and
executed the PROTECT grant agreement with the U.S. Department of Transportation. This
subrecipient agreement will govern the initial phase of work to be completed by the City of
Kalamazoo—the preliminary engineering—and will allow that work to commence. As this
phase is completed and the work approved by MDOT, additional subrecipient agreements will be
developed and govern that work, with the next phase being final engineering. All work must be
completed in accordance with these MDOT subrecipient agreements.
