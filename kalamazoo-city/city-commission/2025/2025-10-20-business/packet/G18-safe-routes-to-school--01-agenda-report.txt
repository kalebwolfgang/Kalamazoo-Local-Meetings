SUBJECT:       Acceptance of Safe Routes to School Grant

RECOMMENDATION:

That the City Commission accept a Safe Routes to School Grant for $407,549 to improve safety
and connectivity for students at Winchell Elementary and Maple Street Magnet School and
authorize the City Manager to sign all related documents.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo began work with Winchell Elementary & Maple Middle Schools in 2019
on a Safe Routes to School (SRTS) program application. The goal of the partnership was to
promote the safety of children walking to and from school. COVID delayed the plans from
moving forward in 2020. The city and the two schools submitted an application in 2021.For
Winchell Elementary School, the application included a new sidewalk on the south side of
Winchell Avenue between Oakland Drive and Rambling Road with multiple crosswalks linking
the south half of the neighborhood to the school. For Maple Middle School, the proposed
infrastructure project included a pedestrian signal at the Howard and Merrill intersection. The
grant also includes funding for an educational/outreach program in each school to encourage
students to walk and bike to school using the new infrastructure.

The application was funded in 2022 but awarded contingent upon successful completion of
NEPA and SHPO requirements, which took almost two years.The project has now completed
these requirements and has been fully awarded the grant, with work to commence in 2026.
