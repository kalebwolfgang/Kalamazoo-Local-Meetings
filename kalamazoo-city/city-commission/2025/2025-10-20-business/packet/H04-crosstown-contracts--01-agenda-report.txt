RECOMMENDATION:

Approval of 17 Contracts for the 150 East Crosstown Facility Renovation Project

BACKGROUND:

In 2003, the Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety (KDPS) headquarters moved from 215 W.
Lovell to 150 E. Crosstown Pkwy., Ste. A. The west portion of the building (Ste. B) was leased
by Kalamazoo County and housed the 8th District Court until the completion of the new Charles
A. Pratt Justice Center in 2023. With the lease expiration, the City of Kalamazoo (COK)
contracted with Intersect Studio to conduct a design and cost impact study, with the goal of
relocating Community Planning and Economic Development (CPED) from the leased space at
245 N. Rose and relocating Information Technology (IT) staff from City Hall to the Crosstown
Facility. The nearly 55,000 square feet of space will accommodate CPED, IT, DEI, and the
communications team.

Intersect Studio (architect), AVB (construction manager), and city staff have worked throughout
2025 on the redesign of the 150 East Crosstown facility to meet the needs of staff that will be
relocating. In September of 2025, Management Services facilitated public bids for all associated
work. There are 17 contracts that are over $100,000, which require City Commission Approval.
