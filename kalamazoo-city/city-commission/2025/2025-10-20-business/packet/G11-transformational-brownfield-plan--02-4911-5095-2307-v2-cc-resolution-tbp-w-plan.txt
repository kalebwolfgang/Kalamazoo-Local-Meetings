        WHEREAS, pursuant to PA 381 of 1996, as amended (“Act 381”), the City of Kalamazoo

Brownfield Redevelopment Authority (the “Authority”) has been established by resolution of the City

Commission of the City of Kalamazoo (the “City”) for the purpose of promoting the revitalization of

certain properties in the City; and

        WHEREAS, under Act 381 the Authority is authorized to develop and propose for adoption

by City Commission a transformational brownfield plan for one (1) or more parcels of eligible

property; and

        WHEREAS, the Authority has prepared a proposed transformational brownfield plan for the

Plazacorp Kalamazoo Redevelopment (the “Plan”); as attached in Exhibit A; and

        WHEREAS, the developer intends to apply with the Michigan Strategic Fund for

transformational brownfield plan approval under Act 381; and

        WHEREAS, if the Plan is approved, the Plazacorp Kalamazoo Redevelopment Project (the

“Project”) may be eligible, subject to an underwriting and financial analysis and other requirements all

set forth in Act 381, to capture up to 100% of income tax capture revenues if applicable eligible

properties within the Plan for the Project are subject to a written, binding affordable housing agreement

to be approved by the City and Authority at a future date; and

        WHEREAS, the Authority approved the implementation of the Plan on October 2, 2025 and

forwarded to the City Commission with a request for the City Commission’s approval of the Plan; and

        WHEREAS, the required notice of the public hearing on the Plan by the City Commission

was given in accordance with Sections 13c(5), 14 and 14(a) of Act 381; and

        WHEREAS, a public hearing on the proposed Plan was held on October 2, 2025; and

        WHEREAS, previously the City considered and approved a proposal by developer, entitled

Chapter 50 of the City’s Revised Brownfield Plan (together, with all related amendments thereto, the

last of which was approved on June 6, 2022, the “Prior Plan”), which is now obsolete in light of the

Plan now being considered; and

        WHEREAS, in accordance with MCL 125.2664(8), Developer having received notice and

having been heard, and waived any right to further notice to terminate or right to be further heard in

relation to termination of the Prior Plan, it is appropriate to terminate the Prior Plan in order to approve

the new Plan.

        NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED THAT:

        1.      The City Commission hereby terminates the Prior Plan, in accordance with MCL

125.2664(8), Developer having received notice and having been heard, and waived any right to further

notice to terminate or right to be further heard in relation to termination of the Prior Plan.

        2.      The City Commission hereby determines that the Plan constitutes a public purpose in

accordance with Section 14(5) of Act 381.

        3.      The City Commission hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the public to

promote the revitalization of certain properties in the City and to proceed with the Plan.

        4.      As required by Act 381, the City Commission has in reviewing the Plan taken into

account the following considerations:

        a. The Plan meets the requirements of Section 2(hhh) of Act 381, and that the Plan is

             calculated to, and has the reasonable likelihood to, have a transformational impact on the

             local economic development and community revitalization based on the extent of

             brownfield redevelopment that will result from the Plan;

        b. The Plan meets the requirements set for in Section 13, 13(b), and 13(c) of Act 381;

        c. The cost of the proposed Eligible Activities identified in the Plan are reasonable and

             necessary to carry out the purposes of Act 381;

        d. Based on an economic and fiscal impact analysis, the Plan will result in an overall positive

             fiscal impact to the State of Michigan;

        e. The Plan takes into account the criteria described in Section 90b(4) of the Michigan

             Strategic Fund Act, 1984 PA 270, MCL 125.2090b;

        f. The proposed method of financing the cost of eligible activities is feasible and the

             Authority has the ability to arrange the financing;

        g. The amounts of Captured Taxable Value, Construction Period Tax Capture Revenues,

             Withholding Tax Capture Revenues and Income Tax Capture Revenues estimated to result

             from adoption of the Plan are reasonable; and

        h. The Plan will have a positive impact on existing investment and development conditions

             in the project area and act as a catalyst for additional revitalization of the area in which it

             is located.

        5.       The Plan as submitted by the Authority is hereby approved and adopted.

        6.       All resolutions and parts of resolutions insofar as they conflict with the provisions of

this resolution be and the same are hereby rescinded.
