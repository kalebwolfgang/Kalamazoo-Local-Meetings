RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended City Commission adopt the Resolution approving the Transformational
Brownfield Plan for the redevelopment project at 227 W Michigan Avenue, 261 E Kalamazoo
Avenue, 619 Porter Street, and 314 and 316 E North Street and terminating the prior plan.

BACKGROUND:

The BRA held a public hearing on the Transformational Brownfield Plan on October 2, 2025.
This application from PlazaCorp Realty Advisors, Inc. is related to brownfield incentives for a
large-scale, mixed-use redevelopment project located at three sites near downtown and the
Northside neighborhood of Kalamazoo. The attached resolution approves and recommends a
transformational brownfield plan for the redevelopment project located at 227 W Michigan
Avenue, 261 E Kalamazoo Avenue, 619 Porter Street, and 314 and 316 E North Street. Adoption
of this resolution approves the Brownfield Plan at the local level and authorizes the BRA to
implement tax increment financing, as required by the Brownfield Redevelopment Financing Act
("Act 381").

Corridor Improvement Authorities

In July, the Downtown Economic Growth Authority ("DEGA") agreed to an Interlocal
Agreement allowing the Brownfield Redevelopment Authority to implement a Transformational
Brownfield Plan to capture Tax Increment Revenues ("TIR") on behalf of the project at 227 W
Michigan Avenue and 261 E Kalamazoo Avenue. In September, the Northside Cultural Business
District Authority ("NCBDA") approved terms of a Voluntary Community Benefits Agreement
("VCBA") for TIR passthrough and in October the chair signed the VCBA and a Step-Aside

Other Incentives

PA 210 – Commercial Rehabilitation District
On September 2, 2025, City Commission established a Commercial Rehabilitation District for
the Spearflex component of the project. On October 20, City Commission will consider an
application for a Commercial Rehabilitation Exemption Certificate for 261 E Kalamazoo
Avenue. If approved, the taxable value of the property will be frozen at its pre-rehabilitation
level for a period of 10 years.

PA 255 – Commercial Redevelopment District
On September 2, 2025, City Commission established a Commercial Redevelopment District for
the Michigan Avenue Courthouse component of the project. On October 20, City Commission
will consider an application for a Commercial Redevelopment Exemption Certificate for 227 W
Michigan Avenue. If approved, the taxable value will be frozen at its current level for a period of
12 years.

PA 147 – Neighborhood Enterprise Zone
City Commission approved the creation of a Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) for the 619
Porter and adjoining parcels on October 6, 2025. Establishing this district would provide an
opportunity for the project to apply for certificates to consider additional incentive to help offset
the increased tax burden associated with large-scale housing investment. The NEZ certificate
would freeze the property’s taxable value at its pre-rehabilitation level for a period of 15 years,
with the abatement phasing out in the final three years of the term.

All of these local incentives are intended to be stacked with the Transformational Brownfield
Plan, providing a coordinated package of tax incentives across multiple project components.

Mixed Income and Work Force Housing

The project will add a total of 115 high-quality residential rental units along E. Kalamazoo
Avenue and E. North Street, including the redevelopment of the Spearflex and 619 Porter
buildings. These investments will bring new housing options to a key corridor and the Northside
Neighborhood, supporting both neighborhood revitalization and the City’s broader housing
goals.

The Spearflex building will include 57 studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom units, and the 619
Porter building will include 58 one and two-bedroom units. The development is primarily
intended to provide workforce and market-rate rental housing, while including a limited number
of units affordable to lower-income households, designed to serve a range of household incomes
and support neighborhood vitality.
