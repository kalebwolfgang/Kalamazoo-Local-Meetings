SUBJECT:       2025 Shop with a Senior Program

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the allocation of $12,000 from the City
Commission Initiatives budget to support the annual Shop with a Senior program administered
by the Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety (KDPS). The is the fifth year for the program.

BACKGROUND:

Shop with a Senior program is a signature community engagement initiative that brings together
KDPS officers, community organizations, and seniors across Kalamazoo during the holiday
season. Since its inception in 2020, the program has provided meaningful support and
companionship to seniors while fostering positive relationships between law enforcement and the
community.
The proposed $12,000 budget will support 55 seniors selected from across Kalamazoo.
Participants will shop at grocery stores located in the Northside, Edison, and Vine
neighborhoods, ensuring an equitable distribution of resources across the city.
The Shop with a Senior program would not be possible without the dedicated support of
numerous organizations, partners, and volunteers who help identify seniors and contribute to the
success of the event. These include:

      • The City of Kalamazoo
      • The City Commission
      • Milestone Senior Services
      • Metro Transit Authority
      • Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety’s Officers & staff
      • City View In The Square Apartments
      • Citizens Public Safety Review and Appeal Board (CPSRAB)
      • Ecumenical Senior Services
