INTERIM CITY MANAGER EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into this 17 day of November 2025 by and between the CITY
OF KALAMAZOO, a Michigan Municipal Corporation with offices at 241 W. South Street,
Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007 (the “City”) and Patsy A. Moore (“Moore’”’).

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Jim Ritsema (“Ritsema’’) has been employed by the City as its City Manager
since November 11, 2013; and

WHEREAS, Ritsema has announced his retirement from the City with his last day of
employment being Tuesday, November 18, 2025; and

WHEREAS, the City engaged in a search for a new City Manager and has selected
Malcolm Hankins as its new City Manager, whose anticipated start date is January 5, 2026; and

WHEREAS, in the interest of making an orderly transition between Ritsema’s last date of
employment and Malcolm Hankins’ employment start date, the City wishes to employ Moore as
Interim City Manager from Wednesday, November 19, 2025, until Malcolm Hankins’ employment
start date; and

WHEREAS, Moore desires to be employed by the City as the Interim City Manager under
the terms and conditions contained in this Agreement.

THEREFORE, in consideration for the mutual promises contained in this Agreement, the
sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the City and Moore agree to the following terms
and conditions:

AGREEMENT

1. Duties and Authority. Moore shall commence her employment with the City as Interim
City Manager beginning at 12:01 a.m. on Wednesday, November 19, 2025, and will
perform the functions and duties of City Manager set forth in the Charter and Codes of
Ordinances of the City of Kalamazoo, as amended, and all other legal and permissible
and proper duties and functions, subject to the general supervision and pursuant to the
order, advice, and direction of the Kalamazoo City Commission. Once a new City Manager
is in place, Moore shall relinquish the title and authority of Interim City Manager.

2. Term. This Agreement shall remain in full force end effect until Malcolm Hankins starts
employment with the City, unless terminated earlier by either the City or Moore as provided
in Section 7. It is expressly understood by the parties that Moore is retained by the City on
an at-will basis; this Agreement may be terminated by the City pursuant to Section 7(A)
for any reason, including convenience.

. Compensation. During the term of this Agreement, the City shall pay Moore an hourly
rate of One-Hundred Twenty-Five Dollars ($125.00) per hour, up to a maximum of 40
hours per week, less applicable deductions and withholdings, and paid in accordance with
the City’s normal payroll practices. The position of Interim City Manager is an executive,
exempt position under the Fair Labor Standards Act and shall not be eligible for cash
overtime or compensatory time in lieu of overtime.

. Employee Benefit Plans and Programs. Moore shall not be entitled to participate in any
other employment benefit plans and programs generally available to City employees,
except sick leave pursuant to the Michigan Earned Sick Time Leave Act and City Policy.
In addition, Moore will not be eligible for additional accrual of pension benefits under the
City’s Employee Retirement System Ordinance. The City will credit Moore with 3
personal days off to take at her leisure during the term of this Agreement. Any unused
personal days off or accumulated sick leave at the termination of this Agreement shall be
forfeited. Moore shall also receive generally recognized legal holiday as defined in the
City’s personnel policies.

. General Business Expenses. The City recognizes that Moore may incur certain expenses
of a non-personal, community, or job-affiliated nature, and agrees to reimburse Moore.
This shall include mileage re1mbursement to Moore for the use of her personal vehicle for
City business, at the rate utilized by the City as of the date that the vehicle is used. The
City’s Chief Financial Officer is hereby authorized to disburse such monies upon
production of appropriate receipts, statements, or affidavits. Moore shall not be reimbursed
for her daily travel to and from her home to City Hall.

. Conflict of Interest; No Offset for Other Income. During the term of this Agreement,
Moore acknowledges that she may accept other teaching, consulting, or business
opportunities, provided that those opportunities will not constitute a conflict of interest with
her obligations under this Agreement. The compensation provided for under this
Agreement shall not be offset by any income Moore may earn from any other source.

. Termination; Severance. For purposes of this Agreement, termination shall occur when
either of the following take place:

A. Termination Without Cause. This Agreement may be terminated by either the City or
Moore at any time without cause. Moore shall provide the City two weeks’ notice of
any intent to terminate this Agreement without cause.

B. Termination for Cause. This Agreement may be terminated immediately and without
notice in the sole discretion of the City upon occurrence of any one of the following
events:

* Moore fails or refuses to comply with the policies, standards, and regulations of
the City as are established periodically, or fails or refuses to comply with
reasonable direction from the City Commission;

¢ Moore commits an act of fraud, dishonesty, misappropriation of funds, intentional

reflecting poorly on the City in the rendering of services on behalf of the City.

C. Upon termination of this Agreement with or without cause, Moore shall not be entitled
to severance but shall be entitled to compensation up until the date of termination as

8. Return of City Property. Upon termination of this Agreement, Moore shall return all City
property in her possession or under her control, including but not limited to any keys,
computer or other office equipment, cell phone, credit card, and the originals and copies of
all paper or electronic files, records, or other documents.

9. Legal Defense. As a City employee, Moore shall be covered under the City’s
Employee/Official Indemnity Policy. In the event Moore is made a party to any legal or
administrative proceeding as a result of Moore’s services and/or authority as Interim City
Manager, or because of decisions made that were within the scope of her services and/or
authority as Interim City Manager, the City will provide a legal defense on Moore’s behalf.
It is understood that the City is not obligated to provide such defense for any matters outside
the scope of this Agreement and/or authority as Interim City Manager, whether they be
intentional, negligent, or criminal.

10. Testimony. If there are any legal or administrative issues, allegations, or hearings that
require Moore’s participation in connection with her role as Interim City Manager as
provided under this Agreement, Moore agrees to cooperate and to make herself available
to provide information and testimony to the extent deemed necessary by the City. If this
occurs during the term of this Agreement, Moore shall not be entitled to any additional
compensation. If it occurs after this Agreement has been terminated, the City agrees to pay
for these consulting services at the rate of two hundred dollars ($200.00) per hour, portal
to portal, plus expenses. The minimum billing for such services shall be four (4) hours.
Any such payments shall also be for work as an independent contractor and Moore shall
be fully and solely responsible for any and all payroll and social security taxes.

11. Entire Agreement. This Agreement shall constitute the entire agreement between the
parties, and supersede all other agreements, whether oral, written, or implied, regarding the
subject matter hereof.

12. Amendment. This Agreement can be modified or amended only in a subsequent written
document signed by both the City and Moore.

13. Waiver of Breach. A waiver of any breach of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver
of any future breach.

14. Binding Effect. This Agreement shall be binding upon the City and Moore, as well as
their heirs, assigns, executors, personal representatives, and successors in interest.

15. Severability. If any provision of this agreement is found to be invalid, all other provisions
shall remain in full force and effect.

16. Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in one or more counterparts, and the
signature pages may be transmitted by facsimile or email, each of which shall be deemed
an original and all of which together shall be considered one and the same Agreement.

17. Jurisdiction and Venue. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of
Michigan. Any litigation relating to this Agreement shall be brought in the Circuit Court
of Kalamazoo County, Michigan.

18. Voluntary Agreement. Moore has entered into this agreement freely and voluntarily, after
having been advised to seek advice of legal counsel and having had adequate opportunity
to do so.

Dated:
Patsy A. Moore, Interim City Manager
