RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an employment agreement with Patsy A.
Moore for the position of Interim City Manager.

BACKGROUND:

At the October 30, 2025, City Commission Special Called meeting, the Commissioner approved
an employment agreement with Malcolm Hankins for the position of City Manager. Hankins’
employment start date is Monday, January 5, 2026. The current City Manager’s employment
end date is Tuesday, November 18, 2025. As a result, there is a need to employ an Interim City
Manager to serve during the period between Wednesday, November 19, 2025 until Sunday,
January 4, 2026.

Former City Deputy City Manager and current retiree, Patsy A. Moore, has agreed to serve as
Interim City Manager. She has agreed to serve as a temporary employee. Since she would
occupy one of the four Charter-appointee positions, an employment agreement has been brought
forward for the Commission’s approval.

For your convenience, below is a summary of the key terms of the agreement:

● Employment start date: Wednesday, November 18, 2025

● Employment end date: Sunday, January 4, 2026

● Compensation:             $125.00/hour up to a maximum 40 hours per week

● Pension Benefit:          None
