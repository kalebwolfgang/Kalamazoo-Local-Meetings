RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an agreement with the United Way South
Central Michigan/Continuum of Care to support winter shelter services in the amount of
$300,000.

BACKGROUND:

The City Commission approved $500,000 in the 2025 budget to provide support and shelter for
Kalamazoo's unhoused community, particularly during the winter months, especially during
extreme weather.

In September, the Kalamazoo County Continuum of Care (CoC), in partnership with the County
and City, released a Request for Proposals to seek qualified and experienced service providers to
operate Winter Shelter Services during the cold weather season (November – March). This
includes Day Shelter, Overnight Shelter (congregate, non-congregate, or emergency Code Blue),
and Shelter Access Coordination services for individuals and families experiencing
homelessness. The objective of Winter Shelter Services is to ensure safety, warmth, and access
to essential resources during winter months. Services must be trauma-informed, culturally
responsive, and designed to connect guests with long-term stability resources. This partnership,
combining County, City, and philanthropic resources, directly addresses a gap in overnight
shelter beds that are anticipated for this winter. Kalamazoo County has allocated $396,750
towards this effort, and it is anticipated that other partners will be contributing resources, both in-
kind and financial.

The CoC received four proposals in response to the RFP. After receiving feedback from a Grant
Review Committee that included community consultants, the CoC Board approved the following
funding allocations:

Of the $500,000 allocated for extreme weather shelter support, the City has allocated $141,050
to date with the following outputs/outcomes:
 Kalamazoo Gospel Ministries (KGM): $97,050 for enhanced security and new shelter
mattresses
 City funds allowed KGM to maintain both the safety and capacity of their services, specifically:

     • Provide professional security coverage for three months (February–April), which
       enabled KGM to safely shelter over 425 individuals each night without overburdening
       staff.
     • Avoid placing limits on the number of guests KGM serves, thus reducing the risk of
       violence, overdoses, and deaths that can occur when individuals are left to sleep outdoors.
     • Purchase 150 new mattresses, replacing worn-out ones and adding much-needed
       sleeping accommodations to support comfort, hygiene, and dignity for KGM guests.
     • Acquire 10 bedrail kits to construct bunk beds, effectively increasing KGM’s sleeping
       capacity within existing space—an efficient, cost-effective way to serve more
       individuals.
     • Decrease the number of police service calls over time, reflecting the stabilizing effect
       of a secure and managed environment. KGM had 122 police service calls in January prior
       to security support and 98 calls in April with security support.

Heads in Beds Hoteling Effort: $44,000 for two, week-long hotel events in response to
extreme cold weather provided by Kalamazoo Together for the Unhoused in partnership with
the Continuum of Care.

Number of individuals housed in rooms across 4 hotels:
