RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the sale of 459 N. Rose to Kalamazoo
County.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo County Administration approached the City of Kalamazoo to purchase the
current parking lot at 459 N. Rose Street for the construction of a parking ramp that would
facilitate County Administration staff parking, Metro Transit, and include additional public
parking for the new justice center. City staff worked with the County to conduct a parking
analysis of the area determining the number of spaces that would be ideal and that the new
design would require the additional property at 459 N. Rose for the design of the ramp.

The ramp will fill a needed gap in the parking system, give Metro Transit needed spaces for
transit riders, and accommodate County staff and Justice Center visitors, jury members, and in
the evening will serve as a public ramp for downtown visitors. The sale of the City's parcel is an
important part of the land assembly needed to facilitate the project.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Economic Vitality - A supportive infrastructure for growing businesses and stabilizing the local
economy for the benefit of all.
