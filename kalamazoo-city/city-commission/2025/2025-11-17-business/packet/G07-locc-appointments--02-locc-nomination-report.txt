                                      Local Officers Compensation Commission
                                    Appointee Nomination Report
                                             Date: 11/10/2025

RECOMMENDATION
The City of Kalamazoo Local Officers Compensation Commission recommends the City Commission approve the
following:

   -   The appointment of William Hilton Reed for a full term expiring March 31, 2032
   -   The appointment of Levi Weintraub for a full term expiring March 31, 2032
   -   The reappointment of Mark Liddle for a full term expiring March 31, 2031
   -   The reappointment of Barbara Miller for a full term expiring March 31, 2031

Selection Process
Applicants Considered:
William Reed and Levi Weintraub

Interview Process and Final Recommendation:
Applicants for the Local Officers Compensation Commission are selected for nomination by the City Clerk. The
Civic Engagement Talent Coordinator conducted phone interviews with Mr. Reed and Mr. Weintraub and
recommends appointment. The City Clerk reviewed these applications and spoke with the applicants'
references. Both Mr. Reed and Mr. Weintraub are being recommended to the Mayor for appointment.

Regarding the reappointments, the City Clerk contacted current members Mark Liddle and Barbara Miller, who
both indicated a willingness to continue serving.
