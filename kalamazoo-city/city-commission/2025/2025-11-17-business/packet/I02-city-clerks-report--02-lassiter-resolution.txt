CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN Kalamazoo City Clerk
RESOLUTION NO.

A RESOLUTION DIRECTING STAFF TO INITIATE ACQUISITION OF THE PROPERTY AT
4301 STADIUM DRIVE FOR CONSERVATION PURPOSES, INCLUDING THE OPTION OF
EMINENT DOMAIN

Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City held on ;
at , local time, at the City Hall.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

WHEREAS, The Kalamazoo City Commission Rules of Procedure, specifically Rule 7
(Communication to the Commission), permit a resident of the City of Kalamazoo to file an
item with the City Clerk's office for the Commission's consideration; and

WHEREAS, a resident of the City of Kalamazoo, Zachary Lassiter, has filed this item with the
City Clerk's office pursuant to Rule 7 for the City Commission's consideration at this time; and

WHEREAS, the City Commission has officially and repeatedly committed to environmental
stewardship, climate action, and natural resource protection, as demonstrated by the
adoption of Resolution No. 19-10-14 (declaring a Climate Emergency in October 2019) and
the subsequent adoption of the Community Sustainability Plan in 2022; and

WHEREAS, the City's goals include the establishment of greenhouse gas baseline reports, a
focus on achieving net-zero carbon emissions by 2050, and the protection of water
resources and natural features; and

WHEREAS, the property located at 4301 Stadium Drive represents one of the largest
remaining contiguous green spaces within the City of Kalamazoo's boundaries; and

WHEREAS, the 4301 Stadium Drive property is located immediately adjacent to the Asylum
Lake Preserve, which is designated as a critical natural resource for regional ecology, water

quality, and passive recreation; and

WHEREAS, the current property owner has made public statements indicating that the
property is not developable under its current zoning classification; and

WHEREAS, the City of Kalamazoo has repeatedly denied the property owner's requests to
rezone the property for commercial use, affirming the appropriateness of the current zoning
classification which limits intensive development; and

WHEREAS, the development or disturbance of the property at 4301 Stadium Drive poses an
unacceptable risk of adverse impacts, including increased impervious surface runoff, erosion,
habitat fragmentation, and compromised water quality, which would undermine the
established conservation goals of the Asylum Lake Preserve and the City's own Wellhead
Protection Program; and

WHEREAS, allowing the development of this specific green space would be in direct conflict
with the City's comprehensive commitment to decreasing carbon emissions, addressing
climate change, and protecting natural features;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Commission of the City of Kalamazoo, that:

1. The City Commission hereby directs appropriate City staff and departments,
including the City Manager and City Attorney's office, to immediately begin the
process of acquiring the property located at 4301 Stadium Drive for conservation
purposes; and

2. The City Manager is specifically directed to make a good faith offer to purchase the
property at 4301 Stadium Drive to the current landowner, based on a current fair
market value appraisal that reflects the property's existing zoning and legal
development limitations; and

3. BEIT FURTHER RESOLVED that should the landowner decline the City's fair market
value offer within ninety (90) days of the offer date, the City Commission directs the
City Attorney and appropriate City departments to commence the eminent domain
process pursuant to Michigan law, and to draft a Notice of Taking and necessary
acquisition resolutions for approval by the City Commission; and

4. The City Manager is further directed to identify potential funding sources for the
acquisition, including but not limited to the Kalamazoo Foundation for Excellence,
dedicated park funds, and available state or federal conservation grants; and

5. The City Clerk is authorized to attest to and make public the adoption of this resolution
and to deliver a certified copy to the current landowner of the property at 4301 Stadium
Drive; and

6. All resolutions or parts of resolutions inconsistent with this Resolution are repealed to the
extent of such inconsistency.
