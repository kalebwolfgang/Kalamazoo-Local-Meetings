SUBJECT:       Approval of Termination of Lease Agreement with Metro Transit at 459 N. Rose
               Street (Bid Ref# 00000-025.120)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission authorize the City Manager to sign the amended lease
agreement for 459 N. Rose St. between the City and the Central County Transportation Authority
(Metro).

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo County Administration (County) desires to build a new parking garage at 459 N.
Rose St. The City (landlord) and the Central County Transporation Authority (Metro) have a
lease for parking at the address. The County has worked with Metro to secure parking spaces at
the new parking garage. As a result, rental of 459 North Rose St. by the Tenant for parking is no
longer needed, and it is necessary to terminate the lease of that property for the Landlord to
convey clear title of 459 North Rose St. to the County.
