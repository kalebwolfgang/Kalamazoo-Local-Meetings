SUBJECT:       Mayor's Appointments to Boards and Commissions

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the Mayor's appointments and
reappointments to the Local Officers Compensation Commission:

- the appointment of William Reed for a full term expiring March 31, 2032

- the appointment of Levi Weintraub for a full term expiring March 31, 2032

- the reappointment of Mark Liddle for a full term expiring March 31, 2031

- the reappointment of Barbara Miller for a full term expiring March 31, 2031

BACKGROUND:

State law and City ordinance require the Mayor to appoint and reappoint members to the Local
Officers Compensation Commission (LOCC) with the approval of the City Commission. I have
reviewed the attached Nomination Reports and appointed/reappointed the nominees as
recommended. I am now requesting the City Commission approve these appointments and
reappointments as required by ordinance. Details of the nominees' qualifications are included in
the attached Nomination Reports.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
