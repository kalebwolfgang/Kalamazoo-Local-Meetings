GRANT AGREEMENT

This is a Grant Agreement between The City of Kalamazoo, a Michigan Municipal corporation,
241 W. South Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007 (hereinafter referred to as "City",) and United
Way of South Central Michigan hereinafter referred to as “United Way”) located at 709 South
Westnedge Avenue, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007.

RECITALS:

1. Severe and extreme cold weather conditions are forecast for the Kalamazoo area in the
immediate future.

2. United Way is willing and able to work with community partners to provide emergency
shelter, resources and support for unhoused members of the community during severe
and extreme cold weather conditions and the City desires to support United Way in such
endeavors on the basis set out in this Agreement.

AGREEMENT:

In consideration of the mutual promises and covenants of the Parties, the City and United
Way agree as follows:

The City will grant $300,000 to United Way in one lump sum on the effective date of

DETAILS OF PROJECT

Project Purpose (Statement of Needs)

Unsheltered homelessness has grown over the past three years in Kalamazoo County, as
evidenced by the annual Point in Time Count, a HUD mandated literal count of individuals
experiencing homelessness in Kalamazoo. In 2025, the Point in Time Count saw the largest
number of individuals who would have been unsheltered if not for short-term emergency
sheltering efforts that were in place at the time of the count. In addition, the largest provider of
emergency shelter in Kalamazoo, Kalamazoo Gospel Ministries, recently began limiting the
number of beds available for emergency shelter, in an effort to promote safety and dignity of
both staff and shelter-users.

To address current community emergency sheltering needs, the United Way/Continuum of Care
have released a Request For Proposals (RFP) that seeks to address the shortfall of available
shelter beds, with an emphasis on adult individuals that are not yet participating in long-term
programming within Kalamazoo County. Proposals have been asked to address Day Shelter,
Overnight Shelter (congregate, non-congregate, or emergency cold weather), and Shelter Access
Coordination.

Performance Indicators, Objectives, Action Plans)

DELIVERABLES (Target Population and How They Will Benefit)

The target population for these funds are individuals experiencing homelessness within
Kalamazoo County, with specific priority given to those who are unsheltered or at imminent risk
of exposure to winter weather conditions. Funds will be utilized by the United Way to issue a
competitive Request for Proposals (RFP) process to identify and contract with qualified
supportive service organizations capable of providing low-barrier, winter-specific sheltering
solutions. Selected providers will be expected to deliver temporary shelter, basic needs services,
and supportive engagement that prioritizes safety, stabilization, and connection to longer-term
housing resources.

TIMELINE (Timetable for Implementation)

The CoC anticipates that services and programs funded through the RFP process will be
operational November 1, 2025 through March 31, 2025. Final reporting on fund utilization, and
all monthly reports provided to the CoC on winter shelter initiatives funded through this grant
will be due to Kalamazoo County by May 31, 2026.

DIVERSITY, EQUITY, AND INCLUSION (How Program Was Designed with Equity In Mind)

This program was intentionally designed to advance equity by prioritizing resources for
individuals who face the greatest barriers to safe shelter during winter months, including people
who are unsheltered, disconnected from traditional service systems, or disproportionately
impacted by systemic inequities such as racism, disability, poverty, and criminal legal system
involvement. The United Way will administer an open and competitive RFP process, and has
encouraged applications from people who identify as Black, Indigenous, and other People of

1. Overview

The Kalamazoo County Continuum of Care is seeking qualified and experienced service providers
to operate Winter Shelter Services during the cold weather season (November — March). This
includes Day Shelter, Overnight Shelter (congregate, non-congregate, or emergency Code
Blue), and Shelter Access Coordination services for individuals and families experiencing
homelessness. The objective is to ensure safety, warmth, and access to essential resources during
winter months.

Additional Context:

Recently, Kalamazoo Gospel Ministries (KGM) announced beginning October 6, 2025, they will limit
their emergency shelter beds to 140 (80 for single men and 60 for single women) and prioritize
access for Kalamazoo County residents. This adjustment applies only to their emergency services
beds. They will continue to operate with a total capacity of over 400 beds, the majority of which are
connected to programming designed to help individuals take the next step towards long-term
stability. Additionally, unsheltered homelessness has grown over the past three years with the
2025 Point in Time Count seeing the largest number of individuals who would have been
unsheltered if not for short-term emergency sheltering efforts.

To address current community emergency sheltering needs, this RFP seeks to address the
shortfall, with particular emphasis on adult men and individuals that are not participating in long-
term programming within Kalamazoo County.

Applicants may also propose temporary or pop-up shelters to expand capacity during emergency
cold-weather events: “Code Blue” Facilities must prioritize Kalamazoo County residents and
describe reasonable verification methods, ensuring no one is turned away during emergencies due
to verification delays.

Applicants should present contingency or surge plans for high-demand nights due to severe
weather, including partnerships or overflow strategies.

Proposals should avoid duplication of existing services and coordinate with known providers.
