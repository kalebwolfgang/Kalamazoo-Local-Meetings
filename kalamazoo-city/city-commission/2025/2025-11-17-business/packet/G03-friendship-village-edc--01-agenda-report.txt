SUBJECT:        Adoption of a Resolution Regarding the Issuance of Bonds by the Economic
                Development Corporation for the Friendship Village of Kalamazoo Project

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a resolution taking the following actions
regarding the proposed project plan and bond issuance for Friendship Village of Kalamazoo: 1)
certifying the designation of the project area, 2) establishing project district area boundaries; 3)
appointing two additional directors to the Economic Development Corporation Board; and 4)
setting a public hearing for December 15, 2025.

BACKGROUND:

At its meeting on October 16, 2025, the Economic Development Corporation (EDC) Board of
Directors approved a resolution requesting City Commission action on the next required steps for
the Friendship Village of Kalamazoo Project.

The project, proposed by Lifecare, Inc. (d/b/a Friendship Village of Kalamazoo), involves the
issuance of EDC Revenue Bonds in an amount not to exceed $120,000,000 to finance the
construction, renovation, furnishing, and equipping of senior living facilities and related
improvements at Friendship Village’s continuing care campus located at 1400 and 1402 North
Drake Road and 4300 Beech Street.

Under Public Act 338 of 1974, City Commission approval is required to (1) certify the EDC’s
designation of the project area, (2) establish project district boundaries, (3) appoint two
additional EDC directors to serve in connection with the project, and (4) call a public hearing on
the proposed Project Plan and bond issuance.
