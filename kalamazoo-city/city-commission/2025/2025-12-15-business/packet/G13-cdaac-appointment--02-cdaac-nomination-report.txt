                               Community Development Act Advisory Committee
                                    Appointee Nomination Report
                                             Date: 12/3/2025

RECOMMENDATION
The City of Kalamazoo Community Development Act Advisory Committee recommends the City Commission
approve the following:

   -   The appointment of Kyle Ackerson for a partial term expiring March 31, 2027

Selection Process
Applicants Considered:

Kyle Ackerson

Interview Process and Final Recommendation:

The entire board participated in the application process. Board members present on the day of the interview
were Jacquelyn Hobson, Nathan Browning, Vivian Segovia-Perez, and Stephanie Vallar. The interview was
conducted in a roundtable-style with each member of the board asking questions to determine Mr. Ackerson’s
qualification as a member. Post-interview, a motion was made to nominate Mr. Ackerson to the board and a
voice vote was recorded. The board unanimously voted to include Mr. Ackerson on CDAAC.
