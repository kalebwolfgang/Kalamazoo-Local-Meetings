                           CITY OF KALAMAZOO MEETING PROCEDURES

     1.     The Consent Agenda is a list of items proposed for City Commission approval to be
            voted upon all at one time. This is a time-saving procedure as most Consent Agenda
            items are housekeeping measures. A citizen may request an item be removed from the
            Consent Agenda for individual consideration or discussion.

     2.     Out of respect for business being conducted during the meeting, please turn off all cell
            phones and pagers prior to the start of the meeting.

     3.     In an effort to maintain order and allow a respectful discussion, please do not make
            comments from the audience area. Audience members should also refrain from
            applause or other audible noise at times not formally recognized as appropriate by the
            meeting chair.

     4.     To address the City Commission, please do so from the podium located in front of the
            dais in the Commission Chambers. Please remember to clearly state your name and
            home address, including whether you reside within the city limits, for the record so that a
            response to your inquiry can be completed if necessary.

     5.     While at the podium, please remember to sign in and limit your comments to four
            minutes.
