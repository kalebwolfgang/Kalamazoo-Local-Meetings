SUBJECT:       Public Hearing for the Friendship Village of Kalamazoo Project

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt the resolution approving the project plan for
the Friendship Village of Kalamazoo Project.

BACKGROUND:

Friendship Village of Kalamazoo, operated by Lifecare, Inc., is a nonprofit continuing care
retirement community that has served residents in the City for several decades. The organization
intends to undertake a multi-phase project that includes the construction and equipping of new
facilities on its campus, the renovation and modernization of certain existing facilities, and the
refinancing of prior indebtedness related to earlier capital improvements.

The Economic Development Corporation of the City of Kalamazoo (EDC) is authorized under
Act 338 of 1974 to support projects that prevent unemployment, encourage commercial and
institutional investment, retain local employers, and strengthen the local economy. The EDC has
reviewed and approved a formal Project Plan describing the proposed improvements and
financing structure and has submitted the plan to the City Commission for consideration.

The EDC adopted a resolution recommending the approval of the Project Plan for the Friendship
Village of Kalamazoo Project to the City Commission at its November 20, 2025 meeting.

Approval of the Project Plan by the City Commission, following a properly noticed public
hearing, is required under Act 338 and the Internal Revenue Code for the EDC to issue tax-
exempt private activity bonds to finance the project. The proposed bonds are limited-obligation
revenue bonds, repayable solely by the Borrower and not by the City.
