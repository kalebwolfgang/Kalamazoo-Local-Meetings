SUBJECT:       Resolution Supporting the Kalamazoo River Urban Wildlife Corridor Plan

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission adopt a RESOLUTION supporting the Kalamazoo
River Urban Wildlife Corridor Plan.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo River Urban Wildlife Corridor Plan began development in 2018 as a result of
the City of Parchment's efforts with the Kalamazoo Community Foundation to bring river
stakeholders and community members together to consider the environmental, recreational,
economic and educational potential of the Kalamazoo River. In 2022 and 2023 the City of
Parchment received funding from the Kalamazoo River Natural Resources Trustee Council.

The Urban Wildlife Corridor was defined as the Kalamazoo River valley from Morrow Dam in
Comstock through the cities of Kalamazoo and Parchment to D Avenue in Cooper Township.
 Phase I of the project is complete and included data collection, development of GIS maps and a
stakeholder meeting that identified issues and opportunities. The Phase I work supported the
creation of an Urban Wildlife Corridor Plan in Phase II which began in the Spring of 2024. The
planning process was led by a steering committee with assistance from the Southwest Michigan
Planning Commission. The process included data collection, meetings with stakeholders and
soliciting public input. Goals, objectives and an action plan were developed for the corridor with
a focus on protecting and improving the Kalamazoo River. The plan prioritizes areas for
restoration and protection and identifies public access, education and interpretation opportunities
within the corridor. The plan will serve as a resource for municipalities, agencies and
organizations to secure funding to implement actions in the plan.
