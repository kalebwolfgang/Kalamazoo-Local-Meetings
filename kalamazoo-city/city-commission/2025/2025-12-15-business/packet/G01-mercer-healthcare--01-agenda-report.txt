RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a one-year contract with Mercer for the
purchase of Health Care Management Services in the amount of $100,622.76.

BACKGROUND:

Mercer is the Healthcare Consultant for the City of Kalamazoo. For a monthly fee of $8,385.23,
Mercer provides healthcare negotiations on behalf of the city. Mercer ensures the city is
compliant with the latest laws regarding healthcare, and they negotiate on the city’s behalf to
attain competitive benefit rates. In addition to negotiating competitive benefits rates, they also
lend their expertise in the city’s benefit plan design.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:
