SUBJECT:       Resolution Approving Five New Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ)
               Homestead Applications in the Vine Neighborhood.

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a resolution approving five new
Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) Homestead Applications in the Vine Neighborhood.

BACKGROUND:

On September 29, 2008, the Kalamazoo City Commission created the Neighborhood Enterprise
Zone (NEZ) in the Vine Neighborhood. The Vine NEZ certificate applications are for owners
who are also occupants of existing homes in the Vine neighborhood. The purpose of this NEZ
program is to provide tax incentives to own and occupy homes in the designated NEZ area with
a small financial investment towards improvements on the property.

Within the Vine NEZ district, homeowners may apply for a certificate for their property to obtain
tax benefits. The approval, if granted, would take effect the following tax year. The City Clerk
has received five applications for new homestead NEZ certificates in the Vine Neighborhood.

617 Locust Street
724 Oak Street
610 Mc Courtie Street
708 Pioneer Street
524 Wheaton Avenue

Each of these applicants own their own homes in the Vine Neighborhood and use them as their
principal residence. As is required under the program, they have each agreed to spend at least
$500 on improvements to their home within the next three years. If approved, the certificates of
all five properties would expire on December 30, 2035

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Complete Neighborhoods - residential areas that support the full range of people's daily needs
Shared Prosperity - Abundant opportunities for all people to prosper

The NEZ program moves forward the Strategic Vision of completes neighborhoods as NEZ
districts encourage home ownership with investment in the housing stock of the City. This can
assist the shared prosperity goals of increasing home values and provide investment in housing
stock of the City.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The five new homestead NEZ certificates are in effect for 10 years and represent a reduction of
50% of the City and County operating taxes for the home. Assuming static values and the city
Millage rate the city would forego these amounts for the next ten years if the NEZ certificates are
approved. The total estimated tax reduction for all five properties for the City's portion would be
$2,511.21 per year. The total tax reduction for the City and County would be $3,922.98 per year.
The benefit in the program is it incentivizes homeownership and investment in the home.
