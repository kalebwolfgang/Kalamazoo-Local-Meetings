RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Tetra Tech, Inc. for the Central Water Treatment Plant Upgrades Project design, in the amount of
$476,400.00.

BACKGROUND:

Central Water Treatment Plant is the primary baseload station for the Kalamazoo Regional
Water System. It was built in 1995 and requires upgrades. Public Services reached out to Tetra
Tech, Inc. to provide a professional engineering services proposal to complete a Granular
Activated Carbon (GAC) treatment pilot plant in coordination with the plant upgrades that are
being designed to confirm the extent of what will be required to add PFAS treatment to the
existing plant. This work scope is being added to make sure we implement necessary upgrades
to be compliant with EPA implementation of PFAS maximum contaminant levels (MCLs)
proposed for 2029. Tetra Tech, Inc. provided a proposal for this additional work in the amount
of $476,400.00.

Tetra Tech, Inc. has performed well with other projects for the City of Kalamazoo, and Public
Services recommends approval of this contract.
