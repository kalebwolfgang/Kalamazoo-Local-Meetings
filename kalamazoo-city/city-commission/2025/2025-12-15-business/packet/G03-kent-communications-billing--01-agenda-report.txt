RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract extension with Kent
Communications for utility bill printing, mailing services, and postage in the amount of
$200,000.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo is currently under a one-year contract extension with Kent
Communications, Inc (KCI) through December 2025. The goal of this contract extension is to
have printing and mailing services continue with Kent Communications Inc., for January -
December 2026. KCI provides design, printing and billing services for the Utility Billing Office.
On average, KCI processes and mails utility notices to over 55,000 customers per quarter. KCI
has continued to demonstrate customer focus in the quality of their work product, project
turnaround time, responsiveness and accuracy.
