SUBJECT:       Approval of the Use of $68,161 from a Sale of Park Property in 2006 for Park
               Improvements and Approval of a Budget Amendment in the Amount of $68,161

RECOMMENDATION:

A recommendation that the City Commission approve a motion from the Parks and Recreation
Advisory Board to use $68,161 from a sale of park property in 2006 for park improvements and
a budget amendment in the amount of $68,161.

BACKGROUND:

In 2006, the Parks and Recreation Department sold a piece of property on the corner of North
Pitcher and Mosel for $152,000. It was recommended that $100,000 of that sale go to the
Mayors Riverfront Endowment and $50,000 be saved for future land purchases. The last use for
the purchase of land from this fund was in 2012 for $2,799. Since that time, the money has
earned interest and is now $68,161 and is reported as Assigned Fund Balance in the General
Fund. The department will be challenged with the ability to acquire any land for the sum of funds
available currently as fund balance. The Parks and Recreation Advisory Board made a motion at
the December 2025 meeting that the funds remaining in fund balance be used for parks
improvement projects. The board voted unanimously to approve this change through the motion.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The approval of the change in use of these funds also brings forward a request for a budget
amendment to transfer the $68,161 to release the assigned fund balance and transfer these funds
from the General Fund to the Capital Improvement Fund. We are requesting a budget
amendment of $68,161 to the capital project fund for use on the Verburg Park improvement
project and the Farmer's Market Multi-Purpose Building. Usage of these funds will allow us to
complete the Verburg Park improvement of additional playground equipment, shelter, bike racks,
etc and Farmer's Market Multi-Purpose Building with data cabling and AV system installation.
