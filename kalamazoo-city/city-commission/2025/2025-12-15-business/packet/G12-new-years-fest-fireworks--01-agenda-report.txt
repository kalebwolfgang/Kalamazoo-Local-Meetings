SUBJECT:       New Year's Fest Fireworks Permit

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a request from New Year's Fest of
Kalamazoo to publicly display fireworks from the Epic Center Parking Structure on Wednesday,
December 31, 2025 at midnight as part of the annual New Year's Fest event.

BACKGROUND:

Pursuant to state law, New Year's Fest of Kalamazoo has applied for a permit to publicly display
fireworks at the Epic Center Parking Structure as part of the annual New Year's Fest event
downtown. The Department of Public Safety has reviewed the request, and Fire Marshal Scott
Brooks has endorsed the application. The City's Building Authority, as the owner of the Epic
Center Parking Ramp, has granted permission for the ramp to be used as the launch site. New
Year's Fest of Kalamazoo has provided the required proof of insurance from the pyrotechnic
operator.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Inviting Public Places - Vibrant streets, exceptional parks, and welcoming activities.

Safe Community- Creating a safe environment for living, working, and playing

Discussion
For many years, a midnight fireworks display has been part of the New Year's Fest event, which
brings thousands of people downtown.

Fireworks event sponsors must receive City approval for public displays of fireworks. This
requirement ensures that the displays are conducted by licensed and trained pyrotechnic
operators, and the execution of the show adheres to state and local fire safety regulations.
