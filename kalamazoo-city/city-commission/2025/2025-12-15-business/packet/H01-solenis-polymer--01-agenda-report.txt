RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract extenstion with Solenis LLC of
Wilmington Delaware for Biosolids Dewatering Polymer in the amount of $1,345,101.78.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant dewaters approximately 284 wet tons of sludge per
day. After being pumped from the primary and secondary processes, sludge is gravity thickened
and comingled. Polymer is added to the comingled sludge prior to the centrifuge process to aid in
dewatering. These dewatered biosolids are then hauled to the landfill for disposal.
