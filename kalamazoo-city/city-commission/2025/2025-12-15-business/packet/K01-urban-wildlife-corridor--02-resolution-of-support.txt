                           CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                  RESOLUTION NO. 25-__

               A RESOLUTION INDICATING SUPPORT OF THE
           KALAMAZOO RIVER URBAN WILDLIFE CORRIDOR PLAN

       Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City of Kalamazoo, County
of Kalamazoo, State of Michigan (the “City”), held on December 15, 2025, at 7:00 p.m., prevailing
Eastern Time.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

       WHEREAS, the Kalamazoo River and its surrounding corridor are vital natural,
cultural, and recreational assets that contribute to the health, well-being, and identity of the
community; and

       WHEREAS, decades of industrial activity and pollution have left lasting impacts
on the river and its ecosystems, creating barriers to public access, environmental health,
and community connection; and

       WHEREAS, the Kalamazoo River Urban Wildlife Corridor Plan provides a
comprehensive framework for restoring habitat, improving water quality, expanding public
access, fostering environmental education, and guiding the redevelopment of blighted or
contaminated sites; and

       WHEREAS, the Kalamazoo River Urban Wildlife Corridor Plan is the result of
extensive collaboration among local governments, regional and local agencies, nonprofit
organizations, and community stakeholders, ensuring that it reflects a shared vision and
broad community values; and

        WHEREAS, successful implementation of the Plan will bring lasting environmental,
social, and economic benefits, including improved biodiversity, safer and more accessible
recreational spaces, reduced flooding risk, and stronger connections between people and the
river;

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the City of Kalamazoo formally
supports the Kalamazoo River Urban Wildlife Corridor Plan and recognizes its importance
in guiding future actions to restore, protect, and enhance the river corridor; and

       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the City of Kalamazoo commits to
collaborating with regional partners, community organizations, and residents to advance the
Plan’s goals, and to explore opportunities for aligning local policies, plans, and projects
with the vision set forth in the Plan.
