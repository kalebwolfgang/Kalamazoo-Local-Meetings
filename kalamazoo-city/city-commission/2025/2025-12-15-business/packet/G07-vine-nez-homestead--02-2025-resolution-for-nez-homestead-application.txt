                            CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                     RESOLUTION NO. ____

   A RESOLUTION APPROVING FIVE NEW APPLICATIONS FOR HOMESTEAD
  NEIGHBORHOOD ENTERPRISE ZONE CERTIFICATES PURSUANT TO PUBLIC
                          ACT 147 OF 1992

     Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City of Kalamazoo on
December 15, 2025 at 7:00 o'clock p.m., local time at City Hall.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

                                              Recitals:

        A. The Kalamazoo City Commission adopted the Vine Neighborhood Homestead
Neighborhood Enterprise Zone on September 29, 2008, so that residents of the City of Kalamazoo
would have the potential to take advantage of residential tax abatements while also providing
incentives to encourage neighborhood revitalization; and

        B. The City of Kalamazoo is in receipt of five applications for Homestead Neighborhood
Enterprise Zone Certificates for: 617 Locust Street (Alexander Demetralis), 524 Wheaton Avenue
(Peter Battani), 724 Oak Street (Clark Buckman), 610 McCourtie Street (Kanika Steele) and 708
Pioneer Street (Elizabeth Marko) who purchased the respective homes in the Vine Neighborhood
Enterprise Zone after September 29, 2008, and are living at the above-listed addresses as their
principal residences; and

        C. The applicants have indicated that they will invest at least $500 in property upgrades
for their respective homes within the first three years of their residence in the homestead properties.

THEREFORE, IT IS RESOLVED, that 617 Locust Street (Parcel No. 06-21-296-010,
Alexander Demetralis), 524 Wheaton Avenue (Parcel No. 06-21-428-010, Peter Battani), 724 Oak
Street (Parcel No. 06-21-281-005, Clark Buckman), 610 McCourtie Street (Parcel No. 06-21-448-
007, Kanika Steele) and 708 Pioneer Street (Parcel No. 06-21-476-015, Elizabeth Marko) will each
be awarded a Certificate of Neighborhood Enterprise Zone for ten (10) years (2025 to 2035),
allowing each of them to pay the reduced “Neighborhood Enterprise Zone Tax” until such time as:

       1. The applicant no longer lives in the above listed address as his/her principal residence;
       or,
       2. The applicant is not current in payment of the Neighborhood Enterprise Zone Tax; or
       3. Ten (10) years has elapsed from the time of the issuance of the certificate.
