RENTAL REHABILITATION

The City of Kalamazoo (“City”), a Michigan municipal corporation, whose address is 241 W. South
Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007, and Local Initiatives Support Corporation (“Subrecipient”), a non-profit
community development financial institution, whose address is 201 West Kalamazoo Avenue, Suite 310,
Kalamazoo Michigan 49007, entered into a Subrecipient Agreement effective September 6, 2023 (the
“Agreement) for the Rental Rehabilitation program (“Program”).

The Agreement was amended on November 19, 2024, to provide an extension through December

31, 2025 to complete the project. The City and the Subrecipient desire to amend the Agreement again to

lengthen the time of performance, and therefore consent to the following:

AGREEMENT AMENDMENTS

1. Subsection |. Scope of Services of the Agreement shall be amended so that the subsection reads:

B. Schedule of Tasks and Goals/Terms

The Program will complete the scope of services based on the following schedule:

Completed By: Activity:

June 1, 2024 Begin to identify and work with Program Partners towards unit
rehabilitation

December 31, 2025 Complete work with selected Program Partners to identify Housing
Choice Voucher holders

June 30, 2026 Complete rehabilitation of all rental units

June 30, 2026 Complete any final rental agreements with identified households

July 20, 2026 Provide final Program completion documents and reports to the City

Total Program outcomes by June 30, 2026: 48 rehabilitated units occupied by Housing Choice
Voucher eligible households.

2. Subsection V. Time of Performance of the Agreement shall be amended so that the subsection

reads:

A. Notice to Proceed

Services of the Subrecipient shall start when a Notice to Proceed is provided by the City and will
end when all reporting requirements have been met, but no later than July 20, 2026. The terms
of this Agreement and the provisions herein shall continue in force during the affordability period
of this Program or if the Subrecipient remains in control of SLFRF or other federal assets related
to this Program.

AGREEMENT AND SEVERABILITY

This Amendment constitutes a portion of the signed Agreement between the City and the Subrecipient
for the use of funds received under the original Agreement signed September 5, 2023. It supersedes and
amends those sections of the September 5, 2023 agreement that corresponds to the above sections of
this Amendment. In all other respects the September 5, 2023 agreement will remain the same and in full

force and effect.

If any provision of this Amendment is held invalid, the remainder of the Agreement shall not be affected

thereby, and all other parts of the Agreement shall nevertheless be in full force and effect.
GOVERNING LAW

This Amendment is governed under applicable Michigan law.

ASSISTANCE OF COUNSEL

Both parties had the assistance of or the opportunity to consult with legal counsel in the negotiation and
preparation of this Amendment. Therefore, no construction or ambiguity of this Amendment is resolved

against either party.
