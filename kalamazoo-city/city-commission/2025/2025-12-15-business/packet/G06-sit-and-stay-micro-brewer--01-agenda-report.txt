SUBJECT:       Approval of a Micro Brewer License for Sit and Stay Winery & Brewery at 260
               & 262 East Michigan Avenue

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission grant local government approval for a Micro
Brewer License for Sit and Stay Winery & Brewery, located at 260 & 262 E. Michigan Avenue,
and authorize the City Clerk to execute all documents required for submission to the Michigan
Liquor Control Commission (MLCC).

BACKGROUND:

Sit and Stay Winery & Brewery currently operates as Sit and Stay Winery and is located at 260
E. Michigan Avenue. On December 18, 2023, the City Commission granted local government
approval for the establishment’s Social District Permit and Commons Area Permit, allowing the
business to participate in the Downtown Social District prior to opening. Since that time, the
business has operated as a wine-focused establishment and has remained in good standing with
all municipal requirements.

The applicant is now seeking to expand its operations to include on-site beer production under a
Michigan Microbrewer License at 260 & 262 E. Michigan Avenue. In accordance with Michigan
Liquor Control Commission (MLCC) procedures, local government approval is required before
the state may act on the application. City staff have reviewed the request and determined that the
proposed use complies with applicable zoning and code provisions, and no outstanding issues or
departmental objections have been identified.
