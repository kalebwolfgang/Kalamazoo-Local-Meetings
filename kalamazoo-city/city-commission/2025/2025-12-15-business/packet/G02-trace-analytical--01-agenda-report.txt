RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a one-year contract extension for analytical
services from Trace Analytical Laboratories, Incorporated for a total amount not to exceed
$158,747.65. The term of this contract extension will be from January 1, 2026 through
December 31, 2026.

BACKGROUND:

The Public Services Environmental Services Laboratory is often required to provide analyses
that are beyond its present capacity in terms of available instrumentation or skilled labor. In this
situation, it is necessary for the Public Services Laboratory to contract with a commercial
laboratory to perform the needed analytical testing. The services to be provided would include
the analysis of drinking water, municipal and industrial wastewater, and residual solids for a
variety of organic and inorganic parameters.

Competitive bids were solicited from several area private laboratories in August 2024.
 Following a rigorous selection process that has been successfully used historically, Trace
Laboratories, Incorporated was determined to be the lowest responsive bidder to provide these
analytical support services and was awarded a three year contract.

The most cost-effective method to analyze samples for unique parameters is the use of contract
laboratory services. This has been determined based on several years of utilizing such services
and comparing them to internal costs. Contract laboratory services are needed for the
continuation of analytical testing to ensure compliance with all local, State EGLE and Federal
EPA Regulatory requirements.
