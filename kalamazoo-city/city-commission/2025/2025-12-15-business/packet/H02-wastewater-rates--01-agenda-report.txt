SUBJECT:       Resolution Detailing the Rates Charged for Wastewater as Authorized by Chapter
               28 of the Kalamazoo City Code

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a resolution to detail the rates charged for
wastewater as authorized by Chapter 28 of the Kalamazoo code.

BACKGROUND:

In accordance with the 2026 wastewater system budget, the City Administration recommends an
overall system revenue increase of 9% for wastewater. Approval and implementation of this
increase will allow the Utilities to continue to operate and maintain the system and make capital
improvements as outlined. Also, the new rates will work to improve reserve funds, debt coverage
ratios and allow for the continued stabilization of the Utility’s financial position. Continued
annual rate increases are needed to continue to support system wide capital needs and financial
health over the next several years.

The Foster Group was commissioned to complete a forecast of 2026 revenue requirements and a
model of financial performance over the next 5-year period.

The 2026 recommended wastewater rate increases will result in an estimated increase to a typical
Inside City residential customer’s quarterly bill by $2.69 monthly for wastewater and an
estimated increase to a typical Outside City residential customer’s quarterly bill by $2.02
monthly for the same services. Actual invoices for metered sales are based on recorded usage
and will vary in accordance with individual wastewater usage.

As the City and municipalities represented by the Regional Commission work to negotiate a new
long term wastewater services agreement an equalized rate model is recommended. 2026
through 2031 represent years of glide path rate equalization where in City and out City rates will
become equalized by 2031. Benefits of an equalized rate model include increased partnership
