                            CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                  RESOLUTION NO.

 A RESOLUTION GRANTING ELIGIBILITY FOR A PAYMENT IN LIEU OF TAXES
TO KALRECOVERY I LDHALP FOR THE PROPERTIES LOCATED AT A PORTION
        OF 333 EAST ALCOTT STREET, PARCEL NO: A PORTION OF
                      PARCEL NO: 3906-27-213-002

Minutes of a regular meeting of the Kalamazoo City Commission held on December 15, 2025 at
7:00 p.m., local time, in the City Commission Chambers at City Hall, 241 W. South Street.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

Recitals:

            A. Hollander Development Corporation (collectively, the “Sponsors”) has formed
               Kalrecovery I, Limited Dividend Housing Association Limited Partnership, for
               the purpose of securing Low Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC) and/or
               mortgage financing through the Michigan State Housing Development Authority
               for a development project located at a portion of 333 East Alcott Street, bounded
               by Reed Street, Belford Street, Bryant Street, and the Portage Creek, a portion of
               Parcel No. 3906-27-213-002 pursuant to Section 35-4 of the Kalamazoo City
               Code, and

            B. In connection with the allocation of low-income housing tax credits and/or the
               Michigan State Housing Development Authority (MSHDA) or Federally Aided
               Mortgage Loan for the project, the Sponsors are seeking a tax exemption pursuant
               to MCL 125.1415a (the “Act”); and

            C. Section 35-4 of the Kalamazoo City Code (“Ordinance”) and State Law MCL
               125.1415a provide that the City may allow owners of property developed and
               reserved for low-income individuals to make a PILOT to the City in the amount
               not to exceed the amount of tax which would be imposed on the property but for
               its exemption from taxation granted. Terms not otherwise defined herein have the
               meaning set forth in the Ordinance.

THEREFORE, IT IS RESOLVED THAT the Sponsors are deemed eligible for PILOT
payments for the 48 eligible housing rental units located at a site yet to be legally formed and
located as a portion of the parcel located at 333 E. Alcott (Parcel NO. 3906-27-213-003) subject
to the following terms and conditions:

          1. PILOT eligibility shall commence upon the Michigan State Housing
             Development Authority (MSHDA) or Federally-Aided Mortgage Loan closing
             and shall continue for as long as the Project is supported by a Mortgage Loan, or
             for 50 years, whichever comes first, so long as the Sponsor maintains the units as
             certified and registered rentals serving as affordable housing pursuant to the area
             median income rent restrictions approved by Kalamazoo City Commission per
             date of this resolution; and

          2. The Sponsor will establish the LDHALP as the owner of the project prior to the
             PILOT taking effect; and complete proper land subdivision; and

          3. The PILOT shall be calculated at a rate of four percent (4 percent) of annual
             shelter rents for 48 units during this time period, pursuant to the Ordinance; and

          4. The PILOT will not become effective until the Michigan State Housing
             Development Authority (“MSHDA”) notifies the City of Kalamazoo Assessor
             that it has certified that the project is eligible for the tax exemption and that the
             exemption would permit the project owner to make a PILOT; and

          5. Upon closing of the Michigan State Housing Development Authority (MSHDA)
             or Federally Aided Mortgage Loan, the project shall be owned by Kalrecovery I
             Limited Dividend Housing Association Limited Partnership.
