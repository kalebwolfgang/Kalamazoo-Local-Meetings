SUBJECT:      Appointments to Boards and Commissions

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the appointment of Kyle Ackerson to the
Community Development Act Advisory Committee as an at-large member for a term expiring
March 31, 2027.

BACKGROUND:

The City Commission appoints and reappoints members to the Community Development Act
Advisory Committee (CDAAC). The CDAAC Board is recommending the appointment of Kyle
Ackerson as an at-large member. The attached Nomination Report describes the nominee's
qualifications.

CDAAC's primary responsibility is to review applications from community organizations for
Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funds and to make recommendations to the City
Commission for the allocation of these funds.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.

Discussion
Ensuring that City boards and commissions have qualified members who are duly appointed has
a significant impact on the "Insights and Analysis" and "Organizational Principles" subgoals
under the Good Governance strategic goal.
