RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
USP Technologies for 2026 Pri-Tech Odor Control Services in the amount of $627,000.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant (KWRP) has added hydrogen peroxide in the
primary thickeners for the past two years with positive results. This hydrogen peroxide addition
will continue to focus on reducing hydrogen sulfide formation in the primary sludge thickening
system and the solids dewatering process. USP Technologies has 25 years of experience utilizing
this process as well as other peroxigen based treatment technologies and currently manages over
300 relationships, a mix of industrial and municipal. This experience around safe management of
peroxigen service-based programs is unmatched in the industry.
