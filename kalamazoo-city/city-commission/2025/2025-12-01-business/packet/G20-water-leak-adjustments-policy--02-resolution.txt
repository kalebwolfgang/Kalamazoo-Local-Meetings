                        CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                RESOLUTION NO. 25-

        A RESOLUTION ADOPTING AN AMENDED AND RESTATED CITY
           COMMISSION POLICY 60.2 - WATER LEAK ADJUSTMENTS

     Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City held on
December 1, 2025, at 7:00 o'clock p.m., local time.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

        WHEREAS, Chapter 38 of the Kalamazoo City Code was amended in 1992 to allow for
the creation and implementation of a water leak adjustment policy; and

       WHEREAS, on June 29, 1992 the City Commission adopted Resolution 92-82, which
created City Commission Policy 60.2, the Water Leak Adjustments policy; and

      WHEREAS, subsequent amendments to Chapter 38 of the Kalamazoo City Code have
changed the legal and operational framework for the City’s water utility; and

      WHEREAS, amendments to Policy 60.2 are necessary to modernize the Water Leak
Adjustments Policy and bring it into compliance with Chapter 38.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the attached amended and restated City
Commission Policy 60.2, Water Leak Adjustments, is hereby adopted.

      The above resolution was offered by ____________________ and seconded by
_________________.

AYES, Commissioners:

NAYS, Commissioners:

ABSTAIN, Commissioners:

RESOLUTION DECLARED ADOPTED.
