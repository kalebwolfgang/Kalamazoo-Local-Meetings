RECOMMENDATION:

The staff recommends that the City Commission offer the Ordinance as a first reading and set a
public hearing for January 26, 2026.

BACKGROUND:

4301 Stadium Drive is an 18.8-acre parcel along the western border of the City of Kalamazoo at
the corner of Drake Road and Stadium Drive. The parcel is wooded and slopes downwards
towards the south-east with some ridges and slopes. The 18.8 acres is currently dual-zoned with
Residential Multi-Dwelling (RM-15) zoning along the Stadium Drive frontage, and the majority
of the area is zoned Residential Single-Dwelling (RS-5). The property is also zoned with the
Natural Features Protection Overlay (NFP-O). The applicant is requesting that the property be
rezoned from the existing residential districts to the Community Commercial (CC) District.

Rezoning this property has been discussed twice previously. In January 2020, the property owner
requested a rezoning of the property to the Community Commercial (CC) District from the
current residential zoning of the then three parcels and requested to remove the NFP-O District.
The Planning Commission reviewed the requests at a public meeting and provided an
unfavorable recommendation on the requests to the City Commission for their consideration.
However, at the applicant's request, the case did not advance to the City Commission, and no
action was taken by the legislative review body on that request. In September 2023, the applicant
again applied to rezone the property from the RM-15 and RS-5 Districts to the CC District, but
this time without the removal of the NFP-O District. That application was withdrawn before the
public hearing by the Planning Commission.

FISCAL IMPACT:

A zoning change of a property does not necessarily have a fiscal impact on the property taxes.
However, the CC District supports a set of uses that could result in a higher tax revenue than the
set of uses permitted in the RS-5 and RM-15 Districts could yield.
