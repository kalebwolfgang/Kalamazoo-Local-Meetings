Nomination Rationale:
The City of Parchment experienced a water crisis in 2018 due to PFAS contamination and a lead action level
exceedance. The Parchment water system was taken out of service; the City of Kalamazoo built new water
stations to serve Parchment and replaced all lead service lines in Parchment. All work was funded via emergency
declaration funding and/or State of Michigan DWSRF funding. The lived experience of a community in crisis due
to PFAS and lead contamination provides valuable insight that can be used to support ongoing planning,
education and outreach. The Parchment City Manager has held a seat on the WSAC since inception due to the
valuable insight and experience that resulted from this crisis. In May 2025 the Parchment City Manager retired
and a new Parchment City Manager was hired by the Parchment City Commission in a public process.
