RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a RESOLUTION approving the schedule of
regular City Commission meetings for the 2026 calendar year.

BACKGROUND:

The proposed 2026 schedule of regular City Commission meetings was designed with the
following parameters in mind:

      • Both the Open Meetings Act (OMA) and City Commission Rule #1 require the
        Commission to establish a schedule of its regular meetings for the calendar year.
      • City Commission Rule #1 specifies the Commission will hold its Regular Business
        Meetings on the first and third Mondays of the month, except as specified in the annual
        schedule of meetings.
      • Section 8(a) of the City Charter requires that the City Commission meet “not less than
        twice each month.”
      • If a City-observed holiday falls on the first or third Monday of the month, the
        Commission meeting has been moved to the next business day. In 2026, this situation
        occurs only in September (Labor Day).
      • Committee of the Whole meetings are scheduled at 5:00 p.m. immediately prior to the
        Regular Business Meetings. Both types of meetings are scheduled to take place in the
        City Commission Chambers to allow for live-streaming.

Departures from the Above Patterns
The proposed schedule moves the second meeting in January from the third Monday (the 19th)
to the fourth Monday (the 26th) due to the observance of the Martin Luther King Jr. Day holiday
on January 19th and the National Day of Racial Healing on January 20th. To avoid conflicts with
community events for both observances, the January 19th meetings will take place a week later.

The proposed schedule moves the first meetings in May, August and November from the first
Monday to the second Monday to allow the City Clerk's Office to set up the Community Room
and City Commission Chambers on the nights before Election Days. The Clerk's Office uses the
Community Room and City Commission Chambers for the Absent Voter Counting Board, which
commences its work early on Election Day. Moving the City Commission meetings away from
the days prior to Election Days will allow Clerk's Office staff to move freely around City Hall
without fear of disturbing the meetings and will enable staff to complete the set-up of those
rooms without delay.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.

Discussion
Preparing the annual schedule of regular City Commission meetings most directly addresses the
Communication and Outreach subgoal under Good Governance.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Appropriate Depth of Engagement
Inform (one-way conversation) – no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

Engagement/Communication Tools
Once adopted, the City Commission's regular meeting schedule for 2026 will be physically
posted at City Hall, posted electronically on the City's website and social media, and emailed to
the people who have subscribed to receive notice when City Commission agendas and agenda
packets have been published.

FISCAL IMPACT:

There is no fiscal impact associated with this recommendation.
