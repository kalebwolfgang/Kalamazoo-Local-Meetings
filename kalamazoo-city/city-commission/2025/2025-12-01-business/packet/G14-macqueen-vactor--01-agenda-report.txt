SUBJECT:       Approve a MiDeal Cooperative purchase with Macqueen Equipment for a New
               Vactor 2100i (Bid Ref#00000-024.54)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a cooperative purchase of a new vactor
2100i from MacQueen Equipement in the amount of $604,500.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo, via the Municipal Stormwater Program MS4 permit, is required by the
Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE) to clean and inspect
11,584 catch basin and inlet structures. To meet this goal and to satisfy EGLE requirements, the
plan is to immediately begin cleaning and inspecting catch basins and inlet structures at a rate of
3,861 structures per year.

Macqueen provides world-class environmental and emergency solutions and services. Macqueen
understands the importance of keeping operations up and running. The MacQueen network spans
twelve states across the upper Midwest to help protect citizens, property and infrastructure.

Strategic Goal Impact:

Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
