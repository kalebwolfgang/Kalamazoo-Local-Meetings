SUBJECT:       Adoption of a Resolution Approving an Amended and Restated Water Leak
               Adjustments Policy

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a resolution adopting an amended and
restated Water Leak Adjustments Policy, which aligns administrative procedures with Chapter
38 of the City of Kalamazoo Water Ordinance and establishes a standardized process for leak-
related water billing adjustments.

BACKGROUND:

In 1992 the Commission approved an amendment to Chapter 38 to include a Leak Adjustment
Policy. Although the policy was established there were no clear guidelines as to the framework
of the policy. To modernize the policy and to be compliant with Chapter 38 of the Kalamazoo
Code of Ordinances which outlines the legal and operational framework for the City’s water
utility, including customer responsibilities, meter accuracy, billing processes, and the
maintenance of private water service infrastructure.

The proposed Leak Adjustments Policy provides clear eligibility requirements, documentation
standards, consistent calculation methods, and ensures fair administration of leak adjustments in
alignment with ordinance requirements.
