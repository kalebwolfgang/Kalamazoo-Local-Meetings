RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Wildlands Construction,
LLC for the Milham Park - Portage Creek Stream Restoration Project (bid reference # 98863-
008.0) in the amount of $356,347.26.

BACKGROUND:

The initial phase of restoration of Portage Creek took place in 2021, which removed the failing
dam and restored the downstream portion of Portage Creek to it's original condition. We have
been working closely with the Michigan Department of Natural Resources and Michigan
Department of Environment, Great Lakes and Energy to complete the work on Portage Creek.
 As part of this second phase, we were awarded a $75,000 grant from the DNR Fisheries Habitat
Grant Program. Once completed, Portage Creek will be a well-established trout stream for
people to fish at. The DNR has also indicated that it will be a trout stocking location in the
future to further establish a strong fish population and increase recreation at Milham Park.
