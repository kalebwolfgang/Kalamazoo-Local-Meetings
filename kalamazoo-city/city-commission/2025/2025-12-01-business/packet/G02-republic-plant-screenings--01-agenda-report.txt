RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Republic Services for
Transportation and Disposal of Plant Screenings in the amount of $100,800.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant (KWRP) mechanically screens plastic and
debris from the influent wastewater with barscreen units and fine screen units. Additionally City
vactor crews remove blockages in sewer lines before they reach the KWRP to prevent sanitary
sewer overflows (SSO’s). The material removed by the screens and from the sanitary sewer lines
are then transported to and disposed of at a landfill.
