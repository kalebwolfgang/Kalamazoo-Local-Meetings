RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a professional service agreement with
Jones and Henry Engineers Ltd for Construction Engineering Services in support of Contract
77A- Newton Ct., Fellows Ave., and John St. Improvement Project in the amount of
$295,500.00.

BACKGROUND:

Public Services has identified an improvement project for existing Sanitary Sewer on John Street
at Crosstown Parkway, as well as improvements to Water and Sanitary Sewer utilities on
Newton Court, and Fellows Avenue.
A 2021 study of sanitary sewer on John Street at Crosstown Parkway was completed by Jones
and Henry Engineers LTD, a pre-qualified engineering firm with the City of Kalamazoo. The
proposed work was outlined as option 3D in the submitted study.

The John Street segment of the project includes revisions to the existing Axtell Creek Culverts,
replacement of the John Street sanitary sewer inverted siphon, modifications of sidewalks and
ADA ramps, full depth reconstruction of John Street from Crosstown Parkway to Lake Street
including new Lake Street intersection radii, and surface improvements to Crosstown Parkway at
its intersection with John Street, and Upjohn Drive from John Street to Boreman Avenue.

The Newton Court segment of the project includes replacement work to drinking water and
sanitary sewer assets. The clay sewer, originally built in 1976 was identified as having missing
sections of pipe along with multiple large fractures spanning its length. The condition of this pipe
determines that it can not safely serve the residents contributing to it, with a high risk of failure

The Fellows Avenue segment of the project includes replacement work to drinking water and
sanitary sewer assets. The clay sewer, originally built in 1912 was identified as having breaks in
the pipe, fractures, joint offsets, and root infiltration along the length of the pipe. The broken and
fractured parts of the pipe contribute to a high risk of failure rating, that would impact the
sanitary sewer of residents to the West, as well as the residents of Fellows Avenue themself.
Additionally, offset joints and root infiltration can be frustrating to both the homeowner and
Public Services Collections staff. Root infiltration often leads to main stoppages, causing sewer
backups to homeowners, worsened by offset joints which make cleaning and inspection more
difficult for Collections staff. The 4” cast iron water main that serves Fellows Avenue was
constructed in 1936 and is also nearing the end of its useful life. Replacing both water and sewer
assets in the same construction window ensures that future disruptions to service, and road cuts
are minimized.

Public Services reached out to Jones & Henry Engineers to provide a quote for professional
design services. Jones & Henry Engineers provided a quote of $295,500.00 for this design work.
Public Services finds this quote acceptable and recommends approval of this purchase order.
