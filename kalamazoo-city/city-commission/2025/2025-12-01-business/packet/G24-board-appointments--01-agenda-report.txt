BACKGROUND:

The Building Board of Appeals (BBA) conducts hearings in three areas. First, they consider
situations where a property owner has been put on notice that the structure on their property has
been determined to be a “dangerous building” as defined in the Building Code. Second, the
board considers appeals regarding the determinations and decisions made by the city’s inspectors
in their enforcement of the Housing Code. Finally, the board grants variances to the Housing
Code where literal application of the code would result in exceptional practical difficulty.

Water System Advisory Council: The State of Michigan requires the operator of public water
supply systems serving more than 50,000 people create a Water System Advisory Council. This
council advises the City's Public Services Director as the operator of the system. The Council
consists of seven members nominated by the Public Services Director and appointed by the City
Commission. They serve staggered terms of three years. To be eligible, candidates must have a
demonstrated interest in or knowledge of lead in drinking water and its effects. At least one
member must be a local resident who is a customer of the water system and does not represent
the interests of any organization. The Water System Advisory Council must meet at least once
per year, and meetings can be called by the Public Services Director or the Council Chairperson.
The City Clerk received an application for appointment to the Water System Advisory from
Sarah Joshi and there is an available vacancy for appointment.
