RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Anlaan Corporation for
South Street Pedestrian Bridge Repair in the amount of $118,919.86.

BACKGROUND:

The South Street Pedestrian Bridge provides a key pedestrian link for visitors, employees, and
event traffic in the Central Business District. Over time, the bridge has experienced deterioration
in several structural and surface components that require repair to maintain the bridge’s safety
and functionality.

The City solicited bids to address these deficiencies through targeted structural and surface
rehabilitation. The work will extend the bridge’s service life and promote continued pedestrian
use. Anlaan Corporation was the low bidder and has a history of effectively completing similar
projects, and Public Works recommends awarding them the contract.
