RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a sole source purchase with D3W
Industries, for odor control chemicals for biosolids processing in the amount of $529,155.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo’s Water Reclamation Plant (KWRP) uses D3W industries odor reducing
chemicals for the treatment of biosolids loading, hauling and disposal. The KWRP has trialed
various chemicals and technologies to reduce biosolids odors over the past few years. D3W’s
bacteriological odor inhibiting chemicals have shown positive results with onsite H2S monitor
readings and reduced the odor complaints from our partnered landfills. The odor inhibiting
formulation is proprietary in nature and only available from D3W industries.
