RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the Economic Development Corporation
(EDC) Economic Initiative Fund (EIF) Loan for Winstons, LLC in the amount of $35,000.

BACKGROUND:

On November 20, 2025, the Economic Development Corporation (EDC) Board voted to
recommend to the City Commission the approval of the EDC Economic Initiative Fund (EIF)
Loan for Winstons, LLC.

Winstons, LLC is located at 224 E Michigan Ave, and owned by restauranteurs Casey Longton
and Rob Nicol, who also own Principle, Roca, and The Waiting Room. The concept for
Winstons, LLC is a classic, approachable, and affordable neighborhood bar that appeals to a
wide demographic. The bar will have a back patio on Bates Alley, joining a fast-growing area of
Downtown Kalamazoo, and the site of the increasingly popular live music program, Beats on
Bates. The capital stack for the project includes owner investment, an SBA Term Loan, and the
EIF loan. Longton and Nicol plan to employ 10 full-time and 5 part-time staff. Construction for
the project began on June 15, 2025, and plans to conclude on March 1, 2026. Longton and Nicol
sought out the EIF loan to assist with closing the gap on their project during a period of high
interest rates.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The Economic Initiative Fund (272-000-00.000-194.244 will advance $35,000 to the Economic
Development Corporation, which will issue a 10-year loan repayable at a 2% interest rate.

The City's Economic Initiative Fund will be replenished with the scheduled loan payments as
outlined in the promissory note. The long-term advance to the Economic Development
Corporation will be released with the receipt of the underlying principal payments.

There is no direct budgetary impact as loan disbursements are held within the balance sheet.
Future interest receipts will provide additional funding to the City EIF fund for future loans.
