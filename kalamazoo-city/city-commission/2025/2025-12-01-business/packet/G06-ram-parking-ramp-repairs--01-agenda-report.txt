               concrete repairs (Bid Ref #: 91300-012.0)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve two change orders with RAM Construction
Services for additional work at the Epic and Kalamazoo Mall parking ramps in the amount of
$227,903. The bulk of this extra work will address the deterioration that has occurred since the
ramps were last inspected, and was not specified in the original request for bids. Because RAM
is currently on-site performing work awarded earlier this year, we have the opportunity to
address additional needed repairs with funds available for that purpose.

BACKGROUND:

In 2024, Walker Consultants conducted a review of the condition of the Epic and Kalamazoo
Mall ramps and identified necessary repairs. The repairs associated with this work are the most
pressing of those needed.
