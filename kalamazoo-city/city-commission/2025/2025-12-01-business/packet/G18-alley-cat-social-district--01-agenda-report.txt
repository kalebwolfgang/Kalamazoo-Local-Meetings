RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a resolution to recommend the approval of a
Social District Permit for HB Social Ventures, LLC dba Alley Cat, located at 266 E Michigan
Ave Ste. B, Kalamazoo, MI 49007.

BACKGROUND:

The Michigan Liquor Control Commission (MLCC) allows licensed establishments to participate
in approved Social Districts, where alcoholic beverages may be sold for consumption in
designated common areas. The Kalamazoo City Commission approved the Downtown
Kalamazoo Social District in August, 2020. There are currently 32 businesses that participate in
this social commons area.

HB Social Ventures, LLC dba Alley Cat, recently opened a new downtown bar at 266 E
Michigan Ave Ste. B.

Alley Cat aims to serve as a welcoming community gathering place for neighbors, visitors, and
families, fostering connection and memorable experiences. The business is committed to
supporting local entrepreneurs, artists, and musicians by sourcing local products and showcasing
local talent. Alley Cat's participation in the social district program will enable them to engage
more fully in Bates Alley events and other Kalamazoo activities, strengthen partnerships with
nearby small businesses, and enhance their ability to welcome and connect with residents and
visitors alike.
