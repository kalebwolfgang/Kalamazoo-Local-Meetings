RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a professional services agreement with
Jones & Henry Engineers for the ML Avenue and 35th Street Transmission Water Main Project,
in the amount of $289,500.00.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo identified the ML Avenue and 35th Street Transmission Water Main
Project as a priority for 2026. This project includes installing 16-inch water main along East ML
Avenue from the existing end of the watermain east to South 35th Street and along South 35th
Street from East ML Avenue to Castle Creek Circle in Comstock Township. This project will
complete a watermain loop to the Castle Creek Development and provide another interconnect
between the High and East Side High Pressure Districts. This will provide redundancy,
increased reliability, and improved water quality. Public Services contracted with Jones &
Henry Engineers for design services for this project in 2025. Public Services requested a quote
for professional construction engineering services. Jones & Henry Engineers provided a quote of
$289,500.00 for this work. Public Services finds this quote acceptable and recommends
approval of this purchase order.
