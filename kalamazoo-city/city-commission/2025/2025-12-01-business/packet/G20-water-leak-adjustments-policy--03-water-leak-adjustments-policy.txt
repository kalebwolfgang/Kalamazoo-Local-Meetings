PURPOSE:

The purpose of this policy is to provide a uniform process for granting billing adjustments for
customers experiencing abnormally highwater usage caused by unintended leaks occurring on
the customer’s side of the meter.

This policy supplements and is fully consistent with Chapter 38 of the City Code, which governs
water service, metering, billing, and rates (§ 38-1 et seq.).

SCOPE:

This policy is administrative and supplementary to Chapter 38 and does not amend or supersede
any ordinance requirements.

Relevant provisions include:

§ 38-10 – Installation and maintenance of meters
Defines City and customer responsibilities for water metering and access.

§ 38-12 – Tampering or interference with meters
Prohibits tampering and clarifies customer liability for private plumbing.

§ 38-33 through § 38-36 – Water rates and charges
Establishes the rate schedules, billing cycles, and minimum service charges that remain in full
effect.

§ 38-41 – Adjustments and disputes
Authorizes the City to make billing corrections or adjustments where warranted.

All leak adjustments under this policy shall conform to the framework above. If any provision of
this policy conflicts with Chapter 38, the ordinance shall prevail.

POLICY:

A.     Eligibility

       1. This policy applies to all customers—residential, commercial, industrial, and multi-
          unit—receiving water service under Chapter 38.

       2. To qualify for adjustment:

           a. The leak must have occurred after the meter (customer’s side of the service line).

           b. The leak must be unintentional, non-recurring, and promptly repaired.

           c. Eligible examples include underground line breaks, burst indoor pipes, or
              defective fixtures.

      3. Ineligible causes include:

           a. Negligence, intentional acts, or improper installation

           b. Continuous irrigation, pool filling, or other discretionary water uses

           c. Repeated failure to maintain plumbing in good repair (§ 38-10)

B.    Customer Responsibilities

      1. Customers are responsible for maintaining private service lines and plumbing in
         compliance with § 38-10.

      2. The leak must be repaired within 30 days of discovery or notification.

      3. Customers must provide proof of repair such as a plumber’s invoice, materials
         receipt, or photographic evidence.

      4. The City may verify repair completion by site inspection or meter re-read.

      5. Failure to cooperate with inspection (§ 38-10 B) may disqualify the adjustment
         request.

C.    Adjustment Criteria

      1. Only one adjustment per account per 12-month period will be granted.

      2. The bill in question must show usage at least 50% higher than the 12-month average.

      3. Adjustment applies to no more than two consecutive billing periods.

      4. Adjustments are limited to unintentional, one-time leaks. Ongoing fixture issues are
         not eligible.

D.    Adjustment Calculation

      1. Determine the customer’s normal average usage based on the previous 12 months.

      2. The excess usage attributable to the leak will be billed at 50% of the applicable water
         and sewer rates under the schedules set forth in § 38-33.

      3. The fixed service and minimum charges in § 38-34 are not subject to reduction.

      4. If the customer demonstrates that the leaked water did not enter the sanitary sewer
         (e.g., exterior service line), the adjustment applies to the water portion only.

      5. No adjustment shall result in a total bill below the normal average usage amount.

E.    Application Process

      1. Submit a completed Leak Adjustment Request Form (as issued by the Utility Billing
         Office) within 60 days of the billing date.

      2. Include proof of repair (receipts or invoice), statement of cause, and repair date.

      3. Incomplete, late, or unverifiable submissions will be denied (§ 38-41 A).

      4. Approved adjustments will appear as a credit on the subsequent bill.

F.    Administration

      1. The Department of Public Services administers this policy.

      2. The Utility Billing Office reviews requests and determines eligibility consistent with
         Chapter 38 and City billing practices.

      3. The City reserves the right to inspect, deny, or revoke adjustments if
         misrepresentation or continued leakage occurs.

      4. Repeated or preventable leaks may result in suspension of future adjustment
         eligibility.

G.    Recordkeeping and Review

      1. All adjustments processed under this policy are subject to verification and audit under
         Chapter 38.

      2. All leak adjustment approvals and denials shall be logged per account for audit
         purposes (§ 38-41 C).

      3. The policy will be reviewed by Public Services staff and the City Attorney’s Office
         whenever Chapter 38 is amended to ensure continued compliance.

      4. The Kalamazoo City Commission may revise or repeal this policy at any time.

H.    Effective Date

      This policy is effective upon adoption by the Kalamazoo City Commission and remains
      in force until superseded.
