RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a sole source three year agreement in the
amount of $558,000 with Zhang Financial LLC for investment consulting services.

BACKGROUND:

Chris Ruppel as the primary contact working with Zhang Financial, has provided investment
consulting services for the assets of the City of Kalamazoo Employees’ Retirement System since
January 2000. Zhang Financial LLC as back-office support for consulting services since 2015
and the primary contractor since 2022. The Retirement Investment Committee (“RIC”) voted
unanimously to recommend extend the contract with Zhang Financial LLC.

Strategic Goal Impact:

       Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to
       effectively implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over
       the long term.
