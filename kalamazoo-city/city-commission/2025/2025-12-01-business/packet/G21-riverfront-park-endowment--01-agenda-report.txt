SUBJECT:       Mayors Riverfront Endowment Disbursement and Budget Amendment

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accept a distribution in the amount of $63,975 from
the Mayors Riverfront Park Endowment Fund for operational support for 2025 and approve a
budget amendment of $48,975.

BACKGROUND:

The Mayors Riverfront Park Endowment Fund was established to help support operational
expenses and equipment specifically for Mayors Riverfront Park and Homer Stryker Field. The
endowment is utilized annually to help offset expenses not included in the general fund budget
for the Parks and Recreation Department. The money drawn from the endowment comes from
interest accrued yearly, and only a portion of that interest is used for the annual draw to support
operations. A detailed list of the expenses can be found below in the Fiscal Impact Summary.
