[2026-03-12: Public Hearing re: the Rezoning of 4301 Stadium Drive]
Public Hearing

At 9:29 p.m. Mayor Anderson opened a public hearing to receive comments on an ordinance to rezone the property at 4301 Stadium Drive from RM-15 (Residential, Multi-Dwelling) & RS-5 (Residential, Single-Dwelling) to CC (Community Commercial).

Mayor Anderson reviewed the process for rezoning applications and clarified that this was the first time the City Commission had considered the rezoning of this property.

Assistant City Planner Bobby Durkee delivered a presentation entitled Request to Change the Zoning from Residential Single Family (RS-5) & Residential Multiple Family (RM-15) to Community Commercial (CC). A copy of the slides from this presentation was filed with the papers for the meeting.

Emily Palacios, legal counsel for the applicant, provided an overview of the rezoning request.

--- page 16 ---

[2026-03-12: Public Hearing re: the Rezoning of 4301 Stadium Drive (cont'd)]
Commissioner Questions:
Following the presentations, Assistant City Planner Durkee and Ms. Palacios responded to Commissioners' questions regarding: the changes between 2020 to 2026 that led the applicant to determine the property could be developed, even with the Natural Features Protection (NFP) Overlay; why the development of this parcel is critical when there are several vacant commercial properties along the Stadium Drive corridor; the qualities of the property that make it unique and a transition zone; recent examples of properties with the NFP Overlay that have been successfully developed as high-density commercial uses; the City's options for redress in the event the developer violates or ignores NFP requirements; the reason staff supports this rezoning now, when they removed the property from the mass commercial rezoning process in 2023; the path forward to keep this property open space; the purchase price for the property and how the public could acquire it; the possibility of a public/private partnership that would allow public recreational access; the reason the applicant purchased the property when it was not zoned commercial.

[2026-03-12: Denial of Rezoning Application for 4301 Stadium Drive]
Mayor Anderson asked for a motion to adopt an ordinance to rezone the property at 4301 Stadium Drive from RM-15 (Residential, Multi-Dwelling) & RS-5 (Residential, Single-Dwelling) to CC (Community Commercial), but no motion was offered.

Vice Mayor Duncan, seconded by Commissioner Praedel, moved to deny the application to rezone the property at 4301 Stadium Drive from RM-15 (Residential, Multi-Dwelling) & RS-5 (Residential, Single-Dwelling) to CC (Community Commercial).

Discussion took place regarding the form of the motion.

City Attorney Leal recommended that the Commissioner who made the motion include factual findings to support the denial.

Vice Mayor Duncan stated the City Commission had heard enough testimony to speak to the motion to deny.

Commissioners Slaby and Praedel reviewed the five zoning criteria and described how the proposed rezoning did not satisfy the criteria.

Commissioner Hess expressed agreement with Commissioners Praedel and Slaby.

With a roll call vote the motion to deny passed 7-0.
AYES: Commissioners Hess, Hoffman, Praedel, Slaby, Wilson, Vice Mayor Duncan, Mayor Anderson
NAYS: None

[2026-03-13: City Manager's Report]
City Manager Hankins referred Commissioners to his written report in the agenda packet. City Manager Hankins acknowledged the hard work of staff and community partners to coordinate emergency shelters and resources during the bitter cold weather.

[Commissioner Comments]
Commissioner Wilson affirmed community concerns about ICE and encouraged Commissioners, staff, and the community to combine their efforts to be intentional about protecting the City and its people. Commissioner Wilson suggested that protections be codified through ordinance changes that outlined exactly how the City would interact with ICE.

Commissioner Praedel remarked on the power and energy of the community coming together, as evidenced by the protest and vigil outside City Hall and the large number of people attending the public hearing inside City Hall. Commissioner Praedel spoke about the Social Justice Youth Awards and the recipients who inspired faith and hope for the future.

Commissioner Hess reported the Kalamazoo County Event Center Assessment District project, the youth indoor event center, was moving forward. Commissioner Hess remarked on the community solidarity displayed through the protest and vigil that evening, and she urged people to stand with their neighbors and show love where there was fear.
