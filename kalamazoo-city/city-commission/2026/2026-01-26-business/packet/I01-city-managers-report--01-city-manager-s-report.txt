Values: I believe the City of Kalamazoo does its best work when we work alongside our community partners
and residents to support, protect, and create opportunities for our neighbors and community. Each update
below reflects how our departments and the community are coming together to get things done. I’m
encouraged by how much we can accomplish when we work together.

1. Emergency Bulk Road Salt Purchase Pending

Keeping Our Streets Safe Together

Due to the sustained and more severe-than-anticipated winter weather events experienced this season, the
City's current salt reserves have unfortunately diminished to low levels. These weather patterns and the
resulting supply and demand conditions surpassed our reasonable planning assumptions, thus necessitating
prompt action to prevent any disruption to our essential snow and ice control operations, which directly
impact public safety. I will continue to share updates and additional details as they become available.

2. Collaboration with Kalamazoo Public Schools

Partnering for Student Success

I had the privilege of sitting down recently with Dr. Slade, the Kalamazoo Public Schools Superintendent, to
talk about shared priorities, including how the City and KPS can continue to work together in support of our
students and families. While we did not outline specific strategies during this conversation, we aligned on our
shared desire to see children succeed, strengthen communities, and expand opportunities for youth. I look
forward to continued dialogue and exploring ways we can implement strategies to advance these goals.

3. Missing Child Response

Protecting Our Most Vulnerable

We are fortunate to report the safe return of a 17-year-old who went missing during extreme cold conditions.
The Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety (KDPS) activated a large-scale response involving more than 40
officers and detectives, combining traditional search methods with advanced technology including drones and
the Fusus camera system, a public/private platform that allows community members and businesses to
voluntarily share camera feeds during emergencies. This effort helped narrow the search area and locate the
child quickly and safely. The successful outcome demonstrates the power of coordinated response and
community support in times of need.

4. Winter Weather Response and Support for Vulnerable Residents

Supporting Our Community in Extreme Weather

During recent extreme winter conditions, City staff led largely by public safety personnel, took enhanced steps
to protect residents, with particular attention to vulnerable populations. KDPS Emergency Management
played a central coordination role throughout the event. This included helping coordinate the opening of 16
warming shelters and centers across the community, securing free citywide transportation to shelters and
warming locations for individuals in need, and distributing critical information through local news outlets,
social media, RAVE Alerts, and in-person communication. The KDPS Community Service Team connected with
businesses, partners, and unhoused residents, distributing over 130 flyers detailing available resources.

As weather conditions continued to evolve, Emergency Management coordinated across City, County, and
KDPS teams to monitor shelter capacity and ensure resources remained available. As a result of the shared
efforts of concerned residents, community partners and agencies, and city and county officials and staff
(among others), 460 individuals were provided emergency shelter on the coldest nights, with approximately
100 individuals using warming centers at any given time. These efforts reflect strong cooperation among City
departments, concerned residents and community partners who join together to protect residents during
severe conditions. Going forward, City staff will continue to work with our partners and community, evaluating
the systems of support and identifying and closing service gaps as they are identified. I am grateful for the
community, organizations and people who demonstrate by their actions the importance of providing safe
places for people in our community.

5. Northside Ministerial Alliance 50th Anniversary & MLK Observance

Celebrating Community and Service

My wife and I had the privilege of attending the Northside Ministerial Alliance’s 50th anniversary celebration
at Mt. Zion Baptist Church, coinciding with the Martin Luther King Jr. holiday. The event highlighted decades of
service by faith leaders and community partners and emphasized the importance of civic engagement and
neighborhood support. The celebration underscored the value of longstanding community relationships in
building a stronger, more connected Kalamazoo.
