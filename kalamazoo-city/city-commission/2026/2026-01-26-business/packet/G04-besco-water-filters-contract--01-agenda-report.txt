RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an inventory contract with Besco Water
Treatment, Inc. for the three-year period, January 2026 – December 2028, for water filtration
systems and filters in the amount of $372,540.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo Purchasing Department invited vendors to submit a bid for this project.
Of the vendors who responded, Besco Water Treatment submitted a bid that met specifications,
terms and conditions.

Besco Water is one of the oldest and largest providers of residential water treatment services in
Michigan, Indiana, and Ohio. Besco Water is a leader in water softening, home water filtration
systems and purified bottled water throughout the Midwest. Besco specializes in residential
drinking water systems and offers a variety of products to meet water treatment needs.

The water filtration systems and filters will address water quality and public health concerns
surrounding lead content that continues to be a priority for the City of Kalamazoo. As part of
these efforts, the City of Kalamazoo provides water filters and filter replacements to our water
supply system customers. These items will be on-hand inventory to ensure demand is met on a
continuous basis.
