RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a two year contract extension with ASU
Group for excess workers' compensation premium in the total amount of $475,338.00.

BACKGROUND:

This is statutorily required workers' compensation coverage for the City. The City was
confronted with a workers' compensation insurance market which is seeking to impose greater
self-insured retention levels on employers (the City has had an $850,000 self-insured retention
(SIR) which has rarely been exceeded).

The City was able to retain its current SIR of $850,000 with a rate increase to .429 cents per
$100 of payroll. Our insurance agent, ASU Group, advised that the City should retain the SIR of
$850,000.
