RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Support Services Agreement for $55,000
with the Foundation for Excellence (FFE) for the period of January 1, 2026, to December 31,
2026.

BACKGROUND:

This Support Services Agreement includes accounting and financial functions as well as benefits
processing, treasury, information technologies, human resources, and purchasing/risk
management functions as detailed in the attached agreement. The agreement benefits both
parties, who mutually seek to ensure the stability of the Foundation for Excellence, which exists
to support the work of the City of Kalamazoo.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Appropriate Depth of Engagement
Inform (one-way conversation) – no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

FISCAL IMPACT:

This payment will be provided to the City of Kalamazoo for services provided as described in the
agreement. In the event that additional services are required or requested, such services shall be
billed to the Foundation for Excellence at the cost of $50.00 per hour plus the actual costs of
materials.

The annual Service Agreement payment of $55,000 is included in the 2026 budget as an income
for the City of Kalamazoo.
