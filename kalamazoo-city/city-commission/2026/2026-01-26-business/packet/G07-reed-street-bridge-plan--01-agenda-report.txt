RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accept a recommendation by the Regional Bridge
Council to include Reed Street Bridge in the three-year bridge plan, providing $126,350 (95%)
of funding for preventative maintenance and committing $6,650 as city match funding for this
project.

BACKGROUND:

At the June 16, 2025, City Commission meeting, approval was granted for the City of
Kalamazoo to request inclusion of the Reed Street Bridge (structure 4669) on the Local Bridge
Program three-year bridge plan. This request was based on OHM Advisors' inspection of the
bridge (attached), which indicated a need for preventative maintenance.

The request for inclusion was approved by the Regional Bridge Council (see attachment), and,
with this approval, the State of Michigan will finance and expend 95% of the project costs. The
city must provide 5% of funding as a match for the project, a total of $6,650. The project is
slated for 2028.
