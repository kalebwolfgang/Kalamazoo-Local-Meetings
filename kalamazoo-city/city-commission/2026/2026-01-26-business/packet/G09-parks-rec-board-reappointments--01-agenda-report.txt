SUBJECT:       Appointments and Reappointments to Boards and Commissions

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve he following reappointments to the Parks
and Recreation Advisory Board:

- the appointment of Isabella Ballez for a term expiring on January 1, 2029.

- the appointment of Kazmira Herberger for a term expiring on January 1, 2029.

BACKGROUND:

Strictly an advisory board, PRAB (the Parks and Recreation Advisory Board) makes
recommendations to the City Manager and City Commission regarding parks and recreation
planning and programming. PRAB includes 7 regular positions and 2 "youth representative"
positions. The City Commission approves the appointment and reappointment of members to
PRAB. The experience and qualifications of the nominees for reappointment are presented in the
attached Nomination Report.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.

Discussion
Ensuring that City boards and commissions have qualified members who are duly appointed has
a significant impact on the "Insights and Analysis" and "Organizational Principles" subgoals
under the Good Governance strategic goal.
