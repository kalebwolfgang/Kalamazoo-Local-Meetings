  4. Application Requirements for Reed Street over Portage Creek

A. Local Agency Contact Person

   Tom Palumbo, PE
   Public Works Division
   palumbot@kalamazoocity.org
   269-303-5142

B. The purpose of this application is for the preventative maintenance of the
   bridge carrying Reed Street over Portage Creek. Funding requested for
   removal and replacement of the HMA overlay, waterproofing and
   sidewalk sealing, substructure patching, and utility work. The City of
   Kalamazoo will commit to providing 5% of the construction cost for the
   local match on this structure. If selected the City would like to combine
   this project with the similar work of preventive maintenance for
   Stockbridge Avenue that was selected for 2027 funding.

C. Economic Importance of the Structure

   This structure is located approximately 0.28 miles west of Portage Street and
   0.23 miles south of Stockbridge Avenue on the south side of Kalamazoo.
   Reed Avenue is classified as a major collector, located in a residential area.

   Reed Street is a moderately traveled residential roadway seeing approximately
   2,900 vehicles a day. Residents use Reed St as a connection to the eastern and
   western neighborhoods south of downtown. Additionally, it serves as a link to
   many roadways providing access to public service agencies, major businesses,
   and commercial shopping.

   The bridge consists of side-by-side box beams with its top surface covered by
   an HMA overlay consisting of two lanes of traffic. Additionally, concrete
   sidewalks are adjacent to both directions of traffic. Overall, the structure is in
   fair condition and rated as a 6. The HMA surface is beginning to crack on the
   bridge and within the approach areas. This could lead to penetration of water
   and deterioration of the beams and abutments. Replacing the HMA surface on
   the deck and approaches, along with providing a waterproofing membrane
   would stop this potential issue. Furthermore, vertical cracking and areas of
   spalling and deteriorated patchwork can be seen in both abutments. A heavily
   rusted utility casing running under the bridge on the south side is present as
   well. Over time these cracks will widen, reducing the capacity of the structure.
   Continual rusting of the utility casing can also lead to deterioration of the
   abutment and reduction of structure capacity. Sealing these cracks and
   replacing the utility casing would mitigate these issues and increase the life of
   the structure.

        D. If there is a current detour, what does it affect?

           the area, hurting local businesses. Additionally, it may force more traffic onto
           adjacent local roads congesting the areas and requiring an increase in
           maintenance of these roadways. Located approximately 0.75 miles south of
           Bronson Hospital, a detour may increase travel times for emergency vehicles,
           a critical variable in times of emergency.

           The structure was built in 1983. In 2009 an HMA overlay was placed on the
