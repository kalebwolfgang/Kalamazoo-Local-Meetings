MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

This Memorandum of Understanding (“MOU”) is entered into between Charter Township
of Cooper and the City of Kalamazoo (together, the “Municipal Parties”) on the one hand and
Georgia-Pacific Consumer Products LP and Georgia-Pacific LLC (together, the “Georgia-Pacific
Parties”) on the other. This MOU refers to the Municipal Parties and the Georgia-Pacific Parties
individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”

RECITALS

A. The Parties have entered into a Release Agreement among the Parties and The 3M
Company that is effective as of October 30, 2020 (the “Release Agreement’).

B. The Release Agreement relates to the presence of per- and poly-fluoroalkyl
substances (“PFAS”) in certain residential wells.

C. The Release Agreement provides for the extension of municipal water by the
Municipal Parties in an area identified in the Release Agreement as the “Project Area.” Pursuant
to the Release Agreement, extension of municipal water includes, without limitation, new water
mains; trunk lines; service lines to homes; hookup from service lines to homes; administrative
costs; connection, frontage, and other fees; engineering, legal, and other contingencies; and all
related project fees and costs.

D. The Georgia-Pacific Parties have fully complied with their obligations under the
Release Agreement. The Georgia-Pacific Parties bear no further liability to pay any sums to the
Municipal Parties related to PFAS contamination or for the costs of the extension of municipal
water contemplated in the Release Agreement.

E. Not all homes in the Project Area that were eligible to receive a municipal water
connection chose to connect following the execution of the Release Agreement.

F, In addition, other homes located between the Project Area and the Kalamazoo River
may not be connected to municipal water at the time of this Agreement.

G. The Parties wish to enter this MOU to provide for voluntary reimbursement of the
connection costs to the City of Kalamazoo by the Georgia-Pacific Parties in order to facilitate
connection to municipal water by those homes that originally chose not to connect and were within
the Project Area, and homes between the Project Area and the Kalamazoo River which as of the
date of this Agreement are not connected to municipal water, all of which are located within the
area outlined on Exhibit B to this Agreement, which area for purposes of this Agreement is referred
to as the “Reimbursement Area.”

AGREEMENT

1. Municipal Parties Obligations. The Municipal Parties agree to undertake the
following:

a. Identification of Eligible Homes. The Municipal Parties shall provide to
the Georgia-Pacific Parties a list of all homes that were eligible to connect
to municipal water under the Release Agreement but did not connect to
municipal water or that are otherwise located within the Reimbursement
Area but are not, as of the date of this Agreement, connected to municipal
water. This is provided as a list as Exhibit A and a map as Exhibit B.

b. Connection Offer. The Municipal Parties shall offer and make available
connection to municipal water to eligible homes within the Reimbursement
Area within 180 days of the Effective Date of this MOU (the “Connection
Offer”) and shall reissue the offer to eligible homes annually thereafter for
5 years. The Connection Offer shall remain open to homeowners until the
date that is 5 years and 180 days after the Municipal Parties make their first
connection offer to any homeowner under this Agreement.

c. Connection Confirmation. The Municipal Parties shall provide notice to
the Georgia-Pacific Parties within 30 days of the Municipal Parties’ receipt
of a homeowner’s acceptance of the Connection Offer.

d. Cost Estimates. Before initiating any work to connect homes in the
Reimbursement Area to municipal water, the Municipal Parties shall
provide to the Georgia-Pacific Parties a written cost estimate for the
proposed connections (the “Cost Estimates”). The Cost Estimates shall
include a description of the scope of work to be performed and the estimated
costs of labor, materials, permits, equipment, and any other costs reasonably
anticipated to be incurred. The Parties agree that a single Cost Estimate may
be applicable to multiple homes in the Reimbursement Area. All work
necessary to connect a home to an existing municipal water main shall be
referred to as the “Connection Work.” All hiring of any contractors
necessary for the Connection Work shall be solely the obligation of the
Municipal Parties. Current costs are detailed in Exhibit C.

e. Documentation of Costs. Within 60 days of completing the Connection
Work for any home within the Reimbursement Area, the Municipal Parties
shall provide to the Georgia-Pacific Parties documentation of all costs
incurred by the Municipal Parties in performing the Connection Work
(“Documentation of Costs”). Documentation of Costs shall include all
costs incurred to perform the Connection Work, and any other supporting
documentation reasonably requested by the Georgia-Pacific Parties.

2. Georgia-Pacific Parties Obligation. The Georgia-Pacific Parties agree to
undertake the following:

a. Review of Cost Estimates. The Georgia-Pacific Parties shall review and
respond in writing to any Cost Estimates within 14 days of receipt. If the
Georgia-Pacific Parties do not provide a response in writing to the
Municipal Parties within that period, the Cost Estimate shall be deemed
approved.

b. Review of Documentation of Costs. The Georgia-Pacific Parties shall
review and respond in writing to all Documentation of Costs provided by
the Municipal Parties within 14 days of receipt (the “Cost Review Period”).
If the Georgia-Pacific Parties do not identify in writing any costs that the
Georgia-Pacific Parties dispute within that time, the Documentation of
Costs shall be deemed approved. Any disputes regarding the
Documentation of Costs shall be resolved according to Paragraph 2(d) of
this MOU. Approval of the Municipal Parties’ Documentation of Costs shall
not be unreasonably withheld.

c. Reimbursement. The Georgia-Pacific Parties shall reimburse the
Municipal Parties for all costs contained in the Documentation of Costs not
disputed by the Georgia-Pacific Parties. Reimbursement under this
Paragraph shall be made within 45 days following the conclusion of the
Cost Review Period. The Georgia-Pacific Parties shall only be required to
make a single payment for each reimbursement, without regard to the
division of costs borne by either Cooper Township or the City of
Kalamazoo.

d. Disputed Costs. If the Georgia-Pacific Parties dispute any portion of the
Documentation of Costs, then the Parties agree to meet and confer within
14 days in a good-faith attempt to resolve the dispute. Notwithstanding any
dispute, the Georgia-Pacific Parties shall be obligated to pay all undisputed
portions of the Documentation of Costs pursuant to the schedule established
in Paragraph 2(b) of this MOU.

e. Obligation Limitations. Nothing in this MOU shall be construed to require
the Georgia-Pacific Parties to undertake any Connection Work.

3. Effect of MOU. Nothing in this MOU shall be construed to create any right in, or
grant any cause of action to, any person not a Party to this MOU.

4. No Admission of Liability. Nothing contained in this MOU and no actions taken
by any Party with respect to this MOU shall be construed as an admission by any Party, person, or
entity of any act of wrongdoing or any liability of any kind. The Georgia-Pacific Parties do not
admit any liability for any of the released claims in the Release Agreement.

5. Entire Understanding of the Parties. This MOU constitutes the entire

understanding of the Parties regarding the matters addressed in this MOU and supersedes any prior
oral agreements between the Parties.

7. Interpretation and Drafting. This MOU is the result of negotiations among the
Parties, and no party shall be deemed to be the drafter of this MOU. No part of this MOU shall be
construed strictly for or against any Party.

8. Amendment. No amendment or modification of this MOU shall be enforceable
unless in writing and signed by the Party against whom enforcement is sought.

9. Severability. In the event that any portion of this MOU is declared or deemed to
be invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect the validity or
enforceability of the remaining portions of this MOU.

10. Governing Law. This MOU shall be interpreted, construed, and enforced
according solely to the laws of the State of Michigan, without regard to the choice of law rules of
that state.

11. Effective Date. This MOU shall be effective on the date the last signatory signs the
MOU.

12. | Separate Documents. The Parties may execute this MOU in multiple counterparts,
each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute the same

instrument.

13. Term and Termination. This MOU shall remain in effect until cessation of the
Connection Offer period established in Agreement Paragraph 1.b.
