RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with the Michigan Department
of Transportation for the 4-to-3 lane conversion of Portage Street with bike lanes from Cork
Street to Stockbridge Avenue in the amount of $1,039,402 of which $750,000 is federal grant
funds, and $289,402 is city share.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo has been granted funds from the Federal Highway Administration and
administered by the Michigan Department of Transportation under the “Vulnerable Road User
(VRU) program. These funds are intended to make streets safe for vulnerable users, including
children, the elderly, non-motorized, and motorized, by engineering calmer streets. This
program is consistent with the city’s “Streets for All Program” and will help the city advance its
efforts.

This project will consist of a 4-to-3 lane conversion with bike lanes along Portage Street from
Cork Street to Stockbridge Avenue, and include solar powered, rectangular rapid flashing
beacons installation along Portage Street at Cambridge Drive, Roseland Avenue, Franklin Street,
Lay Boulevard, Hays Park Avenue, Clinton Avenue and Egleston Avenue, and also include
overband crack fill and fog seal, median refuge islands, concrete curb and gutter, decorative
concrete pavement, curb ramps, pedestrian lighting, pedestrian push buttons, permanent signing
and pavement markings; and all together with necessary related work.

Approval of this agreement will allow the City to complete the project in accordance with
finalized design requirements, regulatory obligations, and MDOT LAP procedures, and to
improve the safety of Portage Street, its sidewalks and crossings, and all of the intersecting side
