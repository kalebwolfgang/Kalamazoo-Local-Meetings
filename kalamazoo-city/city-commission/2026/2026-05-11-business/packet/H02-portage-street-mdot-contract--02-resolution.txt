                           CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                    RESOLUTION NO. __

            A RESOLUTION APPROVING CONTRACT 26-5078 FOR THE 4 TO
                   3 LANE CONVERSION OF PORTAGE STREET

       Minutes of a regular meeting of the Kalamazoo City Commission held on May 11, 2026 at
7:00 p.m., local time, in the City Commission Chambers at City Hall, 241 W. South Street.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

Recitals:

   1. The City of Kalamazoo had determined it is advantageous and desirable to contract with
      the Michigan Department of Transportation (MDOT) for the following public
      infrastructure improvements:

       4-to-3 lane conversion with bike lanes along Portage Street from Cork Street to Stockbridge
       Avenue and solar powered, rectangular rapid flashing beacon installation along Portage
       Street at Cambridge Drive, Roseland Avenue, Franklin Street, Lay Boulevard, Hays Park
       Avenue, Clinton Avenue and Egleston Avenue, including overband crack fill and fog seal,
       median refuge islands, concrete curb and gutter, decorative concrete pavement, curb ramps,
       pedestrian lighting, pedestrian pushbuttons, permanent signing and pavement markings;
       and all together with necessary related work.

   2. MDOT has designated the agreement describing the terms and conditions for this project
      “Contract No. 26-5078”.

   3. MDOT requires a certified resolution from the local jurisdiction identifying the person(s)
      authorized to execute their contracts.

It is Resolved:

   1. Contract No. 26-5078 with the Michigan Department of Transportation is approved.

   2. The following officials are authorized to execute said contract on behalf of the City of
      Kalamazoo:

       Malcolm Hankins, City Manager
