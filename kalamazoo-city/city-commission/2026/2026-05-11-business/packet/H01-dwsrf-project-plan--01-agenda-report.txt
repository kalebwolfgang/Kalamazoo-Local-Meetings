SUBJECT:       Resolution Adopting a Project Plan for Water System Improvements

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission authorize the City Manager to sign a Resolution
Adopting a Project Plan for Water System Improvements and designate James J. Baker, P.E., as
Authorized Project Representative.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo is applying for a FY 2027 Drinking Water State Revolving Fund loan
from the State of Michigan for assistance with the Non-Copper Water Service Replacements.
 The scope of this project is to replace approximately 1900 non-copper service lines focused in
the Milwood, South Westnedge, Oakwood, Winchell, and W Main Hill neighborhoods from
August 2027- November 2029. This project is expected to complete non-copper service line
replacements in the Kalamazoo Regional Water System. Removing non-copper service lines is
beneficial for public health as well as regulatory compliance. This resolution is part of the State
of Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE) requirements.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The project plan is requesting funding assistance for $19 million. This funding opportunity is
being pursued due to the potential for principal forgiveness, which would lower the loan amount.
 Additionally, Drinking Water State Revolving Fund loans offer bond rates lower than what the
City could obtain on the open bond market.
