SUBJECT:       Approval of a Memorandum of Understanding with Georgia Pacific Parties and
               Cooper Township for Water Connections (Bid Ref#00000-026.52)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the Memorandum of Understanding and
authorize the City Manager to sign all related documents.

BACKGROUND:

In 2020, the City, Cooper Township, Georgia Pacific Parties, and The 3M Company entered into
a Release Agreement that related to the presence of per- and poly-fluoroalkyl substances (PFAS)
in certain residential wells. That agreement allowed residents to be connected to the City of
Kalamazoo Regional Water System at no cost to them.

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is to provide voluntary reimbursement of
connection costs to the City of Kalamazoo by the Georgia Pacific Parties in order to facilitate
connection to municipal water by those homes that originally chose not to connect and were
within the project area. Public Services worked with the Georgia Pacific Parties and Cooper
Township to identify the eligible homes.

This MOU is a generous benefit to the community and the customers whose private wells are
impacted by PFAS. Public Services recommends the approval of this MOU.
