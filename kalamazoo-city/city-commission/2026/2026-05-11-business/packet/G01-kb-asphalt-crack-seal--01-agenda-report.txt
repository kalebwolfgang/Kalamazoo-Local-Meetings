RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract extension with K & B Asphalt
for crack seal of City streets in the amount of $116,910.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo Public Works Division places a high degree of importance on
preventative maintenance of streets as a more cost-effective way to provide higher-quality roads.
Part of this effort is to prevent moisture from entering pavement with the use of overband crack
sealing. This is one of the most cost-effective ways to maintain streets.

Public Works previously contracted with K & B Asphalt in 2024, for crack sealing, and was
pleased with the quality of work.
