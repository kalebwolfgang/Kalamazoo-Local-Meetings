SUBJECT:       Kalamazoo Growlers Fireworks Permit

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a request from the Kalamazoo Growlers to
publicly display fireworks at Homer Stryker Field on May 29, June 12, June 26, July 4, July 10,
and July 31, 2026.

BACKGROUND:

Pursuant to State law, the Kalamazoo Growlers, through its pyrotechnic operator, has submitted
an application for a permit to publicly display fireworks at Homer Stryker Field, located at 251
Mills Street. Fire Marshal Scott Brooks has endorsed the application, and the pyrotechnic
operator has provided the required insurance certificate naming the City of Kalamazoo as
additionally insured.

In 2016 the Growlers changed their fireworks displays to mainly 1.4 consumer fire works -
comets and mines which have a nice light show without the height and firepower of a true 1.3
commercial shell. These shows are safer and less noisy than traditional displays.
