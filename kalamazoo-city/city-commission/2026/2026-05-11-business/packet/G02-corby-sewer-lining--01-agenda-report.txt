RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Corby Energy Services Inc for Sanitary Sewer Lining in support of Wastewater’s Asset
Management Plan in the amount of $216,796.30.

BACKGROUND:

Wastewater Collections utilizes Cured in Place Pipe (CIPP) liner inside the sanitary sewer to
serve as means of extending the useful life by decades for Sanitary Sewer pipe assets. The age of
existing assets ranges from 40 - 120 years old. The City of Kalamazoo currently has a
Sourcewell contract with Corby Energy Services (CES), who have performed trenchless CIPP
Lining rehabilitation for the City for many years, and has been tasked with doing this work.

CES provides construction, engineering, and support services throughout the great lakes area and
eastern states. CES maintains the highest level of quality in an efficient, safe and healthy work
environment for their employees, customers, and the public.

Wastewater engineering staff solicited a quote from CES to perform CIPP lining work on
Sanitary Sewer pipe assets with Duke St. and March St., to be executed as a supplemental to the
contract previously approved by the City Commission on August 4th, 2025. The sanitary sewer
work was planned to be performed in phases. This supplemental request is for work performed
ahead of schedule but is consistent with asset prioritization identified in the Wastewater Asset
Management and Capital Improvement Plans.
