RECOMMENDATION
The City of Kalamazoo Civil Rights Board recommends the City Commission approve the following:

      -   The appointment of Keshia Dickason for a partial term expiring March 31, 2027

Selection Process
Applicants Considered:

N/A

Interview Process and Final Recommendation:

The Civil Rights Board accepts board applications year-round. Ms. Dickason was interviewed on February 5th,
2026, by the CRB Nomination Committee. Ms. Dickason was recommended to the full board for approval by the
nomination committee due to their experience in the Kalamazoo County Road Commission, experience in the
housing sector, as well as their volunteer experience. Ms. Dickason is also a city resident which covers the
ordinance requirements for residency. Ms. Dickason was approved by the board unanimously on April 1st, 2026,
to move forward with appointment by the City Commission.

Appointee Name: Keshia Dickason

Nominee Qualifications for Appointment
Training, Experience, Education and Skills:
Ms. Dickason currently works as a CEO/Owner of KD Staffing Solutions and has had previous experiences as the
executive director of Girls on the Run International, District Manager in the Michigan Senate, and executive
director of Partners in Housing Transitions. Ms. Dickason was also a commissioner in the Kalamazoo County
Road Commission from 2021 to 2026. She has a Master’s degree in Healthcare Administration and a Bachelor of
Science in Business Management.

Contribution to Diversity:
Gender: Female                 Race: Black or African American           Ethnicity: Non-Hispanic or Latinx
Age Range: 45-54
Additional Contribution to Diversity: Keshia Dickason is a City of Kalamazoo resident and is deeply involved with
the Kalamazoo community.

Policy and Position Compliance
Dual Board Memberships:
Currently, nominee does not serve as a member of another city advisory board.

Term Limits:
This will be a partial term for this nominee. Their term will end on 3/31/2027.

Residency:
Board Residency Requirements: Maximum of two non-City residents
Nominee Residency Status: Resident
Neighborhood: Oakland/Winchell

Nomination Rationale:
Ms. Dickason was chosen by the nomination committee and approved by the board due to her background in a
commissioner in the Kalamazoo Road Commission and vast experience in housing and non-profit organization,
and their deep academic background.
