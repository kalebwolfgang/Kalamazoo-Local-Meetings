SUBJECT:      Appointments to Boards and Commissions

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the following appointments to boards and
commissions:

- the appointment of Katie Bloom to the Environmental Concerns Committee
  for a term expiring on January 1, 2027.

- the appointment of Elizabeth Kraatz to the Parks and Recreation Advisory Board
  for a term expiring on January 1, 2027.

BACKGROUND:

The Environmental Concerns Committee (ECC) monitors significant environmental trends
affecting the City of Kalamazoo and advises the City Manager and City Commission regarding
environmental matters. The Committee also serves as a forum for environmental issues. The City
Commission approves the appointment and reappointment of members to the ECC. The
experience and qualifications of the nominee are presented in the attached Nomination Report.

Strictly an advisory board, PRAB makes recommendations to the City Manager and City
Commission regarding parks and recreation planning and programming. PRAB includes 7
regular positions and 2 "youth representative" positions. The City Commission approves the
appointment and reappointment of members to PRAB. The experience and qualifications of the
nominee for appointment are presented in the attached Nomination Report.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Good Governance - Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
