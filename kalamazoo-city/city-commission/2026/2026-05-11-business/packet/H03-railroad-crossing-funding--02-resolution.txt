                          CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                   RESOLUTION NO. __

          A RESOLUTION ACCEPTING A $3,225,000 FUNDING PACKAGE,
       INCLUDING A RAILROAD CROSSING ELIMINATION GRANT FROM
            THE FEDERAL RAILROAD AMINISTRATION AND STATE
        INCENTIVE PAYMENTS FROM THE MICHIGAN DEPARTMENT OF
          TRANSPORATION; APPROVING RELATED DOCUMENTS AND
         AUTHORIZING THE SIGNATORIES THEROF; AND APPROVING A
             BUDGET AMEMDMENT IN THE AMOUNT OF $3,225,000

       Minutes of a regular meeting of the Kalamazoo City Commission held on May 11, 2026 at
7:00 p.m., local time, in the City Commission Chambers at City Hall, 241 W. South Street.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

Recitals:

   1. The City of Kalamazoo is in the process of addressing challenges in its downtown
      transportation network.

   2. The City has determined it is advantageous to accept a Railroad Crossing Administration
      Grant from the Federal Railroad Administration to create a plan and comprehensive
      strategy to address deleterious impacts of the downtown railroad network and facilities.
      The name of this project is: Kalamazoo’s Future: Railroad Crossing Elimination.

   3. The City has determined it is advantageous and desirable to contract with the Michigan
      Department of Transportation (MDOT) to receive State Incentive Payments for the
      following public infrastructure work:

       Closures of the Amtrak railroad crossings at Elm Street and the Elm Street Crossover and
       the closure of the Grand Elk railroad crossing at Water Street.

   4. MDOT has designated the agreements describing the terms and conditions for these
      projects “Contract No. 2026-0498,” “Contract No. 2026-0499,” and “Contract No. 2026-
      0500.”

It is Resolved:

Railroad Crossing Administration Grant

   1. A Railroad Crossing Administration Grant from the Federal Railroad Administration in the
      amount of $2,575,000 is hereby accepted and the related grant agreement approved.

   2. The Grants Division Manager is designated as the authorized signatory to sign the grant
      agreement and subsequent reports on behalf of the City of Kalamazoo.

MDOT State Incentive Payment Contracts

   3. Contract No. 2026-0498, Contract No. 2026-0499, and Contract No. 2026-0500 with the
      Michigan Department of Transportation are approved.

   4. The following officials are authorized to execute said MDOT contracts on behalf of the
      City of Kalamazoo:

       Malcolm Hankins, City Manager

       Rebekah Kik, Deputy City Manager

       Laura Lam, Chief Operating Officer

Budget Amendment

   5. A budget amendment in the amount of $3,225,000 is hereby approved.

       The above resolution was offered by Commissioner and seconded by Commissioner ____.
