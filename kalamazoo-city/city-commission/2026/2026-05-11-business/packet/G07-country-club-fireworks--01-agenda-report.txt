SUBJECT:       Kalamazoo Country Club Fireworks Permit

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a request from the Kalamazoo Country
Club for a permit to publicly display fireworks at 1609 Whites Road on Friday, July 3, 2026.

BACKGROUND:

Pursuant to State law, the Kalamazoo Country Club has submitted an application for a permit to
publicly display fireworks at 1609 Whites Road. The Department of Public Safety has reviewed
the request, and Fire Marshal Scott Brooks has endorsed the application. The required insurance
certificate has been provided by the pyrotechnic operator.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Inviting Public Places - Vibrant streets, exceptional parks, and welcoming activities.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Appropriate Depth of Engagement
Inform (one-way conversation) – no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

The applicant will be asked to reach out to surrounding neighborhoods and inform them of the
date and time of the fireworks display.
