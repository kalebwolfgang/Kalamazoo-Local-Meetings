[Regular Agenda]

[2026-18-17, Resolution 26-21: 2027 DWSRF Project Plan]
Public Services Director James Baker delivered a presentation entitled 2027 DWSRF Project Plan Public Meeting - Lead Service Line Replacements: City Hall, Monday, May 11, 2026, 7:00 p.m. A copy of the slides from this presentation was filed with the papers for the meeting.

Commissioner Questions:
    Following the presentation, Director Baker responded to Commissioner questions regarding outreach to residents (including language accessibility), the implementation of a feedback loop, the impact of having received other DWSRF loans on the likelihood the City will be successful with this application, whether all the lead service lines to be replaced in this phase had been identified, the neighborhoods where this work had been completed, and whether smart meters would be installed in conjunction with service line replacements.

Public Comments: none

Commissioner Slaby, seconded by Vice Mayor Duncan, moved to adopt a RESOLUTION adopting a Project Plan for Water System Improvements and designating James J. Baker, P.E. as the authorized project representative.

With a roll call vote this motion passed 7-0.
AYES: Commissioners Hess, Hoffman, Praedel, Slaby, Wilson, Vice Mayor Duncan, Mayor Anderson
NAYS: None

[2026-18-18, Resolution 26-22: MDOT Contract No. 26-5078, Portage Street 4-to-3 Lane Conversion]
Director Baker delivered a presentation entitled Portage Street - Cork to Stockbridge MDOT Contract No. 26-5078: City Commission, Monday, May 11, 2026. A copy of the slides from this presentation was filed with the papers for the meeting.

Commissioner Questions:
    Following the presentation, Director Baker responded to Commissioner questions regarding disruption mitigation for affected businesses, the installation of dual sidewalks rather than bike lanes, how the bike lanes with this project would fit with the larger bike network, whether the project area included Heritage Community, the types of pedestrian refuge islands that would be installed, the merge section just north of Reed Street, the urban chip-seal process, the number of miles of roadway the City maintained, the right turn lane at Miller Road, and the speed limit(s) on Portage after the project.

Public Comments:
    Tobi Hanah-Davies, City resident, expressed support

Commissioner Slaby, seconded by Commissioner Hess, moved to adopt a RESOLUTION approving Contract No. 26-5078 with the Michigan Department of Transportation for the 4-to-3 lane conversion of Portage Street with bike lanes from Cork Street to Stockbridge Avenue in the amount of $1,039,402, which includes $750,000 in federal grant funds and a City share of $289,402.

With a roll call vote this motion passed 6-1.
AYES: Commissioners Hess, Hoffman, Praedel, Slaby, Wilson, Mayor Anderson
NAYS: Vice Mayor Duncan

[2026-18-19, Resolution 26-23: Railroad Crossing Elimination Planning Grant and MDOT Funding]
Director Baker delivered a presentation entitled Railroad Crossing Elimination Planning Grant: City Commission, Monday, May 11, 2026. A copy of the slides from this presentation was filed with the papers for this meeting.

Commissioner Questions:
    Following the presentation, Director Baker responded to Commissioner questions regarding a "do-nothing" option, the transition of ownership for property currently owned by the railroads, the future use of vacated railroad rights-of-way, job losses associated with moving the rail yard, neighborhood support for the project, the timeline for the planning process, next steps once a plan is in place, funding sources for the work to implement the plan, to-date actions the City has taken related to this project, and the impacts of the recent train derailment downtown.

Public Comments: none

Commissioner Hoffman, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, moved to adopt a RESOLUTION accepting a $3,225,000 funding package, including a Railroad Crossing Elimination Grant and State Incentive Payments, and approving a budget amendment in the amount of $3,225,000.
