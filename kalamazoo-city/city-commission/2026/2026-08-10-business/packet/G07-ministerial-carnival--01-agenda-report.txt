SUBJECT:       Budget Allocation for NMA Anti-Gun Violence Community Carnival

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the use of City Commission Initiative
funds for the Northside Ministerial Alliance Anti-Gun Violence Community Carnival in the
amount of $20,000.

BACKGROUND:

The Northside Ministerial Alliance (NMA) has requested $20,000 from the City of Kalamazoo to
support the upcoming 6th Annual NMA Anti-Gun Violence Community Carnival. The event will
take place on August 29, 2026, from 11:00 am – 3:00 pm, behind Lincoln International Studies
School located at 912 N. Burdick Street. The City has supported this event for the past five years,
contributing $15,000 in 2021 and 2022 and $20,000 in the years 2023 to 2025.

The NMA Anti-Gun Violence Community Carnival is a free event to unite local families, faith
leaders, and organizations against gun violence. The event is open to the entire community and
offers carnival rides, games, live music, and family entertainment. In addition to free food and
refreshments, the event includes community resource booths with organizations offering services
and support.

The City Commission Initiative (CCI) fund was created to give the Commission an opportunity
to support agreed-upon priority project(s) without affecting fund balance. The current practice is
to allocate $100,000 each year to the CCI Fund. As CCI funds are from the General Fund,
funding is limited to allowable municipal expenditures and not for private benefit. All CCI
requests must be reviewed by the City Attorney to ensure municipal expenditure compliance.
Allocations from this budget must be approved by a majority vote of the City Commission.

CCI funding is intended to be spent within the budget year it was allocated; however, the CCI
fund had a balance of $88,000 at the start of 2026, in addition to the $100,000 annual allocation.
