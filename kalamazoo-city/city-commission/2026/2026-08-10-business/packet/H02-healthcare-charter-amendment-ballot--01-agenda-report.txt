RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a RESOLUTION to set the election date and
determine the ballot language for a charter amendment to establish a Citizens’ Healthcare Action
Committee and require a Healthcare Action Plan

BACKGROUND:

On July 1, 2026, Community Action Michigan filed with the City Clerk a citizen-initiative
petition. The initiative proposes to amend the Kalamazoo City Charter to provide for the
following:

  ● Establish a Citizens’ Healthcare Action Committee (“Citizens’ Action Committee”);

  ● Create a Healthcare Action Plan and make recommendations to address healthcare costs and
healthcare inaccessibility;

  ● Prescribe the powers and duties of the Citizens’ Action Committee;

  ● Prescribe the Citizens’ Action Committee’s composition and organization; and

  ● Require public overview and adoption of the Healthcare Action Plan.

The purpose of this initiative appears on page 2, Section B of the attached resolution, which
provides:“The health, safety, and welfare of the residents of the City are served by regular,
transparent, and public evaluation of healthcare affordability, accessibility, insurance and benefit
coverage, and medical service availability. A recurring process for collecting public testimony,
reviewing data, and evaluating policy impacts will assist the City and the jurisdiction’s federal

The petition did not contain a ballot question when it was filed with the City Clerk. However,
since then the City Attorney and Community Action Michigan’s legal counsel have worked
together and submit the jointly-agreed ballot question that appears in Section 5 of the resolution.

The City Clerk has verified the signatures on the petition and has determined that the number of
valid signatures on the petition is sufficient under the Home Rule City Act and that the petition is
a valid initiatory petition for a charter amendment under the Home Rule City Act. If the
Commission approves the resolution, the ballot proposal will be added to the Tuesday,
November 3, 2026, general election. Upon approval of the resolution, the City Clerk will
transmit a copy of it to the Governor and Attorney General of the state of Michigan for review
and approval. If the ballot proposal is adopted by the voters, it will be added to the City Charter
under new Section 200.
