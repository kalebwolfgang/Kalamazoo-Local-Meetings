RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve acquisition of six (6) easements for the
Cork St. Improvement Project in the amount of $777.00, and authorize the City Manager to sign
all related and necessary documents regarding acquisition of the easements.

BACKGROUND:

The Cork St. Improvement Project involves construction on Cork Street to implement a road diet
configuration, improving safety conditions for pedestrians, bicyclists, and vehicular traffic along
the corridor. Construction of new sidewalk ramps and other ancillary work requires the
acquisition of permanent infrastructure easements on six (6) parcels adjacent to the corridor.

Metro Consulting Associates (MCA) negotiated the required easements on behalf of the City. A
consistent valuation rate of $107,158 per acre, or $2.46 per square foot, was applied based on the
Market Study Report to calculate landowner compensation. Permanent easements were valued at
100% of this rate, as the landowners lose control of the easement area. One property owner,
Local 150 Building, L.L.C., elected to donate its easement to the City in lieu of compensation.

The enclosed original executed easements require the signature of the City Manager prior to
recordation. Upon execution, the documents will be recorded with the Kalamazoo County
Register of Deeds, and copies of the recorded easements will be provided to the respective
landowners.
