SUBJECT:       Resolution Establishing a Branch of the City Clerk's Office for the November 3,
               2026 Election

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a RESOLUTION establishing a branch of the
City Clerk's Office on the campus of Western Michigan University in support of the election on
November 3, 2026.

BACKGROUND:

Beginning in 2020, the City Clerk's Office has operated a branch office on the campus of
Western Michigan University for City of Kalamazoo voters during General Elections (the even
year November elections). The City Clerk's Office has budgeted for, and would like to continue
offering, a branch office on the campus of Western Michigan University for the General Election
on November 3, 2026.

Three weeks before the November 3rd election, the City Clerk will open a branch office at the
Fetzer Center on the campus of Western Michigan University for the purpose of voter
registration and issuing and receiving absentee ballots. This branch office will open three weeks
prior to Election Day and will continue through Election Day. The hours will follow the hours
for the Early Voting Site at the Fetzer Center (9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.) with the following
exceptions: the hours change for the day prior to Election Day (8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.) and
Election Day itself (7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.) to align with statutory hours for absentee voting and
election day voting precincts. The branch office will be able to serve all City voters, not just
WMU students or people registered to vote in the campus precinct.

The cost for all these activities is included in the FY2026 General Fund Budget for the City
Clerk's Office - no outside funding sources will be used. It should be noted the implementation
of these service enhancements is for the November 3rd election only.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Election Administration is a Community Program, but it does not directly impact any of the
Community Strategic Goals.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Appropriate Depth of Engagement

Inform (one-way conversation) – no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

Engagement/Communication Tools

After the resolution is adopted, the Clerk's Office will communicate about the opportunities for
voter registration and absentee voting through the City's website and social media accounts. Prior
to the November election, a direct mailing will be sent to highlight this information. As required
by law, information about the Clerk's Office hours and locations will be reported to the Secretary
of State, and this information will be available through the Michigan Voter Information Center
(www.mi.gov/vote). The Clerk's Office will work with voter advocacy groups to ensure they
have the correct information to spread through their networks.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The estimated cost to operate the branch office is $20,000. Funds for the branch office are
included in the FY2026 General Fund Budget for the City Clerk's Office - no outside funding
sources will be used.
