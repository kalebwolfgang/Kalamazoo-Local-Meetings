             MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

This Memorandum of Understanding (“Agreement”) is entered into on this
_____ day of _____________, 2026, (“Effective Date”) by and between:
THE CITY OF KALAMAZOO, a Michigan municipal corporation, with offices located at
241 W. South Street, Kalamazoo, MI 49007, hereinafter referred to as the "City."
And PETER AND WANITA COMENSOLI, married individuals, with a mailing address of
762 W. Main Street, Kalamazoo, MI 49006, hereinafter referred to as "Comensoli."
Collectively, the City and Comensoli shall be referred to as the “Parties.”
WHEREAS, Comensoli owns certain real properties located within the City of Kalamazoo,
consisting of two parcels legally described in Exhibit A attached hereto and identified as:
   1. 762 W. Main Street, Kalamazoo, MI 49006 (Parcel ID: 06-16-475-001)
   2. 757 Creek Street, Kalamazoo, MI 49006 (Parcel ID: 06-16-475-040)
      (collectively referred to as the "Comensoli Property"); and

WHEREAS, the City is advancing municipal infrastructure improvements, including the
construction of pedestrian pathways and the infrastructure related to Arcadia Creek -
Phase 1 (W. Main to Westnedge) and Streets for All: West Main Street and Douglas
Avenue Improvements projects; and
WHEREAS, to facilitate these public improvements, the City requires two permanent
Public Infrastructure Easements and two Temporary Grading Permits over designated
portions of the Comensoli Property; and
WHEREAS, the City plans to vacate Elm Street and Creek Street, and any such vacation
shall follow the City’s established street-vacation process and procedures, with the
vacated area apportioned to the respective landowners based on existing title.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, promises, and
considerations set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:

I. GRANT OF EASEMENTS BY THE COMENSOLI
Upon compliance with the terms of this Agreement, Comensoli agrees to execute and
deliver to the City the following: Public Infrastructure Easement and Temporary Grading
Permits covering portions of the Comensoli Property (Parcels 06-16-475-001 and 06-16-
475-040):
   1. Public Infrastructure Easement: A permanent easement for the purpose of
      constructing, operating, maintaining, repairing, improving or replacing components
      of the City’s infrastructure including but not limited to culverts, pipelines,
      connections, leads, mast arms and poles, pavements, sidewalk and all other
      components of the City’s public infrastructure systems for the Arcadia Creek
      Project- Phase 1 (W. Main to Westnedge).

   2. Public Infrastructure Easement: A permanent easement for the for the purpose
      of constructing, operating, maintaining, repairing, improving or replacing
      components of the City’s infrastructure including but not limited to pipelines,
      connections, leads, mast arms and poles, traffic signals, pavements, sidewalk and
      all other components of the City’s public infrastructure systems and public use of
      a Streets for All: West Main Street and Douglas Avenue Improvements.

   3. Temporary Grading Permits: A temporary permit allowing the City to excavate,
      restore and transition the existing parcel as designed for the Arcadia Creek
      Project- Phase 1 (W. Main to Westnedge).

II. CITY OBLIGATIONS
In exchange for the granting of the easements, the City agrees to perform the following
actions:
   1. Obtain an appraisal to determine the value of the proposed acquisitions:
      permanent easements and temporary grading permits. The City shall make a
      good faith offer to acquire said easements and grading permits based on the
      values established by the appraisal.

   2. The City shall restore the disturbed areas as designed including but not limited to:
      removal of existing parking area, reconstruction of a new parking area including,
      pavement, and stormwater drainage improvements at no cost to Comensoli.

   3. Restoration to the Comensoli parcels shall be immediately upon completion of the
      installation of the culvert. Restoration of the parking areas shall happen in stages,
      beginning with a gravel parking area and transiting to a paved parking area with
      striped parking spaces. The City and/or its contractors shall keep Comensoli
      updated on the arability of the parking areas.

III. ZONING GUIDELINES                   &       CONTEMPLATED            SITE      PLAN
PARAMETERS
To facilitate the Comensoli contemplated footprint expansion, the City’s Planning and
Zoning Department shall provide Comensoli with a comprehensive outline of current
municipal regulations. This framework shall address the following requirements for the
proposed site plan:
   1. Parking Requirements: A full breakdown of the City's minimum and maximum
      parking space rules based on the proposed building use and footprint square
      footage.
   2. Green Space & Landscaping: Precise calculations and guidelines governing
      required permeable surface areas, landscaping buffers, and open space
      percentages.
   3. Variance Process: Clear guidelines on procedural steps for obtaining a zoning
      variance from the Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA).

IV. MILESTONES
The Parties explicitly establish the following strict schedule for the progression of
negotiations and project scope:
   1. Milestone: City will provide a copy of the appraisal and a good faith offer.
   2. Milestone: Comensoli will provide a response to the good faith offer within a
      reasonable timeframe.
   3. Milestone: Upon agreement Comensoli shall execute all necessary documents,
      not to be conditioned or delayed.
   4. Project Construction Scope: The City plans to begin physical excavation for
      Arcadia Creek - Phase 1 (W. Main to Westnedge) and the Streets for All: West
      Main Street and Douglas Avenue Improvements projects in the 2027 calendar
      year.

VI. MUTUAL DEPENDENCY
The Parties agree that upon execution of the Public Infrastructure Easements and
Temporary Grading Permits for both the Arcadia Creek - Phase 1 (W. Main to Westnedge)
and the W. Main St. and Douglas Avenue projects, the City shall tender payments as
necessary and proceed with the projects per the plans.
VII. SUCCESSORS AND ASSIGNS
This Agreement is legally binding and shall inure to the benefit of, and be binding upon,
the Parties hereto, their respective heirs, personal representatives, legal successors, and
assigns.
