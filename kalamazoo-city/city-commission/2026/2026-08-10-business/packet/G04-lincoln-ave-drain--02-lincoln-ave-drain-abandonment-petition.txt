                                      CITY OF KALAMAZOO

                                      LINCOLN AVE DRAIN

                          PETITION TO ABANDON LINCOLN AVE DRAIN

To the Kalamazoo County Drain Commissioner:

       City of Kalamazoo hereby petitions for the abandonment of the Lincoln Ave Drain as
provided in Chapter 17 of Public Act 40 of 1956, as amended.

        Your petitioner further shows that the Lincoin Ave Drain has ceased to be of public utility
and is no longer conducive to the public health, convenience, and welfare.

       This petition has been authorized by the City of Kalamazoo governing body. A copy of the
Resolution is attached hereto.
