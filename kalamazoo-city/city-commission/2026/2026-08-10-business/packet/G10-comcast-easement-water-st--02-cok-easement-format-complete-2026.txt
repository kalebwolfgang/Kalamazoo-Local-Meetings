                                     EASEMENT

      THIS AGREEMENT dated __________________, 2026 between Comcast Cable
Communications Management, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, having an
address at 41112 Concept Drive, Plymouth, Michigan 48170 (“ Comcast”) and the City of
Kalamazoo, a Michigan municipal corporation, whose address is 241 West South Street,
Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007(“City” or “Grantor”).

Recitals:

   A. Grantor owns certain parcels of real estate at 128 E. Water Street and 162 E.
      Water Street,
      Kalamazoo MI 49007 in the city of Kalamazoo, Kalamazoo County, Michigan,
      which is more specifically described on the attached Exhibit A (“Property”).

   B. Comcast desires to obtain an easement on, over, under, across and through a
      portion of the Property.

   C. Grantor agrees to grant to Comcast a utility easement on, over, under, across and
      through a portion of the Property for the purposes as set forth in this Agreement.

THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, conditions and agreements set
forth below, the parties agree as follows:

   1. Grant of Nonexclusive Easement. In consideration of only One Dollar ($1.00),
      Grantor grants and conveys to Comcast a nonexclusive, permanent easement and
      right-of-way on, over, under, across and through that portion of the Property as
      described in the attached Exhibit B (“Easement Area”).

   2. Purpose of the Easement. Comcast shall use the Easement Area for the purpose
      of constructing, operating, maintaining, repairing, improving or replacing
      underground and/or above-ground communications, broadband, cable television
      system or other similar facilities, including, without limitation, lines, cables,
      amplifiers and other electronic equipment, towers and poles (the “Facilities”).

   3. Access to the Easement Area. Comcast shall have the right of ingress and egress
      to the Easement Area from the public right-of-way, and only as necessary, through
      the Property, to access the Facilities and perform such installation, construction,

     operation, maintenance, repair, reconstruction, replacement, or removal of the
     Facilities, whether by pedestrian or vehicular access, subject to the following
     conditions:

     a) All activities shall be completed without cost to Grantor and in such a manner
        as not to unreasonably interfere with Grantor’s use of or the business
        operations conducted on the Property.
     b) Repair any damage to the Easement Area, the Property, or the Grantor
        Improvements caused by the exercise of Comcast's rights granted under this
        Agreement. If necessary, such repair shall include re-grading and reseeding
        any lawn areas disturbed by Comcast’s activities.
     c) Exercise reasonable care to protect any fixed structures, landscaping, and
        other improvements on the Property during the performance of Comcast’s
        activities.
     d) Grantor agrees not to construct any buildings or permanent structures on or
        within the Easement Area in a way that interferes with the easement, unless
        prior written approval is given by Comcast for a particular building or structure,
        which approval shall not be unreasonably withheld.
     e) When the Facilities are no longer in use, this Easement shall terminate, and
        Comcast shall remove the Facilities and restore the Easement Area to its
        original condition, normal wear and tear excepted.

  4. Authority of Representatives. The parties represent and warrant to the other that
     this Agreement and its execution by the individual(s) on its behalf are authorized
     by members or governing body of that party.

  5. Binding Effect. This Easement Agreement shall bind the parties, and their
     successors and assigns. The parties do not intend to confer any benefits on any
     person, firm, corporation, or other entity which is not party to this Agreement.

  6. Amendment. This Easement Agreement shall not be amended or modified except
     in writing signed by all parties.

  7. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed under applicable Michigan law. All
     parties had the assistance of legal counsel in the negotiation and preparation of
     this Agreement. Therefore, no construction or ambiguity of this Agreement is
     resolved against either party.

  8. Exemption from Transfer Tax. This easement is exempt from transfer tax pursuant
     to MCL 207.505(5)(a) and MCL 207.526(6)(a). [If consideration is actually paid,
     transfer taxes will be assessed based on the amount paid under both MCL 207.505
     & 526]
