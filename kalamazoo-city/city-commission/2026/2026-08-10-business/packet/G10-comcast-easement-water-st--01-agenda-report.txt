SUBJECT:       Comcast Easement for 459. N. Burdick

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve and accept the easement with Comcast
between the addresses of 128 E Water and 162 E Water St for underground fiber optic upgrades;
and authorize the City Manager to sign all related and necessary documents regarding the
acceptance of the easement.

BACKGROUND:

Comcast has requested the granting of a utility easement across City-owned property located
between 128 E. Water Street and 162 E. Water Street to facilitate the installation, operation,
maintenance, repair, replacement, and future access of underground fiber-optic facilities. The
proposed installation is intended to support planned infrastructure improvements within the
surrounding area while enhancing the reliability, capacity, and resiliency of Comcast's fiber-optic
network. Additionally, the placement of these facilities underground will reduce the presence of
above-ground utility infrastructure, contributing to improved system performance and the overall
aesthetic character of the corridor.
