RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a professional service agreement with
Hurley & Stewart, LLC for Professional Construction Engineering Services for the Burdick
Street (Emerson to Stockbridge) Water Main Replacement Project, in the amount of
$123,500.00.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo identified the Burdick Street (Emerson Street to Lovell Street) Water
Main Replacement Project as a priority for 2026 and 2027. This project includes replacing two
smaller water mains with a single 24-inch water main, fire hydrant replacement, water service
work, sanitary sewer replacement (only Vine St to Lovell St), sanitary sewer lead two-way
cleanout installation, and storm sewer work to accommodate water main separation
requirements. HMA cold milling and resurfacing work, including spot concrete curb and gutter
replacement, spot concrete sidewalk and ramp replacement, and pavement marking work will
also be done due to water main construction activities. The project was planned to be completed
over 2 years with separate contracts for each year. The first phase was planned for Emerson to
Vine with the second phase from Vine to Lovell the following year. The first phase was bid
earlier this year but had no responsive bidders due to Contractor availability for a jack and bore
required at the Crosstown Parkway intersection. It was decided to bid the project again this year
with revised construction limits that did not include work at the Crosstown Parkway intersection.
 The revised limits of Emerson to Stockbridge will make the needed connection from the 24-inch
water main that was installed on Balch Street in 2024 to the Central Treatment Plant at the SW
corner of Stockbridge Avenue. This project will also eliminate a 10-inch water main that has a
history of multiple breaks.
