SUBJECT:       Approve Valley Oaks Project Utility Easement (Bid Ref#00000-026.82)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve and accept the easement with the Board of
Trustees of Western Michigan University, a Constitutional Corporation and institution of higher
education (whose address is 1903 W. Michigan Avenue, Kalamazoo, MI 49008) for water main
installed as part of the Valley Oaks Project; and authorize the City Manager to sign all related
and necessary documents regarding the acquisition of the easement.

BACKGROUND:

Construction of new water main was recently completed for the Valley Oaks Project. As all
public water mains and sanitary sewer are required to be in a dedicated easement or public right
of way, Board of Trustees of Western Michigan University, a Constitutional Corporation and
institution of higher education (whose address is 1903 W. Michigan Avenue, Kalamazoo, MI
49008) in collaboration with the City of Kalamazoo Attorney has drafted this agreement for
approval.
