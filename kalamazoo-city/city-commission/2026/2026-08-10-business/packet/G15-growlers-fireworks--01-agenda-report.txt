SUBJECT:       Kalamazoo Growlers Fireworks Permit

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a request from the Kalamazoo Growlers to
publicly display fireworks at Homer Stryker Field on August 29, 2026.

BACKGROUND:

On May 11th the City Commission approved a permit for the Kalamazoo Growlers to publicly
display fireworks at Homer Stryker Field on six dates, ending on July 31st. One of those displays
was canceled due to inclement weather, and the Growlers would like to reschedule that display
for August 29th. Pursuant to State law, the pyrotechnic operator has submitted an application for
a permit to publicly display fireworks at Homer Stryker Field on August 29th. Fire Marshal Scott
Brooks has endorsed the application, and the pyrotechnic operator has provided the required
insurance certificate naming the City of Kalamazoo as additionally insured.

restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.
