SUBJECT:       Approval of Amendment to Extend the Term of a Lease of the Kalamazoo
               Transportation Center by the National Railroad Passenger Corporation (Amtrak)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an Amendment of the lease with the
National Railroad Passenger Corporation (Amtrak) for lease of space in the Kalamazoo
Transportation Center to extend the term of the lease for a period of 5 years.

BACKGROUND:

The property at 459 N. Burdick and the train depot located there were acquired by the City of
Kalamazoo on June 3, 1976, from Consolidated Rail Corporation (Conrail). Together with other
properties this property became the Kalamazoo Transportation Center which was designed to
house inter-city, intra-city and rail passenger services for the City of Kalamazoo. As a condition
of the purchase a deed restriction was placed on the property which reserved to Amtrak the right
to use so much of the building and adjacent properties as may be required for rail passenger and
related purposes. The deed restriction also required Amtrak to reimburse the City for utility,
maintenance, and tax expenses actually incurred, and required an agreement between the City of
Kalamazoo and Amtrak. A lease was entered into between the City and Amtrak reflecting the
obligations of the deed restriction. From time to time the lease has been reinstated or amended to
update provisions of the lease or to renew its term.
On January 16, 2007, the City of Kalamazoo entered into a lease agreement to renew and
reinstate the immediately preceding lease agreement between the parties that had expired. The
January 16, 2007, lease agreement expired June 30, 2026, and there is a need to extend the term
of the lease to allow Amtrak to continue to use the property and the City to be reimbursed
consistent with the deed restriction. The amendment will be effective retroactively to July 1,
2026.
