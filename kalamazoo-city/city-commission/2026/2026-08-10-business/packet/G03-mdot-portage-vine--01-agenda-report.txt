               improvements to Portage and Vine intersection (Bid Ref#00000-026.84)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an agreement with Michigan Department
of Transportation for improvements to Portage and Vine intersection.

BACKGROUND:

The Michigan Department of Transportation (MDOT) is completing a series of improvements to
Portage and Vine Street rail road crossings. As part of these improvements, they have asked the
City to add a "No Turn on Red" signal at this intersection. The project will cost an estimated
total of $39,513.25 (see agreement) and MDOT is providing funding to pay for 90% of the
requested improvement. The city will provide the additional 10% as match funding.
