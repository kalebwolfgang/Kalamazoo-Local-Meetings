Burdick Street Water Main Project
Our infrastructure initiatives continue with a water main project on South Burdick Street between East Emerson
Street and East Stockbridge Avenue. Closure of this section is expected to remain in place through Friday, Nov.
13. This project will replace aging water mains with a new 24-inch transmission main, connect it to the new
water main that was installed on Balch Street in 2024, and eliminate an older 10-inch water main that has
experienced multiple breaks. All intersections within the project will remain open, and access to homes and
businesses will be maintained throughout construction. Motorists should follow the signed detour route.

There were some initial traffic control and access challenges that needed to be worked out on the Burdick
Street project. Additional access has now been provided through the Central Wellfield Property and
adjustments are being made to traffic control to better support businesses. Staff continue to work with
consultants and the contractor to support local businesses and property owner needs.

Once work in the section is complete, construction will move to the next section of the corridor, with work
continuing north toward Lovell Street during the 2027 construction season. This phased approach helps
minimize the amount of roadway that is under construction at any one time while maintaining access to
homes, businesses, and downtown destinations throughout the project. The investment will improve the long-
term reliability of the City’s water distribution system, while also replacing fire hydrants, upgrading water
services, and restoring the roadway after construction.

More information on this project can be found at www.KalamazooCity.org/BurdickSt We thank everyone’s
patience as we continue investing in the infrastructure that delivers safe, reliable water to our community.

CDBG Application Process
The City of Kalamazoo is beginning its annual competitive application process for Community Development
Block Grant (CDBG)-eligible public service activities. The City anticipates allocating $120,000 in federal grants
from the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) for the 2027 Program Year which is July 1,
2027, through June 30, 2028.

Fundable activities must be eligible under the rules and regulations of HUD, must be consistent with the City of
Kalamazoo’s 2025-2029 Consolidated Plan, and must assist low-income persons within Kalamazoo’s city limits.
Organizations must also be a 501c3 nonprofit organization to apply. A mandatory pre-application training must
be attended by a representative of participating organizations to qualify. Please see the Apply for HUD Grant
Funding webpage on Kalamazoo's website for more details.

Boil Water Advisories
Boil Water Advisories are precautionary. They are issued while we test to confirm the water has not been
affected. Our team completes two separate rounds of testing before advisories are lifted. While under a Boil
Water Advisory, affected customers are advised to boil their tap water for two minutes before consuming it, or
use bottled water until the advisory is lifted. No special actions are needed for water used for hygiene. The
vast majority of Boil Water Advisories are issued out of an abundance of caution and lifted within three days.

Verburg Park Kayak Launch
The new kayak launch at Verburg Park is now open. The new launch provides easier access to the park’s
lagoon for kayakers and canoeists. It also includes an accessible transfer system designed to help people with
mobility disabilities get in and out of their watercraft more easily, making the water more accessible to more
members of the community. The launch is located next to the park’s self-service kayak rental kiosk, making it
easy to rent a kayak or bring your own and enjoy the water.

Verburg Park’s new playground is also complete and open, while work continues on the bicycle pump track
and other park improvements. We’ll continue to share updates as the remaining amenities are completed.
The Verburg Park improvements are a $480,000 project that is funded through the City’s Capital Improvement
Program and was identified as a priority in the Kzoo Parks Master Plan.
