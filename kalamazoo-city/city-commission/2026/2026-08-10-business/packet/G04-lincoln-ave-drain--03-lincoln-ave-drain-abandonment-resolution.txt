                            CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                   RESOLUTION NO. 26-__

                  A RESOLUTION FOR PETITION TO ABANDON THE
                             LINCOLN AVE DRAIN

      Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City of Kalamazoo held on
August 10, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. local time at the City Hall.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

The following resolution was offered by Commissioner                    and supported by
Commissioner                      .

       WHEREAS, the Lincoln Ave Drain is a legally established county drain pursuant to Public
Act 40 of 1956, as amended; and

       WHEREAS, the Lincoln Ave drain to be abandoned is located and established within City
of Kalamazoo; and

       WHEREAS, an inspection of the area where the drain is established shows that the drain
has ceased to be of public utility and is no longer conducive to the public health, convenience, and
welfare; and

        NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT, the Mayor is authorize the filing of a
petition for the abandonment Lincoln Ave of the drain.

        BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED THAT the Mayor is authorized to execute the petition
for the abandonment of the drain.

       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Clerk shall forward to the Kalamazoo County
Drain Commissioner a copy of this Resolution for the petition for the abandonment of the drain.

AYES, Commissioners:

NAYS, Commissioners:

ABSTAIN, Commissioners:
