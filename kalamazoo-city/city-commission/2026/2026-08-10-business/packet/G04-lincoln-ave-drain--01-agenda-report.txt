SUBJECT:       Lincoln Avenue Drain - Petition to Abandon

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a resolution to file a petition to abandon
the Lincoln Ave Drain.

BACKGROUND:

The Lincoln Avenue Drain was superseded and replaced by City of Kalamazoo owned
stormwater drainage structures, and conveyance pipe networks from E. Michigan Avenue at
Wallace, through 1900 E. Michigan Ave to an outlet at the Kalamazoo River prior to1955. The
Lincoln Avenue Drain as a County Drain does not exist and should therefore be abandoned.

Strategic Goal Impact:

implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
