RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Michigan Paving &
Materials for the 2026 Local Streets Paving Project in the amount of $483,596.00.

BACKGROUND:

The 2026 Local Streets Paving Project is part of the City's annual pavement preservation and
rehabilitation program and is funded through the City's Capital Improvement Program (CIP). The
project is intended to improve pavement conditions, extend the service life of local streets,
enhance ride quality, and reduce future maintenance costs.

The project includes pavement milling and resurfacing, hand patching, utility structure
adjustments, monument box adjustments, gate well adjustments, pavement markings, and other
miscellaneous work necessary to complete the roadway improvements. Work will be completed
on the following streets:
• Winton Avenue (Portage Street to Lincolnshire Boulevard)
• Lincolnshire Boulevard (Winton Avenue to Cork Street)
• Lowden Street (Parkview Avenue to End)
• Edwards Street (Paterson Street to Dunkley Avenue)
• Myrtle Street (Pitcher Street to Porter Street)
• Bridge Street (River Street to Dwight Avenue)
• Garland Avenue (westendge Ave to Rose Street)

The project was publicly advertised in accordance with the City's purchasing requirements, and
bids were received and publicly opened. Michigan Paving & Materials submitted the lowest
responsive and responsible bid. Public Works reviewed the bid proposal, verified the contractor's
qualifications and references, and determined that the bid is responsive to the project
