SUBJECT:       Memorandum of Understanding between Comensoli's and the City of Kalamazoo
               to advance infrastructure improvements related to Arcadia Creek and West
               Main/Douglas Avenue Projects.

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accept and enter into all terms to the agreement
with Comensoli's to advance municipal infrastructure improvements, including construction of
pedestrian pathways and the infrastructure related to the Arcadia Creek - Phase 1 (W. Main to
Westnedge) and Streets for All: West Main Street and Douglas Avenue Improvement projects.

BACKGROUND:

 Comensoli's owns certain real properties located within the City of Kalamazoo, consisting of
two parcels legally identified as:

      1. 762 W. Main Street, Kalamazoo, MI 49006 (Parcel ID: 06-16-475-001)
      2. 757 Creek Street, Kalamazoo, MI 49006 (Parcel ID: 06-16-475-040)

The City is advancing municipal infrastructure improvements, including the construction of
pedestrian pathways and the infrastructure related to Arcadia Creek - Phase 1 (W. Main to
Westnedge) and Streets for All: West Main Street and Douglas Avenue Improvement projects
within this area. To facilitate these public improvements, the City requires two permanent Public
Infrastructure Easements and two Temporary Grading Permits over designated portions of the
Comensoli Properties. Formal acquisition of these easements and permits cannot commence until
the PROTECT grant has completed NEPA and Section 106 environmental and historic
preservation reviews, which are currently pending. Accordingly, the city seeks to execute this
Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with Comensoli's to provide assurance that the
necessary easements and grading permits will be secured once regulatory clearance is obtained.

The City is working toward a Phase 1 PROTECT grant obligation deadline of 9/15/2026 and
needs to continue moving forward to acquire the easements and permits necessary to meet this
deadline. This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) demonstrates to the Michigan
Department of Transportation (MDOT) that easement acquisition will be underway in the near
future once NEPA and Section 106 are complete to facilitate that obligation. Property owners
have indicated their readiness to enter negotiations in the near term, and the execution of these
easements is assured. Formal approval of both the easements and grading permits will be
presented at a future City Commission meeting for official adoption.

Upon compliance with the terms of this Agreement, Comensoli's agrees to execute and deliver to
the City the following: Public Infrastructure Easement and Temporary Grading Permits covering
portions of the Comensoli Property (Parcels 06-16-475-001 and 06-16-475-040):

    1. Public Infrastructure Easement: A permanent easement for the purpose of
       constructing, operating, maintaining, repairing, improving, or replacing components of
       the City’s infrastructure, including but not limited to: culverts, pipelines, connections,
       leads, mast arms and poles, pavements, sidewalk and all other components of the City’s
       public infrastructure systems for the Arcadia Creek Project - Phase 1 (W. Main to
       Westnedge).

    2. Public Infrastructure Easement: A permanent easement for the purpose of
       constructing, operating, maintaining, repairing, improving or replacing components of the
       City’s infrastructure, including but not limited to: pipelines, connections, leads, mast
       arms and poles, traffic signals, pavements, sidewalks and all other components of the
       City’s public infrastructure systems and public use of a Streets for All: West Main Street
       and Douglas Avenue Improvements.

    3. Temporary Grading Permits: A temporary permit allowing the City to excavate,
       restore and transition the existing parcel as designed for the Arcadia Creek Project -
       Phase 1 (W. Main to Westnedge).

In exchange for the granting of the easements, the City agrees to perform the following actions:

    1. Obtain an appraisal to determine the value of the proposed acquisitions: permanent
       easements and temporary grading permits. The City shall make a good faith offer to
       acquire said easements and grading permits based on the values established by the
       appraisal.

    2. The City shall restore the disturbed areas as designed, including but not limited to:
       removal of the existing parking area, reconstruction of a new parking area including
       pavement, and stormwater drainage improvements at no cost to Comensoli.

    3. Restoration to the Comensoli parcels shall occur immediately upon completion of the
       installation of the culvert. Restoration of the parking areas shall happen in stages,
       beginning with a gravel parking area and transiting to a paved parking area with striped
       parking spaces. The City and/or its contractors shall keep Comensoli's updated on the
