RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve and accept the easement with the Board of
Trustees of Western Michigan University, a Constitutional Corporation and institution of higher
education (whose address is 1903 W. Michigan Avenue, Kalamazoo, MI 49008) for water main
installed as part of the Hilltop Village Loop Road and Site Project; and authorize the City
Manager to sign all related and necessary documents regarding the acquisition of the easement.

BACKGROUND:

Construction of new water main was recently completed for the Hilltop Village Loop Road and
Site Project. As all public water mains and sanitary sewer are required to be in a dedicated
easement or public right of way, Board of Trustees of Western Michigan University, a
Constitutional Corporation and institution of higher education (whose address is 1903 W.
Michigan Avenue, Kalamazoo, MI 49008) in collaboration with the City of Kalamazoo Attorney
has drafted this agreement for approval.
