SUBJECT:       Consumers Energy easement for 459. N. Burdick

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve and accept the easement with Consumers
Energy at the address of 459 North Burdick for underground electrical upgrades; and authorize
the City Manager to sign all related and necessary documents regarding the acceptance of the
easement.

BACKGROUND:

Consumers Energy has requested a utility easement over City-owned property located at 459 N.
Burdick Street to facilitate the installation, operation, and maintenance of underground electrical
facilities. The proposed underground electric service is needed to support electrical infrastructure
improvements in the area and will enhance the reliability of the electric distribution system while
reducing above-ground utility infrastructure.
