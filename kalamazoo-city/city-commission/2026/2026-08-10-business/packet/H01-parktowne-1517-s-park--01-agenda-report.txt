RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Purchase Agreement with Parktowne,
LLC for the purchase of 1517 South Park Street, in the amount of $605,000.00, and authorize
City Manager to sign all necessary documents.

BACKGROUND:

In 2022, the City of Kalamazoo completed a study to evaluate the consolidation of Water
Treatment Plants for a reduction of entry points to the Kalamazoo Regional Water System for the
Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE). Treatment Plant
consolidation will improve water quality and simplify regulatory requirements. Public Services
owns and operates Central Treatment Plant at 215 West Stockbridge Avenue and Water
Treatment Plant No. 3 at 331 Balch Street. 1517 South Park Street is immediately in between
these two Treatment Plants. Acquiring this property will be beneficial for source water
protection and beneficial for the consolidated Central and No. 3 Treatment Plants.

Public Services has been negotiating for this property with Parktowne, LLC since 2024. Public
Services retained the professional appraisal services of Appraisal Group II, LLC via Metro
Consulting Associates to perform an appraisal of 1517 South Park Street in October 2024. The
appraisal came back with an as-is value of $470,000. A second appraisal was completed by
Cherney & Associates, Inc. for Parktowne, LLC in December 2025 that valued the property at
$620,000. After additional negotiation, Parktowne, LLC has provided Public Services with a
signed Purchase Agreement in the amount of $605,000. Public Services recommends the
approval of this offer and Purchase Agreement.
