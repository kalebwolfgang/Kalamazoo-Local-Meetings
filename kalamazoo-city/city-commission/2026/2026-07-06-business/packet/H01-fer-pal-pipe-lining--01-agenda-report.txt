RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Fer-Pal Construction USA,
LLC for the 2026 Pipe Lining Project, in the amount of $2,792,847.00.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water System experiences approximately 100 water main breaks annually.
Water main breaks have become a more frequent issue that requires constant Public Services
maintenance. Historically, Public Services has replaced water main which can take up road space
for customers. The 2026 Pipe Lining Project will help mitigate these issues by providing a less
invasive construction operation to improve the structural integrity of the existing water main.
The 2026 Pipe Lining Project includes structurally lining approximately 1.5-miles of water main
and verifying service line materials.

Bids were opened for the project on June 9, 2026. Two bids were received, with Fer-Pal
Construction USA, LLC being the low responsive bidder. Fer-Pal Construction USA, LLC has
performed well with other similar projects for the City of Kalamazoo, and Public Services
recommends approval of this contract.
