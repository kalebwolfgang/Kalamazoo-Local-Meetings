Commissioner Hoffman, seconded by Commissioner Slaby, moved to approve a
three-year contract with Wayne County Appraisal, LLC d/b/a WCA Assessing for
assessing and assessor-of-record services in the amount of $1,855,321.92; and
authorize the City Manager to sign all related documents.

Prior to a vote on the motion, Commissioner Slaby expressed concerns about
customer service with WCA Assessing and suggested the City consider the option
of reestablishing the assessing function as an in-house operation at the end of this
contract.

Vice Mayor Duncan asked about performance review options under the proposed
contract.

Discussion took place regarding the standards, tools, and timelines that were in
place, or could be put in place, to monitor the performance of WCA under this
contract.

With a roll call vote this motion passed 7-0.
