SUBJECT:       Approve updated Retirement System Endowment Investment Allocation

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve updated Retirement System Endowment
Investment Allocations, adding the Loomis Sayles Active Core fund.

BACKGROUND:

The Retirement Investment Committee (RIC) is responsible for developing and recommending
investment policies, strategies, and guidelines to the City Commission for the Retirement
Investment Endowment. The Committee regularly monitors the portfolio to ensure alignment
with market conditions, investment research, and long-term objectives.

Over the past year, an RIC subcommittee conducted a comprehensive review of the fixed income
allocation with assistance from Zhang Financial. At the May 27 meeting, the RIC approved a
recommendation to add the Loomis Sayles Active Core fund as an additional fixed income
investment.

Fixed income investments currently represent 25% of the total portfolio and are fully allocated to
the Jennison Intermediate Bond Index. The recommended change will diversify the fixed income
allocation by splitting the investment between Jennison and Loomis Sayles, with each manager
representing 12.5% of the total portfolio.
This recommended adjustment is intended to enhance diversification within the fixed income
allocation, strengthen portfolio management flexibility, and improve long-term expected risk-
adjusted returns.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
