SUBJECT:       Ordinance Authorizing Water Supply System Revenue Bonds, Series 2026B

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt the attached Ordinance Authorizing Water
Supply System Revenue Bonds, Series 2026B in the amount not to exceed $2,500,000.

BACKGROUND:

This ordinance will authorize the issuance of $2,500,000 as junior lien revenue bonds utilizing
the State of Michigan's Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF) program. The related
lead service line replacement project is budgeted for $5,000,000. The DWSRF provides funding
of $2,500,000 in bonds and $2,500,000 in grants.

The EGLE Order of Approval is expected to be issued on August 5th. The closing for the Bonds
will be August 28th and the City can begin requesting draws on the Bonds after that date.The
Bonds are expected to be sold to the Michigan Finance Authority and payable over 20 years at an
interest rate of 2.00%. The City incurs the debt as the project is completed rather than all up
front. These factors create significant savings for the Water System compared to standard
revenue bonds.

The Ordinance contains the mandatory requirements for Revenue Bond Ordinances as required
by the Revenue Bond Act, Act 94, Public Acts of Michigan, 1933, as amended. The Ordinance
sets forth the terms of the Bonds and provides for a negotiated sale of the Bonds to the Michigan
Finance Authority as is required under the DWSRF program.

Pursuant to Section 6 of the Revenue Bond Act, the Ordinance may be adopted in one reading,
regardless of any contrary provision in the City Charter, and is required to be published in full
after approval.
