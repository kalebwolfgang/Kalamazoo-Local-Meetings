RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the sole source purchase and installation of
the Critical Spaces Control Platform (CSCP) upgrade for the Kalamazoo Crime Laboratory
exhaust system by Quality Air Service, LLC, 6701 Quality Way, Portage, Michigan 49002, in
the amount of $132,115. Quality Air Service is the exclusive authorized Phoenix Controls
representative in the State of Michigan and is the only vendor authorized to provide the required
equipment, installation, startup, commissioning, and warranty services associated with the
existing Phoenix Controls infrastructure.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Crime Laboratory utilizes a specialized exhaust and airflow control system that
was installed as part of the original laboratory construction in 2003. This system serves as the
primary means of regulating laboratory airflow, maintaining pressure relationships, and ensuring
the safe removal of hazardous fumes generated during forensic testing and evidence processing
activities.

The existing Phoenix Controls analog system has reached the end of its useful life. Replacement
components are no longer manufactured, and spare parts are increasingly unavailable. Failure of
the existing system would significantly impact laboratory operations and could compromise the
laboratory’s ability to safely process evidence and maintain required environmental controls.

Crime laboratories operate under stringent environmental and occupational safety standards due
to the presence of hazardous chemicals, biological materials, latent print processing agents, and
other substances used during forensic examinations. Maintaining proper airflow, pressure
differentials, and exhaust performance is essential to protecting laboratory personnel, preserving

Quality Air Service has proposed upgrading the laboratory from the existing analog platform to
the Phoenix Controls Critical Spaces Control Platform (CSCP). The project includes replacement
of laboratory control components, fume hood monitoring equipment, room pressure indicators,
temperature and humidity monitoring, system transformers, low-voltage control wiring, and a
centralized laboratory dashboard that will provide real-time monitoring of laboratory conditions.
The upgrade will improve system reliability, increase operational flexibility, enhance monitoring
capabilities, and provide a platform that can support future building automation integration.

Quality Air Service is the exclusive authorized Phoenix Controls representative for the State of
Michigan and maintains the exclusive rights for distribution, startup, commissioning, testing, and
service of Phoenix Controls products and components within Michigan. Phoenix Controls has
advised that the proposed upgrade kits are specifically designed for the existing Phoenix venturi
valve infrastructure installed within the Kalamazoo Crime Laboratory. The manufacturer
maintains proprietary performance data and valve characterization information that is only
available through authorized Phoenix Controls representatives.

Because the existing laboratory airflow system is a Phoenix Controls system, compatibility with
existing equipment, preservation of system performance specifications, factory commissioning
requirements, and maintenance of manufacturer warranties necessitate the use of Phoenix
Controls components and authorized service personnel. As a result, Quality Air Service is the
only vendor capable of providing a fully compatible upgrade and associated services for the
existing infrastructure. Accordingly, this purchase qualifies as a sole source procurement.

The proposed project includes equipment, installation, startup, commissioning, and warranty
coverage. The upgrade will provide continued reliability of a critical public safety asset while
ensuring the Crime Laboratory can maintain safe operations and compliance with forensic
laboratory environmental requirements.
