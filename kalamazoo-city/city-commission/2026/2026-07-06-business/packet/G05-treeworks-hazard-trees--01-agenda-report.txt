SUBJECT:       Approval of a contract extension with Treeworks for Hazardous Tree Removal
               (Bid Ref#59575-031.4)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an 18-month contract extension with
Treeworks for the Hazardous Tree Removal project in the amount of $199,843.07.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo contracts with tree care expert firms to assist in clearing downed and
damaged trees. Treeworks is currently under contract with the city and has greatly assisted the
city in its clean-up efforts after several major storm events.

As the frequency and intensity of storms have been increasing, it is important that the city has
enhanced capabilities to help in its clean-up efforts, most importantly from contractors who
know and understand the City’s needs and processes. Treeworks meets these requirements and
has provided a quote for an extension through 2027. This extension would then allow our
Hazardous Tree Removal contract to be renewed on an annual, calendar-year basis.

Treeworks has shown itself to be a good partner in our efforts to protect our city’s tree canopy,
and is qualified and willing to take on this extension.
