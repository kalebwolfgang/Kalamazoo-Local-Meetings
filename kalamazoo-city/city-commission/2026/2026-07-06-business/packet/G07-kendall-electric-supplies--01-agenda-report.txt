SUBJECT:        Approval of an 18-Month Contract Extension with Kendall Electric Inc for
                Electric Supplies (Bid Ref#28500-002.4)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an 18-month contract extension with
Kendall Electric Inc for inventory and non-inventory electrical supplies in the amount of
$608,000.00

BACKGROUND:

Kendall Electric has contracted with the City of Kalamazoo since 1998 for miscellaneous
electrical supplies. The items supplied under this contract include: fuses, conduit, receptacles,
conduit fittings, junction boxes, and many other miscellaneous electrical parts including controls
equipment and control panels. Kendall Electric has met or exceeded the City’s needs for
providing these electrical supplies. Kendall Electric’s response time, stocking of appropriate
parts and customer service continue to be excellent.

Alternatives to extending this contract for another year include re-bidding the entire contract or
using the Procurement Card system for individual purchases. It is not cost effective to process
the hundreds of electrical items that are purchased every year as individual purchases therefore;
an annual contract is the preferred method of purchasing. Changing to a new vendor would
introduce a change in product lines making repairs more time consuming and potentially more
costly due to incompatibility with existing installations. Additionally, Kendall Electric is the sole
authorized Rockwell Automation representative for our area that supports the Allen Bradley
programmable logic controllers we use.
