RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with P.K. Contracting LLC for
the 2026 Handwork Pavement Marking project in the amount of $111,077.50.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo contracts annually for pavement marking work. Pavement markings on
City streets are essential for user safety, guidance, right-of-way, usage, and well-being.
Handwork pavement markings specify any previous markings that necessitate non-truck-
mounted equipment. It is labor-intensive work and absolutely essential for completing legends,
crosswalk markings, and special hatching on City streets.
