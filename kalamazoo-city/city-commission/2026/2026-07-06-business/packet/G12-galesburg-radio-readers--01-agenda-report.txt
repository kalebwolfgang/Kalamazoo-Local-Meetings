RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an Agreement with City of Galesburg to
Install Radio Readers in Fleetwood Plat for Sewer Billing for $250,000.00.

BACKGROUND:

The Fleetwood Plat is located in Comstock Township and is supplied public drinking water by
City of Galesburg and public sewer by City of Kalamazoo. This requires Public Services to
coordinate meter reading with City of Galesburg to issue sewer bills to these customers.

Smart water meters provide real-time usage information directly to the Utility via radio
networks. The advancement in usage information can provide daily usage trends and can even
alert customers and the Utility to leaks. Smart meters do not require meter readers to deploy and
collect meter reads as previous technologies required. In the future, smart water meters will be
used to support monthly billing, real-time account access to customers and reliable peaking
factor and system demand information.

Public Services staff worked in cooperation with the City of Galesburg to identify a technical,
managerial, and financial capacity project to streamline meter reading for City of Galesburg for
water billing and City of Kalamazoo for sewer billing. City of Galesburg uses water meters that
can be radioed to the City of Kalamazoo’s advanced metering infrastructure (AMI) radio system.
 The purchase of the Radio Readers will be provided on the annual meter contract. There are
approximately 240 locations for Radio Readers to be installed.

customers to schedule Radio Reader installations. Letters will be sent to all customers slated to
receive a new Radio Reader informing them of the work planned, noticing the customer that the
