Recitals:

WHEREAS the Subrecipient provides a needed service in the City assisting low- to moderate-income
households with critical and code enforcement repair on owner-occupied single-family housing units
(the “Program”). Such service protects the health and safety of these residents and ensures their
ability to remain in their homes, and

WHEREAS the Subrecipient received Community Development Block Grant (“CDBG”) funding from
the City for this program, but a gap in service exists for repairs. The Housing Development Fund
(“HDF”) will provide needed dollars to close this gap allowing the Subrecipient to address critical and
code repairs. Additionally, the Subrecipient is supporting the City’s code enforcement repair
program, which has been mandated by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development
(“HUD”), and

WHEREAS the City has determined that the Program is expected to support affordable housing by assisting
low- to moderate-income households maintain and retain their homes. The City finds that the intended
purpose of the grant meets the established HDF guidelines and has therefore approved a grant of
$135,000.00, subject to the following terms and conditions under this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions set forth below, the parties
agree as follows:

A. Recitals: The Recitals set forth above are true and correct and are incorporated by this reference.

B. Grant Purpose: The City agrees to disburse the specified grant amount to the Subrecipient for the sole
purpose of assisting with code enforcement repair costs for 18 households making 80 percent or less
of the area median income. Tasks will include intake of potential clients, assessment of repair needs,
procurement of any needed subcontractors, and rehabilitation or repair activities.

C. Schedule of Tasks: The Subrecipient will complete the task associated with the Program on the
following schedule:

Program delivery will include all costs incurred to implement the Program, including staff costs for

successful completion of the activity.

The Subrecipient may spend their budget as expeditiously as deemed necessary or may incrementally
use the budget over the Agreement period of performance. If the budget is expended before the

expiration of this Agreement, no further financial reporting is required.

E. Notice to Proceed and Disbursement of Funds:

The full grant award will be disbursed upon execution of this agreement and receiving a Notice to
Proceed by the City.

F. Reporting and Neighborly Grant Management

1. Neighborly: All reporting will be completed utilizing Neighborly, an online grant management
software program. At the time of the issuance of the Notice to Proceed, the City will provide the
Subrecipient with the information needed to access Neighborly to manage required monthly
reporting.

2. Monthly Financial Reporting: The Subrecipient shall submit monthly Financial Status Reports by the
20" of the month following each month of performance. If the 20" falls on a weekend or holiday,
reporting will be due on the next business day. Included with this request shall be a Financial Status
Report Form (provided to the Subrecipient following the execution of this Agreement) with
supporting back-up documentation, if applicable.

Reports within Neighborly, which include statistical data that verifies progress towards meeting the
program outcomes. Monthly Accomplishment Reports will be due by the 20" of the month
following each month of performance. If the 20" falls on a weekend or holiday, reporting will be
due on the next business day. If there is a month when no activity occurs, the required Report should
be submitted, indicating no activity.

G. Occupant Requirements: The Subrecipient shall ensure the homes are owner-occupied by households
making 80 percent or less of the area median income as described by HUD on an annual basis.

H. Program Records: The Subrecipient must retain records reasonably necessary to substantiate
Subrecipient’s continuing compliance with the provisions of this Agreement. Reports must be
submitted in such format as prescribed by the City, and the City shall retain the right to change
reporting requirements from time to time as it deems necessary. The Subrecipient must maintain
records for inspection by the City. These include, but are not limited to:

1. Records of all program-related account transactions including deposits, disbursements, and
balances.

2. Income eligibility documentation of Program participants.

3. Records supporting draw of funds.

4. Records of all written agreements and contracts pertaining to the Program.
5. Audits and resolution of audit findings.

All records shall be accessible to the City at reasonable times and under reasonable conditions for the
purpose of determining compliance with this Agreement. If the City desires to audit the Subrecipient’s
books, it may do so and may, at its discretion, retain an independent certified public accountant (CPA)
