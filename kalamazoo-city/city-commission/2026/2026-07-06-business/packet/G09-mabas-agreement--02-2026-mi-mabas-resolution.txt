                       CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                RESOLUTION NO. ________

                A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE 2016 AMENDED
            MICHIGAN MUTUAL AID BOX ALARM SYSTEM (MI-MABAS)
                        INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT

Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City of Kalamazoo, held on July 6,
2026, at or after 7:00 p.m., local time, at City Hall.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

WHEREAS, the City of Kalamazoo (“City”) possesses the power, privilege, and authority to
maintain and operate a department providing fire suppression, rescue, hazardous materials
response, emergency medical response, and other emergency response services (“Fire Services”);
and

WHEREAS, Fire Services can be enhanced through cooperation between political subdivisions
during emergencies, disasters, or other serious threats to public health and safety; and

WHEREAS, the Constitution of the State of Michigan of 1963, Article VII, Section 28, and the
Urban Cooperation Act of 1967, 1967 PA 7, MCL 124.501 et seq., authorize units of local
government to enter into interlocal agreements for joint exercise of shared powers; and

WHEREAS, the Michigan Mutual Aid Box Alarm System (“MI-MABAS”) is a statewide
mutual aid system that provides a prearranged mechanism for requesting and providing
emergency assistance among participating jurisdictions; and

WHEREAS, the City previously adopted the 2014 MABAS Interlocal Agreement and
participated in the Kalamazoo County MABAS Division; and

WHEREAS, MI-MABAS has adopted an Amended (2016) Agreement that supersedes prior
agreements and establishes updated definitions, operational procedures, governance structures,
and legal provisions; and

WHEREAS, the Public Safety Chief recommends that the City adopt and enter into the
Amended (2016) MI-MABAS Agreement to ensure continued participation in coordinated
mutual aid operations within the State of Michigan; and

WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that participation in MI-MABAS is in the best interest
of the City and its residents from both a safety and fiscal standpoint, given the increased capacity
for coordinated emergency response provided through the system.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED:

1. Approval of Agreement.

The City Commission hereby approves and adopts the Amended (2016) Michigan Mutual Aid
Box Alarm System Association Agreement, attached hereto and incorporated by reference, and
authorizes the City Manager to execute the Agreement on behalf of the City of Kalamazoo.

2. Appointment of Representative.

The City Commission approves the City’s participation in the appropriate MI-MABAS Division
and authorizes the Public Safety Chief to serve as the City’s representative to the Division and to
the MI-MABAS Executive Board, with authority to designate an alternate as permitted.

3. Authorization to Fulfill Obligations.

The Public Safety Chief, or designee, is authorized to take necessary and proper action to render
and request mutual aid in accordance with MI-MABAS policies and procedures.

4. Filing Requirements.

The City Clerk is directed to ensure all required filings with the Department of State, the Office
of the Great Seal, and Kalamazoo County are completed in accordance with applicable
requirements.
