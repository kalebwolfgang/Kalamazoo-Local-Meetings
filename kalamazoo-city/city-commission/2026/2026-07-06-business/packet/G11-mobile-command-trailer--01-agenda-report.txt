SUBJECT:       Acceptance of Donation - Mobile Command Trailer from the U.S. Department of
               Energy Office of Radiological Security

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accept the donation of a 2022 Southern Dimensions
Group, Inc. mobile command trailer from the U.S. Department of Energy Office of Radiological
Security (ORS) through Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL). The donated equipment
has an estimated value of $86,000 and will be utilized to support public safety operations,
emergency management activities, and regional specialty team responses.

BACKGROUND:

The U.S. Department of Energy Office of Radiological Security (ORS), through Pacific
Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL), provides radiological response training and equipment
to eligible state and local public safety agencies. Participation in the program is based upon a
regional radiological threat assessment and the agency's ability to support ongoing regional
training opportunities. The Kalamazoo region was determined to be eligible due to the presence
of both mobile and fixed radiological sources.

In December 2025, ORS provided radiological response training to personnel from the
Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety and Kalamazoo County Consolidated Dispatch
Authority, as well as a tabletop exercise for command staff. As part of the program, ORS
donated a fully equipped mobile classroom and command trailer to the City of Kalamazoo. The
donation does not require a contractual commitment and may be utilized for a variety of public
safety purposes.

The donated trailer is a 2022 Southern Dimensions Group, Inc. 18-foot tandem axle trailer
equipped with climate control, scene lighting, power awning, wireless connectivity, computer
workstations, dual-fuel generator, and supporting equipment. The trailer has an estimated value
of $86,000.
