On Thursday, July 3, Roger Bishop and Eric Hawley conducted a 3-inch meter change at Costco.
Unfortunately, the proper restraint procedures were not followed: no threaded-rod anchors were
installed, and a restraint-style coupling was not used. As a result, a severe leak occurred.
According to industry and municipal standards, any large meter (typically larger than 2 inch must
include mechanical restraint—either a restraint-style coupling like Hymax or appropriately sized
threaded-rod anchoring or joint restraint system. The failure to comply with these requirements
directly contributed to the damage and prompted a formal damage claim.
