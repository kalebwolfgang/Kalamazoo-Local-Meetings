RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accept a grant in the amount of $80,000 from the
Irving S. Gilmore Foundation and a donation in the amount of $30,000 from DEGA to fund 2026
Downtown Holiday Events and programming.

BACKGROUND:

For decades, Downtown Kalamazoo has served as the community's central meeting place for
authentic family and community experiences during the holiday season. Kalamazoo is known for
filling its streets, parks and shops with festive holiday spirit and events that draw visitors to the
area. They have become notable family traditions, with many attendees participating for decades
and now attending with their children or grandchildren. These holiday events are important to the
community and truly bring the spirit of Kalamazoo alive throughout the Downtown area. The
city will contract with Kalamazoo Experiential Learning Center (KELC) to execute portions of
the holiday programming and to solicit and collect all sponsorships needed to support that
programming. Holiday events and programming will include: Santa's Workshop, The Holly Jolly
Trolley, Downtown Holiday Lighting, and Bronson Park Tree Lighting Ceremony.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Inviting Public Places - Vibrant streets, exceptional parks, and welcoming activities.
Economic Vitality - A supportive infrastructure for growing businesses and stabilizing the local
economy for the benefit of all.

FISCAL IMPACT:

This Irving S. Gilmore Foundation grant of $80,000 will partially fund the 2026 Holiday Events
and Programming. Funds for the remaining portion of the events will be from sponsorships
collected by KELC in the amount of $25,000, City of Kalamazoo General Fund in the amount of
$19,730, and a DEGA donation of $30,000 to create a comprehensive budget of $154,730. The
city anticipates $154,730 of expenses for the events mentioned above, reflecting a balanced
project budget. The $19,730 in General Fund contribution is in the 2026 appropriated budget
within Parks and Rec Special Events budget (101-757).

Upon acceptance of this grant, a project will be created to track grant and donation expenditures
for reporting purposes.
