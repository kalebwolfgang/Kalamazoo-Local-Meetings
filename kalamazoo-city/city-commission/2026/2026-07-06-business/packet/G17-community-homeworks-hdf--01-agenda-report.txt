RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Housing Development Fund grant to
Community Homeworks for Single-Family Owner-Occupied Critical and Code Enforcement
Repairs in the amount of $135,000.

BACKGROUND:

The Housing Development Fund (HDF) initiative through the Foundation for Excellence is an
effort to provide resources to construct and preserve quality affordable housing that responds to
our community’s vision, as described in Imagine Kalamazoo and the 2025-2029 Consolidated
Plan. Community Homeworks supports this vision through the Critical and Code Enforcement
Repair Program, which provides assistance to low-income homeowners who have received a
code citation by a City Code Inspector. The program impacts the health and safety of owner-
occupied housing by focusing on improvements to their physical environment, eliminating code
violations, and often preventing housing insecurity and displacement by returning the home to
livable conditions.

The City of Kalamazoo has consistently utilized Community Development Block Grant (CDBG)
funds for code enforcement staff to support neighborhood enhancement. The U.S. Department of
Housing and Urban Development (HUD) has indicated that aid needs to be provided to eligible
homeowners who receive a code citation, allowing them to come into compliance. As the City
does not deliver programs directly, staff turned to Community Homeworks for assistance.

HDF will subsidize the CDBG dollars that support this program to resolve a gap in funding.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Inform (one-way conversation) – no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

The desire to maintain affordable housing within the City was a common theme within the
community outreach that was completed for the Imagine Kalamazoo Master Plan. It was also the
main community development priority heard during the public outreach for the development of
the 2025-2029 Consolidated Plan. The Critical and Code Enforcement Repair Program supports
an affordable housing goal by improving the quality of housing for low-income homeowners
within the City. In addition, these funds were vetted for this program by the City Commission
during the 2026 budget process as part of the housing development strategy for the Housing
Development Fund.
