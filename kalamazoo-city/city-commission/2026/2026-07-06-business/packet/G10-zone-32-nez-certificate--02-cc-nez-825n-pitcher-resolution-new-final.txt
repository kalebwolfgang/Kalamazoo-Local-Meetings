                           CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN
                                RESOLUTION NO. ____

             A RESOLUTION APPROVING A NEW NEZ CERTIFICATE
         APPLICATIONS FILED BY ZONE 32 PHASE TWO, LLC, LOCATED
         AT 825 PORTER STREET & 810 N PITCHER STREET, WITHIN THE
        ZONE 32-NEZ DISTRICT, AUTHORIZING THE SUBMISSION OF THE
          NEZ APPLICATIONS BY THE CITY CLERK TO THE MICHIGAN
                         STATE TAX COMMISSION

        Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City of Kalamazoo held on
July 6, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. local time at the City Hall.

       PRESENT,       Commissioners:

       ABSENT,        Commissioners:

        WHEREAS, the Kalamazoo City Commission adopted the Downtown City Center
Neighborhood Enterprise Zone on June 15, 2026 to provide incentives to encourage neighborhood
revitalization; and

        WHEREAS, the City of Kalamazoo is in receipt of one NEZ New Applications of Zone
32 Phase Twp, LLC at 825 Porter Street & 810 N Pitcher, Kalamazoo, Michigan in the Northside
in the Zone 32 Neighborhood Enterprise Zone;

         WHEREAS, the City of Kalamazoo has reviewed the application request to the City policy
for the number of qualifying years and that the request for twelve (12) years of NEZ tax reduction
is in alignment with the City policy based on the number of affordable units per Table E of City
Commission Policy 30.10.

        IT IS RESOLVED THAT the City Commission acknowledges the receipt of the NEZ
New Certificate at 825 N Pitcher & 810 Porter Neighborhood Enterprise Zone; find that the
applications comply with Neighborhood Enterprise Zone Act, Act 147 of the Public Acts of
Michigan of 1992, as amended, the current City NEZ Policy, and hereby approve the submission
of the Rehabilitation Certificate for the above described property to the State Tax Commission,
beginning the effecting date of the NEZ Certificates; and that City Commission recommends the
above described property, if approved by the State Tax Commission, shall receive the 12-year
maximum allowable term of NEZ benefits, beginning from the effective date of the NEZ
certificate.
