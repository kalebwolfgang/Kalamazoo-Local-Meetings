RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the damage claim with AZO Services
Management LLC for the actual cash value (ACV)$47,117.27.

BACKGROUND:

In May 2025, the property located at 2509 Emerald Avenue, Kalamazoo, MI 49001, experienced
a sanitary sewer backup resulting from a defect in the public sewer system. The City of
Kalamazoo has historically accepted responsibility for claims related to defects within the public
sewer infrastructure. In this instance, a defect associated with a prior repair of a public
infrastructure asset near Manhole KC25108 contributed to a blockage, causing a sewer backup
into the property and surrounding yard.

The backup resulted in significant interior property damage. Following an assessment of the
damages, all necessary repairs have been completed.

Pursuant to Michigan law, the City has accepted liability for the sewer backup. Because the
amount of the claim exceeds the $25,000 settlement authority granted to the City Attorney, City
Commission approval is required to authorize final resolution of this matter.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
