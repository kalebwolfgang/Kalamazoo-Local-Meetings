               Deere 544P Wheel Loader (Bid Ref#00000-024.60)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a MIDEAL cooperative purchase with AIS
Construction Equipment for a John Deere 544P Wheel Loader in the amount of $339,990.00.

BACKGROUND:

The purchase of a new loader is necessary to support both the Streets and Forestry divisions. The
current loader will be retired due to catastrophic engine failure. The existing machine has
accumulated more than 9,000 operating hours, and while replacing the engine is possible, the
drivetrain and hydraulic systems have also experienced significant wear over the same period,
making continued investment in the equipment impractical.

The proposed loader includes a 4-in-1 bucket, which is used for construction projects and storm
damage response, as well as an automatic lubrication system to reduce wear, minimize
downtime, and extend the life of the equipment. The purchase also includes a new Tink Claw
attachment for fall leaf collection, replacing the existing attachment that sustained significant
damage during a previous leaf collection season.

This equipment will be purchased through the State of Michigan MiDEAL cooperative
purchasing contract (Contract No. 240000000158), ensuring competitive pricing and compliance
with procurement requirements.
