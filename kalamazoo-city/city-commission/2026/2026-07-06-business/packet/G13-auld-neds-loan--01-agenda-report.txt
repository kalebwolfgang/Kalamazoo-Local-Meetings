RECOMMENDATION:

The Economic Development Corporation (EDC) recommends approval of a $120,000 Economic
Initiative Fund (EIF) loan to Auld Neds Hospitality, LLC for the development of The Emerald
Oak, an Irish pub and restaurant located at 3003 Oakland Drive within Oakwood Plaza.

BACKGROUND:

Auld Neds Hospitality, LLC, owned by Eamonn Nestor, is redeveloping a vacant restaurant
space within Oakwood Plaza at 3003 Oakland Drive into The Emerald Oak, a full-service Irish
pub and restaurant. The 4,406-square-foot project includes significant interior renovations,
including demolition and reconstruction, commercial kitchen installation, HVAC, plumbing and
electrical upgrades, custom millwork, furniture and fixtures, signage, and audiovisual
improvements. The completed space will feature dining areas, a central bar, and a private
whiskey snug designed for small-group gatherings and hospitality experiences. The Emerald Oak
is intended to provide a unique hospitality offering within the Kalamazoo market while serving
as a gathering place for residents, families, sports fans, and community events. The project is
expected to create 8 to 12 full-time jobs and 12 to 20 part-time jobs upon completion.

The total project cost is estimated at approximately $1.2 million and is being financed through a
combination of owner equity and working capital contributions, a tenant improvement
contribution from the property owner, and the requested Economic Initiative Fund (EIF) loan.
On June 18, 2026, the Economic Development Corporation approved an EIF loan in the amount
of $120,000 to Auld Neds Hospitality, LLC. The loan carries a 2% interest rate, a 15-year
repayment term, and quarterly payments, and will be secured by a personal guaranty from the
owner. The EIF loan will provide gap financing necessary to complete the project and represents

FISCAL IMPACT:

The Economic Initiative Fund (272-000-00.000-194.244) will advance $120,000 to the
Economic Development Corporation, which will issue a 15-year loan repayable at a 2% interest
rate.

The City's Economic Initiative Fund will be replenished with the scheduled loan payments as
outlined in the promissory note. The long-term advance to the Economic Development
Corporation will be released with the receipt of the underlying principal payments.

There is no direct budgetary impact as loan disbursements are held within the balance sheet.
Future interest receipts will provide additional funding to the city EIF fund for future loans.
