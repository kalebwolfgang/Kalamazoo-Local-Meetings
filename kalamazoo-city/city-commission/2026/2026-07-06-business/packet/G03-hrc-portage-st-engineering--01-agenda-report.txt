RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a professional services agreement with
Hubbell, Roth & Clark Inc for Construction Engineering services in support of the Portage St
(Cork-Stockbridge) Safety Project in the amount of $130,805.00.

BACKGROUND:

The City was awarded grant funding through the MDOT FY 2026 Safety Grant program for
safety projects focused on improving conditions for Vulnerable Road Users, with an emphasis on
pedestrian safety. The Portage St (Cork-Stockbridge) Safety Project is one of the awarded
projects. Hubbell, Roth & Clark Inc (HRC) completed the design engineering and prepared bid-
ready documents, and the project is being administered through MDOT’s Local Agency Program
(LAP).

The project will resurface Portage St from Cork St to Stockbridge Ave and convert the existing
four-lane roadway to a three-lane configuration, often called a “road diet,” with one through lane
in each direction, a center left-turn lane, and bike lanes on both sides. This layout continues the
three-lane configuration already in place on Portage St north of Stockbridge, creating a more
consistent and predictable corridor for drivers, bicyclists, and pedestrians. Pedestrian safety
improvements include solar-powered rectangular rapid flashing beacons (RRFBs) at several
crossings, median refuge islands, curb ramps, pedestrian lighting and push buttons, decorative
concrete pavement, and updated signing and pavement markings. Construction is anticipated in
late summer or fall of 2026.

to administer the project in accordance with MDOT LAP standards. HRC’s proposal provides
