                           CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                   RESOLUTION NO. 26-

A RESOLUTION TO TENTATIVELY AWARD A CONSTRUCTION CONTRACT FOR
          FY 26 DWSRF NON-COPPER SERVICE REPLACEMENTS

     Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City of Kalamazoo held on July
6, 2026, at 7:00 o’clock p.m., local time, at the City Hall.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

WHEREAS, the City of Kalamazoo (legal name of applicant) wishes to construct
improvements to its existing water treatment and distribution system; and

WHEREAS, the water system improvements project formally adopted on 05/19/2005 (date
of final project plan adoption) will be funded through the state of Michigan’s Drinking
Water Revolving Loan Fund (DWRF) program; and

WHEREAS, the City of Kalamazoo (legal name of applicant) has sought and received
construction bids for the proposed improvements and has received a low bid in the amount
of $4,135,936.10 from SWT Excavating, Inc. (name of the contractor); and

WHEREAS, the (Village’s/City’s/Township’s/County’s) engineer, James J. Baker, PE has
recommended awarding the contract to the low bidder.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the City of Kalamazoo (legal name of
applicant) tentatively awards the contract for construction of the proposed water system
improvements project to SWT Excavating, Inc. (name of the contractor), contingent upon
successful financial arrangements with the DWRF.

The above resolution was offered by Commissioner        and seconded by Commissioner             .

AYES, Commissioners:

NAYS, Commissioners:

ABSTAIN, Commissioners:

RESOLUTION DECLARED ADOPTED.
