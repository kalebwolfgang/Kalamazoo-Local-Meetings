AGREEMENT FOR INSTALLATION OF RADIO READERS
BETWEEN THE CITY OF KALAMAZOO AND THE CITY OF GALESBURG

This Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into on 2026, by and
between the City of Kalamazoo (“Kalamazoo”) and the City of Galesburg (“Galesburg”)

collectively known as (the “Parties”’).

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Kalamazoo seeks the ability to install radio transmitted readers on water
service meters located in the “Fleetwood Platt” under Galesburg’s Water WSSN for the purpose of
sanitary sewer billing.

WHEREAS, Galesburg wishes to allow Kalamazoo to install said radio transmitted
readers onto Galesburg’s water meters located in the “Fleetwood Platt”.

WHEREAS the Parties wish to formalize the terms of their agreement to accomplish the
installation and clarify the financial responsibility after installation of said radio transmitted
readers.

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY AGREED:

1. Radio Transmitted Readers. Kalamazoo has purchased and will continue to purchase

Neptune R900 MIUs (the “Radio Readers”). Kalamazoo is solely responsible for the
financial cost of purchasing the Radio Readers to be installed under this Agreement.
Kalamazoo must provide Galesburg with all necessary Radio Readers and related
equipment to Galesburg within a reasonable time prior to the installation. Although
Kalamazoo will provide these Radio Readers and appurtenances/related equipment to
Galesburg, Kalamazoo shall always retain ownership of the Radio Readers and

appurtenances/related equipment and shall bear the full risk of loss.

Installation.

a. Galesburg, through its employees, contractors, and/or other agents, shall install the
Radio Readers at user homes at reasonable times and intervals as agreed upon by
the parties.

b. Such installation must be in accordance with industry practice and consistent with
manufacturer instructions and specifications.

c. The process for each Radio Reader Installation will be as follows:

i. Kalamazoo contacts Galesburg’s Department of Public Works
Superintendent Joshua Ranes with the details of the Radio Readers they
wish to have installed, including the user location and contact information.

ii. Kalamazoo will send a written notice to the user informing the user that
Galesburg will be contacting the user to set up the installation specifics. A
copy of this notice will also be sent to Galesburg’s Department of Public
Works Superintendent.

iii. Following receipt of the written notice, Galesburg will contact the user with
the information provided by Kalamazoo to arrange a date/time for the Radio
Reader installation.

iv. If Galesburg and the user can agree upon an installation date and time,
Galesburg will complete the Radio Reader installation and bill Kalamazoo
for the service in accordance with this Agreement.

d. Galesburg will not be responsible for any maintenance, repair, or support of the

Radio Readers beyond the initial installation.

. Additional Emergency Work. Galesburg will also conduct such urgent and emergency work

as the parties so agree. As such work will be performed on an emergent basis, no written

modification to this Agreement is required.

. Administration of Radio Readers. The Parties agree that the current administration of the

Radio Readers and all user billing for sanitary sewer is and will be performed by the City

of Kalamazoo.

Meter Compatibility- The parties agree that any incompatible equipment currently in place

shall be upgraded or replaced with a Galesburg approved Equivalent. This upgrade or
replacement shall be at the sole cost of Kalamazoo for the initial Installation.

. Financial Responsibility after installation- The parties agree that after the initial installation

of the equipment, aside from any warranty or failure to comply with the standards in this
agreement, Galesburg will remain solely responsible for the Water meter maintenance,
replacement, or any customer contact regarding the meter. Kalamazoo will be solely
responsible for the maintenance, replacement, or any customer contact regarding the Radio
Readers, the seat into Kalamazoo’s software for Galesburg to utilize to obtain meter reads,
and the equipment for Galesburg to read the meters in the field.

Galesburg Warranty. Galesburg will warrant that its work will be free from significant

defects for a period of one year from the time the work is performed. This warranty only
applies to work performed by Galesburg, and not any Smart Meters or appurtenances
themselves. If any Galesburg work fails to comply with this standard, Galesburg will
remedy the issue within a reasonable time following written notice.

. Term. The installation of Radio Readers is anticipated to be an ongoing project which

currently does not have a fixed timeline. Galesburg shall perform services under this
