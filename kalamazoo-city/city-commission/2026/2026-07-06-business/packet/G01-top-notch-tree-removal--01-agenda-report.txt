SUBJECT:       Approval of a 6-month contract extension with Top Notch Tree Care LLC for
               2026 tree removal (Bid Ref#59575-041.1)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a 6-month contract extension for 2026 tree
removal in the amount of $105,930.

BACKGROUND:

As part of the City’s ongoing urban forestry management efforts, there remains a continued need
to remove hazardous and declining trees within the public right-of-way to ensure public safety
and maintain the long-term health of the City’s urban canopy. This work also supports ongoing
and upcoming planting projects funded through the USDA Urban and Community Forestry
Grant.

Top Notch Tree care, LLC was awarded the original contract following the bid opening held on
September 23, 2025. The contractor has successfully performed the work in accordance with the
contract requirements and has agreed to extend the contract for an additional year at the
negotiated pricing. The Department of Public Services has reviewed the proposed extension and
confirmed that Top Notch Treecare, LLC continues to meet all specifications and schedule
requirements.

The Department frequently partners with local contractors to provide critical tree removal and
forestry services and looks forward to continuing its partnership with Top Notch Treecare, LLC
through this contract extension.
