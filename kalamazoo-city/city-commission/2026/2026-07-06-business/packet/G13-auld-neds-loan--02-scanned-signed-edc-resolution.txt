CITY OF KALAMAZOO ~_.
ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY

RESOLUTION APPROVING AND RECOMMENDING CITY COMMISSION
APPROVAL OF AN ECONOMIC INITIATIVE FUND LOAN FOR AULD
NEDS HOSPITALITY, LLC

Board member Novotny, supported by Board member Ward, moved for the adoption of
the following resolution:

WHEREAS, the City of Kalamazoo Economic Development Corporation (the “EDC”),
pursuant to the provisions of Act 338 of 1974, as amended, seeks to provide resources to alleviate
and prevent conditions of employment by assisting and retaining local industrial and commercial
enterprises and encouraging the location and expansion of industrial and commercial enterprises;
and

WHEREAS, the EDC has established the Economic Initiative Fund (EIF), which is
supported with funds budgeted in the City General Fund and governed in accordance with the
Economic Initiative Fund & Other Economic Development Funds Program Guidelines; and

WHEREAS, Auld Neds Hospitality, LLC is redeveloping a vacant restaurant space
within Oakland Plaza at 3003 Oakland Drive into The Emerald Oak, a full-service Irish pub and
restaurant. The project includes substantial interior renovations, including demolition and
reconstruction, commercial kitchen installation, HVAC, plumbing and electrical upgrades,
custom millwork, furniture and fixtures, signage, audiovisual improvements, and the creation of
a private "Whiskey Snug" gathering space. The total project cost is estimated between $1.1
million and $1.2 million (altogether, the “Project”); and

WHEREAS, Auld Neds Hospitality, LLC, submitted an Economic Initiative Fund
Application dated May 28, 2026 (“EIF Application’’) to the EDC requesting participation in the
financing of the Project through an Economic Initiative Fund loan (“EIF Loan”); and

WHEREAS, the amount of the requested EIF Loan is $120,000, repayable at 2% interest
over a 10-year period with quarterly payments and secured by a personal guarantee; and

WHEREAS, the EDC has reviewed and now desires to approve Auld Neds Hospitality,
LLC's EIF Application and recommend its approval by the City Commission.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED AS FOLLOWS:

l. That the EDC hereby approves Auld Neds Hospitality, LLC's EIF Application and
recommends its approval by the City Commission.

2. Subject to the City Commission’s approval, if required, of the EIF Application, the
promissory note and personal guarantee are approved in the form materially presented at this
meeting, subject to changes recommended by the EDC’s or the City’s legal counsel.

3, Subject to the City Commission’s approval, if required, of the EIF Application, the
EDC Chairperson, Secretary, and/or Treasurer are authorized to take all actions and to sign all
agreements, certificates, instruments and documents necessary to finalize the EIF Loan with
Auld Neds Hospitality, LLC.
