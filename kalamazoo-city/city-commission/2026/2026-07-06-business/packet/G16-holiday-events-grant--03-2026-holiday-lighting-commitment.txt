June 9, 2026

RE: Commitment of Funding for 2026 Downtown Holiday Lighting Program

Dear City Manager Hankins:

This letter serves as formal confirmation that the Downtown Economic Growth Authority
(DEGA) Board of Directors approved a funding commitment of $30,000 for the City of
Kalamazoo's 2026 Downtown Holiday Lighting Program.

At its regular meeting on February 16, 2026, the DEGA Board voted to authorize the
allocation of $30,000 to support holiday lighting and décor improvements within
Downtown Kalamazoo, specifically the Kalamazoo Mall, Michigan Avenue, and South
Street, as outlined in the City's funding request.

The DEGA Board recognizes the value of Downtown holiday lighting and décor in creating a
vibrant and welcoming environment for residents and visitors, supporting local businesses
during the holiday season, and contributing to the overall economic vitality of Downtown
Kalamazoo.

The $30,000 commitment represents approved DEGA funding and may be counted as a
committed local funding source for grant applications and other fundraising efforts
associated with the 2026 Downtown Holiday Lighting Program. These funds have been
authorized by Board action and will be made available in accordance with applicable
DEGA funding and reimbursement procedures.

DEGA is pleased to partner with the City of Kalamazoo and other community stakeholders
in support of this important community initiative. We appreciate the efforts being
undertaken to enhance the Downtown experience during the holiday season and look
forward to the successful implementation of the 2026 program.

Sincerely,

Curt Aardema
Curt Aardema
Chair
Downtown Economic Growth Authority Board of Directors

cc: DEGA Board of Directors
