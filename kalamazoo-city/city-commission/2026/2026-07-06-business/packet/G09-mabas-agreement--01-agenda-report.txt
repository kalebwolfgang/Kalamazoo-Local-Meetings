SUBJECT:        Resolution Approving the Michigan Mutual Aid Box Alarm System Agreement

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt the Mutual Aid Box Alarm System
(MABAS) resolution and authorize the City Manager to sign the 2016 Amended MABAS
Agreement.

BACKGROUND:

On a daily basis, communities encounter emergencies that exceed their local Fire/EMS and
special operations capabilities. In many cases, these incidents do not rise to the level of a State of
Michigan Declaration of Disaster or Declaration of Emergency, both of which are required to
activate statewide mutual aid and the associated statutory protections for reimbursement,
liability, and workers’ compensation.

When such conditions exist, membership in the Michigan Mutual Aid Box Alarm System
(MABAS) provides critical operational and legal benefits to the impacted community, regardless
of geographic location. As a MABAS member agency, the City of Kalamazoo Department of
Public Safety (KDPS) gains access to prearranged, predetermined mutual aid resources from
other participating jurisdictions under a standardized and legally binding framework.
Adoption of the Amended (2016) MABAS Agreement ensures that KDPS operates under the
same terms and protections as all other signatories. Without a formal written mutual aid
agreement, such as MABAS, any request for assistance would remain purely voluntary. This
exposes both the Public Safety Chief and the assisting municipality to significant risk if
apparatus is damaged or if a Public Safety Officer is injured or killed in the line of duty while
responding outside the jurisdiction.
