RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the Resolution in support of the
Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) New Certificate application in the Northside
Neighborhood.

BACKGROUND:

On June 15th, 2026, the City Commission approved the creation of the Zone 32, Phase II NEZ
District, which covers the two parcels addressed as 825 Porter Street & 810 N Pitcher Street.
These parcels will be combined during development into a single parcel for the proposed new
38-unit residential mixed-use building. The building will have a neighborhood-scale retail space
facing N Pitcher Street.

The NEZ Certificate application is the step a developer takes to apply for qualifying tax relief for
up to 15 years. The City Commission NEZ Policy provides guidance and steps for achieving up
to 15 years. This is done through a series of zones that match the County Housing plan, levels of
affordability, types of dwelling units, and reduction in parking options, by our local policy, to
achieve up to 15 years. This project is within the "Urban Center" portion of the City in Table E
and Map E of the policy. By providing more than the minimum of 20% of the Area Median
income (AMI) at the 80th percentile or below, the project meets the requirements for 12 years.
There were options explored with City staff to reduce the parking count to achieve up to 15 years
of tax reduction. However, a reduction of spaces is not feasible for the project and the developer
will establish parking that meets the City standard but is not a reduction the NEZ policy requires
for additional years of tax reduction.

To obtain the NEZ certificate, the developer may apply for one certificate for the entire structure
and all the residential units within it. That request is submitted to the local government and
