RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the water release claim at Costco
Wholesale for the amount of Actual Cash Value of (ACV)$81,273.43

BACKGROUND:

In July 2025, the property located at 1500 Century Avenue, Kalamazoo, MI 49006, experienced
a water release from City-owned infrastructure. The City of Kalamazoo has historically accepted
responsibility for maintaining public infrastructure and ensuring it operates properly.

In this instance, water was released from a newly installed 3-inch water meter, resulting in
flooding and significant damage to the mechanical room, tire shop and showroom, store offices,
pharmacy, hearing and optical departments, as well as inventory and other property contents.
Hausch and Company investigated the incident and, in coordination with City staff, determined
that the meter was not installed or properly restrained in accordance with applicable installation
standards.

Pursuant to Michigan law, the City has accepted liability for the damages resulting from this
incident. Because the amount of the claim exceeds the $25,000 settlement authority granted to
the City Attorney, City Commission approval is required to authorize final resolution of this
matter.

implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
