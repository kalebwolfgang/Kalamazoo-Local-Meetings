Overall Consideration Regarding Combined Policy Changes
While each option is evaluated independently in this matrix, the Commission may choose to adopt multiple procedural changes
simultaneously. The combined effect of those changes may differ from the impacts identified for any single option. For example, moving
general public comment to the end of the meeting could reduce in-person participation because of longer wait times; however, offering
live remote call-in participation may mitigate that effect by allowing residents to remain engaged without being physically present for the
duration of the meeting. Likewise, shorter speaking times combined with remote participation may increase the total number of
residents able to provide comments during a meeting. Commissioners may wish to evaluate not only the individual merits of each
option, but also how combinations of procedural changes interact to affect accessibility, meeting efficiency, public participation, and
overall meeting management.
