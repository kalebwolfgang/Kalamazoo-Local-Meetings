SUBJECT: Public Comment Feedback for Commission Consideration

This information is presented as follow-up to the Discussion Regarding Call-in Public Comments
that occurred during the July 20 City Commission Business Meeting (Item K.1). The current plan
is to bring a recommendation forward for Commission consideration at the August 17 Business
Meeting, but this timeline can be adjusted if additional information or further discussion is needed.

Attachment 1: Public Comment Options Comparison Matrix

This matrix lists options for public comment time limits, remote public participation and placement
of general public comment on the agenda. It also includes an analysis of advantages, potential
drawbacks, operational impact, public engagement impact and legal & accessibility
considerations.

Attachment 2: Board and Commission Public Comment Survey

This table lists the current City Boards and Commissions that currently offer opportunities to call-
in for public comment, length of public comment time, when call-ins occur on the agenda, and
notes from staff liaisons. The table also includes information regarding Kalamazoo County
Commission’s call-in option obtained but Clerk Borling. Considerations for City staff to
administer call-ins for Commission meetings have been included at the bottom.
