               Monitoring and Incident Investigation System (Bid Ref#00000-026.86)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a one-year sole source contract for odor
monitoring, weather monitoring, maintenance, and modeling software with Envirosuite in the
amount of $139,344.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo’s Water Reclamation Plant (KWRP) is dedicated to being a good
neighbor and is committed to establishing a method for citizens to report odor observations.
These odor observations have been from all areas of the City and surrounding townships. Odor
investigation may be challenging in that odors can be very transient and difficult to track down
the source. The odor monitoring and odor investigation system by Envirosuite uses odor sensors,
weather stations, and National Weather Service data to model odor dispersion based on the
sensor and weather data. This information can be used to model the conditions that existed
during the time of an odor observation to determine the potential source of odor. We have
continued to improve upon the original contract with monitoring additions and monitor
relocations to better serve the surrounding communities.
