         A RESOLUTION APPROVING AMENDATORY CONTRACT 26-5303
        WITH THE MICHIGAN DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION FOR
        PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENTS THROUGHOUT THE
             ARCADIA CREEK CORRIDOR FROM OLIVER STREET
                 NORTHEASTERLY TO KALAMAZOO AVENUE

        Minutes of a regular meeting of the Kalamazoo City Commission held on August 17, 2026
at 7:00 p.m., local time, in the City Commission Chambers at City Hall, 241 W. South Street.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

Recitals:

   1. The City of Kalamazoo successfully competed for a federal PROTECT grant to eliminate
      the flood plain on the City's west side and determined that the Michigan Department of
      Transportation (MDOT) should be the direct recipient of that grant.

   2. On October 20, 2025 the Kalamazoo City Commission approved Contract 25-5530 with
      the MDOT, which allocated funding from this grant for the first phase of the following
      public infrastructure improvements:

            Preliminary engineering activities for building resilience in Kalamazoo’s downtown
            transportation network by improving stormwater management throughout the Arcadia
            Creek corridor from Oliver Street northeasterly to Kalamazoo Avenue; and all together
            with necessary related work.

   3. Funding for the second phase of this work is being allocated though an amendatory contract
      to Contract 25-5530.

   4. MDOT has designated the amendatory contract “Contract No. 26-5303”.

   5. MDOT requires a certified resolution from the local jurisdiction identifying the person(s)
      authorized to execute their contracts.

It is Resolved:

   1. Amendatory Contract No. 26-5303 with the Michigan Department of Transportation is
      approved.
