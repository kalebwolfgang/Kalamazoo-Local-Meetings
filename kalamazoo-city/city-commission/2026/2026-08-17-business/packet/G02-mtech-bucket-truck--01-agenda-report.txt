SUBJECT:       Approval of the cooperative purchase of an Aerial Bucket Truck from The Safety
               Company LLC dba MTech per CoPro (Bid Ref# 00000-026.87)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with The Safety Company LLC
dba MTech to supply a new aerial bucket truck per the CoPro+ Contract # 2023-JC-060-M to
replace the current bucket truck, vehicle 4-344, which is currently out of service in the amount of
$154,900.00.

BACKGROUND:

The Safety Company LLC dba MTech, contacted the City of Kalamazoo about available aerial
bucket trucks they have for sale. Vehicle 4-344, the existing aerial bucket truck used by the City
of Kalamazoo Public Services Department, is currently red tagged due to failed hydraulic
equipment. The manufacture will no longer support or repair the existing vehicle due to it being
over 25 years old and liability concerns. Replacement of the vehicle was recommended, and
rental equipment is being used to facilitate repairs currently. This new vehicle will allow
individuals to reach over 44 feet to make repairs and service equipment normally out of reach.
