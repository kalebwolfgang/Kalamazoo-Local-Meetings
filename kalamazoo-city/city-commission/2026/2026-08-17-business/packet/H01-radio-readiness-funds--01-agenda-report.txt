SUBJECT:       Acceptance of Radio Readiness Funds from the Kalamazoo Consolidated
               Dispatch Authority and Approval of a Budget Amendment

RECOMMENDATION:

Approve acceptance of $1,107,210 from the Kalamazoo County Consolidated Dispatch
Authority (KCCDA) Radio Readiness Program, amend the City of Kalamazoo capital
improvement budget to reflect the receipt of the funds, and authorize the City Manager to
execute all documents on behalf of the City.

BACKGROUND:

KCCDA established the two-year Radio Readiness Program using $8 million in Capital Project
Fund resources, with $4 million allocated in 2026 and an additional $4 million expected in 2027.
The program assists Kalamazoo County public safety agencies in modernizing radio
communications, completing law-enforcement migration to Advanced Encryption Standard
(AES) encryption to support Criminal Justice Information Security requirements, and advancing
use of the Kalamazoo Michigan Public Safety Communications System (MPSCS) subsystem.

KCCDA evaluated KDPS’s 2024 public safety radio inventory and determined that 416
subscriber devices are program-eligible: 293 portable radios and 123 mobile radios. Eligible
devices may qualify for reimbursement, replacement with a new device, or both options as
specified in the executed Memorandum of Understanding (MOU). The MOU was executed by
KDPS and KCCDA on June 2, 2026, following City Attorney approval.

A portion of the Radio Readiness Program funding is restricted to the replacement of obsolete
radio equipment identified in KDPS’s 2024 public safety radio inventory. These funds may only
be used to purchase new, program-compliant radios to replace the eligible obsolete devices. The
remaining funding represents reimbursement to the City of Kalamazoo for eligible radios
previously purchased by the City that already comply with the Radio Readiness Program
requirements.

FISCAL IMPACT:

Acceptance will provide KDPS with $1,107,210 in one-time external funding for eligible radio
communication equipment. KCCDA will not be responsible for future maintenance, repair, or
replacement costs; those ongoing costs will remain the City’s responsibility and will be
addressed through future capital budgets, as appropriate. Approval of the acceptance of these
funds is also approval of a budget amendment in the amount of $1,107,210 to the capital
improvement budget. The acceptance of these funds will offset previously bonded for radio
equipment purchases and new radio purchases.
