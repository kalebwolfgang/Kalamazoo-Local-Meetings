         RADIO READINESS PROGRAM MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

THIS MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING is entered into by and between the Kalamazoo County
Consolidated Dispatch Authority (“KCCDA”) and Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety (“KDPS”).

     WHEREAS, the KCCDA Board of Directors allocated four million dollars ($4,000,000) of Capital
     Project Fund monies in 2026 and is expected to allocate the same amount in 2027, for a two-year
     Radio Readiness Program (“Program”) totaling eight million dollars ($8,000,000); and

     WHEREAS, KCCDA’s Program provides funding to assist public safety agencies in Kalamazoo
     County accomplish two critical radio communication goals: complete the transition of law
     enforcement to Advanced Encryption Standard to comply with Criminal Justice Information Security
     policy requirements, and encourage Fire Departments and the Kalamazoo County Medical Control
     Authority to migrate to the Kalamazoo MPSCS Subsystem for primary communications; and

     WHEREAS, the Program is based on the public safety radio inventory survey completed in 2024 and
     only applies to radios/devices directly attributable and dedicated to the delivery of 9-1-1 service
     within Kalamazoo County; and

     WHEREAS, the Program is optional for public safety agencies, and is governed by the Radio
     Readiness Program Guidelines (“Guidelines”) as established by the KCCDA Board of Directors; and

     WHEREAS, the Program shall be administered on a first-come first-serve basis until allocated
     monies are expended or December 31, 2027, whichever comes first; and

     WHEREAS, KDPS has requested to participate in this optional Program.

NOW THEREFORE, the parties agree to the following:

1. In accordance with the Guidelines, KCCDA administration has evaluated KDPS’s 2024 public safety
   radio inventory survey and deemed the following devices to be Program eligible:

      2. KDPS agrees that any reimbursement Program monies received from KCCDA for eligible devices, must
         be used to bring the device(s) into compliance with the Guidelines as soon as possible but no later than
         April 1, 2028.

      3. KDPS agrees that any new devices purchased and activated as part of this Program shall be compliant
         with the Guidelines at the time the new device is activated as a subscriber unit on the Kalamazoo
         MPSCS Subsystem.

      4. All Program funding will be distributed to KDPS as part of KCCDA's normative accounts payable
         schedule.
              a. Reimbursement Program monies will be distributed via check after supporting documentation is
                  provided to KCCDA to verify proof of purchase and equipment received dates.
              b. New device Program monies will be distributed via check after supporting documentation is
                  provided to KCCDA to verify proof of purchase and the equipment has been received by KDPS's
                  vendor.

      5. These are one-time Program monies and KCCDA holds no responsibility for maintenance, repair, or
         replacement of any radio communication devices.

      6. The parties shall work together with KDPS's vendor(s) of choice. KCCDA administration reserves the
         right to request any/all supporting documentation as it deems appropriate and necessary to validate or
         verify radio communication devices adhere to the Program Guidelines.
