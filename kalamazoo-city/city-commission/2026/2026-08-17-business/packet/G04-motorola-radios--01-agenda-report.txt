RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a cooperative MIDEAL purchase of public
safety radios, accessories, encryption software, and DVRS activation upgrades from Motorola
Solutions, in an amount not to exceed $264,058.86. The purchase is funded through the
Kalamazoo County Consolidated Dispatch Authority Radio Readiness Program and amend the
2026 capital improvement budget for the radio purchase.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo County Consolidated Dispatch Authority (KCCDA) established the Radio
Readiness Program to help public safety agencies modernize radio communications, complete
the transition of law-enforcement communications to Advanced Encryption Standard (AES), and
improve interoperability on the Kalamazoo Michigan Public Safety Communications System
subsystem. KCCDA evaluated KDPS’s 2024 public safety radio inventory and identified eligible
radios and devices under the program.

A portion of the Radio Readiness Program award is restricted to replacing obsolete radios
identified in the inventory. The remaining eligible funding reimburses the City for qualifying,
program-compliant radios previously purchased by the City. This proposed purchase addresses
the replacement and upgrade component of the program by replacing obsolete subscriber units
and bringing eligible existing equipment into compliance with current encryption and
interoperability requirements.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The total purchase price is $264,058.86. The radios, software, accessories, and upgrades are
eligible expenses under the KCCDA Radio Readiness Program and will be paid from the City’s
$1,107,210 program award. No City General Fund contribution is anticipated for this purchase.
Consistent with the program MOU, future maintenance, repair, and replacement costs will
remain the City’s responsibility.

The approval of this request will amend the capital improvement budget by $264,058.86 and will
create a new capital project for the KCCDA Radio Readiness Program.
