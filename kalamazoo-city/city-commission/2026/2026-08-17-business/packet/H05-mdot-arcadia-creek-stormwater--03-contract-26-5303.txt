       WITNESSETH:

       WHEREAS, the parties to the Amendment have heretofore, by a Contract dated
December 5, 2025, hereinafter referred to as "CONTRACT 25-5530" provided for the

and is described as follows:

       The performance of preliminary engineering activities for building resilience in

      1.     Section 4. of CONTRACT 25-5530 is revised to read as follows:

                     4.     The PROJECT COST shall be met in part by contributions by

             by the DEPARTMENT to the REQUESTING PARTY. The balance of the
