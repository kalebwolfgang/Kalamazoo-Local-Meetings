SUBJECT:       Kalamazoo Christian High School Fireworks Permit

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a request from Kalamazoo Christian
Schools to publicly display fireworks at Mayor's Riverfront Park on September 25, 2026.

BACKGROUND:

Pursuant to requirements in state law, Kalamazoo Christian Schools, through its pyrotechnic
operator, has submitted an application for a permit to publicly display fireworks at Mayor's
Riverfront Park, located at 251 Mills Street. Fire Marshal Scott Brooks has endorsed the
application, and the pyrotechnic operator has provided the required insurance certificate naming
the City of Kalamazoo as additionally insured.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:

Inviting Public Places - Vibrant streets, exceptional parks, and welcoming activities.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Appropriate Depth of Engagement

Inform (one-way conversation) – no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

The applicant will be asked to reach out to surrounding neighborhoods and inform them of the
date and time of the fireworks display.
