                Kalamazoo, Michigan.

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a RESOLUTION to establish a moratorium
on the acceptance and/or processing of applications for Data Centers, Battery Energy Storage
Systems, Cryptocurrency Mining Centers, and Artificial Intelligence Data Centers within the
City of Kalamazoo, Michigan.

BACKGROUND:

The City Commission is responsible for the health, safety, and general welfare of the City’s
residents. Over the last several months, the City Commission has observed a recent increase in
the number of proposed data centers and battery energy storage systems in nearby Kalamazoo
County communities, as well as in Southwest and West Michigan counties. In addition, many
residents have voiced their concerns about these types of facilities potentially locating in the City
and in adjacent communities. The potential impacts of these data centers and battery energy
storage systems include, but are not limited to:

      • Significant noise generation from cooling systems and backup generators affecting
        adjoining land uses and quality of life, which can cause chronic sleep disturbances
      • Substantial water usage for cooling purposes
      • Potential environmental impacts, including air and water pollution
      • Increased fire risk due to the great amount of heat generated by these facilities, as well as
        the increased difficulty extinguishing fires to lithium-ion batteries used by battery energy
        storage systems
      • Extremely high energy consumption and strain on existing utility infrastructure

These types of operations present unique challenges not adequately addressed by Kalamazoo’s
current Zoning Ordinance and City Code. In fact, many other Michigan communities also do not
have the necessary zoning and regulatory ordinances to address data centers and battery energy
storage systems. This has resulted in the adoption of moratoriums across the state of Michigan to
allow communities sufficient time to research and develop regulations tailored to these types of
operations. This moratorium would provide the City of Kalamazoo with time to research and
develop the necessary regulations. The City Commission has the authority to impose this
moratorium in accordance with the Michigan Home Rule City Act (Act 279 of 1909), as
amended.

The moratorium will temporarily pause the acceptance and/or processing of applications for the
following types of development proposals:

     • Data Centers
     • Battery Energy Storage Centers (“BESS”)
     • Cryptocurrency Mining Centers
     • Artificial Intelligence (“AI”) Data Centers

The proposed length of the moratorium is for one year, until August 16, 2027. It may be
extinguished earlier if the necessary City ordinance amendments are adopted before that date.
Finally, the moratorium can be extended as appropriate to allow sufficient time to complete
amendments to its ordinances and regulations.
