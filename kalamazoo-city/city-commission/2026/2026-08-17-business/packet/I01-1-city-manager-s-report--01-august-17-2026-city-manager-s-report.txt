City Hall Water Project
The City of Kalamazoo plans to bring a new water pipe system online within City Hall starting this weekend. This
domestic water project will impact access to drinking water, sinks and faucets, and City Hall restrooms. The
water project changeover will begin on Saturday, August 22. Starting Monday, August 24, the only restrooms
that will be available are the restrooms on the first floor. All restrooms, sinks, and water sources other than on
the first floor will shut down for the week beginning August 24 through August 28.

Connecting With Community
As part of my ongoing efforts to engage with City staff, community organizations, neighborhood leaders, and
residents, it was a pleasure to attend a new “All Things Possible Camp” offering hosted by Parks & Recreation
last Friday, Aug. 14. The Kzoo Parks pilot program, called “Ignite- Youth Innovation & AI Entrepreneurship,” was
an event held for students ages 13-15 in the Richmond Center for Visual Arts on the campus of Western
Michigan University. The program brought teens together to solve real-world problems, explore creative ideas,
build digital projects, and learn how to use Artificial Intelligence responsibly. Approximately 20 kids participated
in the program and engaged in hands-on activities, teamwork, and guidance from mentors and WMU
students. The goal was to help them develop confidence, build creativity and communication skills, and leave
with a project they can proudly showcase.
