Call-in Staffing Considerations
  As other boards/commissions are utilizing call-in function, the feature can be easily activated for Commission meetings.
  Currently, board liaisons/staff administer the call-in function for meetings. IT does not manage call-ins for any meetings at this time.
  IT covering CC meetings would create an additional duty outside of normal daily operations for IT. IT already has multiple staff that work regularly outside of business hours.
 Estimated 10 hours/month, or 6% of total monthly hours (for 1 FTE) to cover call-ins for City Commission meetings.
 IT has identified 2 staff and 1 back-up that are most familiar with Commission Chambers technology. If IT takes on this role, workload impacts could include: Tuesday morning
 service desk response times being slower/delayed; short term delays to cloud migration project or server admin work; gaps in capacity for handling “patch” work that occurs
 after-hours.
 PMN has indicated that they will need to make sure they get the call-in audio for the stream, but this should be straightforward.
