RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an amendment to the subrecipient
agreement (26-5303) with the Michigan Department of Transportation and a budget amendment
in the amount of $2,661,433.50 for engineering activities as part of the PROTECT grant.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo successfully competed for a PROTECT grant to eliminate the flood plain
on the city's west side and determined that the Michigan Department of Transportation (MDOT)
should be the direct recipient of that grant. The first phase of this funding was previously
allocated and accepted by the City for Preliminary Engineering and that work is well under way
This amended agreement between MDOT and the City of Kalamazoo allocates the second phase
of funds through the PROTECT grant, supporting "the performance of preliminary engineering
activities for building resilience in Kalamazoo’s downtown transportation network by improving
stormwater management throughout the Arcadia Creek corridor from Oliver Street northeasterly
to Kalamazoo Avenue; and all together with necessary related work."
