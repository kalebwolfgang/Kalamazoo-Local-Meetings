        WHEREAS, the City does hereby determine that it is necessary to pay all or part of the
costs of certain public improvements in the City, including a) acquiring and constructing major
street and local street improvements, including traffic signal upgrades; b) acquiring, constructing,
furnishing and equipping public safety facilities improvements and equipment; c) acquiring and
constructing parks and recreation improvements; d) acquiring information technology equipment;
e) acquiring public services facilities improvements and equipment; and f) parking system
improvements; including all related equipment, site improvements, appurtenances and attachments
(together, the “Projects”); and

      WHEREAS, the cost of the Projects are estimated to be approximately Nine Million Four
Hundred Thousand Dollars ($9,400,000); and

       WHEREAS, to finance the cost of the Projects, the City Commission deems it necessary
to borrow the principal amount of not to exceed Nine Million Four Hundred Thousand Dollars
($9,400,000) and issue capital improvement bonds pursuant to Act 34, Public Acts of Michigan,
2001, as amended (“Act 34”) to pay the cost of the Projects; and

        WHEREAS, a notice of intent to issue bonds was published in accordance with Act 34
which provides that the capital improvement bonds may be issued without a vote of the electors
of the City unless a proper petition for an election on the question of the issuance of the bonds is
filed with the City Clerk within a period of forty-five (45) days from the date of publication and
as of this date no petition has been filed with the Clerk.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
