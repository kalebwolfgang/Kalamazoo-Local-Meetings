SUBJECT:       Resolution Authorizing 2026 Capital Improvement Bonds (Limited Tax General
               Obligation)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a Resolution for the issuance of General
Obligation Limited Tax Bonds in an amount not to exceed Nine Million Four Hundred Thousand
($9.400,000), for the purpose of financing fiscal year 2026 capital projects.

BACKGROUND:

The City Commission adopted a Notice of Intent Resolutions on June 15, 2026 to issue Series
2026 Capital Improvement Bonds in a total amount not to exceed $9,400,000. This Resolution
initiates the process of issuing debt to finance the City’s FY 2026 Capital Improvement program,
in accordance with the Adopted FY 2026 Budget. These funds will be used on projects in the
General Capital Improvement program, including facility upgrades, park improvements,
technology improvements, and heavy equipment. They will also be used for the Local and
Major Streets capital programs.

Under the provisions of the Resolution and State law, the public was notified of the City’s intent
to issue this debt, and has been provided with a 45-day period to circulate petitions for a
referendum. That 45-day periods expired with no petition having been issued, the City is now
authorized to issue this debt.

The Bonds are a limited tax general obligation of the City, secured by the City’s limited tax
pledge but are payable from various sources, including the General Fund, major and local street
funds, golf course revenues, parking revenues, and other City sources. The Resolution meets the
requirements for capital improvement bonds as required by the Municipal Finance Act, Act 34,
Public Acts of Michigan, 2001, as amended. The Resolution sets forth the terms of the Bonds
and provides for a competitive sale of the Bonds. The Resolution is in a form similar to previous
bond resolutions of the City.
