RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with L.D. Docsa Associates, Inc.
for the Central Treatment Plant Filter Media Replacement Project, in the amount of
$3,225,300.00.

BACKGROUND:

Central Water Treatment Plant is the primary baseload station for the Kalamazoo Regional
Water System. It was built in 1995. One portion of the treatment process is filters that use
media to remove iron and manganese minerals from the water to improve the aesthetic quality.
 The media in the filters is original to the plant and has exceeded its useful life. One of eight
cells has completely plugged and is no longer functional. This cell needs the media replaced to
be operational again. The other seven cells also need the media replaced.

Public Services worked with Tetra Tech to put together bid documents to complete this work.
 Public Services included Alternate 1 media for an in-kind replacement of the existing media and
Alternate 2 media of granular activated carbon. Based on conversations with staff at the
Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE), Alternate 2 cannot be
installed until further pilot study work has been completed for issuance of the construction
permit. Therefore, Public Services has elected to award this contract only based on Alternate 1
media.

Bids were opened on July 23, 2026. Two responsive bids were received. RK Davis, Inc. (a
business located outside of Kalamazoo County) being the low responsive bidder and L.D. Docsa
Associates, Inc. (LDD, a business located inside Kalamazoo County) being the second lowest
bidder. LDD’s bid was within 2% of RK Davis, Inc.’s bid and L.D. Docsa Associates, Inc. has
accepted the Local Preference Opportunity, agreeing to reduce their bid to be equal to RK Davis,
