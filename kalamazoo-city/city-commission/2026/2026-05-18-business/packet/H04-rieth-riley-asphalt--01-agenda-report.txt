SUBJECT:       Approval of a contract extension with Rieth-Riley for Asphalt Paving Materials
               (Bid Ref#74507-018.1)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract extension with Rieth-Riley for
asphalt paving materials in the amount of $1,635,400.00.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo has an extensive local street repaving program in addition to the repair,
maintenance, and upkeep of many different streets and facilities that use hot mix asphalt (HMA)
and asphalt materials. To facilitate a constant and cost-effective supply of this important
construction material, Public Services contracts on a yearly basis with a local plant to provide
mixes in such quantity and type to meet City needs.

Renwal pricing for 2026 Asphalt Paving Materials were requested. Rieth-Riley has provided
HMA mix to the City in past years with satisfactory results, and Public Services thus
recommends awarding the contract.
