RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a sole source purchase with OpenGov for a
one-year software renewal in the amount of $123,800.99.

BACKGROUND:

The City has been successfully using this software since 2018. It allows the City to utilize
modern budgeting approaches to more efficiently and effectively gather budget requests and
prepare the annual budget. This software provides transparency in financial reporting,
performance measures for current goals and strategic initiatives, and budget transparency.
OpenGov is the sole source provider of the City's current open data initiative.

The OpenGov Suite of software includes Operating & Capital Budgeting, Online Budget Book,
Reporting & Analysis, Transparency, Open Town Hall, Performance Measures, Workforce
Planning, Dashboards, Story Builder, Non-Financial Integration, Premium Support, and
OpenGov University.
