SUBJECT:       Approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Northern Pump & Well
               Company for Well Rehabilitations (Bid Ref#91216-003.0.1)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission approve a professional service agreement with Northern
Pump & Well Company (Northern) for well rehabilitation and associated maintenance in the
amount of $128,431.00.

BACKGROUND:

In 2024, Purchasing pre-approved three-year contracts with two drilling companies identified by
staff as being qualified to perform hydrogeological services for the City of Kalamazoo for work
on an as-needed-basis. These companies are Peerless-Midwest Inc. and Northern Pump and Well
Company.

Water Resources contacted Northern to provide quotes to perform 3 well rehabilitations. Water
Resources has been very satisfied with the work Northern has performed on previous well
rehabilitations and recommends approval of this purchase order.

This work will focus on rehabilitation of wells at Station No. 1 and Station No. 11. Well
rehabilitation is necessary to restore pumping capacity and performance of the well and to
maintain shaft, screen, casing, column pipe, and spider assemblies through inspection, repair, and
replacement.
