RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a three-year extension of a contract with
Wayne County Appraisal, LLC d/b/a WCA Assessing in the amount of $1,398,611.16 for the
duration of the contract, from June 1, 2026, through May 31, 2029.

Of this total amount, $443,651.40 is the first-year amount for property maintenance for real
property pertinent to building permits, personal property canvass, entry and analysis in the City
of Kalamazoo, real property inspections and canvass based upon a ten (10) year re-inspection
cycle, Board of Review assistance, clerical services, and small claims appeals performed by
WCA Assessing of the three-year extension. Including the first year, year two amount is
$465,834.00, and the year three amount is $489,125.76 for a contract extension total of
$1,398,611.16.

BACKGROUND:

In January 2012, the City Commission approved the Adopted FY 2012 Budget inclusive of a
plan to contract out assessing field work. This plan has had the effect of eliminating 3 full-time
real property field appraisers and 1 full-time personal property appraiser. WCA has been
providing assessment services since April 2012. On an annual basis, WCA Assessing has
completed field inspections on all building permits and reviewed the assessment records on more
than 10% of all parcels to comply with State guidelines that all parcels be inspected within a 7 to
10-year cycle. Utilizing a contracted service has kept costs below what it would be anticipated
to cost the City for full time staffing. WCA has also provided excellent customer service
throughout their time contracted for assessing services.
