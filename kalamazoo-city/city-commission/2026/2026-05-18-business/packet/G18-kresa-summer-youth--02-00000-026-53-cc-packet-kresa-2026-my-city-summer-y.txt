Memorandum of Understanding between the City of Kalamazoo and Kalamazoo Regional
Educational Service Agency, Career Connect (June 2026)

The City of Kalamazoo will provide funding for the Kalamazoo Regional Educational Service Agency (KRESA),
Career Connect will provide employment and training opportunities for youth. This letter serves as an
agreement between KRESA Career Connect and the City of Kalamazoo (City).

All services will be provided in accordance with the following terms:

A Purchase Order will be issued by the City of Kalamazoo to KRESA in the amount $416,000.00. This is the
maximum contractual amount of funding for the 2026 My-CITY/Summer Youth Employment Program, which the
City agrees to pay. Any unused funds will be returned to the City.

Upon receiving an invoice, the City will make three payments to KRESA, one on or before August 7, one on or
before August 31, and the final payment will be due 10 days following receipt by the City of the final report
submitted by KRESA Career Connect following the completion of the program.

The youth and career coaches employed for this program are employees of KRESA; they will not be considered
employees of the City of Kalamazoo.

The budget amount of $416,000.00 will support the hiring of approximately 80-120 youth between the ages of 14 -
21 to participate in work experience, career exploration and employability skills training. Funds may also be
utilized during the school year to provide additional opportunities for paid internships, trainings, talent tours, job
shadows, and continued career coaching for youth.

Additional work may be identified by the City and KRESA Career Connect and conducted within the Kalamazoo
Public School District and other City of Kalamazoo neighborhoods.

Youth will be employed from June 22, 2026, through August 7, 2026. KRESA Summer Career Coaches will be
employed from June 15, 2026, through August 14, 2026. KRESA staff will be employed with the summer project
from approximately April 6, 2026 — August 28, 2026.

Youth may have the opportunity to continue employment during the school year and KRESA Career Coaches will
be employed with the project during that time.

The City and KRESA Career Connect will work closely with each other to determine the specificity of data to be
collected and identify any special needs or requests.

KRESA Career Connect will submit a final report for this project due 30 days after the end of the program. The
report shall include a summary of expenditures, and program statistics, such as number of youths hired, number of
career coaches, participant demographics, and a summary of all training conducted.

AGREEMENT

I/we acknowledge and agree to the terms of this Memorandum of Understanding.

For the City of Kalamazoo For Kalamazoo RESA

By: Malcolm Hankins, City Manager By: Dr. Dedrick Martin, Superintendent
