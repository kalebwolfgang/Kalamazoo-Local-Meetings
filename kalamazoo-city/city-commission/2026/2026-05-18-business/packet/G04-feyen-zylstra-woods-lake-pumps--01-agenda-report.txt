RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Feyen-Zylstra LLC to supply, install and relocate two new sanitary lift station pumps at the
Woods Lake lift station in the amount of $252,381.00.

BACKGROUND:

On August 1st, 2022, the City of Kalamazoo awarded a contract to Feyen-Zylstra LLC for
instrumentation, controls, and communication improvements at several sanitary lift station
locations. Woods Lake improvements originally included a new control panel, and two new VFD
(variable frequency drives) panels to control the speed of the pumps. To fit all this equipment
into the existing lift station building and meet National Electric Code spacing requirements
additional space is needed at the ground level of the station. To create this space the pumps were
discussed and planned to be relocated downstairs by changing them to dry pit submersible
pumps. These modifications were originally planned during design of these electrical
improvements, but needs of the system caused priorities to change, and the pumps still require
replacement. This additional scope of work will improve the pump and station design and create
a safe, more efficient sanitary lift station.
