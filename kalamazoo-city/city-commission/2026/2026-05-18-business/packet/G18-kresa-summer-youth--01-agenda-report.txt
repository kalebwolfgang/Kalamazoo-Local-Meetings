RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Memorandum of Understanding with the
Kalamazoo Regional Educational Service Agency / Career Connect for the summer youth
employment and training program in the amount of $416,000.

BACKGROUND:

The City annually participates in a partnership with the Kalamazoo Regional Educational Service
Agency/Career Connect to provide summer employment and training opportunities for youth.
The budgeted amount will support the hiring of approximately 80-120 youth between the ages
of 14-21 to participate in career exploration, employability skills training and paid internships.
Funds may also be utilized during the school year to provide additional opportunities for paid
internships, additional training, talent tours, job shadows, and continued career coaching for
youth.
