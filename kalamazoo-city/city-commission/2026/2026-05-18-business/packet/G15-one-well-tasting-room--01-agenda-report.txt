SUBJECT:       Approval of an Off-Premises Tasting Room Permit for One Well Brewing,
               located at 4213 Portage St., Kalamazoo, MI 49001.

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission grant local government approval for an Off-
Premises Tasting Room Permit for One Well Brewing, located at 4213 Portage St., Kalamazoo,
MI 49001, and authorize the City Clerk to execute all documents required for submission to the
Michigan Liquor Control Commission (MLCC).

BACKGROUND:

One Well Brewing is seeking approval of an Off-Premises Tasting Room Permit in connection
with its existing Distiller License located at 3618 Gembrit Circle, Kalamazoo, Michigan. The
proposed off-premises tasting room would operate at 4213 Portage Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan.
Under Michigan law, an Off-Premises Tasting Room Permit allows a licensed manufacturer to
sell and serve its products for onsite consumption at a location separate from the primary
licensed production facility. Approval of the permit would support the continued growth and
expansion of the business. The applicant will remain subject to all applicable Michigan Liquor
Control Commission requirements, as well as local zoning, building, and operational regulations.
