RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with L D Docsa Associates Inc
for labor and materials to install launder covers on the secondary clarifiers five (5) through eight
(8) at the Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant located at the Harrison Facility in the amount of
$406,177.00.

BACKGROUND:

Natural algae formed on the secondary clarifier effluent launders creates an operational challenge
on the tertiary disc filters when it detaches from the concrete wall and enters into the treatment
flow. Covering the final clarifier effluent launders will prevent the algae from growing. To
mitigate these challenges on an advanced timeline, the City posted a bid package on February 12,
2026 with a bid opening occurring on March 17, 2026.
