RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract extension with UNIQUE Paving
Materials Corp. for Asphalt Patching Materials in the amount of $293,000.00.

BACKGROUND:

Renewal pricing was obtained from UNIQUE Paving Materials. Asphalt patching continues to
be a core function of Public Works operations. To ensure the best results, the City has
maintained the alternative cold mix design used in recent years, which has demonstrated
improved performance.
