RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Purchase Agreement with Stephen and
Linda French for the purchase of Parcel No. 07-27-280-012, in the amount of $57,000.00, and
authorize City Manager to sign all necessary documents.

BACKGROUND:

Public Services operates Treatment Plant No. 39 located at 8801 Miller Drive. Treatment Plant
No. 39 is a baseload station for the East Side High District. It is a unique plant as it is the only
plant in the Kalamazoo Regional Water System with a Ranney Well. A Ranney Well features a
large vertical caisson with horizontal radial collector laterals. This plant was constructed in 1988
and is approaching its useful life.

Stephen and Linda French approached Public Services and inquired if the City would be
interested in purchasing Parcel No. 07-27-280-012, the property adjacent to Treatment Plant No.
39. As Public Services expects to make future treatment plant upgrades, and in an effort to
achieve superior source water protection through additional isolation distance around the existing
wellhead, Public Services was intrigued by the inquiry. Public Services retained the professional
appraisal services of Appraisal Group II, LLC via Metro Consulting Associates to perform an
appraisal of the property. Public Services offered $57,000 based on the appraisal report. This
offer has been accepted, and Public Services recommends approval of this purchase agreement.
