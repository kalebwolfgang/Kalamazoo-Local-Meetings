SUBJECT:       Approval of a contract with Republic Services for removal of waste and
               recyclable materials at various city properties (Bid Ref # 91027-029.0)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Republic Services for
removal of waste and recyclable materials at various city properties in the amount of
$136,578.48.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo contracts waste and recyclable removal from all City facilities.
Purchasing facilitated the procurement process to attain bids for removal of waste and recyclable
materials at various city properties. Republic was the lowest responding vendor and is being
recommended to provide this service.
