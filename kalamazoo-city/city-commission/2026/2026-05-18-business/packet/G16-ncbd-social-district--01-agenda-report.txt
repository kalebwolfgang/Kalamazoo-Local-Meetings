SUBJECT:       Approval of Social District in the Northside Cultural Business District

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a resolution approving the establishment of a
social district in the Northside Cultural Business District.

BACKGROUND:

The Northside Cultural Business District Authority (NCBDA) was created to support Northside
resident-owned businesses and expand business ownership in the neighborhood; build the
cultural identity of the neighborhood and preserve significant places; and improve infrastructure,
facades, and streets.

In July 2020, in order to help offset statewide impacts of COVID 19, Public Act 124 of 2020 was
signed into law. PA 124 allows municipalities to designate “social districts” and “commons
areas” within those districts, into which customers of licensed establishments could serve
alcoholic drinks. Patrons could take a drink into the commons area, wander about, shop in store,
browse a gallery, or pause to enjoy their drink on a bench or table in the public realm. A
commons area could be established any place in the City that has three or more licensed
establishments contiguous to the commons area.

The City's first social district and commons area has already been established along the
Kalamazoo Mall, Michigan Avenue, and associated adjacent blocks with data supporting its
subsequent success. The NCBDA, in partnership with local small businesses, want to establish
its first social district. Similar to the downtown district, the proposed commons area would
operate seven days a week, 10am-10pm Sunday-Thursday, and 10am-12am Friday and Saturday.
In accordance with PA 124, establishments will be required to serve in plastic cups no larger
than 16oz that identify the social district, and the licensed establishment serving them. No other
open containers will be permitted in the commons area. The area will be maintained by the local

The proposed program would be monitored regularly for compliance, cleanliness, and economic
impact. Adjustments to hours, operations, or other policies may be made at a later date, should

in other parts of the district or other neighborhood nodes express interest.

This action is intended to boost capacity to support the economic vitality of businesses within the
district. This area will provide an opportunity for larger gatherings and events, bringing much
need attention and activity in the district. It is anticipated that this will have a positive impact on
the economic condition of businesses within the Northside Cultural Business District. Small,
locally owned businesses, and their employees will be the primary benefactors of this program.

There is no fiscal impact to the City of Kalamazoo. All expenses for the social district will come
