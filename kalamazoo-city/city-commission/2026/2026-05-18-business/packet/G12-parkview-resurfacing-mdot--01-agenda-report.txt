RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with the Michigan Department
of Transportation for the resurfacing of Parkview Avenue from Drake Road to Tamsin Avenue in
the amount of $351,900 of which $288,030 is federal grant funds, and $63,870 is the city's share.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo has been granted funds from the Federal Highway Administration and
administered by the Michigan Department of Transportation under the “Surface Transportation –
Urban” program. These funds are intended to improve and preserve streets and is consistent with
the city’s “Streets for All Program” and will help the city advance its efforts.

This project will consist of cold milling and hot mix asphalt paving along Parkview Avenue from
Drake Road to Tamsin Avenue, including aggregate shoulder, structure adjustments, and
permanent pavement markings, along with all necessary related work.

Approval of this agreement will allow the City to complete the project in accordance with
finalized design requirements, regulatory obligations, and MDOT LAP procedures.
