RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a cooperative purchase with MacQueen
Equipment LLC for a combination sewer cleaner in the amount of $593,958.76.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo maintains approximately 300 miles of sanitary sewer pipe assets within
the corporate limits of the City. In addition, the City maintains approximately 400 miles of
sanitary sewer pipe assets within the City’s partner communities. The City of Kalamazoo, Public
Services Department, Wastewater Division routinely cleans debris and obstructions from the
sanitary sewer system as best management practices within the asset management program as
well as critical response to identified sewer blockages and overflows.

In addition to the combined approximate 700 miles of sanitary sewer pipes, the City also
maintains 66 sanitary sewer lift stations. The wet wells of the lift stations also require routine
cleaning maintenance utilizing a combination sewer cleaner as best management practices within
the asset management program.

This equipment provides the City the opportunity to continue maintaining the City of
Kalamazoo’s Sanitary Sewer assets and respond to customer needs.
