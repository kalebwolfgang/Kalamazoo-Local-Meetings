SUBJECT:       City Manager Temporary Housing Allowance Extension

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a six-month extension of the City
Manager’s housing allowance in accordance with the terms of the City Manager Employment
Agreement.

BACKGROUND:

At the October 30, 2025, City Commission meeting, the Commission approved an employment
agreement with the City Manager. The agreement included a Temporary Housing Allowance
beginning in January 2026 for a six-month period. The City Manager Employment Agreement
also includes a provision authorizing the City Commission to extend the temporary housing
allowance for an additional six months to provide the City Manager adequate time to continue
pursuing a home purchase. The requested extension would be consistent with the allowance
provisions contemplated within the employment agreement and would provide continued
flexibility as the City Manager evaluates long-term area housing options.
