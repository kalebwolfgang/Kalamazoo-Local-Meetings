241 W South St

Kalamazoo, MI 49007

Dear City Assessor Powers,

On Monday, May 18", 2026 at 7:00 p.m., the Kalamazoo City Commission will conduct a public hearing
regarding the proposed Zone 32 NEZ Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (“NEZ”). The NEZ provides a tax incentive
for a defined period of time for new construction and redevelopment and is intended to improve to spur residential
investment where it might not otherwise occur. The public hearing is being held pursuant to PA 147 of 1992

Neighborhood Enterprise Zone Act, as amended, and will occur in the City Commission Chambers of City Hall,
at 241 West South Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan.

You are invited to attend the hearing to learn more about the proposed NEZ and provide comment. Per section
207.773(3) of Act 147 of 1992 (The Neighborhood Enterprise Zone Act), the city is required to notify the
governing body and assessor of each taxing jurisdiction that levies ad valorem property taxes in the proposed

neighborhood enterprise zone of our intention to create NEZ. A map of the proposed NEZ is enclosed for your
review.

If you wish to express your views and recommendations prior to the hearing, or if you have any questions
regarding the fiscal, economic or general implications of the proposed NEZ, please feel free to contact Bobby
Durkee, Assistant City Planner at 269-337-8172 or durkeer@kalamazoocity.org. After the public hearing on May
18th, the City Commission will consider action to create the Zone 32 NEZ at their June 15, 2026 regular meeting.

Sincerely,

Shelby Moss
Deputy City Clerk
