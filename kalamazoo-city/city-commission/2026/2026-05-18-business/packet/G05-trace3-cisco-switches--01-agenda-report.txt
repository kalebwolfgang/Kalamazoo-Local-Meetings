SUBJECT:       Approval of a cooperative MiDeal purchase with Trace3 for Cisco switches as
               part of hardware lifecycle replacement (Bid Ref#00000-021.151)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the cooperative MIDEAL purchase of
Cisco switches as part of hardware lifecycle replacement in the amount of $268,831.18.

BACKGROUND:

The City replaces network devices like switches and wireless access points on a regular schedule
to keep systems reliable, secure, and supported. Upgrading hardware is needed as older models
lose support, create security risks, and become less dependable.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
