COO Lam Honored with 2026 YWCA Women of Achievement
Our very own Laura Lam is being recognized and honored as a 2026 Woman of Achievement by YWCA
Kalamazoo. Laura’s dedicated service to the City of Kalamazoo spans more than 13 years, during which she
has consistently demonstrated exceptional leadership, professionalism, and a deep commitment to public
service.

Throughout her tenure, Laura has played an instrumental role in advancing key City initiatives, strengthening
organizational operations, and supporting both staff and the broader community with integrity and
compassion. Her recognition as a Woman of Achievement reflects not only her professional accomplishments,
but also the positive and lasting impact she continues to make within the organization and across the
Kalamazoo community.

We congratulate Laura on this well-deserved honor and thank her for her continued leadership and service to
the City of Kalamazoo.

Kalamazoo Mall Seasonal Refresh
The Kalamazoo Mall is currently undergoing a seasonal refresh as crews work to prepare the downtown corridor
for the busy spring and summer season. Planters are being cleaned and refreshed, streets and sidewalks are
being thoroughly cleaned, and public spaces are being power washed to improve the overall appearance
and pedestrian experience throughout the Mall.

This effort provides a much cleaner, more welcoming environment for residents, visitors, downtown businesses,
and event attendees while helping preserve the condition and appearance of one of Kalamazoo’s most
recognizable public spaces. Regular maintenance and beautification efforts like these play an important role
in supporting downtown vitality, encouraging foot traffic, and enhancing community pride.

The Kalamazoo Promise 20th Anniversary
The City of Kalamazoo is proud to support The Kalamazoo Promise in recognizing the 20-year anniversary of The
Promise and its transformational impact on students, families, and the broader community. As part of this effort,
the City of Kalamazoo is assisting with the refresh and enhancement of city limit signage to help commemorate
this important milestone and celebrate Kalamazoo’s longstanding commitment to education and opportunity.

Street banners and some downtown crosswalk painting may be included in the City’s commemoration as well.
The refreshing will serve as both a visual acknowledgment of The Promise’s legacy and a welcoming feature for
residents and visitors entering the community. The Kalamazoo Promise continues to play a significant role in

Connecting With Our Community
This past week, I had the pleasure of officially starting my role as a Board Member for Communities in Schools of
Kalamazoo, an organization dedicated to surrounding students with a community of support so they can stay
in school and achieve success in life. I also recently toured the program and found the level of coordination,
and commitment from staff and partners to be impressive. Seeing firsthand how students are supported
through mentoring, behavioral health resources, attendance interventions, and family engagement reinforced
the important role this organization plays in strengthening outcomes for young people across our community.

I look forward to collaborating with fellow board members, community partners, and school leaders to
continue advancing opportunities for Kalamazoo students and families.
