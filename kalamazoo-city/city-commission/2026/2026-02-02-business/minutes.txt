[2026-05-03: Public Hearing re: the Establishment of The B Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) at Vine and Burdick Streets]
At 7:59 p.m. Mayor Anderson opened a public hearing to receive comments on the establishment of a Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) at the southwest corner of Vine Street & S Burdick Street.

Assistant City Planner Bobby Durkee delivered a presentation entitled The B on Burdick - NEZ: New NEZ District, February 2, 2026. A copy of the slides from this presentation was filed with the papers for this meeting.

Commissioner Questions:
    Following the presentation, Assistant City Planner Durkee responded to Commissioner questions regarding: the connection between the development of this NEZ and the housing corridor study presented during the Committee of the Whole meeting; how this development would further the City's housing goals, including demographic goals and the desire to address "the missing middle"; whether there was a benefit to an NEZ being on a single parcel rather than multiple parcels; the potential duration of the tax exemption vis-a-vis conflicting information in the agenda report; and whether the developer had contracts in place with a specific daycare provider and grocer.

Public Comments:
    Jeff Messer, City resident, regarding the land value tax system, which did not punish development like the current property tax system.
    Chris Glasser, City resident, regarding the performative process for approving NEZ's.
    Edward Nickerson, City resident, regarding the dangers of using contractors from outside the city.

At 8:26 p.m. Mayor Anderson closed the public hearing.

[Consent Agenda]
Consent Agenda items were presented as follows with a recommendation to approve the items and authorize the City Manager to sign all related documents on behalf of the city:
    2026-05-04: approval of a contract with Balkema Excavating, Inc. for the Kalamazoo Airport Water Main Looping Project in the amount of $120,772.
    2026-05-05: approval of a cooperative purchase with Sportsfield Specialties for the Homer Stryker Field safety netting system in the amount of $170,154.75.
    2026-05-06: approval of a sole source purchase with Interkal for new seating at Homer Stryker Field in the amount of $182,902.
    2026-05-07: approval of a contract with Playcore Wisconsin Inc, DBA Gametime for the purchase of materials and labor to complete the Verburg Park Improvement Project in the amount of $195,880.20.
    2026-05-08: approval of an amendment to the lease agreement with the Northwoods League/Kalamazoo Growlers for two additional 5-year terms beginning January 1, 2029 through December 31, 2038.

Commissioner Praedel, seconded by Commissioner Slaby, moved to approve the consent agenda requests and authorize the City Manager to sign all related documents on behalf of the City.

With a roll call vote this motion passed 7-0.
AYES: Commissioners Hess, Hoffman, Praedel, Slaby, Wilson, Vice Mayor Duncan, Mayor Anderson
NAYS: None

[City Manager's Report]
Public Safety Chief David Boysen reported on the use of Flock cameras to read license plates and the policies and practices around how the images were stored, accessed, and shared. Chief Boysen spoke about the benefits of the license plate readers and how they had helped KDPS solve various crimes.

[Commissioner Comments]
Commissioner Praedel offered congratulations to Commissioner Hoffman and Neighborhood Activator Cee Maul for their recognition by Kalamazoo Valley Community College. Commissioner Praedel thanked staff and community members for serving the unhoused during the bitterly cold weather, and he expressed appreciation to staff from the Community Planning and Economic Development Department for their Imagine Kalamazoo 2035 public engagement sessions.

Commissioner Hess thanked City staff who were working outside, and she expressed gratitude to Pastor Millard Southern for organizing the recent concert and community conversation with noted trumpet player Winton Marsalis.

Mayor Anderson offered remarks on the passing of community activist Jean Baraka-Love and the funeral for a young person who lost their life to gun violence. Mayor Anderson expressed gratitude to people and organizations who helped the unhoused during the past two weeks of bitterly cold weather.
