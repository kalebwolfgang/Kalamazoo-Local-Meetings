               Stryker Field safety netting system (Bid Ref#00000-026.12)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a cooperative purchase with Sportsfield
Specialties for the Homer Stryker Field safety netting system in the amount of $170,154.75.

BACKGROUND:

The estimated costs are detailed below. A budget amendment is also being requested for this
purchase. The funds are available in the fund balance and would be sufficient to cover this
purchase. Upon receiving the updated quote, the price exceeded the requested budget. Original
pricing was sought in the spring of 2025, thus leaving us subject to a price increase that was not
fully accounted for in the budget request. Additionally, the scope underwent a minor expansion
to increase fan safety.

Nationwide, many professional and collegiate baseball organizations have installed safety netting
as a standard and required safety practice. The installation will ensure the City of Kalamazoo is
in alignment with industry standards and providing a safer environment for patrons who attend
baseball games at Homer Styker Field. The installation of the safety netting system will protect
spectators from potential injuries from baseballs leaving the field of play. Currently, the safety
netting only covers the main grand stands behind home plate up to the dugouts. With this
upgraded safety netting system we will be extending our netting down each baseline and into the
outfield.
