RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a sole source purchase with Interkal for
new seating at Homer Stryker Field in the amount of $182,902.

BACKGROUND:

Homer Stryker Field was constructed in 2001 by the City of Kalamazoo as part of the Mayors
Riverfront Park project, and the original seats for the stadium were installed by Interkal
(Kalamazoo Company), which manufactures stadium seating across the country. Mayors
Riverfront Park and Homer Stryker Field are city assets overseen by the Parks and Recreation
Department. The department is responsible for the park and all its amenities. The seats have
lasted for 25 years and are no longer compliant with the International Code Councils standard for
bleachers and stadium seating as of 2023. This purchase would update all the existing stadium
seating at Homer Stryker Field to be compliant with ICC standards for safety and accessibility.
 The current seating also does not have readily available parts to allow for repairs, which will be
resolved with new seating. The total number of seats being replaced is 1,028. Homer Stryker
Fields total seating capacity is 4,500.
