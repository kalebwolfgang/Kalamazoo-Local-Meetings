               Watermain Looping Project (Bid Ref# 91244-049.0)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Balkema Excavating, Inc.
for the Kalamazoo Airport Watermain Looping Project, in the amount of $120,772.00.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo identified the Kalamazoo Airport Watermain Looping Project as a
priority for the 2026 construction season. This project includes approximately 280-feet of 8-inch
ductile iron water main that will provide a loop in the High Pressure District. This will provide
additional system redundancy and increased reliability.

Bids were opened on December 2, 2025 and 7 bids were received. Balkema Excavating, Inc.
was the low responsive bidder with a bid of $ 120,772.00. Balkema Excavating, Inc. has
performed well in the past and Public Services recommends the approval of this contract and
purchase order.
