THIRD AMENDMENT TO

BASEBALL STADIUM USE, CONCESSION SERVICES, AND BUILDING
LEASE AGREEMENT

In 2013, the CITY OF KALAMAZOO (“City”), a Michigan municipal corporation whose address is
241 W. South Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan, 49007 and Kalamazoo Baseball, L.L.C. (“Team”), a
Michigan limited liability company whose address is 251 Mills St, Kalamazoo, MI 49048 and who
shall be a member of the Northwoods League™, and Northwoods League, Inc, a Florida
corporation (“League”) whose address is 2900 4th St. SW, Rochester, Minnesota, 55902
previously entered into an original five year agreement (2014-2018) and two additional five year
terms (2019-2023 & 2024-2028) for the Team to use, and to operate as a concessionaire for, the
Homer Stryker Baseball Stadium and, and also to lease space in a particular City-owned building
(collectively, the “Field”).

The parties to that agreement desire to extend their relationship and to extend the terms of the
Agreement and make other changes for the benefit of the City, Team, and League. Therefore, the
parties enter into this Third Amendment to amend certain sections of the Baseball Stadium Use,
Concession Services, and Building Lease Agreement (“Agreement”) as follows:

1. Part 1: Baseball Stadium Use Agreement, Section 3. Compensation is amended to read as
follows:

The Team shall pay to the City $ 35,000, as annual compensation for the use of the Field
to be paid in one payment every year on May 15 for each year of this Agreement.

If the use of the Field exceeds 40 game dates, an additional $750 per game date will be
required. For purposes of this Agreement, a “game date” means any contest in which at
least one pitch is made to a batter, even though not enough innings are played to
constitute a regulation or official game; a game date may include up to two contests, i.e.,
a “double header”.

If the annual compensation fails to cover the expenses incurred by the City for the
preparation of the Field, any deficit shall be invoiced by the City to the Team by
September 30 of each year and shall be made up by the Team through a single payment
to the City by October 31 of each year. This payment shall not exceed 5% of the annual
compensation.

Notwithstanding the above schedule of payments, the annual compensation for the use
of the Field will be reviewed at the end of each season. The City reserves the right to

2. Part 1: Baseball Stadium Use Agreement, Section 5. Stadium & Field Use is amended to read
as follows:

Repairs and Maintenance — The City shall be responsible for maintenance and repair of

the stadium bleachers, structures and fences, and parking areas. The City agrees to
maintain lighting to Northwoods League standards. The Team shall be responsible for turf
care, mowing the field, watering, trimming, seeding, fertilizing, aeration, initial lining of
the field prior to a game, and replacing and otherwise maintaining the grass. The Team
will be allowed to use the City’s equipment, if available, to maintain the field. The City will
supply up to $12,000 per year in field materials (brick dust, clay, paint, and chalk). Any
additional cost for materials will be the Team’s responsibility. The annual amount
supplied by the City for field materials will be reviewed at the end of each season. The

City reserves the right to increase the amount supplied, but not by more than the annual
amount of a mutually agreed upon CPI, in any given year during the term of the
Agreement, as amended from time to time. The Team shall be responsible for repairs, or
shall reimburse the City for making repairs, when the repairs are made necessary because
of damage done by the Team’s employees, players, visitors, or invitees.

Field Maintenance — The Team shall be responsible for the cost of the following: All field
maintenance, during and immediately after the game, and setting up equipment needed
(batting practice equipment, tarp, etc.), professional daily custodial cleaning for the
interior of the stadium, including daily cleaning of the office area, and, after each use of
other areas, for the field, bleachers, press box, locker rooms and umpire rooms. The scope

of this work specifically incudes the sweeping of the stadium seating and concourse areas,

3. Part 1: Baseball Stadium Use Agreement, Section 6. Special Events is amended to read as
follows:

In addition to baseball games, the Team will have the ability to rent the field for special
events. All special events shall comply with City requirements and City policies. The City
shall be notified of all special event requests. The Team’s final decision shall be approved
in writing by the City of Kalamazoo, Department of Parks and Recreation. The City shall
have the ability to use the stadium for City hosted events with proper notice.

The Initial Term of this Agreement was for five (5) years beginning January 1, 2014
through December 31, 2018. The term was extended in the First Amendment for two
Additional Terms of five (5) years each, the first Additional Term being January 1, 2019
through December 31, 2023 and the second Additional Term being January 1, 2024
through December 31, 2028. The term of this Agreement shall be extended for two
Additional Terms of five (5) years each, the third Additional Term being January 1, 2029,
through December 31, 2033 and the fourth Additional Term being January 1, 2034
through December 31, 2038. The Parties shall, however, have the right to terminate this
Agreement at any time prior to December 1 of the 4th year of each term for any terms
that remain. Such a termination shall be submitted to the other party in writing. The Team
shall be entitled to terminate less than all of the Agreement (the concessions agreement
and/or the building lease) but only with the express written consent of the City.

The City may consider a request, in its sole discretion and in the years to come, to extend
the Agreement for additional years. The City shall notify the Team and League in writing
within 90 days of its receipt of the request of its decision whether to extend the term of
this Agreement, and such extension shall be finalized by March 1, 2037.

6. The parties agree that all other provisions of the Agreement, First Amendment, and Further
Revised Second Amendment remain unchanged and in effect for the period of the Initial Term
and any Additional Terms.
