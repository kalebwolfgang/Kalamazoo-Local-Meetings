RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an amendment to the Northwoods
League/Kalamazoo Growlers lease of two additional 5 year terms beginning January 1, 2029
through December 31, 2038.

BACKGROUND:

The initial term of the agreement with the Northwoods League/Kalamazoo Growlers was for five
(5) years beginning January 1, 2014 through December 31, 2018. The term was extended in the
First Amendment for two additional terms of five (5) years each, the first additional term being
January 1, 2019 through December 31, 2023 and the second additional term being January 1,
2024 through December 31, 2028. The term of this agreement shall be extended for two
additional terms of five (5) years each, the third term being January 1, 2029 through December
31, 2033 and the fourth additional term being January 1, 2024 through December 31, 2038. In
this updated agreement the Kalamazoo Growlers will take over maintenance of Homer Stryker
Field for all games, have the ability to rent Homer Stryker field for special events, and pursue
Stadium Naming Rights of the venue. The naming rights will be subject to the City's approval.
 This agreement extension is a continuation of the long standing partnership between the
Kalamazoo Growlers and the City of Kalamazoo.
