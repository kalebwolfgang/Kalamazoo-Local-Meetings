RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Playcore Wisconsin Inc,
DBA Gametime for the purchase of materials and labor to complete the Verburg Park
Improvement Project in the amount of $195,880.20.

BACKGROUND:

Verburg Park was scheduled for improvements in 2025. Due to bids coming in over budget, staff
and our consultant had to reconsider the project and how we would approach bringing the park
the improvements it requires. By making minor changes to some of the materials, we found that
working with Playcore Wisconsin would afford us the opportunity to complete this project
within budget while not reducing the scope. The project will include site excavation and
restoration, concrete work, playground materials and installation, pavilion, and site furniture.
This contract will include prepping the surface for a bicycle pump track, which will be
completed by a separate vendor and contract.
