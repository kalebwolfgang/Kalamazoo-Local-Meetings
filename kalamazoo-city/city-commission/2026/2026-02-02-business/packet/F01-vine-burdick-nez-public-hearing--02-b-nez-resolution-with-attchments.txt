                       CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                               RESOLUTION NO.

                   A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE
                ESTABLISHMENT OF PROPERTY AT THE
              SOUTHWEST CORNER OF S BURDICK STREET
                    & VINE STREETAS THE B-NEZ

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

        WHEREAS The Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) Program was
established by Public Act 147 of 1992, as amended (the “Act”) to allow local
governments to provide a tax incentive for the development and rehabilitation of
residential housing in underdeveloped or distressed areas where neighborhood
revitalization, and the development and rehabilitation of residential housing might not
otherwise occur.

        WHEREAS the City of Kalamazoo is a qualified local government authorized to
establish one or more NEZ's.

        WHEREAS the City Commission has established a policy for NEZ’s through
Resolution 23-47 and terms of establishment of a district and qualifying terms for
certificates in an NEZ zone.

        WHEREAS the City Commission finds that the neighborhood in the City of
Kalamazoo at the southwest corner of S Burdick Street and Vine Street; as shown on
the map (Attachment A) and legal description (Attachment B) attached to this Resolution,
(To be known as the B NEZ) is an underdeveloped area consisting of undeveloped
property, property with surface parking lots, vacant structures, and property in need of
rehabilitation.

       WHEREAS the City Commission finds that the neighborhood revitalization,
and development and rehabilitation of residential housing in the B NEZ block needed to
spur economic growth may not otherwise occur without the incentive for such that
would be provided by the establishment of a NEZ.

       WHEREAS the City Commission finds that the B-NEZ is compact and contiguous,
contains enough platted parcels and is less than the maximum percentage of acreage
required by the Act, otherwise meets the requirements of the Act for designation as an NEZ.

        WHEREAS the City Commission finds that the zoning designation of the area
within the BNEZ, zoned Downtown 3 (D3) per the City of 50-1.2 Zoning Map & 1.8 C of
the Appendix A Zoning Ordinance, is considered a Qualified Downtown Revitalization
District per the Act. The Live-Work Zone per City of Kalamazoo Code 50-3.1 (f)
demonstrate that that this zone district is within the boundaries of an area zoned and
primarily used for business as defined by the City of Kalamazoo.

        WHEREAS the City Commission finds that there is a need in the City of Kalamazoo
for affordable housing and additional housing units which designation of the B-NEZ would
help address.

        WHEREAS the City Commission finds that its designation of the B-NEZ is consistent
with the Master Plan, the commercial goals and actions of the Vine Neighborhood plan, and
the neighborhood preservation and economic development goals of the City of Kalamazoo.
       WHEREAS City staff has recommended that the designation of the B-NEZ as an
NEZ District remains in effect for a period of five years from the date of such designation.
Any Certificates approved within that five year period shall be valid for the number of years
approved by the City Commission and in compliance with the terms approved by the City
Commission Nez Policy 23-47.

        NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT the B-NEZ is designated as a
Neighborhood Enterprise Zone pursuant to Act 147 of 1992, as amended for a period of five
years from the date of the adoption of this Resolution.

        The above resolution was offered by and supported by

 AYES, Commissioners:

 NAYS, Commissioners:

 ABSTAIN, Commissioners:
