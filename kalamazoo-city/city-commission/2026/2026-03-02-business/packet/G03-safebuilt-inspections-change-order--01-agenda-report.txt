SUBJECT:       Approval of a contract supplemental/change order with Safebuilt for Mechanical,

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Safebuilt for Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, and Building Inspections in the amount of
$110,000.

BACKGROUND:

Currently, the City of Kalamazoo contracts with Safebuilt to provide Mechanical, Electrical, and
Plumbing inspections as well as building plan reviews. Over the past several years, inspection
volumes have increased significantly and are projected to continue rising as development activity
remains strong.

In 2025, the City experienced a 3 to 4-week backlog in inspections. To address this and maintain
timely service for residents, contractors, and developers, the 2026 budget was increased to
support continued use of Safebuilt. This ensures continuity of service and prevents project delays
that can impact economic activity and housing availability.
