RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an Omnia cooperative contract with
Design2Wellness (D2W), P.O. Box 398, Lewis Center, OH 43035, in the amount of $140,503.61
for the purchase and installation of fitness equipment as part of the 150 East Crosstown Facility
Renovation Project and authorize the City Manager to sign all related documents.

BACKGROUND:

The 150 East Crosstown Facility Renovation Project includes the development of a new,
expanded fitness room intended to support employee wellness across all City departments. This
space will replace the small, outdated gym currently located within the Public Safety portion of
the building and will significantly improve accessibility, functionality, and capacity for
employee use.

D2W provides commercial-grade fitness equipment, installation, and configuration services that
meet the City’s specifications for durability, safety, and long-term operational reliability under
the Omnia Cooperative Purchasing Agreement-Contract #157521. Equipment included in the
scope of this project includes strength machines, cardio units, functional training systems,
flooring components, and related installation labor. The vendor was selected based on pricing,
equipment quality, installation capabilities, and ability to meet project timelines.

Once completed, the new gym will be available for use by all City employees, supporting
organizational wellness initiatives, reducing barriers to physical fitness, and enhancing the
overall work environment.
