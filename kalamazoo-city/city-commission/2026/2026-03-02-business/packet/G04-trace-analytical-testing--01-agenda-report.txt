RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental and PO for
Analytical testing services with Trace Analytical Laboratories, Incorporated in the amount of
$115,480.32.

BACKGROUND:

The Public Services Environmental Services Laboratory is often required to provide analyses
that are beyond its present capacity in terms of available instrumentation or skilled labor. In this
situation, it is necessary for the Public Services Laboratory to contract with a commercial
laboratory to perform the needed analytical testing. The services to be provided would include
the analysis of industrial wastewater compatibles for the Industrial Pretreatment monitoring
program.

City of Kalamazoo Environmental Services is currently contracted with Trace Laboratories
Incorporated (Bid Ref. #96148-002.0) and need additional analytical and sampling services for
2026 to meet required IPP compatible monitoring under the new City of Kalamazoo Sewer Use
Ordinance Resolution No. 23-72.
