Temporary Property Right of Entry Agreement

Milbocker & Sons, Inc. has a contract with the Michigan Department of Transportation (MDOT) to build
a sound wall for US-131. The project location is adjacent to City of Kalamazoo owned properties:

4401 Siesta Street, Kalamazoo, 49009, Parcel ID: 06-31-380-001
5001 South 12" Street, Portage, 49024, Parcel ID: 10-00006-040-0

Milbocker & Sons, Inc. kindly requests authorization from the City of Kalamazoo Public Services to utilize
the aforementioned properties for a staging area for construction material and equipment. Also
included is the right to trim trees, remove shrubs and vegetation, earthwork and grading, as necessary
to provide for clear access.

Milbocker & Sons, Inc. acknowledges and agrees that:

1. The City of Kalamazoo will not be held liable for any damage or theft of any equipment or
material that is stored on the property.

2. There will be no storage of diesel or gasoline tanks on site and that equipment will be kept in
leak-free condition.

3. Spill containment kits will be kept on site for use in the event of a spill or leak.

4. The City of Kalamazoo reserved its rights to bring federal and state cost recovery actions against
the present owners, and any other potentially responsible parties, arising out of release of
hazardous substances on the property.

5. Access to the property will be maintained for Department of Public Services operations.

6. Any damage to the property will be restored to the City’s satisfaction and to equal or better
than its current condition.

7. Said Agreement shall expire upon completion of the project or October 2, 2026, whichever
comes first.
