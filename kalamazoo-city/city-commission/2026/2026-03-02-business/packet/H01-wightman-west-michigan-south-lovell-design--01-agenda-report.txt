SUBJECT:       Approval of a contract with Wightman for the design of West Michigan, South,
               and Lovell Street (Bid Ref#92549-004.0)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Wightman for the design of
West Michigan, South, and Lovell Street in the amount of $3,000,000 funded through a RAISE
2022 Planning Grant.

BACKGROUND:

This design work is the first phase of the project to convert West Michigan, South, and Lovell
streets (including the "spaghetti bowl" intersection) from one way to two way operation and
support multi-modal transportation through this western end of the Downtown Kalamazoo
Transportation Network. The goals of the project include improved safety, connectivity, and
ease in use for all modes of tranport. The project is funded through the RAISE Planning grant
received by the City of Kalamazoo in 2023. These changes meet the community’s goals of
streets that are safe and comfortable for all users. This work will have a profound impact on
economic vitality in and around Downtown and reconnect the Downtown with adjacent
neighborhoods.

Eight proposals were received by the deadline given in the City’s publicly issued and advertised
Request for Proposal process; one of these proposals was deemed non-responsive. A
comprehensive project team was assembled to review and compare the seven remaining
proposals using an MDOT-approved qualifications-based selection method. Wightman's
proposal had the highest cumulative score and was selected based on its conveyed understanding
of the project scope, the firm's experience, and its project delivery abilities.
