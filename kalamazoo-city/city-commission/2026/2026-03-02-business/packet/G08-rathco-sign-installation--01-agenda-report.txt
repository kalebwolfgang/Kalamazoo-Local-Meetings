SUBJECT:       Approval of a cooperative contract with Rathco Supply, Inc for Sign Installation
               (Bid Ref# 96880-007.0)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that City Commission approve a cooperative contract with Rathco Safety
Supply, Inc. for sign installation & maintenance services in the amount of $268,282.50.

BACKGROUND:

In April 2022, the City of Kalamazoo and Road Commission of Kalamazoo County (RCKC)
partnered together to form a joint contract for sign installation and maintenance services. Rathco
was the low bidder for the effort. RCKC and the City, by joining in the same contract, save on
unit pricing and overall costs for the effort. These services are essential for the public’s safety,
awareness, and understanding of the roadway, right-of-way, directions, lane designation, and
other essential items. This change order is needed to perform anticipated sign work and provide
funding through the remainder of the contract.
