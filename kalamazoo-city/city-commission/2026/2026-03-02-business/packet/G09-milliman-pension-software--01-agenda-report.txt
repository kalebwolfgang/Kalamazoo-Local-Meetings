RECOMMENDATION:

Approval of contract with Milliman for a Pension Administration Software System in an amount
not to exceed $312,000.

BACKGROUND:

The City administers multiple defined benefit pension plans, which require highly specialized
expertise, reliable technology, and strict regulatory compliance. Over time, the City identified
operational challenges associated with aging systems, increasing regulatory complexity, and
growing demands for transparency, reporting, and member services.

To address these challenges, staff initiated a formal review of pension administration software
platforms and service providers capable of supporting:

      • Accurate benefit calculations

      • Regulatory compliance and reporting

      • Secure data management

      • Workflow automation

      • Enhanced participant services

      • Long-term scalability and system support

After a thorough review of written proposals, demonstrations, and background checks; staff
selected Milliman as the preferred vendor. Milliman has extensive experience administering
municipal pension plans nationwide, including plans in Michigan of similar size and complexity.
Their deep understanding of governmental pension regulations, reporting standards, and actuarial
requirements reduces implementation risk and ensures compliance.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Appropriate Depth of Engagement
Inform (one-way conversation) – no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

FISCAL IMPACT:

Funds for this project will be charged to the Pension Trust Fund (Fund 731). Year one will
include $78,000 for implementation and SAAS fees of $6,500 per month over three years.
