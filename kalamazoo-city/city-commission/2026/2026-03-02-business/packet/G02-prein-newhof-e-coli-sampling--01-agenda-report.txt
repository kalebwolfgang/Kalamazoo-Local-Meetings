RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission approve a professional service agreement with Prein &
Newhof for E. Coli Sampling and Site Assessment-MS4 TMDL Monitoring Support in the
amount of $102,500.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo is implementing its MS4 Total Maximum Daily Load (TMDL)
Monitoring Plan for E. coli as required by its NPDES Permit issued by the Michigan Department
of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE). Prein & Newhof is a City-approved, Pre-
Qualified Engineering and Consulting firm selected through the City’s professional services
qualification process and is currently included on the City’s roster for stormwater and surface
water management services. Their experience with municipal stormwater systems, regulatory
compliance, and watershed-based monitoring supports the technical requirements of the City’s
MS4 permit obligations.

The objective of this project is to perform wet-weather testing at 66 locations meeting the criteria
for sampling ≥30-inch diameter outfalls during qualifying storm events. As outlined in the City’s
TMDL Implementation Plan. Monitoring locations include: Arcadia Creek, Davis Creek and
Axtell Creek. Sampling will occur during Michigan’s recreational season (May–October).
Laboratory analysis will be conducted within the required 8–24 hour holding time. It is
anticipated that three independent personnel will collect samples during qualifying events,
requiring approximately seven storm events to complete all 66 sample locations.
