SUBJECT:       Resolution Designating the Polling Facility for Precinct 10

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a resolution designating the polling facility to
be used for Precinct 10 during the election on May 5, 2026 and for each election thereafter.

BACKGROUND:

Precinct 10 covers the Spring Valley/Burke Acres area in the northeast corner of the City. Since
2021, voters in Precinct 10 have been casting their ballots at Temple Baptist Church on Mt.
Olivet Road, but in the fall of 2025 the church indicated they no longer wanted to be a polling
location. Staff have been searching for a suitable facility that meets the statutory and operational
requirements to be a polling location. Staff is recommending that Tabernacle Church of God in
Christ, 3210 Virginia Avenue, be designated as the new polling location for Precinct 10. The
facility meets the requirements, and the church is excited to serve the community in this way.

Discussion: Michigan Election Law requires polling facilities to be accessible under the
Americans with Disabilities Act, regardless of whether a facility is "grandfathered" and not
required to comply for the day-to-day use of the property. In addition, the administrative and
practical requirements for polling facilities (adequate parking and interior space, enough working
electrical outlets, etc.) result in few options for many precincts — and this is before any
consideration of property owners' willingness to host a voting precinct. In short, the legal and
administrative requirements for polling facilities result in very few options.

Engagement/Communication Tools
This change will be communicated to affected voters (approximately 2,660) in several ways.
First, information in the Qualified Voter File will be updated. This information will be available
to City staff, including 311 agents, and to voters directly through the Michigan Voter
Information Center. Second, the Election Division will send each affected voter a new Voter
Identification Card that highlights the polling location change. Third, for the elections in 2026
and 2027, a sign will be posted at the former polling facility, directing voters to the new polling
location. Fourth, the city’s website will be updated with the current information. Finally, the City
Clerk's Office will provide updated polling location information to partners, like the County
Clerk's Office.

FISCAL IMPACT:

Noticing Costs
Approximately 2,660 voters will be sent new Voter ID Cards at a cost of approximately $1,000.
The funds for this expense are included in the Election Division’s FY2026 Budget.

Polling Facility Rental Costs
In 2026, the City will pay $100 per election to rent this polling facility. The property rental fees
are included in the annual operating budget for the Elections Division.
