RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Nexgen Construction for
erosion repairs at Water Station 24, in the amount of $335,943.90.

BACKGROUND:

Water Station 24 is located at the Al Sabo land preserve in Texas Township. This station is a
base load treatment plant. Public Services identified four areas within the wellfield that require
repairs due to erosion. Two areas will be stabilized with Flexamat, a vegetated concrete block
mat that is specifically designed to stabilize slopes. Two wellheads will have retaining walls
built around them to prevent any additional erosion from burying the them. Other areas will
have minor grading, drainage upgrades, and seeding.

The most critical work will be on the land bridge that provides access to the portion of the
wellfield south of the West Fork of the Portage Creek. It has a culvert that goes through it as
well as wellfield water main and a high-pressure gas main. It was constructed in the 1960’s and
appears to be primarily built out of rubble. The slope adjacent to the culvert collapsed and
plugged the culvert. An emergency repair was completed to remove the material and restore
flow to the culvert and temporarily stabilize the slope. The permanent repair requires an EGLE
permit due to the proximity to the surface water. Flexamat will be placed on the slope and
railings placed at the top of the slope to deter foot traffic from traversing the slope. Public
Services takes water level measurements and samples at the culvert, so a boardwalk and
sampling platform will be installed for this work to eliminate personnel from being on the slope.
