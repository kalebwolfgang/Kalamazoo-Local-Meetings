                           CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                    RESOLUTION NO. __

                A RESOLUTION SETTING THE SALARY OF THE CITY
                          COMMISSION APPOINTEES

         Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City held on March 2, 2026,
at or after 7:00 o'clock p.m., local time, at 241 W. South Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

       The following preamble and resolution was offered by Commissioner ________________
and supported by Commissioner ___________________.

        WHEREAS, Section 168 of the Kalamazoo City Charter provides that the City
Commission shall fix by resolution the salary or rate of compensation of all officers and employees
of the City except their own; and

        WHEREAS, the City Commission has reviewed the salaries of the City Attorney, City
Clerk, and the Internal Auditor, and has recommended that the annual salaries of the City Attorney,
City Clerk, and the Internal Auditor be increased for the year 2026. The Commission recommends
that the City Attorney receive 4.5% in base wages; that the City Clerk receive 4.5% increase in
base wages; and that the Internal Auditor receive 4.0% increase in base wages, effective March 2,
2026. The base wage includes the 3% increase for the Cost-of-Living-Adjustment (COLA), 1.5%
merit for the City Attorney and City Clerk, and 1.0% merit for the Internal Auditor for the 2025
performance evaluation cycle.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that, pursuant to Section 168 of the Charter,
the City Commission hereby fixes and sets the following salaries for the City Commission
Appointees effective March 2, 2026:

       City Attorney Aaron Leal                                             $197,421.74
       City Clerk Scott Borling                                             $141,173.33
       Internal Auditor Ann Videtich                                        $ 92,879.08
