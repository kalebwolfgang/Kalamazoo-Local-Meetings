RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve and accept the Temporary Property Right
of Entry Agreement with Milbocker & Sons, Inc. for Parcel #06-31-380-001 & #10-00006-040-
0; and authorize the City Manager to sign all related and necessary documents.

BACKGROUND:

Milbocker & Sons, Inc. has a contract with the Michigan Department of Transportation to install
new sound wall on US-131 adjacent to City owned properties. Milbocker & Sons, Inc. requested
use of City properties to store materials and equipment as well as access the project site.
