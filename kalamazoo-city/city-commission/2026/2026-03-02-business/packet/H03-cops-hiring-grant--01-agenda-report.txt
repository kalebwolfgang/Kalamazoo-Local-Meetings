RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accepts the 2025 Community Oriented Policing
Services (COPS) Hiring Program (CHP) award in the amount of $1,250,000 from the U.S.
Department of Justice and authorize the City Manager to sign the grant award and special
conditions documents. It is recommended that the City Commission approve 10 new officer
positions and a budget amendment in the amount of $1,520,000.

BACKGROUND:

In July 2025, the Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety applied for the 2025 COPS-CHP grant
under the Reducing Gun Violence category and was awarded $1,250,000 to hire ten (10) new
sworn officers. Acceptance of this award aligns with ongoing efforts to reduce violent crime,
expand neighborhood-based policing, and support increasing service demands associated with
continued economic growth within the City of Kalamazoo.

KDPS’s 2025 crime data reflects meaningful progress but also highlights the continued need for
sustainable staffing levels to maintain momentum. Violent crime decreased 14% from 2024 and
22% over the past two years, while property crime declined 13% from last year and 27% over
two years. Assaults involving firearms decreased 22% from 2024, and KDPS seized 407 firearms
in 2025, demonstrating both a high level of enforcement activity and the ongoing presence of
illegal firearms within the community. These reductions were achieved through proactive patrol
work, community engagement, focused deterrence efforts, and consistent investigative follow-
up.

Despite these improvements, call complexity continues to rise. Officers are increasingly handling
behavioral-health incidents, weapons-related calls, and downtown disturbances linked to

Beginning in 2026, the new Kalamazoo Event Center/Arena will introduce substantial additional

disorder-related calls, and after-event dispersal operations. Comparable regional venues

dedicated, reliable staffing to support safe operations in the growing downtown entertainment
district.

Hiring ten officers through the COPS-CHP award will allow KDPS to:

     • Sustain and expand reductions in violent crime and gun violence.
     • Provide adequate staffing for downtown growth and the arena’s operational footprint.
     • Improve response-time reliability during peak periods.
     • Enhance community-oriented policing and neighborhood engagement.
     • Support investigative capacity and workload distribution.

Acceptance of the COPS-CHP award ensures sworn staffing levels remain aligned with
Kalamazoo’s growth, resident expectations, and the evolving operational demands of a modern
public-safety environment.
