                             CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                    RESOLUTION NO. 26-

   A RESOLUTION DESIGNATING THE POLLING FACILITY FOR PRECINCT 10 TO
   BE USED FOR THE ELECTION ON MAY 5, 2026 AND AT EACH ELECTION HELD
                 IN THE CITY OF KALAMAZOO THEREAFTER

      Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City of Kalamazoo held on
March 2, 2026, at 7:00 o'clock p.m., local time, at City Hall.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

       RESOLVED: the Kalamazoo City Commission hereby designates the following polling facility
to be used during the election to be held on May 5, 2026 and at each election held in the City of
Kalamazoo thereafter:

         Precinct 10, to vote at Tabernacle Church of God in Christ – 3210 Virginia Avenue

       IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED: the City Clerk is directed to provide notice to voters of any
precinct and polling location changes as required by law.

      The above resolution was offered by _____________________ and supported by
_______________________.

AYES, Commissioners:

NAYS, Commissioners:

ABSTAIN, Commissioners:

RESOLUTION DECLARED ADOPTED.
