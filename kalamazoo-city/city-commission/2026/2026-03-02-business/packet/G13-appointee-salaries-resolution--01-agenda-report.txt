SUBJECT:       2026 City Commission Appointee Compensation Resolution

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a RESOLUTION setting forth the salaries of
the City Commission appointees (City Attorney, City Clerk, and the Internal Auditor), effective
March 2, 2026.

BACKGROUND:

Section 168 of the City Charter provides that the City Commission shall set the salaries of its
appointees by resolution. The attached resolution satisfies this requirement.

The City Commission evaluates their direct reports (appointees) annually and assigns an overall
rating based on the Custom Insight/Focal360 evaluation system. Appointees who receive an
overall score of 5.0 will receive a 2% increase, 4.0 – 4.9 will receive a 1.5% increase and 3.0 –
3.9 will receive a 1% increase. This merit system aligns with the Non-Bargaining Unit
Employees of the City of Kalamazoo. In addition, the Cost-of-Living-Adjustment (COLA) for
NBU employees is 3%.

The Fiscal Impact section below and the attached resolution therefore reflect increases of 4.5%
increase for the City Attorney, 4.5% for the City Clerk and 4.0% for the Internal Auditor.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:

Good Governance - Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
