RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a sole source professional service
agreement with Main & Meridian, LLC, former City of Kalamazoo Employee, Jeff Chamberlain
for Consulting Services not to exceed $49,000.

BACKGROUND:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a consulting contract with Main &
Meridian, LLC, which is owned by retiree Jeff Chamberlain, at a rate of $195.00 per hour with a
term not to exceed 1 year. This contract will cover consulting in areas requiring specific
knowledge of City operations. The City has attempted to utilize outside counsel for some of the
topics in the past without success due to the specific knowledge necessary to adequately opine on
the topics.

The topics to be covered by the agreement include:
Wastewater Contract Negotiations with Townships – Lead City team in monthly (or more
frequent) contract negotiations. Work with staff to prepare for negotiation sessions. Propose
contract language consistent with City goals.
Allied Landfill – Consultation and assistance for staff regarding the future state of the City. Be
available for consultations with EPA or other regulatory agencies.
Bronson Park – Consultation sessions with Match-E-Be-Nash-She-Wish Band of Pottawatomi
(Gun Lake Tribe). Assist staff with obtaining funding and placement of historical markers related
to the mound in Bronson Park.
