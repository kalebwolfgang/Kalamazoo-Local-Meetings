               Old Tertiary Renovation (Bid Ref # 92500-005.0.6).

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a professional service agreement with
Wightman and Associates for tertiary renovation of Building 20 in the amount of $157,670.

BACKGROUND:

Through regulatory engagements after the City of Kalamazoo commissioned the Kalamazoo
Water Reclamation Plant’s (KWRP) new tertiary treatment micro-filtration process, building
improvements to an existing building were identified. The improvements include: the complete
decommissioning of the existing tertiary sand filtration infrastructure to provide a regulatory
approved flow pathway better suited for long term operations of the KWRP; replacement of the
building’s heating system to maintain long term useability of the building; and re-route an
existing facility untreated storm water outfall Kalamazoo River by diverting the flow to the
headworks of the KWRP for complete treatment prior to discharge into the Kalamazoo River.
