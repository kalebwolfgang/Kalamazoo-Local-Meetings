A regular meeting of the Kalamazoo City Commission was held on Monday, March 2, 2026 at 7:01 p.m. in the City Commission Chambers at City Hall.

[Roll Call]
COMMISSIONERS PRESENT: Mayor David Anderson, Vice Mayor Drew Duncan, Jeanne Hess, Stephanie Hoffman, Chris Praedel, Jacqueline Slaby, Alonzo Wilson
COMMISSIONERS ABSENT: None
Also present were City Manager Malcolm Hankins, City Attorney Aaron Leal, and City Clerk Scott Borling.

[Invocation/Pledge]
The invocation, given by Sister Patricia Warbritton from the Congregation of St. Joseph, was followed by the Pledge of Allegiance.

[Adoption of the Agenda] [2026-09-01]
By unanimous consent the City Commission adopted its meeting agenda with the following change:
    City Manager Hankins requested that Item H-3, acceptance of a COPS Hiring Program Award, be removed from the agenda.

[Public Comments]
Next, when an opportunity was given for general public comments, the following people addressed the City Commission:
    Jim Hess, City resident, regarding Mayor Anderson and Assistant Public Safety Chief Victor Green speaking to his boys mentoring group.
    Edward Nickerson, City resident, regarding a recent call to 211 and people trying to steal his car.
    Joel Snyder, non-resident and a member of Celebris Ensemble, played "My Heart's in Kalamazoo," a song he wrote with Jane Kozhevnikova.
    Rachel Thompson, City resident, regarding a watermain break on Grand Prairie Road and poor detour signage that resulted in people driving through her yard to avoid the road closure.
    Elizabeth O'Dell, President of the Kalamazoo Alumni Chapter of Delta Theta Sigma Sorority, regarding a recent workshop on economic development for black run businesses, and the inequities of the national mental health crisis.
    [2026-09-02] Gloria Foster-Wimbley, Social Action Chair of the Kalamazoo Alumni Chapter of Delta Theta Sigma Sorority, regarding a letter sent to the Mayor asking for information on the City's policies and outreach in the area of health equity. (document filed)
    Rachel Viñales, non-resident, regarding her candidacy for Judge of 8th District Court.

[Consent Agenda (cont'd)]
Commissioner Hoffman, seconded by Commissioner Slaby, moved to approve the consent agenda requests and authorize the City Manager to sign all related documents on behalf of the City.

With a roll call vote this motion passed 7-0.
AYES: Commissioners Hess, Hoffman Praedel, Slaby, Wilson, Vice Mayor Duncan, Mayor Anderson
NAYS: None

[Regular Agenda]

[2026-09-20: Contract with Wightman Associates for the Design of West Michigan Avenue, South, and Lovell Streets]
Public Works Division Manager Dennis Randolph delivered a presentation entitled West Michigan, South & Lovell Engineering Design Project RAISE 2022 Grant Infrastructure Investment: City Commission Briefing, City of Kalamazoo Department of Public Services, March 2026. A copy of the slides from this presentation was filed with the papers for this meeting.

Commissioner Questions:
    Following the presentation, Public Works Manager Randolph responded to Commissioner questions regarding the significance of the color-coding on the "Grant Projects" map, how extra funds would be used if the cost of the design work was less than the grant amount, and the value of having the same engineers working on multiple downtown projects.

Public Comments: none

Commissioner Slaby, seconded by Commissioner Hess, moved to approve a contract with Wightman for the design of West Michigan Avenue, South, and Lovell Streets in the amount of $3,000,000, funded through a RAISE 2022 Planning Grant.

With a roll call vote this motion passed 7-0.
AYES: Commissioners Hess, Hoffman Praedel, Slaby, Wilson, Vice Mayor Duncan, Mayor Anderson
NAYS: None

[2026-09-21: Resolution 26-13 Establishing The B-NEZ at Burdick and Vine Streets]
City Manager Hankins introduced for consideration a resolution establishing the B-NEZ Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) District at the southwest corner of South Burdick and Vine Streets.

Commissioner Questions: none

Public Comments:
    Jeff Messer, City resident, regarding the impact to the project if the City Commission denied the tax abatement.

[2026-09-21: Resolution 26-13 Establishing The B-NEZ at Burdick and Vine Streets (cont'd)]
Commissioner Hess, seconded by Commissioner Wilson, moved to adopt a RESOLUTION establishing the B-NEZ NEZ District at the southwest corner of South Burdick Street & Vine Street.

With a roll call vote this motion passed 5-0 with two abstentions.
AYES: Commissioners Hess, Hoffman, Slaby, Wilson, Mayor Anderson
NAYS: None
ABSTAIN: Commissioner Praedel and Vice Mayor Duncan

[2026-09-22: City Manager's Report]
City Manager Hankins highlighted items in the written City Manager's Report and spoke about the City's community engagement efforts on the Howard Street project. City Manager Hankins announced the annual City Commission retreat would take place on Saturday, March 14th at the KRESA Career Connect Campus, 3500 Vanrick Drive, from 8:30 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

[Commissioner Comments]
Commissioner Slaby announced their absence from the March 16th meetings because of the National League of Cities conference.

Commissioner Hess offered comments on Trenches Church, the Portage firefighter and son who were in a serious accident at Douglas and North, and the river cleanup event on March 28th.

Commissioner Praedel offered remarks on the Blueprint for Peace presentation from that morning and the impact of educators and mentors on the community.

Vice Mayor Duncan raised concerns from downtown businesses about parking enforcement, offered remarks on the Blueprint for Peace presentation, and thanked Delta Sigma Theta Sorority for their work in the community.

Mayor Anderson spoke about the many people and organizations doing community-building work, and he highlighted the Police Athletic League as an example of the non-traditional work being done by the Public Safety Department.

[Adjournment]
The meeting adjourned at 8:58 p.m.

[signature] Scott A. Borling, City Clerk
