RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that City Commission approve a contract with Utility Metering Solutions,
LLC for the 2026 Water Meter Replacement Project in the amount of $3,038,791.00.

BACKGROUND:

In 2018, the City of Kalamazoo Public Services initiated a pilot program to test the capabilities
of installing Neptune Smart Meters across the water system. After reviewing the data collected,
Public Services determined that the water meters exceeded expectations and proceeded with a
phased replacement of all water service meters in the system. Beginning January 1st, 2019,
smart meters were installed for all new service installations, as well as locations where the
existing meter required replacement. Also, as lead water services are replaced, new smart meters
are installed. In 2020 all the meters in Parchment were replaced with the new meters.

The next phase of meter replacements occurred in 2022 and concentrated on meters west of US
131 and south of Stadium Drive so as not to interfere with the replacement of lead water services
that are occurring within the City and Kalamazoo Township. With the help of City crews on
non-responsive customers and services with valve issues, over 99% of Texas Township water
services now have Neptune Smart Meters.

This current phase continues with meter replacement on the west side of Kalamazoo in Oshtemo,
Kalamazoo, and Cooper Townships. Additionally, meter replacements are planned for some
large apartment complexes near the East G Avenue and Sprinkle Road intersection. This phase
includes areas with older infrastructure that may not have valves on both sides of the meter, non-
functional valves, and fittings that do not meet current standards. Since the City of Kalamazoo is
responsible for maintaining water services from the water main to the customer’s meter, this
project also includes the replacement of non-functioning and missing valves to ensure Public
