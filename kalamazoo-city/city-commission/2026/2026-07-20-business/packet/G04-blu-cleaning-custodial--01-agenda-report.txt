RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a 17-month contract extension with Blu
Cleaning for custodial services at various city properties in the amount of $379,650.88. It is also
recommended that the City Commission approve a budget amendment in the amount of $17,800.

BACKGROUND:

Blu Cleaning provides commercial cleaning, disinfecting and janitorial services. The City of
Kalamazoo has previously contracted Blu Cleaning for custodial services. These outside
contractual services have been used for building custodial services at all buildings except City
Hall. The 17-month Blu Cleaning contract renewal price of $379,650.88, which is $111,888.99
for the remainder of 2026 (8/1/26 – 12/31/26) and $267,761.89 for calendar year 2027.
