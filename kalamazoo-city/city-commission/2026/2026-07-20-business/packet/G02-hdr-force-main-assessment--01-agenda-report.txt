It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Professional Service Agreement with
HDR Michigan Inc for City of Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant Raw Sewage Force Main
Condition Assessment in the amount of $135,100.00.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant’s raw pump force main is approximately 1,443 linear
feet used to convey raw sewage from the collections and into the treatment process at the facility.
 The 54-inch diameter force main is an embedded cylinder prestressed concrete cylinder pipe
(PCCP) manufactured by Interpace Corporation in 1978. The force main has been in continuous
service since the completion of the project in the early 1980’s.

PCCP of this manufacturing vintage have failed in applications across the north America. The
recycle force main which, connects directly into the raw pump force main, experienced a minor
failure circa 2013, causing a short duration emergency shutdown of flow from the collections
systems into the facility while the failure point was isolated and repaired under emergency
efforts.

The condition assessment of this critical asset for the wastewater system as a whole is in
alignment with wastewater’s asset management plan. The assessment will provide estimated
future life expectancy and inspection frequency so to minimize the future risk of failure to the
best of our abilities.
