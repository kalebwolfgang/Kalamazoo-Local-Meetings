RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a one-year contract extension with Gabriel,
Roeder, Smith & Company for the purchase of actuarial services in the amount of $129,500.

BACKGROUND:

Gabriel, Roeder, Smith & Company (GRS) has historically provided actuarial services for the
City’s Pension and Other Post-Employment Benefits (OPEB) systems. Maintaining continuity
with a single firm is essential. It allows for year-over-year comparability in actuarial studies and
supports staff in developing long-term strategies for the health and sustainability of the City’s
retirement systems.

GRS will provide the following services:

      • Preparation of actuarial valuation studies for the City’s Pension and OPEB systems, using
        consistent historical methodologies.
      • Preparation of actuarial reports compliant with applicable accounting standards (e.g.,
        Governmental Accounting Standards Board requirements) for inclusion in the City’s
        audited financial statements.
      • Preparation of Public Act 202 reports to meet State of Michigan reporting requirements.
      • Additional actuarial consulting services as requested on a project or per-item basis.

Actuarial valuation reports provide critical information on the funded status of the City’s pension
and OPEB plans. These reports are used to:

      • Assess the long-term financial health of the plans.
      • Inform contribution requirements (actuarially determined contributions, if applicable).
