SUBJECT:        Mayor's Appointments to Boards and Commissions

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the Mayor's appointment to boards,
commissions, and committees:

- the appointment of Daniel Kastner to the Historic Preservation Commission for a term
  expiring on March 31, 2028.

BACKGROUND:

The Historic Preservation Commission (HPC) advises the City Commission on historic
preservation issues, including the establishment of historic districts and landmarks and the
nomination of such districts and landmarks to the National Register of Historic Places and the
state register. The HPC encourages and promotes historic preservation through publications,
education, the collection of historical materials, and the identification of historical resources that
warrant preservation. City ordinance establishes that the Mayor appoints and reappoints
members to the HPC. After reviewing the attached Nomination Report, I have appointed Daniel
Kastner to the HPC. I am now requesting City Commission approval of this appointment as
required by ordinance. The attached Nomination Report describes the nominee's qualifications.
