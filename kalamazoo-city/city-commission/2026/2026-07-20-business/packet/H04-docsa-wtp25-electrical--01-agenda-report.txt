RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with LD Docsa Associates, Inc.
for the Treatment Plant No. 25 Electrical Reliability Project, in the amount of $11,227,000.00.

BACKGROUND:

Public Services identified the Water Treatment Plant No. 25 Electrical Reliability Project as a
priority. The motor control center and electrical controls of Water Treatment Plant No. 25 are
original to the 1978 construction of the plant and are obsolete. The existing equipment is in the
same room as the pumps, motors, and piping. This creates a very wet and humid environment
that is not ideal for electrical equipment. With the replacement of the electrical equipment, this
project will also relocate the equipment to a dedicated building that will increase the reliability
and longevity of the new equipment. The wiring from the building to the wells is also being
replaced as the wiring to wells 25-1 and 25-5 has already failed due to having surpassed its life
expectancy.
In addition, the project will add a permanent natural gas standby generator and automatic transfer
switch with capacity to power the entire Treatment Plant. This will replace the failed diesel
generator. The new generator requires the installation of a natural gas line to the site. This work
is being coordinated with the local gas utility company and will require extensive site work.
Furthermore, booster pumps and variable frequency drives are being replaced; chlorination
equipment is being added to allow for flow pacing and remote monitoring of the chlorine
residual. This will enhance the treatment at this facility.

Bids were opened for this project on July 1, 2026. Two responsive bids were received with LD
Docsa Associates, Inc. being the low responsive bidder. LD Docsa Associates, Inc.has
performed well with other projects for the City of Kalamazoo, and Public Services recommends
approval of this contract.
