RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Monoform LLC for Manhole Rehabilitation in the amount of $301,405.00.

BACKGROUND:

On March 21st, 2022, the City Commission approved a contract with Monoform LLC for
manhole rehabilitation.

Engineering has identified additional cast in place manhole lining needs for 2026 based on the
risk assessment conducted while updating the wastewater collections asset management plan.
 The manholes included as part of this recommendation reside within minor sewer sheds 5 and 6
located within the City’s Northside Neighborhood.
