RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with L D Docsa for Kalamazoo
Water Reclamation Plant Secondary Clarifier Catwalk Rehabilitation in the amount of
$457,900.00.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant Secondary Clarifiers, 5 through 8, were constructed
roughly 45 years ago. Over the years, extensive preventative maintenance has been performed
on the structural elements to preserve the integrity of the catwalks as long as possible. In recent
years preventative maintenance activities have not been enough to continue to preserve the
structural integrity of the steel members to maintain adequate life safety standards.

In 2025 the City partnered with OHM advisors to perform a structural lift safety analysis which
included repair recommendations. The report indicated significant structural steel member
section loss due to corrosion. The structural engineer’s recommendation was to limit human
occupancy to only a single individual at a time, only when critically necessary, and only when a
2nd individual is present to assist in recovery if the catwalk failed while being occupied. The
report also recommended the installation of life safety throw rings near the entrance to each
catwalk, this effort was immediately completed by City staff.

Upon the findings of the report, the City once again partnered with OHM to provide the City bid
documents to complete the work outlined in Remediation Option 2 in the report. The bid
document package was completed and posted for bid in spring of 2026. Bid were opened on
Tuesday, June 9, 2026.
