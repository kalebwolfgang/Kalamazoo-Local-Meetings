RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a professional service agreement
supplemental/change order with Tetra Tech, Inc. for the Water Treatment Plant No. 25 Electrical
Reliability Project Construction Engineering in the amount of $753,400.00.

BACKGROUND:

Public Services identified the Water Treatment Plant No. 25 Electrical Reliability Project as a
priority. The motor control center and electrical controls of Water Treatment Plant No. 25 are
original to the 1978 construction of the plant and are obsolete. The existing equipment is in the
same room as the pumps, motors, and piping. This creates a very wet and humid environment
that is not ideal for the electrical equipment. With the replacement of the electrical equipment,
this project will also relocate the equipment to a dedicated building that will increase the
reliability and longevity of the new equipment. Tetra Tech, Inc. provided the design of this
project.

Public Services reached out to Tetra Tech, Inc. for a proposal for construction engineering for
this project. Tetra Tech, Inc. provided a quote of $753,400. Tetra Tech, Inc. has performed well
with other projects for the City of Kalamazoo, and Public Services recommends approval of this
supplemental.
