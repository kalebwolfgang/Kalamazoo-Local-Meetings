RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Professional Service Agreement with
Tetra Tech Inc, for design engineering services for Secondary Clarifier Rehabilitation at City of
Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant in the amount of $1,500,000.00.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant Secondary Clarifiers and appurtenances, 5 through 8,
have been in service since circa 1984 with a replacement of the drives circa 1994. The existing
non-mechanical, electrical, and piping infrastructure needs rehabilitation from being in-service
for over 42 years. In addition, the mechanical, electrical, piping, and appurtenances have
reached the end of their useful life after over 32 years of being in-service.

Latest treatment modeling results have indicated the secondary clarifiers as being a limiting
factor in the ability of the Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant to handle increased flow rates
while keeping loading rates constant. Recent computational fluid dynamics modeling of
proposed replacement infrastructure indicates the proposed infrastructure can minimize this
limitation.

In order for the Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant to successfully maintain NPDES permitted
effluent compliance during the rehabilitation of secondary clarifiers 5 – 8, secondary clarifiers 1,
2, and 4 (clarifier 3 was abandoned under a project in the 1990’s and is now the location where
the Tertiary Disc Filter treatment process) will be required to be operated. Clarifiers 1, 2, and 4
received a major overhaul in the mid-1990’s. Due to operational inefficiencies of the overhaul as
