The Kalamazoo City Commission met as a Committee of the Whole on Monday, June 15, 2026 at 5:02 p.m. in the City Commission Chambers at City Hall.

[Roll Call]
COMMISSIONERS PRESENT: Vice Mayor Drew Duncan, Jeanne Hess, Chris Praedel, Jacqueline Slaby, Alonzo Wilson
COMMISSIONERS ABSENT: Mayor David Anderson, Stephanie Hoffman
Also present were City Manager Malcolm Hankins, City Attorney Aaron Leal, and City Clerk Scott Borling.

[Commissioners Excused]
Commissioner Slaby, seconded by Commissioner Praedel, moved to excuse the absences of Commissioner Hoffman and Mayor Anderson. With a voice vote the motion passed.

Communications

[Communications]
City Manager Hankins announced non-essential City offices would be closed on Friday, June 19th in observance of the Juneteenth holiday.

Work Session

[2026-23-01: Presentation re: Incentives for the Zone 32 Phase II Development]
Development Manager Jamie McCarthy and Logan Mulholland from Fishbeck delivered a presentation entitled Development and Housing Incentive Briefing: Zone 32 Phase II, 810 N Pitcher & 825 Porter Streets. A copy of the slides from this presentation was filed with the papers for this meeting.

Commissioner Questions:
Following the presentation, Development Manager McCarthy responded to Commissioner questions regarding the impact of this project on the tax base, the land acquisition process, and community benefits associated with the project.

[2026-23-02: Presentation re: Two-Way Street Conversion Project Details and Timelines]
Community Planning and Economic Development Deputy Director Christina Anderson introduced and played a video entitled Kalamazoo Streets For All.

Deputy Director Anderson and Public Services Director James Baker delivered a presentation entitled Kalamazoo Avenue Start Dates & Timelines, Project & Construction Details, Alternate Routes & Traffic Calming, and Construction Communication Plans. A copy of the slides from this presentation was filed with the papers for the meeting.

Commissioner Questions:
Following the presentation, Deputy Director Anderson and Director Baker responded to Commissioner questions regarding: underground utilities; the impact of construction on bus routes; the need for communications to bus riders, rideshare drivers, downtown visitors, and Amtrak riders; the impact of the opening of the Kalamazoo Event Center on traffic patterns and alternate routes; the reduction of truck traffic through downtown and the Northside Neighborhood; the timeframe for opening downtown streets to two-way traffic; coordination with the railroads; channels for opt-in communications regarding the project; accessibility to underground utilities for repair and maintenance; and the possibility of having a live cam of the construction area.

Commissioner Comments

[Commissioner Comments]
Finally, an opportunity was given for Commissioner comments, but no comments were offered.
