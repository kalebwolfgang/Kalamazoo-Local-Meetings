Zone 32 Phase II
[Architectural rendering by TowerPinkster of a two-story apartment building with "901 N PITCHER" over the entrance.]
• Builds upon first phase completed in 2024
• Combines multiple parcels
• Northside Neighborhood
• Redevelopment of vacant brownfield site transformed to mixed-income residential.
● Two-story, new construction
● 38 Residential Units
  ○ 10 Studios
  ○ 18 One-bedrooms
  ○ 10 Two-bedrooms
● Rents between 40% and 90% of area median income (AMI)
  ○ 10 Units ≤ 60% AMI for 20 years
  ○ Specifically, 8 units at 60% AMI and 2 units at 40% AMI (13-year period, as part of brownfield incentive package)
● In-unit laundry, energy efficient appliances, high-quality finishes
● Community room, bicycle storage, outdoor recreation/ green space
