                               Addendum to Subrecipient Agreement
                  Conversion of ARPA Grant to ARPA Loan and Amendment of Scope

This Addendum is made to the Contract Agreement dated October 22, 2024 (the “Agreement”), by and
between the City of Kalamazoo, Michigan ("City") and Kalrecovery I Limited Dividend Housing Association
Limited Partnership ("Developer").

Purpose and Effect of Addendum

The purpose of this Addendum is to amend the Agreement by extinguishing the previously approved ARPA
grant award and replacing it with an ARPA loan, to clarify the scope of the loan funded activities, and to
amend certain administrative and repayment provisions to ensure compliance with federal requirements
and Low-Income Housing Tax Credit financing standards.

Effective upon this Amendment, all references to “Subrecipient” are replaced with “Developer,” and the
counterparty shall no longer be considered a subrecipient.

Effective upon execution, this Addendum supersedes and replaces any inconsistent grant related
provisions of the Agreement. Except as expressly amended herein, all remaining terms and conditions of
the Agreement remain in full force and effect.

Termination of Grant and Replacement with Loan

Effective upon execution of this Addendum, the award type under the Agreement is converted from a
grant to a loan. Any and all provisions of the Agreement referring to the ARPA award as a grant, grant
payment, non-repayable assistance, or similar terminology are terminated and shall have no further force
or effect.

The parties acknowledge and agree that the total ARPA award amount remains unchanged and continues
to be Seven Hundred Thousand Dollars ($700,000). The conversion from grant to loan does not increase
or decrease the total award amount. All funds previously disbursed or to be disbursed under the
Agreement shall be deemed loan proceeds subject to repayment in accordance with this Addendum and
the related loan documents.

Scope of Service and Loan Supported Activities

Upon issuance of a Notice to Proceed by the City, the Developer shall construct the Kalrecovery housing
project (“Project”), a new forty-eight (48) unit affordable housing development. Forty-six (46) units shall
be permanent supportive housing for persons recovering from opioid and other substance use disorders
and two (2) units shall be reserved for on-site staff.

The project will be developed at 1900 Belford Street, parcel number 06-27-209-001 , and more specifically
described in the Mortgage (Attachment A). This description includes what is termed the Restoration Area
(“Restoration Area”) and the Development Area (“Development Area”) in the Declaration of Restrictive
Covenants (“Restrictive Covenants”) recorded in the Kalamazoo County Register of Deeds at 2017-036146.
Consistent with the terms of the Restrictive Covenants, the Developer has agreed that no portion of the
Project shall be located in any portion of the Restoration Area, nor shall any of the proceeds of this loan

Residents of the Project shall be participants or recent graduates of local drug treatment courts, and not
less than one half of all residential units shall be targeted to individuals transitioning out of homelessness.

The Project shall serve households earning fifty percent (50 percent) or less of the area median income as
established annually by the Michigan State Housing Development Authority.

The ARPA loan shall support twelve (12) units within the Project for the duration of a thirty (30) year
affordability period.

The Developer shall administer all tasks related to the Project in compliance with all applicable federal,
state, and local rules and regulations governing these funds, including ARPA requirements, and in a
manner satisfactory to the City.

Schedule of Tasks and Program Outcomes

The Developer shall utilize reasonable efforts to complete the Project in accordance with the schedule
below. All dates are estimates and may be revised based on construction conditions.

May 2026
Submission of closing documentation and proof of primary financing. Construction begins.

December 2026
Construction is approximately fifty percent complete.

June 2027
Submission of all required documentation to the City prior to beginning lease activities.

June 2027
Commencement of lease up. Potential ARPA assisted unit tenants reviewed by the City for eligibility.

August 2027
Submission of certificate of occupancy and rental certification. Affordable Housing Restrictive Covenant
is recorded with Kalamazoo County.

December 2027
ARPA assisted units rented to eligible households.

By December 31, 2027, twelve (12) income eligible households shall hold executed leases within the
Project. This schedule may be accelerated without prior City approval. If program goals cannot be met
within the timeframe described, the Developer may request an extension of the Agreement. Extensions
are not guaranteed and will be evaluated based on performance and compliance.

Affordability Period

The twelve (12) permanent supportive housing units supported by the ARPA loan shall be subject to a
thirty (30) year affordability period secured through an Affordable Housing Restrictive Covenant
(Attachment C) recorded against the property.

Staffing

The Developer shall provide its own property management, staff, and office space necessary to carry out
the Project. The Developer shall designate a Project Manager as the primary point of contact for the City
and shall notify the City in writing prior to any change in Project Manager designation.

Budget and Use of Funds

The total amount of ARPA funds to be loaned by the City under this Agreement shall not exceed Seven
Hundred Thousand Dollars ($700,000) in ARPA funds. The conversion of the award from a grant to a loan
does not alter the total award amount.

Funding remains contingent upon the City’s receipt of ARPA funding and authorization from the United
States Department of the Treasury to utilize such funds. Any reduction in Treasury funding may result in
a reduction or elimination of funding under this Agreement.

Loan proceeds shall be used exclusively for eligible construction costs associated with the permanent
supportive housing units. The Developer shall not obligate, encumber, or expend loan funds for any
purpose not included in the approved Project Budget without the City’s prior written approval.

Time of Performance and Notice to Proceed

Services under this Agreement shall commence only after issuance of a Notice to Proceed by the City and
shall conclude when all reporting and compliance requirements have been satisfied. The term of this
Agreement shall extend for any period during which the Developer remains in control of ARPA loan funds
or ARPA assisted assets.

Prior to issuance of a Notice to Proceed, the Developer shall provide the following documentation to the
City:

    •      Primary project financing secured
    •      Executed ARPA Mortgage and Promissory Note
    •      City approved insurance coverage
    •      Signed Affordable Housing Restrictive Covenant

Any costs incurred prior to issuance of the Notice to Proceed shall be ineligible for reimbursement and
may result in withdrawal of City funds pending review.

Method of Payment

The City shall disburse the ARPA loan to the Developer in a single loan payment to be used for eligible
Project costs as further described in the Agreement. Payment is contingent upon the Developer’s

compliance with all provisions of the Agreement, ARPA regulations at 31 CFR Part 35, OMB Uniform

ARPA Loan Repayment and Default

The City shall provide a zero percent (0 percent) interest ARPA loan in the amount of Seven Hundred
Thousand Dollars ($700,000) pursuant to the terms of the Mortgage (Attachment A) and Promissory Note
(Attachment B) attached to the Agreement.

Failure to maintain the required thirty (30) year affordability period shall constitute a default under this
Agreement. Any default not cured within ninety (90) days following written notice from the City shall
constitute an Event of Default.

Upon an Event of Default, the City may declare the entire outstanding loan balance immediately due and
payable and may exercise all remedies available at law, including foreclosure proceedings.

Any cure made or tendered by the Developer’s partner or a future owner of the Project shall be deemed
a cure by the Developer and shall be accepted or rejected on the same basis as if made directly by the
Developer.
