THIS AGREEMENT entered by and between the City of Kalamazoo, a Michigan Municipal Corporation, 241
West South Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007 ("City") and Kalrecovery I Limited Dividend Housing
Association Limited Partnership,1105 Portage Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49001 ("Developer").

WHEREAS the City has determined through the approval of the 2025-2029 Consolidated Plan that
affordable housing for vulnerable populations is a priority for funding which can be supported by the
Housing Development Fund (HDF); and

WHEREAS the City is undertaking certain activities to provide affordable housing, principally for persons
and households of low-income, as fully described in the 2025-2029 Consolidated Plan.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein, including any
attachments or exhibits, and subject to the terms and conditions stated, the City and Developer
understand and agree that:

I.           PROJECT DESCRIPTION

             A.   Upon issuance of a Notice to Proceed as provided in this Agreement, the Developer will construct
                  the Kalrecovery housing project, a new forty-eight (48) unit affordable housing development
                  ("Project"). Forty-six (46) units will be permanent supportive housing for persons recovering from
                  opioid and other substance use disorders and two (2) units will be for on-site staff.

             B.   The project will be developed at 1900 Belford Street, parcel number 06-27-209-001, and more
                  specifically described in the Mortgage (Attachment A). This description includes what is termed
                  the Restoration Area ("Restoration Area") and the Development Area ("Development Area") in
                  the Declaration of Restrictive Covenants ("Restrictive Covenants") recorded in the Kalamazoo
                  County Register of Deeds at 2017-036146. Consistent with the terms of the Restrictive Covenants,
                  the Developer has agreed that no portion of the Project shall be located in any portion of the
                  Restoration Area, nor shall any of the proceeds of this loan be used for any construction or
                  development within, maintenance of, or alteration to, any portion of the Restoration Area.

             C.   Residents will be participants or recent graduates of local drug treatment courts, and at least half
                  of all units will be targeted to individuals transitioning out of homelessness.

             D.   The Project will be for households making 50 percent or less of the area median income
                  established by the Michigan State Housing Development Authority on an annual basis.

             E.   The HDF loan will support six (6) units within the Project for the duration of a thirty (30) year
                  affordability period.

       A.   The City will provide a zero (0) percent interest HDF loan in the in the amount of Three

VIII.   PROJECT REQUIREMENTS

        A.   Per Unit Subsidy

             A total subsidy of $58,333.33 per unit is permitted under this budget. In order to ensure that
             no more HDF funds have been invested than are required to make the development of
             affordable housing possible, the City caused the Developer to prepare a pro forma which was
             reviewed for subsidy layering and underwriting.

        B.   Property Standards/Requirements

             The Developer shall ensure that all work, which must be done by licensed persons, is done
             only by such persons, and shall ensure that all permits, reviews, approvals, and inspections
             required by the Kalamazoo City Building Code are obtained and shall ensure the completed
             work is approved by an authorized person. Dwelling units may not be occupied until an
             authorized Certificate of Occupancy and Rental Certification is issued by the City of
             Kalamazoo.

        C.   Qualifications as Affordable Housing

             1.   The Developer shall designate the six (6) HDF-assisted units for the duration of the thirty
                  (30) year affordability period which shall be comparable to all other units in the project
                  based on numbers of bedrooms, living space square footage, and other amenities.

             2.   The six (6) HDF-assisted units must remain affordable for thirty (30) years. To qualify as
                  affordable, rents may not exceed those allowable by the Michigan State Housing
                  Development Authority established annually, less any monthly allowances for the utilities
                  and/or services to be paid by the tenant.

             3.   To ensure the affordability period, an Affordable Housing Restrictive Covenant shall be
                  recorded with the Kalamazoo County Register of Deeds.

             4.   Upon disposition or transfer within the affordability period, the project shall not involve
                  change in land use from residential to nonresidential, or from one class of residential use
                  to another class of residential use.

             5.   The Developer may sell the property to any willing buyer at whatever price the market
                  will bear. However, the sale of the property during the affordability period triggers
                  repayment of the direct HDF subsidy that the Developer received unless the buyer agrees
                  to assume the affordability period as outlined in the Affordable Housing Restrictive
                  Covenant.
