RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
MES, 12 Turnberry Lane, Second Floor, Sandy Hook, CT 06482, in the amount of $527,142 for
the purchase of a second set of structural firefighting turnout gear for Kalamazoo Department of
Public Safety (KDPS) Operations Division personnel, utilizing pricing available through the City
of Grand Rapids Fire Department contract #340-34-19.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety (KDPS) operates under a fully integrated public
safety model in which sworn personnel are cross-trained to provide fire suppression, emergency
medical services, and law enforcement. Operations Division personnel are routinely exposed to
hazardous environments, including products of combustion known to contain carcinogens and
other toxic substances.

KDPS currently maintains an existing contractual relationship with MES for the provision of
turnout gear. This change order expands the scope of that contract to include the procurement of
a second set of turnout gear for Operations Division personnel.

Providing a second set of turnout gear is a nationally recognized best practice aligned with
modern firefighter health and safety standards, including NFPA 1970. A second set of gear
allows personnel to properly clean, decontaminate, and dry contaminated equipment between
incidents, significantly reducing prolonged exposure to carcinogens and improving overall
occupational health outcomes.
