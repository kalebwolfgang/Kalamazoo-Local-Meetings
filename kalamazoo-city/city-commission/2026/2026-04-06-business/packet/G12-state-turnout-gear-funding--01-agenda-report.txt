RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accept $500,000 in appropriated funding from the
State of Michigan FY 2026 Budget to purchase a second set of structural firefighting turnout
gear for personnel assigned to the Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety (KDPS) Operations
Division, and authorize the City Manager to sign all related documents. It is also recommended
that the City Commission approve a budget amendment in the amount of $500,000 to expend the
funding.

BACKGROUND:

The Operations Division of the Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety responded to 13,943 fire
and rescue incidents in 2025, representing a sustained and high-volume operational tempo across
fire suppression, rescue, hazardous materials, emergency medical response, and technical
incident types.

Structural firefighting turnout gear is exposed to toxic byproducts of combustion—including
polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), volatile organic compounds (VOCs), and residual
particulates—during nearly all fireground and overhaul phases. These contaminants are
recognized occupational carcinogens and are strongly correlated with elevated cancer incidence
among firefighters. Ensuring that firefighters have access to a clean, dry, and fully compliant
second set of turnout gear is now an established national best practice and is supported by
multiple consensus standards, including emerging guidance in National Fire Protection
Association (NFPA) 1851: Standard on Selection, Care, and Maintenance of Protective
Ensembles for Structural Firefighting and Proximity Firefighting.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The City will receive $500,000 from the State of Michigan FY 2026 Budget specifically
designated for the purchase of a second set of structural firefighting turnout gear for Operations
Division personnel. An account will be set up to receive the grant funds and track the
expenditures related to the grant funding. With acceptance of this grant, we are also requesting
the City Commission to amend the budget, increasing both revenues and expenditures by
$500,000 in the grant fund. The funds will be used to purchase equipment, specifically
firefighting turnout gear.
