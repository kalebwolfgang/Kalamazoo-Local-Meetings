RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission approve a Housing Development Fund deferred loan in
the amount of $350,000 to Kalrecovery I Limited Dividend Housing Association Limited
Partnership to construct a new 48-unit housing project to include 46 permanent supportive
housing units for those recovering from opiate and methamphetamine use disorders.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalrecovery project is expected to provide 46 housing units for individuals and families
impacted by substance-use disorders and two additional units for on-site recovery coaches/site
managers, for a total of 48 units. The development has been awarded Low Income Housing Tax
Credits through the Michigan State Housing Development Authority, preserving affordability for
the long-term. Rental rates will be accessible to households making 30 to 50 percent of the area
median income. Additionally, 100 percent of the supportive housing units have project-based
vouchers, so residents will pay no more than 30 percent of their income towards housing
expenses. The loan provided by the City will be deferred for a 30-year affordability period
required through a promissory note and affordable housing deed restriction.

Increasing numbers of people with substance-use disorders are impacted by the criminal justice
system. Regional behavioral health systems and Michigan’s Problem-Solving Courts have
proven effective in promoting short-term recovery and reducing criminal recidivism. However, a
major barrier to long-term recovery for individuals participating in voluntary or court-ordered
programs is a lack of permanent supportive housing. The Kalrecovery permanent supportive
housing development helps to meet this need.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Shared Prosperity - Abundant opportunities for all people to prosper

This agreement will support the Shared Prosperity goal of the Imagine Kalamazoo Strategic Plan
as it results in permanent supportive and affordable housing for residents in crises. Achieving
this goal requires focus and collaboration among a spectrum of partners and placing a strong
focus on wraparound services. This is a primary component of the Kalrecovery projects,
assisting residents with long-term success in addressing their substance-use disorder. Finally, the
Shared Prosperity goal encourages coordination of services and resources to support people
returning from prison. The project will utilize the Kalamazoo County courts as a priority to
connect residents with available units.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Inform (one-way conversation) – no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

The developer of the Kalrecovery project has been in conversations with the Edison
Neighborhood Association (ENA) throughout many iterations of the project structure to ensure
the desires and concerns of the neighborhood were heard and addressed. Multiple discussions
were held regarding the project with the ENA Board. The letter of ENA support was a
unanimous decision and has been received. Two separate neighborhood input sessions were
conducted, and no negative feedback was received. This project was presented to both the
Planning Commission on September 20, 2021, and the Zoning Board of Appeals on July 14,
2022, and two Natural Features Protection Review Board meetings. This project has also been
presented at multiple City Commission meetings for funding support and Payment in Lieu of
Taxes (PILOT) requests.
