                                      B on Burdick Project Fact Sheet
Project Overview: The proposed project will facilitate the redevelopment of one parcel totaling 2.053 acres in the
City of Kalamazoo to make way for 85 residential rental units. The development is anticipated to include three
ground-floor commercial suites totaling approximately 13,500 square feet that is expected to include a gym,
bodega, and YMCA.
