technical assistance grant in the amount of $25,000 through the Community Economic

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo became a Redevelopment Ready Community in 2017. Redevelopment
Ready Communities® (RRC) is a voluntary technical assistance initiative offered through the
Michigan Economic Development Corporation (MEDC) which empowers communities to shape
their future by building a foundation of planning, zoning, and economic development best
practices and integrating them into their everyday functions. Communities who engage in the
RRC process commit to improving development readiness by agreeing to undergo a rigorous
assessment and working locally to integrate the best practices as described by the RRC process.
Kalamazoo recertifies each year to keep their RRC status.

As a part of being an RRC community, the MEDC offers technical assistance funds to further
advancement of best practices. The city requested $25,000 of the funds this year to partner with
the Kalamazoo County Land Bank to engage with Mddl. The analysis will examine policies and
land use to further the implementation of housing development on city and Land Bank parcels.

Mddl works directly with municipalities to identify and reduce regulatory obstacles that hinder
the development of middle housing. Their efforts aim to streamline approval processes and
promote supportive and market-aligned policy changes, creating a more favorable environment
for diverse housing options. The proposal attached will support the City of Kalamazoo and the

The technical assistance grant will achieve the following program activities:

1. Policy review and comparative municipality research: Mddl will conduct a comprehensive
review of regulations and guidelines impacting middle housing development in Kalamazoo,
supplemented by a comparative analysis of strategies employed in similar cities.
2. Interest holder surveys and virtual interviews: For this phase of the project, Mddl will engage
with the community and industry, focusing on representatives of neighborhood organizations, the
development sector, non-profit and government agencies, and other groups.
3. GIS lot analysis, white card modeling and pro forma analyses: Using open data provided by
the City, we will map lot dimensions across cities to identify common lot sizes and infill
opportunities.
4. Policy recommendations and report development: Building on the learnings from Activities 1-
3, the final report will be produced providing an overview of the project and major areas of focus
in City housing policy; a summary of the current policy and regulatory context in Kalamazoo; a
review of comparative policies in middle housing from other cities.

Project Outcomes:
- Evidence-based policy recommendations and clear roadmap for implementation to better
support the development of infill housing.
- Increased number of infill projects initiated in Kalamazoo.
- More diverse, affordable, and sustainable housing options for Kalamazoo residents.
