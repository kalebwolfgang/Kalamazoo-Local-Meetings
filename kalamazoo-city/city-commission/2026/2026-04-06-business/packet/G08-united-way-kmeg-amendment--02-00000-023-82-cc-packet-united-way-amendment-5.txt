FIFTH AMENDMENT TO THE SERVICES AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF
KALAMAZOO AND THE UNITED WAY OF THE BATTLE CREEK AND KALAMAZOO REGION

The City of Kalamazoo (“City”), whose address is 241 W. South Street, Kalamazoo,
Michigan 49007, and the United Way of South Central Michigan f/k/a United Way of the
Battle Creek and Kalamazoo Region (“United Way”), whose address is 709 S. Westnedge
Avenue, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007, entered into a Services Agreement effective March 1,
2021, as amended (the “Original Agreement”), for United Way to administer the City’s
Kalamazoo Small Business Loan Fund (“KSBLF”) and Kalamazoo Micro-Enterprise Grant
Fund (“KMEG”).

The Original Agreement was previously amended by an agreement effective October 25,
2021 (the “First Amendment”), an amendment effective October 8, 2023 (the “Second
Amendment”), an amendment effective March 3, 2024 (the “Third Amendment”), and an
amendment effective March 31, 2025 (the “Fourth Amendment”).

The City and United Way desire to amend the Agreement, as amended, to extend the term
of the Agreement beginning of April 1 2026 and end March 31 2027 period; to specify
funding allocations for the City’s 2026 fiscal year; and to revise the administration of the
specified grant and loan funds during the 2026 fiscal year; and therefore agree to amend
the Agreement as follows:

1. Term Extension
The following paragraph is added to the Agreement: This Agreement is extended
through March 31, 2027, unless earlier terminated in accordance with the
Agreement.

2. Amendment to Funding Authorization

Subsection (d) of the First Amendment, Second Amendment, and Third Amendment
is amended so that, as amended, that subsection reads as follows:

(d) United Way is authorized to transfer the sum of Four Hundred Ten Thousand
Eight Hundred Thirty-Three Dollars and Thirty-Three Cents ($410,833.33) from

the KSBLF loan program to support the following initiatives:

A. $250,000 to fund a Spring 2026 KMEG grant round;

. City Commission Approval

This Amendment shall not be effective unless and until approved by the Kalamazoo
City Commission prior to execution.

. Counterparts

This Amendment may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which shall
be deemed original, and all of which, taken together, shall be deemed one and the
same instrument.

. Effect of Amendment

In all other respects, the Agreement, as previously amended, is unchanged and is
hereby ratified and affirmed by the parties.
