                                      Natural Features Protection Review Board
                                     Appointee Nomination Report
                                               Date: 3/27/2026

RECOMMENDATION
The City of Kalamazoo Natural Features Protection Review Board recommends the City Commission approve the
following:

    -   The appointment of Sarah Munchow for a partial term expiring March 31, 2025

Selection Process
Applicants Considered:

One NFP Board Member term expire in March 2025. Since March 2025 staff have been promoting various
commission / board opportunities using the following tools: City social media, Professional Networks, Contacting
Previous Applicants. Two applications were received. One applicant did not respond to staff communications. In
2026, a second application was submitted. The NFP Board Liaison conducted an initial interview with the
applicant who stated they were still interested in serving on the NFP Board. At the February 2026 NFP Regular
Meeting, a Nominating Subcommittee of NFP Board Members was created to interview / review applications
and qualifications. The applicant met requirements to serve on the NFP Review Board and are moving forward
for City Commission / Mayoral approval.

Interview Process and Final Recommendation:

Staff have been attempting to fill one NFP Review Board position since March 2025. All staff expertise areas are
filled so outreach was focused on letting people know that anyone with interest in serving, could indeed apply.
The applicant was interviewed twice. Once with just the NFP Board Liaison and then again with the NFP
Nominating Subcommittee. The goal of the nominating subcommittee is to act as an extension of the NFP Board
and report back to the full board on the applicant interview and make a recommendation to City Commission /
Mayor for appointment.

Appointee Name: Sarah Munchow

Nominee Qualifications for Appointment
Training, Experience, Education and Skills:
Sarah Munchow is a Architect III / Sustainability Manager for Eckert Wordell Architecture and Engineering.
They’ve held that position for 4 years. They have earned an M.S. in Architecture from the University of Michigan
(2017). They are a Licensed architect with 8+ years of experience in education, healthcare, and community-
based projects and champion of sustainable design practices, embodied and operational carbon reduction, and
energy Modeling. They are open to opportunities to grow in responsible, resilient design. In addition to their
work, they are also a LEED Green Associate and are a Kalamazoo Area Wild Ones member and enthusiast of
native plant species.

Contribution to Diversity:
Gender: Female                 Race: White            Ethnicity: Non-Hispanic or Latinx
Age Range: 35-44
Additional Contribution to Diversity:
Sarah Munchow contributes to the diversity and balance to the NFP Board in their Experience, Architectural
Knowledge, and Demographic background. They are also located on the edge of the Douglas Neighborhood
which is a geography not represented.
