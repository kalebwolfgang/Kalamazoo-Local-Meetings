                               Proposed Program Activities:

                               1. Policy review and comparative municipality research: We will conduct a
                                  comprehensive review of regulations and guidelines impacting middle housing
                                  development in Kalamazoo, supplemented by a comparative analysis of strategies
                                  employed in similar cities. This analysis will encompass relevant standards, regulations, and
                                  implementation policies, including financing mechanisms. To foster a deeper understanding
                                  of Kalamazoo’s unique context and cultivate strong working relationships with City staff, our
                                  team will also conduct an on-site visit including meetings with key personnel and a tour of
                                  relevant areas.
                               2. Interest holder surveys and virtual interviews: For this phase of the project we will
                                  engage with community and industry, focusing on representatives of neighbourhood
                                  organizations, the development sector, non-profit and government agencies, and other
                                  groups. Recognizing that not all interested parties may be able to attend virtual or in-person
                                  live engagement sessions, our team will also deploy detailed surveys accessible online.
                                  These surveys will be designed to capture the perspectives, preferences, and suggestions
                                  of a wider audience. From this work we will provide a “What We Heard Report” to highlight
                                  these discussions and identify areas of consideration and concern for our white card
                                  modeling, pro forma analyses and policy development.
                               3. GIS lot analysis, white card modeling and pro forma analyses: Using open data
                                  provided by the City, we will map lot dimensions across cities to identify common lot sizes
                                  and infill opportunities. This data analysis filters lots based on eligible criteria including,
                                  but not limited to, age of house, policy overlays, lot grading, servicing availability, lane
                                  conditions, “rectangulation” parameters to ensure lots are regular-shaped for infill, among
                                  other key criteria. This analysis informs the creation of several“white card” models that
                                  test current regulations and alternative scenarios based on proposed recommendations.
                                  Each model is paired with financial testing through mddl’s pro forma tool sets. This
                                  financial analyses combines lot-level data with assumptions drawn from rental and
                                  sale market analysis and validated through consultation with local property managers,
                                  builders, developers, and real estate professionals. Through this study we are able to
                                  determine what models under current regulations perform financially “good,” “fair” or
                                  “poor.” These models are then fed into correlation matrices to understand positive and
                                  negative correlations between different criteria (lot sizes, bedrooms, coverage, return-on-
                                  investment, etc.) to identify opportunities to optimize model outputs to fall under the “good”
                                  category and ultimately informing the final policy recommendations.
                               4. Policy recommendations and report development: Building on the learnings from
                                  Activities 1-3, the final report will be produced providing an overview of the project and
                                  major areas of focus in City housing policy; a summary of the current policy and regulatory
                                  context in Kalamazoo; a “What We Heard Report” summary, including results from major
                                  engagement and possible action items based on feedback from participants; a review of

Future Programming:

In addition to the specific services outlined in this proposal, mddl offers a range of
complementary programs and tools that can further support Kalamazoo’s housing goals. These
include:
˥ Pre-Approved Building Permit Plans: Development of a catalogue of approval ready
     building-permit plans that are standardized for common lot typologies to streamline the
     approval process and reduce time and risk for local builds.
˥ GIS Lot Identification and Pro Forma Tools: An open-access digital platform that iden-
     tifies where and how middle housing can be built at the parcel level, lowering barriers to
     information and bridging planning information with the real estate market, helping identify
     viable redevelopment opportunities and further lowering barriers to middle housing deliv-
     ery. The mddl Pro Forma Tool is designed to give new and first time developers a simple
     way to understand and calculate high-level development finances. Users can input details
     such as unit size, rental price, land cost, and loan amounts to generate rental and sale per-
     formance metrics.
˥ mddl School: mddl School is a full-day, intensive in-person course designed to teach par-
     ticipants the essential skills needed to deliver middle housing.
˥ mddl Accelerator: mddl Accelerator leverages the momentum established by mddl
     School, offering participants continuous access to education and support from our com-
     munity of experts. Thisrogram features monthly presentations by industry leaders and
     personalized assistance through one-on-one working sessions with a network of over 50
     leading Experts-in-Residence.
˥ mddl Masters: This integrated development support program provides education, expert
     network access, project review and oversight at critical stages.

These services are not included in the scope of this proposal but could be valuable additions
to future phases of Kalamazoo’s infill housing initiatives. We are happy to explore and discuss
these additional options at the right time to further the groundwork of the Policy Harmonization
programming.

     Background
     CEDAM has received funding through MEDC to support several Certified Redevelopment Ready Communities,
     focused on priority sites identified during the RRC Certification process. These funds are intended solely for
     predevelopment technical assistance in promoting real estate redevelopment projects intended for commercial

                1. Assisting cities by overseeing the conduct of market studies of appropriate scope and scale for each of

                2. Planning and executing hands-on, interactive Workshops in each community, if necessary or

                   renderings, internal pro forma in MEDC format, package of development incentives, and marketing
                   content, as well as methods for local replication of work products;
                3. Delivering products determined necessary in a scope developed for each community assigned. Such
                   products may include technical materials; professional services such as surveys, environmental
                   studies, and architectural services; renderings; and RFQs. Additionally, summaries of Workshop
                   activities and assistance in actively marketing each priority site to local and national developers.

     All products and deliverables must be provided to the Client in a method and format acceptable to Client and the
     RRC Community on a mutually agreed upon timeline. MEDC may carry out additional report finalization,
