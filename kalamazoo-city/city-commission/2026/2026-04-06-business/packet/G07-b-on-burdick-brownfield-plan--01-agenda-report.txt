RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission adopt the resolution Adoption of a Resolution
Approving the Act 381 Brownfield Plan for B on Burdick Redevelopment Project Located at 802
S Burdick Street, Kalamazoo, MI.

BACKGROUND:

The B on Burdick is a proposed project at 802 S Burdick involving the redevelopment of a key
site with a mixed-use development that includes 85 units of residential apartments and ground-
floor commercial space. The project is intended to activate underutilized property and support
continued investment along the South Burdick corridor.

The residential component will include a mix of primarily below-market apartments targeting
workforce rental rates (80–120% of Area Median Income). The project supports the City’s
broader housing goals by increasing the supply of rental units at a range of price points. Ground-
floor commercial space is planned to accommodate retail and daily needs, enhancing street-level
activity and corridor vitality.

The project is expected to provide broader community and economic development benefits,
including increased housing supply, private investment, job creation, and long-term tax base
growth. The residential units offered are expected to be a mix of 18 studio apartments, 48 one-
bedroom apartments, and 19 two-bedroom apartments available for rent. The project intends to
income qualify 17 studio apartments, approximately 20% of the development, for a duration of
20 years. Construction on the project is planned to begin in the summer of 2026 and will be
completed in 2027.

As part of the Brownfield Plan, the applicant is seeking reimbursement of eligible activities
consistent with Act 381, for a total of $5,055,033 in developer reimbursement estimated over 25
years. The tax increment financing (TIF) support includes approximately $1.8 million in gap
financing and $3.2 million in hard and soft costs. Eligible activities may include pre-approved
activities, lead/asbestos/mold abatement, site preparation, demolition, infrastructure, preparation
and implementation of the brownfield plan, and other brownfield eligible activities to support
redevelopment of the property. The plan includes approximately $471,546 in reimbursement to
the BRA for administrative costs and $1,234,930 deposited into the Local Brownfield Revolving
Fund.

An independent third-party financial review of the project was completed, including a but-for
analysis which determined the project would not be financially feasible without public
participation. The review also included underwriting of the project’s financing structure to
evaluate its ability to support debt service and operate sustainably.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:
Economic Vitality - A supportive infrastructure for growing businesses and stabilizing the local
economy for the benefit of all.

Sub-Goal:
Economic Assistance - Use investments, incentives, and policies to reach community-wide
goals for affordable housing, increase public transportation, local business success, and to avoid
involuntary displacement.

The B on Burdick project will use Brownfield TIF as an economic assistance tool to support
private investment by leveraging bank financing, helping make the project financially feasible. In
addition, the Housing TIF provision under Act 381 provides support for units with below-market
rents, offsetting the difference between what the project can charge and the rent needed to make
the building financially viable and units affordable as workforce housing. This project is
committing 17 units at rents affordable to households at or below 80% of Area Median Income,
ensuring these units remain accessible while supporting overall project stability.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Appropriate Depth of Engagement
Inform (one-way conversation) – Following PA 381 public noticing requirements, impacted
taxing jurisdictions were given notice of the public hearing, notice was published in MLive, and
jurisdictions and the public was provided opportunity to speak in regards to the Plan. No input
was received prior to or during the public hearing.

FISCAL IMPACT:

No direct fiscal impact on the FY 2026 budget. The project implementation costs in 2027 and
beyond will be funded through an administrative fee covered by the Brownfield Plan. The
breakdown in capture values and future tax revenues are as follows:

     • 30 Years of Brownfield Plan Capture
     • $471,546 Authority Administration Fees (10% Local-Only Estimate)
     • $5,055,033 Maximum Amount of Developer Eligible Activities
     • $1,234,930 Estimated Amount to be Deposited in Local Brownfield Revolving Fund
     • Increase in taxable value of property from $448,549 (2025 value) to $4,350,000
       (estimated value after construction period)
