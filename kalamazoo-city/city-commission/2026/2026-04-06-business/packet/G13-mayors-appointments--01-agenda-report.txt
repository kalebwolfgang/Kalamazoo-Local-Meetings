BACKGROUND:

Downtown Development Authority/Downtown Economic Growth Authority

The Downtown Development Authority (DDA) Board is the governing body of a special purpose
local authority established under state enabling law to halt deterioration and to foster
development of the downtown business area.

The Downtown Economic Growth Authority (DEGA) was established by the Kalamazoo City
Commission in October 2018 under the Corridor Improvement Authority (CIA) Act. Downtown
authorities are designed to be catalysts in the development of a community’s downtown district
and to assist with funding improvements along commercial corridors. Under the CIA Act, the
DEGA has the authority to create a development plan, mapping out specific investments and
improvements to be made within its boundaries, and to create a tax increment financing (TIF)
plan to fund planned improvements.

The membership of the DEGA and DDA Boards is the same by design. State law and City
ordinance prescribe that the Mayor appoints and reappoints members of the DDA and DEGA
boards, with the approval of the City Commission. I have reviewed the Nomination Reports for
Connor Sampson and Harvin Sandhu and have appointed them. As required by law, I am
requesting that the City Commission approve these appointments. The attached Nomination
Report provides the details of their qualifications.

Historic District Commission

The Historic District Commission conducts design review hearings in a quasi-judicial capacity to
decide issues relating to the enforcement of the Historic District ordinance and to permit, within
standards established by the ordinance, exceptions to strict compliance with the terms of the
ordinance in recognized historic districts and as applied to designated landmarks.

City ordinance prescribes that the Mayor appoints and reappoints members of the Historic
District Commission (HDC), with the approval of the City Commission. I have reviewed the
nomination report from the HDC and have appointed Nelson Nave. As required by ordinance, I
am requesting that the City Commission approve this appointment. The attached Nomination
Report provides details of the nominee's qualifications.

Natural Features Protection Review Board

The purpose of the Natural Features Protection Review Board is to assist staff in the Site Plan
Review process by providing expert knowledge on relevant natural features and development or
construction. Approval from the Board is necessary for a project to obtain full site plan review
approval as part of the development, permitting, and construction process. The intent of the
Board is to guide the development of land in the Natural Features Protection Overlay District in
order to protect natural features in the City.

Planning Commission

The Planning Commission is required and regulated by state law. One of its major duties is the
development of the city's Master Land Use Plan (IK 2025). In accordance with this plan and the
current Zoning Ordinance, the Planning Commission provides recommendations to the City
Commission on re-zoning requests and changes to the Zoning Ordinance text. The Planning
Commission also has responsibility for reviewing and approving Special Use Permits and some
site plans and Temporary Use Permits.

State law and City ordinance prescribe that the Mayor appoints and reappoints members of the
Planning Commission, with the approval of the City Commission. I have reviewed the
Nomination Report for Drew Duncan, Diego Sandoval, and Sakhi Vyas and have appointed them
to the Planning Commission. As required by law, I am requesting that the City Commission
approve these appointments. The attached Nomination Report provides details of their
qualifications.
