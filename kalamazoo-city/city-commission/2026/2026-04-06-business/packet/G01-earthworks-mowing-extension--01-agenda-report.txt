SUBJECT:       Approval of a contract extension with Earthworks Lawn and Landscape for 13
               and 26 Frequency Property Mowing (Bid Ref#98852-066.2.2)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract extension with Earth Works
Lawn and Landscape for 13 and 26 Frequency 2026 Property Mowing services in the amount of
$168,760.

BACKGROUND:

In March 2026, the City’s Purchasing Division solicited renewal pricing for maintenance of City
owned properties and right of ways.

Earth Works Lawn and Landscape provided pricing with no increase, they have held various
mowing contracts with the City since 2017 and staff are pleased with their level of service. They
have become familiar with the properties and are extremely responsive to the demands of this
contract.
