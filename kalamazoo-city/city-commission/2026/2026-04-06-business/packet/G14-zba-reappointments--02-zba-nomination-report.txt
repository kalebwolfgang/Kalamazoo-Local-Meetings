                                                  Zoning Board of Appeals
                                     Appointee Nomination Report
                                               Date: 3/30/2026

RECOMMENDATION
The City of Kalamazoo Zoning Board of Appeals recommends the City Commission approve the following:

    -   The reappointment of Gary Wark for a full term expiring March 31, 2029
    -   The reappointment of Allison Haan for a full term expiring March 31, 2029
    -   The reappointment of Jack Urban for a full term expiring March 01, 2029

Selection Process
Applicants Considered:

Gary Wark has served on the ZBA since 2023 and has held the board officer position of Vice Chair for multiple
years. This is a reappointment to his second full term. Allison Hahn has served on the ZBA since 2023. She has
been an asset to the board with her knowledge of the community and human resources background. This is a
reappointment for a first full term. Jack Urban has served on the ZBA since 2025. He is not new to the ZBA as he
has served on this board in the past. Further, as a former City Commissioner he brings a wealth of insight to the
board about the history of the city. This is a reappointment for a first full term.

Interview Process and Final Recommendation:

No interviews were held. All three of these individuals are current members of the ZBA in good standing.

Appointee Name: Gary Wark

Nominee Qualifications for Reappointment
Training, Experience, Education and Skills:
Gary Wark has lived in the city for many years, mostly in the Stuart Neighborhood. He has served on past
boards including: The Dangerous Buildings Board, Building Board of Appeals, Housing Board of Appeals. He is
currently the Chair of the Stuart Historic Neighborhood Association. Mr. Wark has decades of experience in real
estate, land development and property management.

Contribution to Diversity:
Gender: Male                   Race: White             Ethnicity: Non-Hispanic or Latinx
Age Range: Unknown
Additional Contribution to Diversity:
Gary Wark is the only Board representative from the Stuart Historic Neighborhood. His prior board experience,
real estate / development background, and work with the Stuart Historic Neighborhood Association have all
been an asset to the ZBA.

Policy and Position Compliance
Dual Board Memberships:
Currently, nominee does not serve as a member of another city advisory board.

Term Limits:
This will be the second full term for Gary Wark. They will not be eligible for additional reappointments.
Their term will end on 3/31/2029.

Position Requirements (if any) for this Board Position:
Zoning Board of Appeals members must be residents of the City of Kalamazoo and should have knowledge of
the City, including some familiarity with the Zoning Ordinance, as well as knowledge of the current issues in the
community.

Nomination Rationale:
Gary Wark has prior board experience and a depth of real estate and development knowledge. Mr. Wark has
also served in the capacity of Vice Chair of the ZBA for the last couple of years.
