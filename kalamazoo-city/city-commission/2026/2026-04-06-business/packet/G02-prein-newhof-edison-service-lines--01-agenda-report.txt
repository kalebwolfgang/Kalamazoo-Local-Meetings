RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Prein & Newhof for Professional Services related to the FY2024 DWSRF Edison Non-Copper
Service Replacement Project, in the amount of $270,000.00.

BACKGROUND:

Each year, EGLE requests applications for projects to receive Drinking Water Revolving Fund
(DWRF) funding, which includes low interest rate loans, with the possibility for partial or full
forgiveness. Public Services applied for DWRF funding and received $20.35-million in grant
and loan funding. This funding will be applied to two construction contracts: Contract 1 –
Edison Non-Copper Service Replacement Project which includes inspecting, replacing, or
abandoning non-copper services in approximately 2000 locations. Contract 2 – Knollwood Pipe
Lining which includes lining approximately 8800-ft of existing water main in the Knollwood
Neighborhood.

Public Services worked with Prein & Newhof on the design for these two contracts and has
requested a proposal for professional services related to construction engineering for these
contracts. Construction has been taking more time and oversight resources than anticipated, so a
supplemental/change order in the amount of $270,000 is being requested. Prein & Newhof has
performed similar work in the past and Public Services was very pleased with the results. Public
Services recommends approval of the supplemental.
