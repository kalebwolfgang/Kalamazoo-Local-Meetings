RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Michigan Office Environments, 177 Portage Street, Kalamazoo, MI 49007, in the amount of
$976,623.39 for office furniture associated with the 150 East Crosstown Facility Renovation
Project and authorize the City Manager to sign all associated documents.

BACKGROUND:

The 150 East Crosstown Facility Renovation Project represents a significant capital investment
by the City of Kalamazoo to modernize approximately 38,000 square feet of municipal
workspace to support Community Planning and Economic Development, Information
Technology, Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEI), Communications, and shared community
functions.

As the project has progressed beyond the structural and systems phases and is now over 50
percent complete, it is necessary to procure and install office furniture to support operational
readiness upon project completion. The proposed phase 2 and 3 furniture package includes
workstations, private office furnishings, conference room tables and seating, collaborative
workspace elements, reception areas, and ancillary furnishings necessary to fully activate the
building.

The furniture has been selected to align with modern workplace design standards, emphasizing
flexibility, durability, ergonomic support, and efficient space utilization. Additionally, the
selected systems support hybrid work environments, collaborative planning functions, and
public-facing service delivery.
