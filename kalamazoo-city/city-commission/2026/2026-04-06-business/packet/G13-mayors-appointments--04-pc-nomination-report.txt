Appointee Name: Drew Duncan

Nominee Qualifications for Appointment
Training, Experience, Education and Skills:
Drew Duncan is a Kalamazoo resident and long‑time construction and housing professional, currently serving as
a Construction Manager with Community Homeworks. His background includes licensure as a Michigan Builder,
former Building Inspector & Plan Reviewer for the City of Kalamazoo. He previously served as Vice Chair of the
Stuart Neighborhood Association. Drew brings strong technical knowledge of building codes, redevelopment
processes, and neighborhood dynamics, skills directly relevant to land‑use decision‑making. Drew resides in the
Stuart Neighborhood.

understanding of the city's long‑term planning frameworks. Sakhi resides in the Winchell Neighborhood.
