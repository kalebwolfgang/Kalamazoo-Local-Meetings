SUBJECT:       Approval of a Social District Permit for Old Skool Bars, LLC (125 S Kalamazoo
               Mall)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Social District Permit for Old Skool
Bars, LLC, located at 115 S Kalamazoo Mall.

BACKGROUND:

The Michigan Liquor Control Commission (MLCC) allows licensed establishments to participate
in approved Social Districts, where alcoholic beverages may be sold for consumption in
designated common areas. The City of Kalamazoo previously established a Social District in the
downtown area and may approve additional eligible establishments to participate, subject to
MLCC requirements and local regulations.

The Old Skool Bar, located at 125 S Kalamazoo Mall, is a licensed establishment within the
boundaries of the existing Social District and is seeking approval to participate. The business
meets all applicable MLCC requirements and has agreed to comply with local Social District
regulations, including the use of approved containers, adherence to established district hours, and
coordination with enforcement and operational standards.

The addition of Old Skool Bar will expand the number of participating establishments within the
district, supporting continued activity and increased utilization of the district.
