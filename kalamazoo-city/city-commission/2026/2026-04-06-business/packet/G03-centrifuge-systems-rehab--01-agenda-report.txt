RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a sole source purchase with Centrifuge-
Systems LLC, for the repair of our damaged centrifuge equipment in the amount of $301,040.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant has three centrifuges to process wet thickened
biosolids into dewatered cake for disposal. These centrifuges pass unprocessed thickened waste
through as it is rotated at high speeds, average 2000 rpm, to de-water into processed dry solids.

Two of the three centrifuges experienced major internal parts failure and need emergency
repairs. The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant relies on our dewatering system to process
approximately 285 wet tons per day from this facility. Failure to process solids could result in
treatment issues and potential NPDES permit violations.

Wastewater staff hosted an emergency meeting with the equipment manufacturer, Centrisys to
receive technical assistance and recommendations on alternatives that will best support the
operational readiness and permit compliance of the Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant.
Refurbishing these failed parts was determined to be the best and most timely solution for
repairs.

Until these repairs are made to the centrifuge units, we are relying on one working centrifuge
unit, and one rental unit to dewater all 285 wet tons per day.
