SUBJECT:       Approval of a cooperative contract with Spohn Ranch, Inc. for the purchase of a
               bicycle pump track at Verburg Park (Bid Ref#00000-026.32)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a cooperative contract with Spohn Ranch,
Inc. for the purchase of all drawings, materials and labor to construct a bicycle pump track at
Verburg Park in the amount of $140,000.

BACKGROUND:

In conjunction with the site and amenity work being completed by Playcore Wisconsin, Inc., the
pump track is the final piece to the Verburg Park project. Once site work has been completed, the
asphalt pump track can be installed and will be open to bicyclists of all ages, strategically
connected to the KRVT.
The idea was developed through our department master plan community outreach and is well-
supported as part of this park project.

Under the Sourcewell Cooperative purchasing contract, we are able to utilize Spohn Ranch to
complete the bicycle pump track improvements.
