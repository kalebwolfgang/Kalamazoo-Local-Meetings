                           CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                   RESOLUTION NO. 26-

         RESOLUTION APPROVING SUBMISSION OF STATE HIGH WATER
                        INFRASTRUCTURE GRANT

       Minutes of a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City held on March 16, 2026
2023, at 7:00 o'clock p.m., local time, at City Hall.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

       WHEREAS the City of Kalamazoo has need of a Citywide Existing Stormwater
Capacity Analysis.

        WHEREAS the current version of this analysis was generated in 1972 and has outlived
its useful life. A new analysis would modernize the current version and make it more accurate
and accessible for all stakeholders.

        WHEREAS funding is available from the State High Water Infrstructure Grant program
of the Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy.

       WHEREAS the total project cost is estimated to be no more than $540,000; 20 percent
of which would be funded be local match.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Kalamazoo City Commission approves
the submission of the grant application tp the State High Water Infrastructure Grant Program not
to exceed $450,000 for the completion of a Citywide Existing Stormwater Capacity Analysis and
commits that the local match shall be provided if the project is funded.
