RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Rathco for the 2026 Rental of Traffic Control Devices, in the amount of $250,000.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo uses temporary traffic control and sign installation services each year to
complete a myriad of various projects, emergencies, route changes and special events. The City
seeks a service provider who can provide the needed service each year. The contract for traffic
control device and sign installation is used by Public Services to include Public Works, Water
and Wastewater projects for a variety of purposes including street resurfacing, emergency
projects, flooding, traffic-calming, special events, road construction, utility services and much
more. This supplemental is needed to pay current invoices and add funds to support the
remainder of this contract.
