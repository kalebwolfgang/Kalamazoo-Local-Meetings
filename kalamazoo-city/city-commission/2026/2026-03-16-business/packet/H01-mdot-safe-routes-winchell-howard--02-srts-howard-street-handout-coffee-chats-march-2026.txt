Safe Routes to School (SRTS): Coﬀee & Crosswalk Discussion
Shared Value. We are committed to a safe route to school crossing of Howard Street connecting students
from the Vine Neighborhood to Maple Magnet Middle School.

Timeline to Get There.

March 16th. Approval of contract for City of Kalamazoo’s matching funds ($47,367) to the State’s Safe Route to
School funding ($387,825) for Maple Magnet Middle and Winchell. Without commitment to our match, the
City risks losing the SRTS funding.

April to Fall.

        City will work to review the plans with KPS (both busing and Maple Middle School) and the
        neighborhood, considering such elements as pedestrian island design, crossing location, signage, and
        lighting.

        Any designs changes will be handled as a change order with the Michigan Department of
        Transportation (MDOT)’s selected contractor.

        Howard Street 4 to 3 lane conversion will shift from 2028 to 2027 to allow the safe routes to school
        (SRTS) work to occur with the Howard Street redesign project.

        City staﬀ commit to continuous conversation with Vine residents and KPS between now and project
        implementation.

2027. Construction of Howard Street and the SRTS crossing is planned for 2027, likely a summer/fall 2027 start.
Background.

2020. Safe Routes to School Plan for Winchell Elementary & Maple Middle School. The plan was created after
many outreach and engagement events with the schools, students, parents, and neighbors

2021. City makes formal request for funding for SRTS projects.

2022/23. City hired a design ﬁrm to take the plan and translate to an engineered document. Limited, but direct
outreach was done to some stakeholders at the time to detail the work from plan to engineered designs.

2024. SRTS crossing and the Howard Street 4 to 3 lane conversion projects were planned to occur; both
projects were delayed. SRTS work was delayed at the state level due to National Environmental Policy Act
(NEPA) and State Historic Preservation Oﬃce (SHPO) Section 106 review.

2025. SRTS reviewed through the NEPA and SHPO process.

2026. SRTS project clears state NEPA and SHPO review.

        City Commission asked to approve City’s match or contribution to the project.

        City Commission delayed action until 03/16 to allow staﬀ time to share information with and answer
        questions from Vine residents, talk timing with MDOT, and consider work on Howard Street.
