SUBJECT:       Approval of a contract extension with Dale W Hubbard Inc. for Disposal of
               Vactor Spoils (Bid Reference 92645-001.2)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission approve a contract extension with Dale W. Hubbard for
services related to disposing of vactor spoils for the Water, Wastewater, and Stormwater
departments in the amount of $135,725.00.

BACKGROUND:

The practice of disposing vactor spoils with Clean Earth initially resulted from a shortage of
sawdust that was being used as an absorbent at the KWRP. Once we reduced the volume of
spoils being dumped at the Wastewater Plant, we were able to analyze the process more
thoroughly. We realized that we were having to “handle” all dumping twice, which is highly
inefficient for Wastewater. We also noticed that prior to limiting dumping we had an inability to
dewater the Stormwater solids effectively causing issues with material being on site longer than
necessary and contributing to odors. Since we have eliminated Water and Stormwater spoils, we
are able to handle the volume of material and reduce odors. Due to these findings, we are no
longer allowing any Water or Stormwater dumping at Harrison. Clean Earth Environmental is
the only vendor that has the capability to accept, handle, store, dry and dispose of vactor materiel
using approved methods.
