RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a sole source purchase with Centrifuge-
Systems, LLC, 9586 58th Place, Kenosha WI, in the amount of $171,00.00 for the equipment
lease and testing agreement and authorize the City Manager to sign all related documents.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant has three centrifuges that process wet thickened
biosolids into cake for disposal. Two of the centrifuges have major internal parts that have
experienced failure and need to be repaired or replaced. Wastewater staff hosted an emergency
meeting with the equipment manufacturer, Centrisys (V/N 034615), to receive technical
assistance and recommendations on alternatives that will best support the operational readiness
and permit compliance of the Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant.

Due to the volume of solids processed daily, it is recommended that temporary rental of a
centrifuge be utilized while the units are repaired to maintain operations. Two centrifuges will be
factory reconditioned to extend their useful life. The centrifuges were removed from service and
packaged onto a semi-truck en route to Wisconsin on Monday, March 9, 2026.

Centisys is the sole source provider and manufacturer of this equipment.
