RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Anlaan Corporation for
Kilgore -Portage Shared Use Path project in the amount of $ 516,528.00.

BACKGROUND:

Public Works requests approval to award a one-time construction contract for the Kilgore–
Portage Shared Use Path Project, which primarily consists of constructing new sidewalk and
ADA-compliant ramps to improve pedestrian safety and accessibility. The improvements will
enhance connectivity for families with children accessing Milham Park, provide safer access to
nearby shops and offices, and improve pedestrian connections to the nearby MDOT office,
consistent with City standards and ADA requirements. This project is funded through a
combination of Michigan Shared Streets and Spaces (MSSG) grant funds and FFE Sidewalk
funding. Bids were opened on February 4, 2026, and the lowest responsive and responsible bid
was submitted by Anlaan Corporation in the amount of $516,528. The vendor meets all
qualification requirements and has previously performed satisfactory work for Public Services;
therefore, Public Works recommends awarding the contract to Anlaan Corporation in the amount
of $516,528.00
