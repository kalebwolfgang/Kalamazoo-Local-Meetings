RECOMMENDATION:

That the City Commission approve a resolution to submit a State High Water Infrastructure
Grant Application to the Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo will submit an application to complete a citywide existing stormwater
capacity analysis. When complete, this analysis will modernize, digitize, and improve
accessibility to accurate stormwater capacity data for all residents and stakeholders. When
complete, the city will have an accurate and detailed GIS layer of stormwater capacity. This type
of analysis was last completed in 1972 and, while very outdated, is still used by staff to
determine capacity and guide development. The analysis is a key component of a Stormwater
Master Plan and will combine with the Capital Improvement Plan and recently completed
Kalamazoo Stormwater Utility Business Plan to support comprehensive and strategic stormwater
planning.

The city is still determining an accurate cost estimate for this project, but can confirm that the
grant request will not exceed $450,000 with $90,000 provided as match funding.
