RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Ferguson Waterworks for Neptune Water Meters, in the amount of $269,889.60.

BACKGROUND:

In 2018, the City of Kalamazoo initiated a Smart Meter replacement program, replacing older
model meters with Neptune water meters system wide. The new meters are able to provide near
real time data, which can be used by the City of Kalamazoo as well as it’s customers for
functions such as leak detection, water usage optimization, as well as many others. The Neptune
meters have performed as well as expected.

As a utility it is our goal to increase the number of Smart Meters in the system, and we need to
work toward obtaining total system transition to Smart Meters for full program implementation.
Full system implementation is estimated at $40 million given our current position.

We are prioritizing an optimized and partnered approach to increase our current rate of attrition
to Smart Meters. Areas of prioritized optimization and technical, managerial and financial
capacity partnership are:
• Smart Meter replacement in conjunction with Lead Service Line Replacement (LSLR)
• Smart Meter replacement in targeted areas – west of US131 and areas further from the City
limits
• Smart Meter replacement partnership with Gull Lake Sewer Water Authority in Richland
Township and Richland Village.

awarded LSLR contractor with Smart Meter components and to require Smart Meter replacement
