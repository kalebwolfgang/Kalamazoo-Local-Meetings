RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a resolution approving the attached budget
amendments.

BACKGROUND:

State law and the City's Budget Policies adopted by the City Commission require that the City's
legislatively appropriated expenditures be adequate to cover actual expenditures. Amendments
to the 2026 budget require approval from the City Commission for expenditures planned in the
2025 operating budget that will be completed in 2026. Amendments to the 2025 budget
recognize adjustments that were required for final year-end expenditures and final year-end
closing adjustments.

Based on these requirements, staff recommends City Commission approve the budget
amendments as attached.

The City's Budget Policies also require that the City bring capital and grant carryforward
amounts to City Commission as an informational item. The carryforwards from 2025 to 2026
are below:

Capital Fund Carryforward
The City’s budget policies authorize approved capital project budgets to be re-appropriated at the
end of the year. For informational purposes, the attached schedule includes $243,197,898.57 in
budgeted capital projects carried forward from 2025 to 2026.

supplemental listing of carryforward by project amount has been provided.The attached

total amount of carryforward will not exceed the amount in these documents by fund.
