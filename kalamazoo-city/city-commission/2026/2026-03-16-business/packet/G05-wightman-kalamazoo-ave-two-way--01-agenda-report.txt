RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Wightman and Associates Inc for Design of Kalamazoo Avenue 2 Way Conversion in the
amount of $165,900.00.

BACKGROUND:

Wightman is under contract to provide professional engineering services for the Kalamazoo Ave
2 Way Conversion project. Services include public engagement, access management planning,
traffic analysis, storm sewer assessment, and phased design for the two-way conversion of
Kalamazoo Avenue. The work completed to date includes concept development, alternatives
analysis, coordination with City staff and stakeholders, preparation of design plans and
specifications, traffic modeling, MDOT LAP coordination, NEPA and Section 106 support, cost
estimating, and assistance with project phasing and grant-related requirements. This work has
advanced the project from early planning through detailed design and positioned it for final
design completion and eventual advertisement for construction.

The two proposals attached total $165,9000. The work needed is outlined in Proposals 1 and 2
below:

Proposal 1, dated September 19, 2024, provides for right-of-way acquisition needed for ADA-
compliant pedestrian improvements. This includes securing one permanent easement and up to
twenty-three temporary grading permits, coordinating MDOT-compliant acquisition and
certification, and incorporating the acquired rights into the final plans. These services were not
included in the original scope and are required to complete the project, with work performed on a
time-and-materials basis not to exceed $49,900.

Proposal 2, dated September 29, 2025, is an extension of professional services necessary to
complete design, coordination, public engagement, NEPA clearance, and bidding support for
Kalamazoo Ave, with related coordination for W Main St and Douglas Ave. The additional
work addresses extended project timelines, evolving design and phasing, coordination with
adjacent projects and grant requirements, and completion of MDOT LAP–compliant bid
documents, with services proposed on an hourly basis not to exceed $116,000.

Approval of these additional services will allow the City to complete the project in accordance
with finalized design requirements, regulatory obligations, and MDOT LAP procedures, and to
carry the Kalamazoo Avenue project through final design and advertisement for construction.
Authorizing this work ensures continuity with the existing consultant, avoids delays to the
project schedule, and provides a clear and accountable path to project completion within defined
not-to-exceed limits.
