SUBJECT:       Approval of a Professional Service Agreement with Traffic Tech Services LLC,
               for Traffic Related Services (Bid Ref#00000-026.34)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission approve a professional service agreement with Traffic
Tech Services for technical traffic related work in the amount of $50,000.00 over 24 months.

BACKGROUND:

In 2023, quotes were solicited for this service with Traffic Tech Services being the only
responsive bidder. Traffic Tech Services is owned and operated by Brian Burlingham. Mr.
Burlingham is a retiree from the City of Kalamazoo and worked as a Traffic Technician for
many years. He has owned and operated his own business since he retired in 2013 and is highly
regarded throughout the Kalamazoo County area as a sought-after traffic professional. He
currently has a contract with the Kalamazoo Area Transportation Study to which we participate
in providing traffic counts.

Traffic Tech Services, LLC will provide professional services including: pavement marking
layout and inspection for new projects, recommendations for existing pavement marking
upgrades/changes; traffic studies, sign layout, placement consulting and staking; consulting
services for traffic studies; help current staff as needed; assist the Traffic Engineer as needed;
consulting for any other traffic related issues; and any special projects as requested by the City of
Kalamazoo.
