RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a professional service agreement with
O'Boyle, Cowell, Blaylock & Associates, Inc. for design services and construction
administration for Milham Park improvements in the amount of $140,445.00.

BACKGROUND:

In 2023, O'Boyle, Cowell, Blaylock and Associates (OCBA) worked on the development of the
Milham Park Master Plan through community input meetings, design, and Natural Features
Protection reporting. We are now ready to begin park improvements through site plan approval,
EGLE permitting, and construction bidding. This contract will be to utilize OCBA's expertise to
deliver the park improvements for Milham Park utilizing department Capital Improvement funds
and the Michigan Department of Natural Resources Land and Water Conservation Fund grant
that was received for the project.
