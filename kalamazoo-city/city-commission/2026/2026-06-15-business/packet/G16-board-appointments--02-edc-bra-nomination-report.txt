RECOMMENDATION
The City of Kalamazoo Brownfield Redevelopment Authority Board/Economic Development Corporation Board
recommends the City Commission approve the following:

    -   The reappointment of Lucas Middleton for a full term expiring March 31, 2032
    -   The reappointment of Michael Gurnee for a full term expiring March 31, 2032

Selection Process
Applicants Considered:

Directors Michael Gurnee & Lucas Middleton are existing board members who were considered by the Executive
Committee and recommended for reappointment by the Board of Directors.

Interview Process and Final Recommendation:

At the 5/21/2026 BRA Meeting, the staff liaison, Ms. McCarthy mentioned that Director Middleton has had a
previous full term and noted that Director Gurnee filled a partial term and is completing their first full term.
Board Chair Gulau stated they have enjoyed serving with both directors and think they are doing a wonderful
job. The Executive Committee supported their reappointment and the Board voted unanimously to recommend
City Commission reappointment both Directors.

Appointee Name: Michael Gurnee

Nominee Qualifications for Reappointment
Training, Experience, Education and Skills:
Mr. Gurnee is the statewide Remediation and Redevelopment Outreach Specialist for the Michigan Department
of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE). He formerly served as the EGLE district coordinator for
brownfield redevelopment projects. As Brownfield Redevelopment Coordinator, he evaluated and oversaw local
brownfield redevelopment projects throughout southwest Michigan that received EGLE grant, loan, and tax
increment financing support. His new role at EGLE allows him the opportunity to serve Kalamazoo in a different
capacity. He is a resident of Kalamazoo.
