                                               Historic District Commission

RECOMMENDATION
The City of Kalamazoo Historic District Commission recommends the City Commission approve the following:

    -   The appointment of Mark Dunham for a partial term expiring January 01, 2029
    -   The appointment of Elijah Isch for a partial term expiring January 01, 2028

Selection Process
Applicants Considered:

Elijah Isch, Mark Dunham

Interview Process and Final Recommendation:

Both applicants were interviewed by the Historic Preservation Coordinator prior to being interviewed by the
Historic District Commission. During this interview with the Coordinator, the role of the HDC was described to
the applicants. Both applicants were then interviewed by the HDC at the 19 May 2026 meeting.
