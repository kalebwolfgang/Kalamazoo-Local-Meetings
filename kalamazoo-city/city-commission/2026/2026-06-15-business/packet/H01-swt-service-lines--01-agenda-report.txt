RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with SWT Excavating, Inc. for
the FY 2026 DWSRF Non-Copper Service Line Replacements, in the amount of $4,135,936.10.

BACKGROUND:

The Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE) has recommended
that all existing lead service lines be replaced. Each year, EGLE requests applications for
projects to receive Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF) funding, which includes low
interest rate loans, with the possibility for partial or full forgiveness. Public Services applied for
DWSRF funding and received $5-million in grant and loan funding. This funding is being
applied to Non-Copper Service Line Replacements which includes replacing approximately 600
non-copper service lines.

Bids were opened for the project on May 12, 2026. Two bids were received, with SWT
Excavating, Inc. being the low responsive bidder. Bids came in favorably, so Public Services, in
collaboration with Purchasing, and EGLE is requesting to execute a post-bid addendum to
increase the number of services from 500 to 600 service replacements to utilize the full earmark
of funding. SWT Excavating, Inc. has performed well with other similar projects for the City of
Kalamazoo, and Public Services recommends approval of this contract.
