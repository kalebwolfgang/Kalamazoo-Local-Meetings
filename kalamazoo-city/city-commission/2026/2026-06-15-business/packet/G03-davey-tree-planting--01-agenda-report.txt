               (Bid Ref # 5975-042.0)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Davey Resource Group Inc
for the 2026 Tree Planting Contract in the amount of $112,759.70.

BACKGROUND:

Public Works requests approval to award a long-term, annually renewed tree-planting contract to
enhance the City’s urban canopy in a cost-effective and efficient manner. The program will
provide strategic replacement and new plantings consistent with City standards and schedules.

The contract was bid in 2026, with bids opened on May 21. One conforming bid was received
from Davey Resource group in the amount of $112,759.70. The vendor meets all specifications
and schedule requirements. We request approval to proceed within the existing program budget
of $145,307.25, which has been reviewed and verbally confirmed with the vendor.
