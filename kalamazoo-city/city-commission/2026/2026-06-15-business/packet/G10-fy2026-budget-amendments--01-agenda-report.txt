SUBJECT:       Resolution Approving Fiscal Year 2026 Budget Amendments

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a resolution approving the attached budget
amendments.

BACKGROUND:

State law and the City's Budget Policies adopted by the City Commission require that the City's
legislatively appropriated expenditures be adequate to cover actual expenditures. Amendments
to the 2026 budget require approval from the City Commission for for expenditures that are
going to exceed the initial appropriation.

Based on these requirements, staff recommends City Commission approve the budget
amendments as attached.

Annual Budget Preparation

Strategic Goal Impact:

        Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and
        resources to effectively implement the community's Strategic Vision in a
        way that is sustainable over the long term.
