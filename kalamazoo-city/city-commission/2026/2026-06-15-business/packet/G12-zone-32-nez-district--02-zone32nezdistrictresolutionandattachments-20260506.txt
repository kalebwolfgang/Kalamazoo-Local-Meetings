                           CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 26-__

                     A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE
       ESTABLISHMENT OF A NEIGHBORHOOD ENTERPRISE ZONE (NEZ)
       PROPERTY AT 825 PORTER STREET & 810 PITCHER STREET IN THE
       NORTHSIDE NEIGHBORHOOD TO BE KNOWN AS THE ZONE-32 NEZ

Minutes of a regular meeting of the Kalamazoo City Commission held on June 15, 2026 at 7:00
p.m., local time, in the City Commission Chambers at City Hall, 241 W. South Street.

PRESENT,                  Commissioners:

ABSENT,                   Commissioners:

WHEREAS, The Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) Program was established by Public
Act 147 of 1992, as amended (the “Act”) to allow local governments to provide a tax
incentive for the development and rehabilitation of residential housing in underdeveloped or
distressed areas where neighborhood revitalization, and the development and rehabilitation of
residential housing might not otherwise occur; and

WHEREAS, the City of Kalamazoo is a qualified local government authorized to
establish one or more NEZ's; and

WHEREAS, the City Commission has established a policy for NEZ’s through
Resolution 23-47 and terms of establishment of a district and the qualifying terms
for the establishment of an NEZ zone

WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that the location of the proposed NEZ District at is
an underdeveloped area with legal description (Attachment A) and map (Attachment B), more
commonly known as 825 Porter St & 810 N Pitcher St, consisting of undeveloped property,
property with surface parking lots, vacant structures, and property in need of Rehabilitation;
and

WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that the neighborhood revitalization,
and development and rehabilitation of residential housing in the Zone 32 NEZ block needed
to spur economic growth may not otherwise occur without the incentive for such that
would be provided by the establishment of a NEZ; and

WHEREAS, the City Commission ﬁnds that the Zone 32 NEZ is compact and contiguous,
contains enough platted parcels and is less than the maximum percentage of acreage required by
the Act, otherwise meets the requirements of the Act for designation as an NEZ; and

WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that the zoning designation of the area within the Zone
32 NEZ, zoned Live-Work 1 (LW-1) per the City of 50-1.2 Zoning Map & 1.8 C of the
Appendix A Zoning Ordinance, is considered a Qualified Downtown Revitalization District per
the Act. The Live-Work 1 Zone per City of Kalamazoo Code 50-3.1 (b) demonstrate that this
zone district is within the boundaries of an area zoned and primarily used for business as defined
by the City of Kalamazoo; and

WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that there is a need in the City of Kalamazoo for
affordable housing and additional housing units which designation of the B-NEZ would help
address; and

WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that its designation of the Zone 32 NEZ is
consistent with the Master Plan, the commercial goals and actions of the Vine
Neighborhood plan, and the neighborhood preservation and economic development goals
of the City of Kalamazoo; and

WHEREAS, City staff has recommended that the designation of the Zone 32 NEZ as an NEZ
District remain in effect for a period of five (5) years from the date of such designation. Any
Certificates approved within that five year period shall be valid for the number of years
approved by the City Commission and in compliance with the terms approved by the City
Commission Nez Policy 23-47; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT the B-NEZ is designated as a
Neighborhood Enterprise Zone pursuant to Act 147 of 1992, as amended for a period of
five years from the date of the adoption of this Resolution; and

AYES, Commissioners:

NAYS, Commissioners:

ABSTAIN, Commissioners:

RESOLUTION DECLARED ADOPTED.
