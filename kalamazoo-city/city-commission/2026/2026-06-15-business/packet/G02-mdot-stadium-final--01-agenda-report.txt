RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
the Michigan Department of Transportation for Contract 39000-206269 – Stadium Drive (from
700ft southwest of Howard Street to Lovell Street) for final accounting in the amount of
$112,396.10.

BACKGROUND:

The Michigan Department of Transportation has completed its final accounting for the Stadium
Drive project (from 700ft southwest of Howard Street to Lovell Street) and determined that the
remaining city share owed is $112,396.10. A budget transfer has been submitted to cover this
cost. We are submitting a requisition to pay this invoice since the original PO has been closed.

Note that for all federal-aid projects handled through the Michigan Department of
Transportation’s Local Agency Program, the contracts and payments are under the control of
MDOT, and all processing is dependent on MDOT’s internal scheduling and procedures. As a
local agency, we have no control over the timing of MDOT audits of projects, which require
actions such as this one.
