RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Division 7 Building
Contractors for section 4 roof replacement at Stockbridge Facility in the amount of $216,390.00

BACKGROUND:

Section 4 of the Administration facility at 415 E Stockbridge has deteriorated and is allowing
water in the building. To proceed with the installation of new roofing, the existing ballast must
be removed, insulation added, and a new roofing membrane installed. The project was submitted
through the standard procurement process, and Division 7 submitted the lowest bid.

Strategic Goal Impact:
