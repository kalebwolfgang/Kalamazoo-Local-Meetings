RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
RW LaPine for additional labor and materials pertaining to the Galvanized Water Pipe Project at
City Hall in the amount of $150,322.00.

BACKGROUND:

When work began to replace the aging galvanized water pipes at City Hall, crews found that
several of the planned routes for the new pipes could not be accessed because of the building’s
layout and the need to keep City Hall open and operational during construction. As a result, the
project team must adjust the installation plan, which will make the work more complex and
increase project costs. During the evaluation, it was also recommended that a water softener be
installed. A water softener helps reduce mineral buildup in the pipes and equipment, which can
extend the life of the new plumbing system and help protect the City’s investment.
