RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Housing Development Fund loan in the
amount of $350,000 to Zone 32 Phase Two, LLC for gap financing to construct affordable
housing units as part of Phase II of the Zone 32 apartment project.

BACKGROUND:

Bogan Development, through Zone 32 Phase Two, LLC, plans to construct a new 38-unit
apartment community in a two-story walk-up building. This project will include a mix of studios,
one-bedroom, and two-bedroom units designed to meet the needs of working households and
families. The new apartment complex will be built adjacent to an existing Bogan Development
apartment (Zone 32, Phase 1) previously supported by the city’s Housing Development Fund.

A total of ten of the 38 units will be designated for households making 60 percent or less of the
Kalamazoo County area median income. Rent and income restrictions will be based on the
Michigan State Housing Development Authority limits established on an annual basis. Annual
reviews of rents and incomes will be conducted to ensure ongoing affordability compliance.

The developer has submitted an application for Housing Development Fund assistance in the
amount of $350,000, which is approximately three percent of the total estimated project costs of
$10.8 million. The proposed terms of the loan are a deferment until January 1, 2032, and then an
annual payment over 20 years at 0% interest.

By increasing the supply of high-quality rental housing on underutilized parcels, this project
helps stabilize the area while providing new housing opportunities for both existing and new

FISCAL IMPACT:

The funds required for this gap financing loan are $350,000, and are bailable within the
Foundation for Excellence (FFE) funded Housing Development Fund budget, which has received
ongoing support for the development of affordable units since 2021. The proposed $350,000 loan
will be repaid over a 20-year term at 0% interest beginning with the first annual payment in
January 2032. Those repaid funds will then revolve back into the community for additional
affordable housing projects and to ensure compliance with existing projects. We are requesting
the approval of this loan using the funds below.
