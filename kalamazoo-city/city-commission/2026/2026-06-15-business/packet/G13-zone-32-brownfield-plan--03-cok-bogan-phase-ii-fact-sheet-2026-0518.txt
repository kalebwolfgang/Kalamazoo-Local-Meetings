                                            Zone 32 Phase II
                                             Project Fact Sheet
Project Overview: The proposed project involves the redevelopment of two parcels, totaling approximately 0.57
acres, located in the City of Kalamazoo. The site will be prepared to accommodate 38 residential rental units,
including 10 studio apartments, 18 one-bedroom apartments, and 10 two-bedroom apartments available for rent.
The development is anticipated to include one ground-floor commercial suite totaling approximately 325 square
feet with a yet-to-be-determined tenant. The project intends to income restrict approximately 26% of the
development for a duration of 13 years. The income restricted units are expected to include 10 studio
apartments, including 2 studio units affordable for households earning approximately 40% of Area Median
Income (AMI) and 8 studio units affordable to households earning approximately 60% of AMI. Construction on the
project is planned to begin in the summer of 2026 and will be completed by fall of 2027.
Parcels included: 810 N Pitcher Street, 06-15-250-031; 825 Porter Street, 06-15-221-060

Eligible Costs
School and Local
▪   Pre-Approved Activities: Phase I Environmental Site Assessment; $3,000.
▪   Infrastructure and Safety Improvements: Sewer and Water Connections, Leads, and Taps, Parking Areas,
    Sidewalks, Gas and Electric Infrastructure, Curbs and Gutters, Stormwater, Landscaping, Fire Protection,
    Street Lighting, Site and Building Security; $420,044.
▪   Site Preparation: Clearing and Grubbing, Compaction and Sub-Base Preparation, Cut and Fill, Geotechnical
    Engineering, Grading, Relocation of Active Utilities, Staking, Temporary Erosion Control, Temporary Facilities,
    Temporary Traffic Control, and Temporary Site Control; $270,500.
▪   Financing Gap: There are anticipated to be 10 income qualified studio units as a part of this development,
    including 2 at 40% of AMI and 8 at 60% of AMI. The income qualified units will be income restricted for a 13-
    year period. The total financing gap requested is $1,151,280.
▪   Brownfield Plan/Work Plan Preparation: Anticipated cost of $30,000.
▪   Brownfield Plan/Work Plan Implementation: Anticipated cost of $50,000.
▪   15% Contingency: Calculated on demolition, infrastructure and safety improvements, and site preparation
    activities. The total contingency cost is anticipated at $103,582.

    Total: $2,028,406

    It is anticipated that a Neighborhood Enterprise Zone (NEZ) Tax Abatement will be requested for the
    development for up to 15 years, a $970,140 financial benefit to the project.

Estimated Outcomes:
▪   Total Investment: $11M
▪   Residential Units Upon Completion: 38 units, 26% income qualified:
        o 2 income-qualified studio units at 40% of AMI
        o 8 income qualified studio units at 60% of AMI
