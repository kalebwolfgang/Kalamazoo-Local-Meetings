RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a one-year sole source agreement with
MJM America Inc. for professional and technical engineering services, in the amount of
$168,042.24, and authorize the City Manager to sign all documents.

BACKGROUND:

The Department of Public Services is charged with the maintenance and enhancement of critical
public infrastructure, encompassing water, wastewater, stormwater, transportation, fleet,
facilities, and other municipal operations. Periodically, specialized expertise or supplementary
staffing resources become essential to address operational demands, complete projects, meet
regulatory requirements, or support service delivery objectives.

MJM America Inc. specializes in professional and technical staffing services and has affirmed its
experience and qualifications to meet the City's requirements. Under the proposed agreement,
services may be procured and authorized through individual purchase orders as specific needs
arise.

This agreement aligns with the City's Strategic Vision by ensuring the Department of Public
Services has access to qualified professional resources necessary to maintain reliable public
infrastructure, deliver high-quality municipal services, and efficiently respond to operational and
project-related needs.

Agreement with MJM America Inc. for the provision of professional and technical engineering
services. This agreement will establish contractual terms, define service expectations, and set
