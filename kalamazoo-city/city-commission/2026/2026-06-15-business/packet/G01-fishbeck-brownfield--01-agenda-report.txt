RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve an 18-month contract extension with
Fishbeck for brownfield redevelopment consulting services in the amount of $110,000.00

BACKGROUND:

Fishbeck provides brownfield redevelopment consulting services to the Brownfield
Redevelopment Authority (BRA) on an as-needed basis. Services include attendance at BRA
meetings; preparation and review of brownfield plans, grants, and work plans; assistance with
due diligence and due care activities; and technical consultation regarding brownfield
redevelopment projects and implementation of Act 381.

The BRA competitively selected Fishbeck through a Request for Proposal process and has
utilized their services to support redevelopment projects throughout the City. The proposed
contract extension would authorize a not-to-exceed amount of $110,000 ($40,000 for 2026 and
$70,000 for 2027). Funding for these services is provided through the BRA Operational Fund
(243-735-00.000-801.000) and does not utilize General Fund resources.

Strategic Goal Impact:

This contract supports Economic Vitality by providing technical expertise necessary to facilitate
brownfield redevelopment projects, leverage public and private investment, and return
underutilized properties to productive use. These services help implement redevelopment tools
that support business growth, housing development, and long-term expansion of the local tax
base.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Appropriate Depth of Engagement
Inform (one-way conversation) – no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

The BRA originally procured these services through a public Request for Proposal process that
included evaluation of qualified respondents by a selection committee. The proposed contract
extension was reviewed by the BRA Board during its public meeting on May 21, 2026, and the
Board voted to recommend approval of the extension. Because this action pertains to the
continuation of services under an existing competitively procured contract, public engagement at
this stage is limited to informing the community through the public meeting and agenda process.
