RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract supplemental/change order with
Tetra Tech, Inc. for the Water Treatment Plant No. 39 Chlorine Upgrades Project in the amount
of $71,500.00 for a total contract of $193,500.00.

BACKGROUND:

Water Treatment Plant No. 39 is a base load station for the East Side High district of the
Kalamazoo Regional Water System. The well was built in 1986 and has unique construction as
it is a Ranney Well instead of a vertical borehole well. The Ranney Well at Treatment Plant No.
39 has a 12-foot center caisson that is approximately 59-feet deep with three horizontal 8-inch
laterals that extend 108-feet to 144-feet from the well and two vertical turbine pumps inside the
caisson.

Chlorine is used for disinfection in drinking water treatment. The chlorine injection system at
Water Treatment Plant No. 39 is in need of upgrades to provide additional residual chlorine in
the distribution system. Tetra Tech completed a review of the water quality and existing
chlorination system as well as the upgrades design. Public Services requested Tetra Tech
provide a proposal for bidding assistance and construction engineering. Tetra Tech provided a
cost of $71,500. Public Services finds this proposal acceptable and recommends approval.
