SUBJECT:        Authorize a new Official Representative and Certifying Officer for the remainder
                of the 2026 HUD Program Year (through June 30, 2027).

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a resolution that:

      1. Authorizes the Community Development Manager to act as the official representative of
         the city and sign all required U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development grant
         documents until June 30, 2027.
      2. Authorizes the Community Development Manager as the Certifying Officer for the
         federally required Environmental Review Record until June 30, 2027.

BACKGROUND:

The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) requires the City Commission
to appoint a signatory for all grant documents and a Certifying Officer for the required National
Environmental Policy Act and other federal laws related to the required Environmental Review
Record. This allows the signatory to execute all contracts with HUD, as well as agreements and
agreement amendments established between the City and our nonprofit partners. It also offers
timely signatures for correspondence, HUD’s Integrated Disbursement and Information System
needs, requests from outside agencies for Consolidated Plan compliance, and other annual
compliance needs. The Certifying Officer must also provide timely signatures on multiple
environmental review documents throughout the year.

This process was completed as part of the 2026 Action Plan and a Resolution was approved by
City Commission designating the Director of Community Planning and Economic Development
(CPED) to this role.

With the expected change of Directors within CPED, staff discussed the need for a substitute
signatory and Certifying Officer with the Detroit field office of HUD. It was recommended that
this position be transitioned to the Community Development Manager within CPED until the end
of the 2026 Program Year (June 30, 2027).

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to effectively
implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.

Designation of a HUD signatory and Certifying Officer primarily aligns with Good Governance
of the Strategic Vision. The goal of Good Governance is to ensure the city organization has the
capacity and resources to effectively implement the community’s Strategic Vision in a way that
is sustainable over the long term. While this goal could be applied in a number of ways, for HUD
activities it is about effective and timely communication between CPED and HUD, our
subrecipients, and the community. Utilizing the Community Development Manager of CPED for
this interim transition period is allowable by HUD and deemed best practice for successful
communication.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Inform (one-way conversation)– no feasible alternatives available due to legal/regulatory
restrictions, standards and best practices, or resource limitations; the community will be made
aware of the project.

Through the public process of adopting the recommended resolution, city residents will be
informed that the City Commission has appointed the Community Development Manager of
CPED as the representative for all HUD related documentation.

FISCAL IMPACT:

No fiscal impact.
