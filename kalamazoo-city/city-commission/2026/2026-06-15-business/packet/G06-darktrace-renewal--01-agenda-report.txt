RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the 1-year cooperative purchase of
DarkTrace Licensing from SHI International in the amount of $169,169.75.

BACKGROUND:

Darktrace is a software provider that detects and responds to cybersecurity threats. By enabling
both detect and respond features, the City of Kalamazoo will not only have visibility into our
digital infrastructure, but also the machine learning ability to respond to anomalies or deviations
from the baseline normal behaviors.
