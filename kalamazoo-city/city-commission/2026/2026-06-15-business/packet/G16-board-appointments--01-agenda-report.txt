BACKGROUND:

Economic Development Corporation and Brownfield Redevelopment Authority

Under state law and city ordinance, the Mayor is responsible for appointing and reappointing
members to the Economic Development Corporation and Brownfield Redevelopment Authority
(EDC/BRA), with the approval of the City Commission. The City Clerk’s Office receives
applications for these boards and forwards them to the staff liaisons. It is then the responsibility
of the various boards to recommend members for appointment and reappointment. I have
reviewed the attached Nomination Report from the EDC/BRA and have reappointed Lucas
Middleton and Michaell Gurnee as recommended. I am now requesting the City Commission
approve these reappointments. The attached Nomination Report details the nominees'
qualifications.

Historic District Commission

The Historic District Commission conducts design review hearings in a quasi-judicial capacity to
decide issues relating to the enforcement of the Historic District ordinance and to permit, within
standards established by the ordinance, exceptions to strict compliance with the terms of the
ordinance in recognized historic districts and as applied to designated landmarks.

City ordinance prescribes that the Mayor appoints and reappoints members of the Historic
District Commission (HDC), with the approval of the City Commission. I have reviewed the
nomination report from the HDC and have appointed Mark Dunham and Elijah Isch and have
reappointed James Johnson and Kristi Breisach. As required by ordinance, I am requesting that
the City Commission approve these appointments and reappointments. The attached Nomination
Report provides details of the nominee's qualifications.
