THIS AGREEMENT entered by and between the City of Kalamazoo, a Michigan Municipal Corporation, 241
West South Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007 (“City”) and Zone 32 Phase Two, LLC, 801 North Pitcher
Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007 (“Developer”).

WHEREAS the City has determined through the approval of the 2025-2029 Consolidated Plan that
affordable housing is a priority for funding which can be supported by the Housing Development Fund
(HDF); and

WHEREAS the City is undertaking certain activities to provide affordable housing, principally for persons
and households of low-income, as fully described in the 2025-2029 Consolidated Plan.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein, including any
attachments or exhibits, and subject to the terms and conditions stated, the City and Developer
understand and agree that:

1. PROJECT DESCRIPTION

A. Upon issuance of a Notice to Proceed as provided in this Agreement, the Developer will construct
Phase li of the Zone 32 housing project, a new thirty-eight (38) unit housing development
(“Project”).

B. The Project will be developed at 825 Porter Street and 810 N. Pitcher, parcel numbers 06-15-221-
060 and 06-15-220-010 and more specifically described in the Mortgage (Attachment A).

C. The Project will include at least ten (10) units for households making 60 percent or less of the
Kalamazoo County area median income established by the Michigan State Housing Development
Authority on an annual basis.

D. Rents for the ten {10) units will be based on annuai rent limits for Kalamazoo County as
established by the Michigan State Housing Development Authority based on number of
bedrooms.

E. The HDF loan will support ten (10) units within the Project for the duration of a twenty (20) year
affordability period.

Vil. == PROJECT REQUIREMENTS

A. Per Unit Subsidy

A total subsidy of $35,000 per unit is permitted under this budget. In order to ensure that no
more HDF funds have been invested than are required to make the development of affordable
housing possible, the City caused the Developer to prepare a pro forma which was reviewed
for subsidy layering and underwriting.

B. Property Standards/Requirements

The Developer shall ensure that all work, which must be done by licensed persons, is done
only by such persons, and shall ensure that all permits, reviews, approvals, and inspections
required by the Kalamazoo City Building Code are obtained and shail ensure the completed
work is approved by an authorized person. Dwelling units may not be occupied until an
authorized Certificate of Occupancy and Rental Certification is issued by the City of
Kalamazoo.

C. Qualifications as Affordable Housing

1. The Developer shall designate the ten (10) HDF-assisted units for the duration of the
twenty (20) year affordability period which shall be comparable to all other units in the
project based on the quality of finishings and all other amenities provided.

2. The ten (10) HDF-assisted units must remain affordable for twenty (20) years. To qualify
as affordable, rents may not exceed those allowable by the Michigan State Housing
Development Authority established annually, less any monthly allowances for the utilities
and/or services to be paid by the tenant.

3. To ensure the affordability period, an Affordable Housing Deed Restriction shall be
recorded with the Kalamazoo County Register of Deeds.

4. Upon disposition or transfer within the affordability period, the project shall not involve
change in land use from residential to nonresidential, or from one class of residential use
to another class of residential use.

5. The Developer may sell the property to any willing buyer at whatever price the market
will bear. However, the sale of the property during the affordability period triggers
repayment of the direct HDF subsidy that the Developer received unless the buyer agrees
to assume the affordability period as outlined in the Affordable Housing Deed Restriction.

If the buyer does not agree to assume the affordability period, the City will recapture the
full HDF subsidy from available net proceeds of the sale. Net proceeds from the sale of
