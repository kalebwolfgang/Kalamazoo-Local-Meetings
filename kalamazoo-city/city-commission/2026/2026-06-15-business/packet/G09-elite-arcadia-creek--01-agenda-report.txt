RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a Professional Services Agreement with
Elite Companies for construction management services of Phase 1, 1A, and Phase 2 renovation
of Arcadia Creek Festival Place in the amount of $562,500.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo is working with design partners and community stakeholders to redesign
Arcadia Creek Festival Place into a dynamic public space that serves residents, workers, and
visitors throughout the year. It has long been known as an event venue, but the park’s full
potential as a vibrant, everyday community space remains untapped. With major downtown
street improvements starting in 2026 and a new event center under construction just a few blocks
away, this redesign presents a unique opportunity to reimagine the festival place and shape the
future of downtown.

In April 2025, the City hosted a multi-day design charrette to gather input from a wide range of
stakeholders, including event organizers, downtown residents & business owners, economic
development professionals, and utility & infrastructure partners. Participants discussed current
challenges, future opportunities, and ways to ensure the park can best support downtown
vibrancy, inclusive community use, and efficient event logistics.

In June 2025, the group reconvened to further refine the original design concepts. A preferred
plan was presented to the community at an open house with the project team. Arcadia Creek
Festival Place has been closed for pre-construction activities related to this project. Reopening is
currently projected for late 2027 or early 2028, depending on the coordination of phases, funding
