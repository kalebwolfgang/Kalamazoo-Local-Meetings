RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accept a grant from the Irving S. Gilmore
Foundation for $50,000 in support of the Downtown Ambassadors program and authorize the
City Manager to sign all related documents.

BACKGROUND:

Downtown Kalamazoo is experiencing an unprecedented transformation of investment and
growth, spurring a 24-hour presence of both visiting and local denizens eager to partake of the
City's burgeoning hospitality and commerce options. As downtown businesses and private
investment continue to expand and attract consumers and citizens, public spaces, facilities, and
works will inevitably require additional daily maintenance and upkeep. Storefronts and streets
must be kept clean and attractive, light landscaping and litter and waste bin clean-up must be
observed, and a general air of clean, hospitable, and safe, and commercial districts and busy
pedestrian areas will require additional personnel and resources to keep them that way.

The Downtown Ambassadors program is built upon a best practice model employed by
successful downtowns and business districts on a national level. Downtown Ambassadors
representatives will navigate our streets and communities sever days per week to ensure that
Downtown Kalamazoo is a clean, safe, and welcoming environment appealing to visitors,
residents, employees, and investors. With the funding help of Irving S. Gilmore Foundation, the
City of Kalamazoo will continue the existing Downtown Ambassador program using the same
model - subcontracting with the "Block by Block" organization to complete the maintenance and
upkeep tasks required on a daily, weekly, and seasonal basis. The Ambassadors program has
long-term, proven impact on the City, keeping downtown secure, friendly, and hygienic for those
living, working, and being entertained within its expanding confines.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Appropriate Depth of Engagement
Consultation/Two-way conversation The community will have a chance to react to and
provide feedback about the program. This program was implemented in direct response to
resident and visitor feedback about the need for cleanliness and safety of the downtown area -
especially as investment significantly expands in the near future. Since originally implemented,
public and private constituents have overwhelmingly supported the program and its outcomes
and voiced their desire for the program to continue and expand.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The City of Kalamazoo will receive $50,000 in 2026 from the Irving S. Gilmore Foundation to
support the Downtown Ambassador Program. The City of Kalamazoo will transfer those funds to
DEGA, who operates the program.

Upon acceptance of the grant, a project code will be created to track the receipt of the funds and
the transfer to the DEGA.
