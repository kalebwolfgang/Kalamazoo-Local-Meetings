                           KALAMAZOO INSTITUTE OF ARTS
                                                  Art is for everyone.

​
    Dear City Clerk Borling, Deputy Clerk Moss or to whom it may concern,

    Please accept this letter in request that the Kalamazoo City Commission adopt a Local Governing
    Body Resolution for Charitable Gaming License for the Kalamazoo Institute of Arts (KIA). This
    resolution has been accepted by the City in the past, however, the Michigan Charitable Gaming
    Division is requesting that the KIA re-apply for qualification to be eligible for Charitable Gaming
    Licensure as it has been a number of years since we last applied.

    The Kalamazoo Institute of Arts’ mission is to cultivate the creation and appreciation of the visual
    arts in West Michigan. Founded in 1924, the KIA is a non-profit organization and a cornerstone of
    the arts and culture community in Kalamazoo. The KIA holds a permanent collection of nearly 5,000
    works of art, presents touring and collection exhibitions in our 10 galleries; offers four terms of art
    classes at the Kirk Newman Art School along with other educational programming; and houses the
    KIA Gallery Shop, which features artwork by local and international artisans.

    The KIA is requesting this resolution so that we may hold a large, in-house raffle at our upcoming
    event Future: REMIX which will be held on March 20th, 2026. This will be a ticketed event inspired
    by our upcoming exhibition, Future Now: Virtual Sneakers to Cutting Edge Kicks. The event will
    feature DJs, local food and beverages, and all galleries will be open for guests to explore. Guests in
    attendance will be eligible to purchase raffle tickets and winners will be announced during the
    event. A minimum of three winners will be drawn with the top prize being a voucher for an art class
    at the Kirk Newman Arts School with a value of up to $380.00.

    All funds raised from the raffle will be put towards the KIA’s general operating fund which makes it
    possible for the institution to live its belief that the arts are for everyone, ensuring a robust slate of
    free and accessible programming for the Kalamazoo community. Thank you for considering this
    request, please reach out with any questions. The Charitable Gaming Division does require 4 to 6
    weeks to review all applications and requests so your swift attention to this request is greatly
    appreciated.

    Sincerely,

    Nancy Waldoch
