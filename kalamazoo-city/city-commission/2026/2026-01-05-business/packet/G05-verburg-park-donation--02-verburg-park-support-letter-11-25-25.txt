KALAMAZOO

Friends of
Recreation

November 25, 2025

Dear Ashton,

We understand the City of Kalamazoo Parks and Recreation Department is renovating Verburg
Park and there may be a need for additional funding to complete this project. This is an
important part of the mission of the Kalamazoo Friends of Recreation, and we value our
partnership with the City of Kalamazoo Parks & Rec department.

With that in mind, the Kalamazoo Friends of Recreation Board has agreed to provide financial
support based on cost estimates for the park project. On behalf of the board, we approve up to
$30,000 for the Verburg Park project to act solely as a contingency fund if the project expenses
exceed what is currently allocated through the City of Kalamazoo for this project. In addition,
we would appreciate appropriate recognition at Verburg Park acknowledging KFOR’s support,
should it be needed.

Please let me know if you have any questions or if further discussion is required.
