SUBJECT:       Approval of the FY 2026 Northside Cultural Business District Authority
               (NCBDA) Operating Budget

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the proposed FY 2026 Northside Cultural
Business District Authority (NCBDA) Operating Budget

BACKGROUND:

The City resolution, which established the Northside Cultural Business District Authority
(NCBDA), requires that the NCBDA annually submit its recommended budget to the City
Commission for adoption.

2026 Revenues: Revenues include a tax increment financing capture.

2026 Expenses: Expenses include professional development, professional and contractual
services and advertising/community engagement.

There are sufficient revenues and reserves to cover the expenditures requested in the budgets.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

This program/project has a high degree of impact on the following Strategic Goal(s):
         Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to
         effectively implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the
         long term.
