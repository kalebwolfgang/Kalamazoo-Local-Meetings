RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the proposed FY 2026 Brownfield
Redevelopment Authority (BRA) budget as submitted by the BRA Board.

BACKGROUND:

The BRA Board annually prepares and approves a budget for the operation of the Authority and
the Local Brownfield Revolving Fund (LBRF) for the upcoming fiscal year. The City
Commission is not required by law to adopt the annual appropriation for the BRA; however, the
City Administration has chosen to request City Commission approval. There are sufficient
revenues and reserves to cover the expenditures requested in the budgets.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

Strategic Goal Impact:

         the local economy for the benefit of all.
