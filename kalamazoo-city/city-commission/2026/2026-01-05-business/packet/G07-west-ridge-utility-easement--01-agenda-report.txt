SUBJECT:       West Ridge Site Condo Utility Easement

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve and accept the easements with Green
Development Ventures, LLC for water main installed as part of the West Ridge Site Condo
Development; and authorize the City Manager to sign all related and necessary documents
regarding the acquisition of the easements.

BACKGROUND:

Construction of new water main is underway for the West Ridge Site Condo Development. As
all public water mains and sanitary sewer are required to be in a dedicated easement or public
right of way, Green Development Ventures, LLC in collaboration with the City of Kalamazoo
Attorney has drafted these agreements for approval.
