                               CITY OF KALAMAZOO, MICHIGAN

                                    RESOLUTION NO. ________

    A RESOLUTION TO MAKE THE ANNUAL APPROPRIATION FOR THE YEAR 2026,
    TO ADOPT THE BUDGET, TO ESTABLISH THE GENERAL PROPERTY TAX LEVY,
         TO AUTHORIZE INTERIM APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE YEAR 2027,
           AND TO DEFINE ADMINISTRATIVE AND FINANCIAL POLICIES
                  FOR THE CITY BUDGET FOR THE YEAR 2026

At a regular meeting of the City Commission of the City of Kalamazoo, Kalamazoo County held at 241
W South Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan, 49007 on the 5th day of January, 2026 at 7:00 o'clock in the
evening, local time.

PRESENT, Commissioners:

ABSENT, Commissioners:

WHEREAS, the City Charter of Kalamazoo, Sections 67, 68 and 85 and MCL 141.434 of the Uniform
Budgeting and Accounting Act required that the City Manager submit to the City Commission an
estimate of revenues and expenditures for the upcoming fiscal year by December 1 of each year, that the
City Commission adopts this annual general appropriation resolution by February 1 of each year, and that
the City Commission has the authority to review the annual general appropriation resolution following
receipt of the assessment roll;

WHEREAS, in accordance with MCL 141.436 of the Uniform Budgeting and Accounting Act, as
amended, the City of Kalamazoo is required to adopt a General Appropriations Act that sets forth:

    •   the total number of ad valorem property tax mills to be levied;
    •   the amounts to be appropriated to meet public expenditures and liabilities;
    •   and the estimated revenues by source for the general fund and each special revenue fund,
        ensuring that all budgets are balanced in revenues and expenditures and comply with the uniform
        chart of accounts as prescribed by the Michigan Department of Treasury;
WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Kalamazoo desires to establish certain policies covering
City Administration of the budget for 2026

WHEREAS, pursuant to MCL 141.422b(3) of the Uniform Budgeting and Accounting Act, as amended,
the City Manager shall serve as the “Chief Administrative Officer” (CAO) of the City of Kalamazoo and
shall be responsible for the execution and enforcement of the annual adopted General Appropriation Act;

WHEREAS, a public hearing was conducted on the proposed budget in accordance with Public Act 43 of
1963 (MCL 141.411 to 141.415), and consistent with the requirements of the Open Meetings Act, Public
Act 267 of 1976 (MCL 15.261 to 15.275), to ensure transparency, proper public notice, and the
opportunity for public input prior to final adoption;

WHEREAS, in compliance with MCL 141.439 of the Uniform Budgeting and Accounting Act, as
amended, the CAO may not authorize the expense of public funds except in accordance with this
appropriations act, but may execute transfers within the limits between appropriations;

WHEREAS, in accordance with the Fiscal Stabilization Act, Public Act 80 of 1981 (MCL 141.1001 to
141.1011), and related budgetary best practices outlined by the Michigan Department of Treasury, the
City of Kalamazoo shall ensure that total estimated expenditures, including any accrued deficit, do not
exceed total estimated revenues, including any available surplus, and the proceeds or principal balance of
any fiscal stabilization bonds or obligations legally issued;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED, that subject to the rights of the
City Commission provided in Section 85 of the City Charter, the budget attached hereto and made a
part hereof by reference is hereby taken up, considered, passed, and adopted as the annual
appropriation of the City of Kalamazoo to cover the expenditures and operations thereof for the year
2026; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that upon the confirmation of the assessment rolls and subject to such
revision as the City Commission may have made on this budget upon said review, and subject to the
procedures of MCL 211.24(e) of the General Property Tax Act being fulfilled, if required, the City Clerk
is thereupon authorized and directed to deliver a copy of this annual appropriation resolution, together
with such revisions as may have been made therein, and a warrant from the City Commission to the City
Treasurer authorizing and directing the City Treasurer to collect from persons named in said assessment
roll the taxes applicable thereto as provided in the City Charter and statutes in such cases made and
provided.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the total number of mills to be levied under the General Property
Tax Act and the purpose for which that millage is levied for purposes of said budget and annual
appropriation, are as follows

            a. City Operating: 12.0000 mills
            b. Solid Waste: 1.8000 mills

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the estimated city General Fund revenues, including an allocated
millage of 12.000 mills or less pursuant to Headlee rollback for General Fund; voter-authorized millage of
2.8047 mills or less pursuant to Headlee rollback for Solid Waste Fund, Special Revenue Funds, and
Enterprise Funds operating shall total $216,223,620.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the City Commission adopts the 2026 fiscal year general fund
budget for revenues by source, and expenditures by activity. City officials responsible for the

                                                                                                  Page 103 of 500

expenditures authorized in the budget may expend city funds up to, but not to exceed, the total
appropriation authorized for each activity and may make transfers among the various line items contained
in the activity appropriation. Transfers of appropriations between activities, within the same fund, may
occur with approval by the Chief Financial Officer. No transfers of appropriations for line items related to
personnel may be made without prior approval by the Chief Financial Officer.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that if the budget is not adopted by December 31, the Chief Financial
Officer is authorized to continue payroll and necessary operations using the prior year’s appropriation
until the new budget is adopted.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that only the City Commission shall make new or additional
appropriations. Budgets may be amended after adoption with approval of the City Commission if changes
result in an increase in appropriation. Budget amendment requests must be made in writing and approval
is required from the appropriate department director, the Chief Financial Officer, and the City Manager.
The City Manager ultimately requests approval from the City Commission.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the salaries of appointees and creation of positions, shall be made
only by resolution or motion of the City Commission. The Chief Financial Officer is authorized to revise
any payroll, fringe benefits, and other expenses paid by the City to conform to the terms of any labor
contracts or other commitments, which have been approved by the City Commission.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the City Manager is authorized to accept grants and donations under
$25,000. A summary of grants and donations accepted by the City Manager will be presented to the City
Commission twice a year as an informational item. All grants and donations over $25,000 are presented to
City Commission at the time of acceptance for approval. The acceptance of grants shall be considered as
authority to expend funds for those purposes.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that all fees, fines, and service charges outlined in the budget
document and fee schedule are hereby adopted. Updates to fees during the fiscal year require Commission
approval.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that all previously approved capital improvement projects not
completed as of December 31, shall be presented to the City Commission only as an informational item at
a regular City Commission meeting. Carry forward requests for non-capital items, shall be submitted to
the City Commission for approval.
