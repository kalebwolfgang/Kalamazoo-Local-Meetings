RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission Adopt a Resolution to adopt the City of Kalamazoo
Safe Streets for All Safety Action Plan in continuation of work that began in 2024 with the
Approval of a contract with Alta Planning & Design, Inc. for the development of a Safe Streets
for All (SS4A) Intersection Upgrade and Pedestrian Safety Plan (Bid Ref# 92593-003.0)

BACKGROUND:

The Safe Streets for All (SS4A) Federal grant program helps local communities develop a
Comprehensive Safety Action Plan to guide transportation safety improvements and helps
unlock federal funding in the future to make successful safety improvements permanent. In 2023,
the City of Kalamazoo was awarded a $750,000 Safe Streets for All Grant (SS4A) from the U.S.
Department of Transportation. This was utilized to implement pilot safety projects throughout
Kalamazoo and work with a team of Consultants on drafting a Safety Action Plan for the City of
Kalamazoo. The Plan is designed to be actionable and strategic, with a clear focus on four high-
impact areas related to improving safety for all users of our transportation network:

      • Intersections,
      • sidewalks,
      • Street lighting, and
      • bus stops
