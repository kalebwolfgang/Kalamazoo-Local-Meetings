SUBJECT:       Approval of the FY 2026 Downtown Economic Growth Authority (DEGA)
               Operating Budget

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the proposed FY 2026 Downtown
Economic Growth Authority (DEGA) Operating Budget.

BACKGROUND:

The City resolution, which established the Downtown Economic Growth Authority (DEGA),
requires that the DEGA annually submit its recommended budget to the City Commission for
adoption.

2026 Revenues: The DEGA budget is funded by a TIF Capture, contributions and sponsorships,
and other revenue.

2026 Expenses: Expenses include event operating expenses, personnel, and Debt Service/Other
Obligations.

The FY 2026 DEGA budget includes a new position (Downtown Project Assistant). This
position will be a City employee and is listed as one of the positions being proposed in the 2026
budget. A memorandum of understanding will be approved by both the DEGA Board and City
Commission detailing the funding for and expectations of this position.

There are sufficient revenues and reserves to cover the expenditures requested in the budgets.
