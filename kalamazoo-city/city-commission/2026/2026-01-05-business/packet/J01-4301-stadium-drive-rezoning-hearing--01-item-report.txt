RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission set a public hearing for January 26, 2026 to receive
comments on an ordinance to rezone the property at 4301 Stadium Drive from Residential,
Multi-Dwelling (RM-15) and Residential, Single-Dwelling (RS-5) to Community Commercial
(CC).

BACKGROUND:

4301 Stadium Drive is an 18.8-acre parcel along the western border of the City of Kalamazoo at
the corner of Drake Road and Stadium Drive. The parcel is wooded and slopes downwards
towards the south-east with some ridges and slopes. The 18.8 acres is currently dual-zoned with
Residential Multi-Dwelling (RM-15) zoning along the Stadium Drive frontage, and the majority
of the area is zoned Residential Single-Dwelling (RS-5). The property is also zoned with the
Natural Features Protection Overlay (NFP-O). The applicant is requesting that the property be
rezoned from its current residential districts to the Community Commercial (CC) District.

Rezoning this property has been requested previously. In January 2020, the property owner
requested a rezoning of the property to the Community Commercial (CC) District from its
current residential zoning, which applied to the then three parcels, and requested the removal of
the NFP-O District. The Planning Commission reviewed the requests at a public meeting and
provided an unfavorable recommendation on the requests to the City Commission for their
consideration. However, at the applicant's request, the case did not advance to the City
Commission, and no action was taken by the legislative review body on that request. In
September 2023, the applicant again applied to rezone the property from the RM-15 and RS-5
Districts to the CC District, but this time without the removal of the NFP-O District. That
application was withdrawn before the public hearing by the Planning Commission.

This fall, following their application, the applicant held an informational meeting at their request.
The meeting occurred on Thursday, October 30, 2025, at Anna Whitten Hall, Downtown KVCC
campus. This meeting was held following the City's Public Participation Policy (PPP). As
provided in the PPP, the applicant sent notice to properties within 300 feet of 4301 Stadium
Drive and to the Winchell Neighborhood Association. Before the Planning Commission heard
this case, City staff received a volume of written comments on the request to rezone. The
Planning Commission held a public hearing on the rezoning request at City Hall on November
6th, 2025. At this meeting, City staff provided an overview of the application and recommended
that the Commission approve the request, outlining how it met the zoning criteria used to
consider rezoning requests.

There was a lengthy public comment period with speakers against the request and a full City
Commission Chambers of interested residents. After the public comment period, the Planning
Commission discussed the request, its application, and testimony provided during the public
hearing. The Planning Commission made their unanimous vote not to recommend support of the
request to rezone the property to the City Commission. All rezoning requests go first to the
Planning Commission, who provide a recommendation (positive or negative) to the City
Commission. The City Commission enters the item into the record with the first reading and sets
a public hearing date for consideration. At the scheduled public hearing, the City Commission
will hear presentations from staff and the applicant and public input from the community before
considering the request.

At the December 1st City Commission meeting to set the public hearing, there was discussion
amongst the Commission on the venue location, capacity, accessibility, speaking times and other
logistical items for the public hearing after receiving public comment and in consideration of the
high attendance at the November 6th Planning Commission meeting.
