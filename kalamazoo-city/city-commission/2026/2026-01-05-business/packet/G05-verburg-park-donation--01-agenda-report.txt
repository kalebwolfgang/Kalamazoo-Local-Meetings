RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accept a donation, and approve a budget
amendment, in the amount of up to $30,000 from Kalamazoo Friends of Recreation for
contingency funding to complete the Verburg Park Improvement Project.

BACKGROUND:

Verburg Park was called out as one of 10 Priority Parks to focus specific attention to in our most
recent Parks and Recreation Master Plan. Planning for improvements to this park began several
years ago. However, in 2024 we engaged the community on what they would like to see in the
park, combined with elements from the department master plan to develop the design we are
ready to implement.

Pricing for this park project came in close to the actual budget so Kalamazoo Friends of
Recreation has pledged up to $30,000 to act as contingency funding to ensure this project gets
completed within budget without reducing the scope.
