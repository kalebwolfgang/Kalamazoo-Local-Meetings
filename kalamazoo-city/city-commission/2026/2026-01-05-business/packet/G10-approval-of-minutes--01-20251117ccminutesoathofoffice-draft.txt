City Clerk Borling presented the following report from the Kalamazoo County          2025-48-05
Board of Canvassers:                                                                 Report of the Board of
                                                                                     Canvassers
    The Kalamazoo County Board of Canvassers has certified the following results
    from the Municipal Election held on November 4, 2025: having received
    6,473 votes, David Anderson was elected Mayor; having received 5,820 votes,
    Drew Duncan was elected City Commissioner and is, by charter, the Vice-
    Mayor; having received 4,914 votes, Stephanie D. Hoffman was elected City
    Commissioner; and having received 4,588 votes, Jacqueline Slaby was elected
    City Commissioner.

The report of the Board of Canvassers was received into the record.

At 5:50 p.m. Mayor Anderson adjourned the meeting sine die.                          Adjournment Sine Die

City Clerk Borling administered the Constitutional Oath of Office to newly elected   Oath of Office Ceremony
City Commissioners Jacqueline Slaby and Stephanie Hoffman; newly elected Vice
Mayor Drew Duncan; and newly elected Mayor David Anderson.

Prior to taking the oath of office, each newly elected official was given the
opportunity to have a person of their choosing present to them and affix a pin
bearing the City Seal.
