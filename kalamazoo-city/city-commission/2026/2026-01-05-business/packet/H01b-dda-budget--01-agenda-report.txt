SUBJECT:       Approval of the FY 2026 Downtown Development Authority (DDA) Operating
               Budget, and the existing two-mill DDA Tax Levy

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the proposed FY 2026 Downtown
Development Authority (DDA) Operating Budget, and the existing two-mill DDA Tax Levy.

BACKGROUND:

The City ordinance, which established the Downtown Development Authority, requires that the
DDA annually submit its recommended budget to the City Commission for adoption.

The Downtown Development Authority Operating Budget is mostly funded by the proposed
DDA millage of 1.9638 (which is the original authorized 2 Mills and reduced by the Headlee
Amendment).

There are sufficient revenues and reserves to cover the expenditures requested in the budgets.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

This program/project has a high degree of impact on the following Strategic Goal(s):
                Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to
                effectively implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable
                over the long term.
This proposed budget demonstrates the ability of the DDA to provide downtown operating services
planned for FY 2025.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Consult (two-way conversation) the community will be made aware of the budget through the
hearing process.

The Downtown Development Authority Board has reviewed the budget request and is
recommending City Commission approval of the FY 2025 Proposed Budget as well as
requesting approval of the two-mill tax levy.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The approval of the two-mill tax levy and the proposed budget will provide the DDA with the
authority to adopt and implement the budget and related activities of the DDA during FY 2026.
