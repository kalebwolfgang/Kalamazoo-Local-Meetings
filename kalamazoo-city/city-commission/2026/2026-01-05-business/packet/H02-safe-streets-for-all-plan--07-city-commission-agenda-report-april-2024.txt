RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with Alta Planning & Design,
Inc. for the development of Safe Streets for All (SS4A) Intersection Upgrade and Pedestrian
Safety Plan in the amount of $149,299.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo has long sought for safer streets. The City has a higher crash-per-capita
ratio when compared with other major Michigan cities, with almost one additional crash per day,
or 365 crashes per year. With 16,747 total crashes and 26 fatal crashes in the last five years;
safety on our streets is needed more than ever before. As a City, we have an average of over 9
crashes per day in the City limits alone; totaling more nearly 3,350 crashes a year.

In 2022, the City applied for and was awarded the Safe Streets for All (SS4A) Supplementary
Safety Action Plan grant. This grant allows the City to develop a Safety Action Plan which
emphasizes safer use of streets for vulnerable road users (VRU’s) & all who would travel.

The primary result of the SS4A for the City of Kalamazoo is the development of a Safety Action
Plan. City staff broke down the effort into two main efforts: 1) development of key safety sub-
plans and 2) an effort to assemble these safety sub-plans with existing City plans to create a
cohesive safety action plan that can be used in future infrastructure projects and grant
applications.
Completing this key Safety Action Plan would provide the City with the needed way-forward to
effective safety changes to streets; cutting down on the excessive, costly, and life-impacting
crashes in the City. Additionally, the plan would give the City the necessary requirements to
compete for grant funding for SS4A implementation grants and other federal and state grants

This solicitation was issued through the City's competitive RFP process and received seven (7)

evaluation committee. Alta Planning & Design, Inc. has extensive experience in the design and
planning of projects utilizing the Safe System approach and has extensive experience with Safe
Streets for All (SS4A) programs. Additionally, the firm provided a very thorough work and price
proposal with the capacity to complete the work within the grant timing. This will provide the
City with the necessary planning effort to continue to make streets safer and viable places of
transportation for all users.
