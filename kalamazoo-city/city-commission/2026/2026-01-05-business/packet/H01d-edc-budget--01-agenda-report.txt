RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the proposed FY 2026 Economic
Development Corporation (EDC) budget as submitted by the EDC Board.

BACKGROUND:

The EDC board annually prepares and adopts a fiscal year budget. The City Commission is not
required by law to adopt the annual appropriation for the EDC; however the City Administration
has chosen to request City Commission approval. There are sufficient revenues and reserves to
cover the expenditures requested in the budgets.

STRATEGIC VISION ALIGNMENT:

This program/project has a high degree of impact on the following Strategic Goal(s):
            Good Governance- Ensuring the City organization has the capacity and resources to
            effectively implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over
            the long term.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT:

Consult (two-way conversation) – the community will have a chance to react to the project
through two-way conversation.

The Executive Committee of the EDC reviewed the FY 2025 Proposed Budget.
