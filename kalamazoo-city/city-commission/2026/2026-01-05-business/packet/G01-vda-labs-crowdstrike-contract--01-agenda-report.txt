RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the purchase of a sole source three-year
agreement with VDA Labs for Crowdstrike Falcon MSSP for Cyber Security protection in the
amount of $274,483.50

BACKGROUND:

Crowdstrike is used for its powerful cloud-native, AI-powered cybersecurity platform that offers
rapid and effective threat detection and prevention. Adding a Managed Security Service Provider
(MSSP) provides continuous, expert management of the platform, effectively acting as an
outsourced 24/7 security team.

Strategic Goal Impact:

implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
