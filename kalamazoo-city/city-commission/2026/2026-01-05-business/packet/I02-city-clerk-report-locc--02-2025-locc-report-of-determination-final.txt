                                               R EPORT O F D E T E R M I N AT I O N
                                                                                      DECEMBER, 2025

The Local Officers’ Compensation Commission (LOCC) has determined that the compensation for the
Mayor, Vice Mayor, and City Commissioners of the City of Kalamazoo shall be increased to the following
levels: Mayor—$11,700 per year; Vice Mayor—$9,900 per year; and City Commissioners—$8,700 per
year.

In making this determination, the LOCC took into account four factors:

            the philosophical basis for the compensation of elected officials in Kalamazoo

            the cost of living since the end of 2023, when current compensation levels were established

            the general state of the economy and the City’s improved short and mid-term fiscal outlook

            the level of compensation for elected officials in the City of Kalamazoo as compared to oth-
              er cities in Michigan

First, the LOCC reaffirmed its position on the philosophical basis for compensating elected officials in the
City of Kalamazoo. The LOCC views the compensation for Kalamazoo officials as a stipend or a gratuity
designed to defray the expense associated with the responsibilities of being an elected official.

Second, the LOCC considered Consumer Price Index (CPI) data from the U.S. Department of Labor for
the Midwest Region. The LOCC continues to be concerned about maintaining the purchasing power of
elected official’s compensation due to the history of erosion of that compensation by inflation. The sum-
mary Kalamazoo City Commission Compensation Rates Compared to CPI: December 2023 — October
2025 is included in this report by reference.

Third, the current levels of compensation for elected officials in Kalamazoo are relatively comparable to the
compensation for elected officials in other Michigan cities of similar size. The chart Compensation Rates
for Various Michigan Municipalities, December 2025 is included in this report by reference.

In closing, it is the unanimous opinion of the LOCC that the dedication and service of the Mayor, Vice
Mayor, and City Commissioners of the City of Kalamazoo are highly valued and greatly appreciated.
