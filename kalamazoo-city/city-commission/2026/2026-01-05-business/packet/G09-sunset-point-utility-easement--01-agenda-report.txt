SUBJECT:       Sunset Point Utility Easement

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve and accept the easements with SHOJ, LLC
for water main installed as part of the Sunset Point Development; and authorize the City
Manager to sign all related and necessary documents regarding the acquisition of the easements.

BACKGROUND:

Construction of new water main is underway for the Sunset Point Development. As all public
water mains and sanitary sewer are required to be in a dedicated easement or public right of way,
SHOJ, LLC in collaboration with the City of Kalamazoo Attorney has drafted these agreements
for approval.
