BACKGROUND:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the following budget amendments and
authorize the City Chief Financial Officer to execute intra-fund budget transfers as needed to
ensure year-end compliance. The State of Michigan requires budgetary compliance at the activity
level rather than the fund level. Granting this authority will allow departments to transfer
available budget within their respective activities without changing any fund’s overall budget.

This authority had been provided to the CFO/City Manager in prior years; however, it was not
explicitly included in the original Budget Resolution memo following the shift in compliance
requirements from the fund level to the activity level.

Details of all adjustments are included in the attachments.
