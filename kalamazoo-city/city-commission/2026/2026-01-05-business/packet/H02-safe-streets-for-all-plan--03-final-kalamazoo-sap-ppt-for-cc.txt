Key Recommendations

Overview of Plan Recommendations:
Infrastructure
Intersection Safety: Add curb extensions, high-visibility crosswalks, leading pedestrian
intervals, and median refuge islands.

Lighting Improvements: Upgrade fixtures and spacing along top 10 high-crash
corridors.

Sidewalk + Crossing Gaps: Close missing links on arterial and collector streets
prioritized for connectivity and equity.

Transit Access: Add ADA pads, shelters, and crosswalks at priority bus stops.

Quick-Build Opportunities: Pilot safety measures at key intersections before full
reconstruction.                                                                     Page 183 of 500
