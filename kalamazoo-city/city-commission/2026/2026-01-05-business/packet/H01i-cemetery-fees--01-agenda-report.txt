SUBJECT:       Resolution Establishing Cemetery Fees and Charges for 2026

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission adopt a RESOLUTION to establish the Cemeteries'
fees and charges for 2026

BACKGROUND:

Cemetery fees provide funding for operations and ongoing perpetual care of City-owned
cemeteries. These fees provide funding for burials, grounds maintenance and mowing, tree
trimming and removal, headstone foundation placement and trash disposal. The 2026 rates
remain include an approximate 5% increase to maintain the properties and related infrastructure.

The Cemetery master plan details the capital improvements and ongoing operations costs needed
to continue to fund cemetery operations at the City's two cemeteries spanning over 112 acres.
 Philanthropic support through grants have supported many projects at the Mountain Home
Cemetery. However, the Riverside Cemetery continues to experience level of service, condition
and age-related infrastructure investment needs in the roadway, storm sewer, retaining walls and
facilities.

         long term.
