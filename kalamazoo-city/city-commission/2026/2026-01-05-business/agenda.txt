     (Action: Motion to approve items “1-10” and authorize the City Manager to sign all

     1.     Approval of a sole source three-year contract with VDA Labs for Crowdstrike
            Falcon MSSP for Cyber Security protection in the amount of $274,483.50.

     2.     Adoption of a RESOLUTION recognizing the Kalamazoo Institute of Arts as a
            non-profit organization operating in the community for the purpose of obtaining a
            charitable gaming license.

     3.     Adoption of a RESOLUTION approving changes in the street lighting contract
            with Consumers Energy for the removal of a street light at 459 N Rose.

             4.     Adoption of a RESOLUTION approving Fiscal Year 2025 year-end budget
                    amendments and authorizing the City Chief Financial Officer to make intra-fund
                    budget transfers.

             5.     Acceptance of a donation in the amount of up to $30,000 from Kalamazoo Friends
                    of Recreation for Verburg Park renovations and approval of a budget amendment

      The Kalamazoo City Commission’s business meetings are held on the first and third Mondays at 7:00

      Channel.
