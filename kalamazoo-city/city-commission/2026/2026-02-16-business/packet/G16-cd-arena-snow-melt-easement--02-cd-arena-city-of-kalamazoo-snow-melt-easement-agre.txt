                EASEMENT AGREEMENT FOR SNOW MELT SYSTEM

       This Easement Agreement for Snow Melt System (this “Agreement”) dated as of
                      , 2026 is made by and between the CITY OF KALAMAZOO, a Michigan
municipal corporation, having an address of 241 West South Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007
(the “City”) and CD ARENA, LLC, having an address of 100 W. Michigan Avenue, Suite 300,
Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007 (the “Company”).

                                       BACKGROUND:

       A.     The Company owns certain real property (the “Benefitted Property”) situated in
downtown Kalamazoo, upon which Company plans to construct a state-of-the-art landmark
development, designed to host collegiate and professional sporting events, concerts, and
community gatherings, together with associated facilities and amenities for students and
professional athletes, performers, spectators and the general public (the “Arena”). The legal
description of the Benefitted Property is attached as Exhibit A. The Benefitted Property is bounded
on each of its four sides by the City-controlled right-of-ways that contain West Kalamazoo
Avenue, West Water Street, North Park Steet and North Westnedge Avenue (collectively, the
“City Streets”).

       B.   The Company developed detailed plans for the Arena project (the “Plans”), which
were considered and approved by action of the City’s municipal planning authority.

       C.     The design of the Arena proposed by Company and approved by the City in the Plans
includes an underground system designed to prevent accumulation of snow and ice on sidewalks
and certain other areas around the periphery of the Arena. The system may include, without
limitation, boilers, heat exchangers, pipes, tubes, working fluids, electrical conduit, electrical
wiring, controllers, sensors, switches, valves, insulation, shielding, underground vaults, manholes
and manhole covers, and other related improvements (collectively, the “Snow Melt System”).
The Snow Melt System will be constructed partially on the Benefitted Property, and partially
within those areas of the City Streets as shown on Exhibit B (the “Easement Area”).

       D.     The Easement Area is located entirely within the public right-of-ways dedicated to
the City Streets. Pursuant to Michigan law and the City's Charter, the City has absolute control of
the City Streets and the underlying real property upon which the City Streets are constructed.

Therefore, Company has requested that the City grant certain easements for the Easement Area
on, over, under and through the City Streets.

       E.     The granting of an easement in support of the innovative and iconic design of the
Arena is aligned with the interests of the City and the general public.

                                         AGREEMENT:

      THEREFORE, in consideration of the above and the mutual covenants, conditions and
agreements set forth below, the parties agree as follows:

        1.     Recitals. The statements in the Background section above are true and correct and
are incorporated herein by this reference.

       2.      Grant of Easements. Subject to and in accordance with the terms of this
Agreement, the City grants to the Company an exclusive and perpetual easement appurtenant to
the Benefitted Property (the “Snow Melt Easement”) to construct, operate, use, maintain, repair,
and replace the Snow Melt System, over, across, within and through the Easement Area as
described with more specificity on Exhibit C.

       3.     Construction and Repair of Snow Melt System.

              3.1.   Construction of Snow Melt System. Company may use the Easement Area
       for the construction of the Snow Melt System, including any related temporary
       improvements necessary for the construction of the Snow Melt System within the
       Easement Area, pursuant to construction plans materially consistent with the Plans.

               3.2.     Entry on City Streets and Sidewalks. The Snow Melt Easement includes
       the right for the Company to enter upon the right-of-way of the City Streets in connection
       with the construction, operation, maintenance and repair of the Snow Melt System. Upon
       prior notice to the City and coordination with the City, Company may close portions of the
       City Streets and/or the sidewalks adjacent to the City Streets in the Easement Area and
       adjacent thereto during construction, repair or replacement of the Snow Melt System. The
       costs for any right-of-way permits, street closures, detours, and other obstructions to right-
       of-way access are Company's responsibility.

              3.3.    ‘As-Built’ Adjustment to Easement Area. Upon completion of
       construction of the Arena and the Snow Melt System, Company will promptly provide the
       City with 'as built plans' detailing the actual dimensions and elevations of the Snow Melt
       System as constructed; and if those plans alter any of the dimensions of the Easement Area
       from what is described in this Agreement, the parties agree to record an amendment to this
       Agreement to accurately reflect the Easement Area as established by the 'as built plans.'

               3.4.   Maintenance of Snow Melt System. As between the City and the
       Company, Company shall be solely responsible for the construction, maintenance and
       repair, replacement and operation of the Snow Melt System. Upon completion of such

construction, maintenance, repairs or replacement, Company shall restore the surface of
any affected area pursuant to the Plans, or, if the Plans are not applicable, to the prevailing
conditions of the impacted property as it existed immediately prior to the construction,
maintenance, repairs or replacement of the Snow Melt System.

        3.5.    Duration of Snow Melt System Operations; City Option to Assume
Responsibility. Company shall operate and maintain the Snow Melt System, as set forth
in this Agreement, for a period of not less than 20 years from the date of this Agreement.
Upon the expiration of the 20-year period, or Company’s decision to abandon and no longer
operate and maintain the Snow Melt System, whichever later occurs, this Snow Melt
Easement shall terminate, and Company shall have no further obligation to operate,
maintain, repair or replace the Snow Melt System, and Company may record a notice of
abandonment terminating this Agreement in the public records of Kalamazoo County,
Michigan; provided, however, that Company must provide the City prior written notice of
its decision to abandon the Snow Melt System not less than twelve (12) months before the
date of abandonment. If, after receipt of such notice from Company, the City should desire
to assume responsibility for the operation and maintenance of the Snow Melt System, the
parties shall use the twelve (12) month notice period to engage in good faith negotiations
to transfer some or all of the components of the Snow Melt System to the City, at a nominal
cost, to enable the City to continue operating and maintaining the Snow Melt System,
provided that such continued operation does not unreasonably interfere with Company’s
use of the Benefitted Property.

         3.6.   Abandonment of Snow Melt System. In the event that Company decides
to abandon the Snow Melt System, and the City elects not to assume responsibility for
further operation and maintenance, Company shall be responsible to decommission all
components of the Snow Melt System within the Easement Area in a matter that complies
with applicable laws. Prior to decommissioning, Company shall provide decommissioning
plans to City that include at minimum and without limitation the following elements: (i)
drain all remaining working fluid from Snow Melt System pipes in the Easement Area, and
dispose of any waste working fluid in a lawful manner; (ii) flush the drained pipes with
water to reasonably remove traces of working fluid, and dispose of the flushing effluent in
a lawful manner; (iii) purge flushed pipes with air to remove residual water; (iv) fill any
flushed and emptied lines larger than four inches inner diameter (4” ID) with flowable inert
fill; (v) disconnect main lines from associated pumps; (vii) remove any above-ground or
at-grade Snow Melt System components from the surface of the Easement Area (such as
control boxes, access points, manhole covers and the like) and finish to grade to match
existing adjacent surfaces; (viii) remove all signage associated with the Snow Melt System
from adjacent buildings, sidewalks, or other infrastructure; (ix) provide opportunity for
City to inspect and verify that the Snow Melt System has been drained and
decommissioned; and (x) provide City with copies of as-built diagrams describing Snow
Melt System components remaining in the Easement Area after decommissioning has been
completed. Company shall replace any portions of sidewalk within the Easement Area that
are damaged at the time of abandonment of the Snow Melt System. For two (2) years after
abandonment, Company shall be responsible (on notice from City, to be given not more
than once per each six-month period of the two years following abandonment) to replace

        4.     Indemnification. The Company, and any party claiming rights by, through or
under Company with respect to any easements granted by this Agreement shall defend, indemnify
and hold the City and all its elected and appointed officers, agents, contractors and employees
harmless from (a) all claims by persons, firms or other entities for labor, services, materials or

responsible relating to any activity involving the use of the Easement Area. The Company's

        5.     No Liens. The City is not responsible to pay or satisfy any obligations or liabilities

result in a claim being asserted or a judgment being entered against the City relating to the
Easement Area, and Company shall promptly bond over or otherwise remove any claim of lien
against the Easement Area within ten days of receiving notice or demand from the City therefor.

to seek and obtain any necessary relief or remedy including, but not limited to, a judgment for
money damages. However, the City shall not have the right, under any circumstances, to file a
claim requesting the termination or rescission of this Agreement.

6.     Use of Easement Area.

        6.1.     Company shall construct and maintain all elements of the Snow Melt
System within the Easement Area in compliance with the provisions of all applicable
federal, state or City laws, ordinances and regulations over the construction and use of any
improvements made within the Easement Area.

        6.2.    Company shall maintain the Snow Melt System within the Easement Area
in good condition, and accomplish the maintenance in a manner that does not interfere with
the use, maintenance or operation of the public right-of-way. However, during construction
of the Arena and the Snow Melt System (“Construction Period Use”), Company may
temporarily obstruct, including the complete closure for vehicle travel and parking, one or
more of the City Streets as reasonably necessary to perform construction. Company shall
further obtain the approval or permit by the City for any closure of City Streets prior to and
during the construction of the Arena and Snow Melt System. Company and City shall for
purposes of the approval or permitting process determine the minimum period that is
reasonably necessary to completely close any City Streets.

        6.3.    Company shall register a representative of the Arena to receive notifications
as a “Private Facility Member” with Michigan’s utility safety notification system MISS
DIG 811. For so long as the Snow Melt Easement remains in effect, Company shall
maintain a designated representative as a member of MISS DIG 811 to allow for
notifications to Company or its successor of plans to conduct excavation activity with
proximity to the Snow Melt System. Company shall reasonably cooperate with MISS DIG
811 agents and other system members to mark underground Snow Melt System
components in accordance with the standards required by the MISS DIG 811 system, and
Company shall take such other actions as may be reasonably necessary or desirable to
comply with requirements imposed on a private facility owner as described in the MISS
DIG Underground Facility Damage Prevention and Safety Act, being PA 174 of 2013,
MCL 460.721 et seq.

        6.4.    If, in the reasonable judgment of the City, Company’s failure to discharge
its maintenance obligations causes unreasonable interference with, or jeopardizes the use
or operation of the public right-of-way for the City Streets, then after written notice to the
Company and failure of the Company to abate or eliminate such interference within thirty
(30) days after such notice (provided that the thirty (30) day period shall be extended so
long as Company began efforts to abate or eliminate such interference within thirty (30)
days and is diligently pursuing the same), the City may, but is not obligated to, enter onto
the portions of the Benefitted Property that contain the Snow Melt System and perform any
work reasonably necessary in order to satisfy the Company’s obligations under this
subparagraph. The Company shall, upon written request of the City, promptly reimburse
the City for all reasonable expenses the City may incur in discharging any of the
Company’s obligations under this subparagraph. The City shall provide the Company with
reasonable documentation for all the reasonable expenses incurred.

              6.5.     Except for Construction Period Use and except for the Snow Melt System,
