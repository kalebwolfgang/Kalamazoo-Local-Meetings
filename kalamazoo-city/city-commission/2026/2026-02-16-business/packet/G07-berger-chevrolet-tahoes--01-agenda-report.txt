RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve the cooperative MiDEAL purchase of four
(4) Chevrolet Tahoes from Berger Chevrolet Inc., 2525-28th Street, Grand Rapids, MI 49512, in
the amount of $221,568.

BACKGROUND:

Fully marked Public Safety vehicles are essential to the Kalamazoo Department of Public
Safety’s (KDPS) ability to provide safe, timely, and effective police, fire, and medical
emergency response. The department maintains a fleet of approximately fifty-six (56) marked
units to support these operations. Due to the high-mileage nature of public safety work and the
significant maintenance costs that accompany aging vehicles, several front-line units must be
replaced each year to maintain operational readiness and cost-effective fleet performance.

Public Safety Officers carry an extensive array of gear and specialized equipment in their
assigned vehicles. As a result, many pursuit-rated sedans and smaller SUVs do not meet the
space, load-bearing, and configuration requirements necessary for KDPS operations. The
Chevrolet Tahoe platform continues to be the most suitable and reliable option for housing
emergency equipment, supporting integrated technology systems, and sustaining the heavy-duty
use associated with public safety functions.

The proposed purchase of four (4) Chevrolet Tahoe vehicles will replace existing front-line units
that have reached the end of their optimal service life. Once the new vehicles are deployed, the
outgoing units will either be rotated to secondary functions within the department or retired from
service, depending on their condition. Front-line public safety vehicles are operated
continuously—often by multiple officers over a 24-hour period, seven days a week. This

FISCAL IMPACT:

The best pricing for the proposed vehicle purchase was the MiDeal Purchasing Program. The
State of Michigan drafted bid specifications for the manufacture of vehicles used by law
enforcement departments, and in turn, received bids from interested automobile companies.
Funds for purchase of the four (4) proposed Public Safety fleet vehicles are included in the
adopted 2026 General fund Budget in Public Safety capital outlay.
