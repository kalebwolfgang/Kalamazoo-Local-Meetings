                           UTILITY EASEMENT AGREEMENT
                         FOR WATER MAIN AND STORM SEWER

       This Utility Easement Agreement for Water Main and Storm Sewer (this “Agreement”)
dated as of                               , 2026 is made by and between the CITY OF
KALAMAZOO, a Michigan municipal corporation, having an address of 241 West South Street,
Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007 (the “City”) and CD ARENA, LLC, having an address of 100 W.
Michigan Avenue, Suite 300, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007 (the “Company”).

                                         BACKGROUND:

       A.     The Company owns certain real property (the “Benefitted Property”) situated in
downtown Kalamazoo, upon which Company plans to construct a state-of-the-art landmark
development, designed to host collegiate and professional sporting events, concerts, and
community gatherings, together with associated facilities and amenities for students and
professional athletes, performers, spectators and the general public (the “Arena”). The legal
description of the Benefitted Property is attached as Exhibit A. The Benefitted Property is bounded
on its North and East sides by the City-controlled right-of-ways that contain West Kalamazoo
Avenue and North Park Steet (collectively, the “City Streets”).

       B.   The Company developed detailed plans for the Arena project (the “Plans”), which
were considered and approved by action of the City’s Planning authority.

       C.     The design of the Arena proposed by Company and approved by the City in the Plans
includes certain facilities for the provision of municipal water supply to the Benefitted Property
(the “Water Mains”), and certain other facilities for distribution of stormwater collected from
impervious surfaces on the Benefitted Property (the “Storm Sewers,” and together with the Water
Mains, the “Utilities”). Portions of the Utilities will be constructed within areas of the City Streets
as shown on Exhibit B (the “Easement Area”).

       D.     The Easement Area is located entirely within the public right-of-ways dedicated to
certain of the City Streets. Pursuant to Michigan law and the City's Charter, the City has absolute
control of the City Streets and the underlying real property upon which the City Streets are
constructed. Therefore, Company has requested that the City grant certain easements for the
Easement Area on, over, under and through the City Streets.

       E.   The granting of an easement in support of the Utilities in connection with the
development of the Arena is aligned with the interests of the City and the general public.

                                         AGREEMENT:

      THEREFORE, in consideration of the above and the mutual covenants, conditions and
agreements set forth below, the parties agree as follows:

        1.     Recitals. The statements in the Background section above are true and correct and
are incorporated herein by this reference.

        2.      Grant of Easements. Subject to and in accordance with the terms of this
Agreement, the City grants to the Company an exclusive and perpetual easement appurtenant to
the Benefitted Property (the “Utility Easement”) to construct, operate, use, maintain, repair, and
replace the Utilities, over, across, within and through the Easement Area as described with more
specificity on Exhibit C.

       3.      Construction and Repair of Utilities.

              3.1.    Construction of Utilities. Company may use the Easement Area for the
       construction of the Utilities, including any related temporary improvements necessary for
       the construction of the Utilities within the Easement Area, pursuant to construction plans
       materially consistent with the Plans.

               3.2.    Entry on City Streets and Sidewalks. The Utility Easement includes the
       right for the Company to enter upon the right-of-way of the City Streets in connection with
       the construction, operation, maintenance and repair of the Utilities. Upon prior notice to
       the City and coordination with the City, Company may close portions of the City Streets
       and/or the sidewalks adjacent to the City Streets in the Easement Area and adjacent thereto
       during construction, repair or replacement of the Utilities. The costs for any right-of-way
       permits, street closures, detours, and other obstructions to right-of-way access are
       Company's responsibility.

                3.3.    ‘As-Built’ Adjustment to Easement Area. Upon completion of
       construction of the Arena and the Utilities, Company will promptly provide the City with
       'as built plans' detailing the actual dimensions and elevations of the Utilities as constructed;
       and if those plans alter any of the dimensions of the Easement Area from what is described
       in this Agreement, the parties agree to record an amendment to this Agreement to
       accurately reflect the Easement Area as established by the 'as built plans.'

               3.4.   Maintenance of Utilities. As between the City and the Company, the
       Company shall be solely responsible for the construction, maintenance and repair,
       replacement and operation of the Utilities within the Easement Area. Upon completion of
       such construction, maintenance, repairs or replacement, Company shall restore the surface
       of any affected area pursuant to the Plans, or, if the Plans are not applicable, to the
       prevailing conditions of the impacted property as it existed immediately prior to the

       construction, maintenance, repairs or replacement of the Utilities. In the event that any
       contemplated maintenance, repair or replacement of municipal infrastructure that is outside
       the Easement Area is expected to also require work on the Utilities, City and Company
       shall use best efforts to reasonably cooperate and coordinate regarding the contemplated
       project, and the parties shall allocate costs between them equitably and in proportion to the
       extent of the Utilities to be impacted in relation to the overall scope of the contemplated
       project as a whole. For example and solely by way of illustration, if the City contemplates
       a project to replace one hundred (100) linear feet of water main infrastructure along West
       Kalamazoo Avenue, and the length of the Utilities to be involved in the project is ten (10)
       linear feet, Company would be responsible for at most 10 feet / 100 feet = 10% of the
       overall cost of the project.

               3.5.   Marking Underground Facilities.              Company shall register a
       representative of the Arena to receive notifications as a “Private Facility Member” with
       Michigan’s utility safety notification system MISS DIG 811. For so long as the Utility
       Easement remains in effect, Company shall maintain a designated representative as a
       member of MISS DIG 811 to allow for notifications to Company or its successor of plans
       to conduct excavation activity with proximity to the Utilities. Company shall reasonably
       cooperate with MISS DIG 811 agents and other system members to mark underground
       Utilities components in accordance with the standards required by the MISS DIG 811
       system, and Company shall take such other actions as may be reasonably necessary or
       desirable to comply with requirements imposed on a private facility owner as described in
       the MISS DIG Underground Facility Damage Prevention and Safety Act, being PA 174 of
       2013, MCL 460.721 et seq.

        4.     Indemnification. The Company, and any party claiming rights by, through or
under Company with respect to any easements granted by this Agreement shall defend, indemnify
and hold the City and all its elected and appointed officers, agents, contractors and employees
harmless from (a) all claims by persons, firms or other entities for labor, services, materials or
supplies provided to the Company for the construction, installation and maintenance of any
improvement made in the Easement Area; and (b) all claims for injuries to or death of any and all
individuals using or accessing the Utilities or for loss of or damage to property from any
environmental damage, degradation, response and clean-up costs and all reasonable and actual
attorney fees and related costs which the City may incur arising out of or which may arise out of
any activity the Company or its agents and invitees engaged in or for which any of them are
responsible relating to any activity involving the use of the Easement Area. The Company's
obligations to defend, indemnify, and hold the City harmless as stated above shall survive the
termination of this Agreement for a period of six (6) years after the date of termination.

        5.     No Liens. The City is not responsible to pay or satisfy any obligations or liabilities
by contractors of any of the Company, their subcontractors or any other person not a party to this
Agreement. The Company shall not engage in any activity or conduct which arises either directly
or indirectly out of its obligations, responsibilities and duties under this Agreement, which may
result in a claim being asserted or a judgment being entered against the City relating to the
Easement Area, and Company shall promptly bond over or otherwise remove any claim of lien
against the Easement Area within ten days of receiving notice or demand from the City therefor.

If Company breaches any of the provisions of the preceding sentence, the City shall have the right
to seek and obtain any necessary relief or remedy including, but not limited to, a judgment for
money damages. However, the City shall not have the right, under any circumstances, to file a
claim requesting the termination or rescission of this Agreement.

        6.     Use of Easement Area. Company shall, in connection with the use of the Easement
Area:

               6.1.   Construct and maintain all elements of the Utilities within the Easement
        Area in compliance with the provisions of all applicable federal, state or City laws,
        ordinances and regulations over the construction and use of any improvements made within
        the Easement Area.

                6.2.   Maintain the Utilities within the Easement Area in good condition, and
        accomplish the maintenance in a manner that does not interfere with the use, maintenance
        or operation of the public right-of-way. However, during construction of the Arena and the
        Utilities (“Construction Period Use”), Company may temporarily obstruct, including the
        complete closure for vehicle travel and parking, one or more of the City Streets as
        reasonably necessary to perform construction. Company shall further obtain the approval
        or permit by the City for any closure of City Streets prior to and during the construction of
        the Arena and Utilities. Company and City shall for purposes of the approval or permitting
        process determine the minimum period that is reasonably necessary to completely close
        any City Streets.

                6.3.    If, in the reasonable judgment of the City, Company’s failure to discharge
        its maintenance obligations causes unreasonable interference with, or jeopardizes the use
        or operation of the public right-of-way for the City Streets, then after written notice to the
        Company and failure of the Company to abate or eliminate such interference within thirty
        (30) days after such notice (provided that the thirty (30) day period shall be extended so
        long as Company began efforts to abate or eliminate such interference within thirty (30)
        days and is diligently pursuing the same), the City may, but is not obligated to, enter onto
        the portions of the Benefitted Property that contain the Utilities and perform any work
        reasonably necessary in order to satisfy the Company’s obligations under this
        subparagraph. The Company shall, upon written request of the City, promptly reimburse
        the City for all reasonable expenses the City may incur in discharging any of the
        Company’s obligations under this subparagraph. The City shall provide the Company with
        reasonable documentation for all the reasonable expenses incurred.

                6.4.     Except for Construction Period Use and except for the Utilities, Company
        and all its officers, directors, members, managers, agents, contractors and employees shall
        not place any improvements or fixtures or conduct any activity within the Easement Area
        which will unreasonably interfere in any way with the use or enjoyment of the public right-
        of-way by the City or the general public.

                6.5. Company may not store, allow the storage of or discharge any radioactive,
        toxic, flammable, poisonous, explosive or other dangerous, hazardous materials or waste

       8.     Miscellaneous.

               8.1.    Authority. The City represents and warrants to Company that it has the
       authority to grant this easement and execute this Agreement.

             8.2.   Amendment. This Agreement may only be amended by a written
       agreement executed by the City and the Company.

               8.3.   No Waiver. The waiver of any breach of any provision of this Agreement
       by a party shall not operate as or be construed as a waiver by the other party of any
       subsequent breach. Each right, remedy and power granted to a party under this Agreement
       or allowed by law shall be cumulative and not exclusive of any other.

               8.4.    Separability. If any of the provisions of this Agreement or its application
       to any party under any circumstances is determined to be invalid or unenforceable, such
       invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other provision of this Agreement or its
       application.

               8.5.   Covenants to Run with Land. The easements created by this Agreement
       and the terms and conditions of this Agreement shall run with the land and shall be binding
       upon and inure to the benefit of the parties hereto and their heirs and successors and assigns
       as the owner of the Benefitted Property.

               8.6.   Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement
       between the parties regarding the matters set forth in this Agreement. This Agreement
       supersedes and terminates any other previous or contemporaneous communications,
       representations, understandings, agreements, negotiations and discussions, whether oral or
       written, between the parties with respect to the Easement Area. There are no other written
       or oral agreements, understandings or representations directly or indirectly related to the
       Easement Area that are not expressly set forth in this Agreement.

              8.7.    Notice. All notices, demands and requests required or permitted to be given
       under the provisions of this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given (a)
       when personally delivered to the party to be given such notice or other communication, (b)
       on the third business day following the date of deposit in the United States mail if such
