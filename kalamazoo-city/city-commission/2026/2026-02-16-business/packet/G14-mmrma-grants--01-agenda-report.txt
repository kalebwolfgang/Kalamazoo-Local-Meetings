RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accept Risk Avoidance Program (RAP) and
Certification and Accreditation (CAP) grant funding from the Michigan Municipal Risk
Management Association (MMRMA), 14001 Merriman Rd, Livonia, MI 48154, in the amount of
$193,775.

BACKGROUND:

In 2024, the City of Kalamazoo entered into a ten-year contract with Axon Enterprise, Inc. for
the replacement and modernization of the Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety’s (KDPS)
body-worn cameras, in-car camera systems, TASER conducted-energy weapons, and the
associated digital-evidence management ecosystem. The agreement also included Axon’s
software and data-storage components used to support KDPS’s unmanned aerial system (UAS)
program; however, the contract did not include the purchase of drones themselves.

All Axon hardware, software, and evidence-management components procured under the
contract have since been acquired and placed into operational use. Following implementation,
KDPS submitted an application to the Michigan Municipal Risk Management Authority
(MMRMA) Risk Avoidance Program seeking reimbursement to offset a portion of the capital
expenditures already incurred. MMRMA has awarded the City $185,000 to support these
technology upgrades, which will directly reduce the City’s net cost for the long-term Axon
contract. No additional matching funds are required.

KDPS also applied for and received an additional $8,775 in MMRMA Certification and
Accreditation Program (CAP) grant funding. This reimbursement covers training costs for three
KDPS members who completed the Michigan State University (MSU) School of Criminal
Justice Police Staff and Command Program, a cornerstone professional-development course that
