RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that City Commission approve a contract with SWT Excavating, Inc.for the
2026 New Water Service Installation Project in the amount of $894,519.90.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo regional water systems serves public drinking water to approximately
200,000 customers across 11 jurisdictions. Appendix C of the Water Services Agreement with
the partner communities defines water service installation to be a service provided by the City.
 In support of this responsibility, Public Services prepared bid documents for the work to be
completed by a qualified contractor to supplement work performed by City Crews. Maintaining
City oversight of new small water service installations is beneficial to the overall system.
 Centralized management improves water system security by preventing unauthorized or
improper connections and ensures that all installations are inspected and verified for compliance
with approved material specifications and construction standards. This coordinated approach also
supports consistent recordkeeping and system integrity across all jurisdictions.

This approach is also favorable for customers. A standardized process ensures that water service
installations are consistent and accessible for all users. By utilizing a single annual contract for
all new small service installations, the City is able to achieve lower costs, resulting in more
affordable installation costs. Customers benefit from a simplified process and are relieved of the
burden of independently soliciting or evaluating quotes from contractors.

Bids were opened for the project on January 20, 2026. Two bids were received with SWT
Excavating, Inc. being the low responsive bidder. SWT Excavating, Inc. has performed well on
similar work in the past and Public Services recommends approval of this contract.
