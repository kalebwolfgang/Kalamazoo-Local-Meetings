SUBJECT:       Approval of a contract with Enterprise Fleet Management for 26 Public Safety
               Vehicle Lease Replacement (Bid Ref#000-00-017.3)

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that City Commission approve the lease replacement of 26 vehicles for Public
Safety from Enterprise Fleet Management in the amount of $836,119.80 over 60 months.

BACKGROUND:

The City of Kalamazoo Administration established in July 2016 that Enterprise Fleet
Management would match or surpass MiDeal pricing. The Equity Lease Program with Enterprise
Fleet Management was implemented for leasing light duty vehicles. Light duty vehicles are
defined as SUVs, pick-up trucks, vans, and sedans. This process was integrated into a pilot phase
followed by five separate phases. The program is currently in the replacement phase of vehicles
purchased at the beginning of the cycle.

The Public Safety Officers often operate a front-line vehicle 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
The vehicles are subject to extreme wear and tear. The 26 vehicle lease requests will replace
existing vehicles leased. The current 26 vehicles have an annual cost of $96,602.16. After
applying roll gains, the replacement of 26 vehicles will have an annual cost of $167,223.96 per
year for five years. The overall expected lease increase is approximately $353,109 over 60
months (or $70,621.80 per year).
