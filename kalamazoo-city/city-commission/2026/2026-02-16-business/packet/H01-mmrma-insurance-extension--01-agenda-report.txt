RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a one year contract extension with the
Michigan Municipal Risk Management Authority (MMRMA) through Ibex Insurance Agency,
for property & casualty insurance annual premium not to exceed $1,130,954 and a deposit to the
member retention fund of $100,000. It is recommended that the City Commission approve a
budget amendment in the amount of $174,589 for execution of this contract.

BACKGROUND:

The coverage provided for this year's renewal includes $10 million limits for general and auto
liability, $404,509,572 in "all-risk" replacement cost coverage for City-owned buildings and
other property, loss of electronic data coverage, employee fidelity bonds, and other coverage.
This year's base premium is 5.4% higher than the 2025 premium driven mostly by an increase
related to property increases of 23.9%.

MMRMA is also distributing a "return of net assets" of $466,390 as a result of favorable
investment return and loss experience. This will be credited to the insurance fund to help offset
the cost of the premium.
