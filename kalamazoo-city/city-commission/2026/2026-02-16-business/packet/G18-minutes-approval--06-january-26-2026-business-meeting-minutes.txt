Vice Mayor Duncan, seconded by Commissioner Praedel, moved to deny the
application to rezone the property at 4301 Stadium Drive from RM-15
(Residential, Multi-Dwelling) & RS-5 (Residential, Single-Dwelling) to CC
(Community Commercial).

Discussion took place regarding the form of the motion.

City Attorney Leal recommended that the Commissioner who made the motion
include factual findings to support the denial.

Vice Mayor Duncan stated the City Commission had heard enough testimony to
speak to the motion to deny.

Commissioners Slaby and Praedel reviewed the five zoning criteria and described
how the proposed rezoning did not satisfy the criteria.

Commissioner Hess expressed agreement with Commissioners Praedel and Slaby.

With a roll call vote the motion to deny passed 7-0.
