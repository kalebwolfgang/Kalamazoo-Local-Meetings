RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract extension with Santiago
Services for Mowing and Trimming of Parks and Greenspaces in the amount of $199,904.00.

BACKGROUND:

In December 2024, the City's Purchasing Division solicited sealed bids on a per park basis for
mowing and trimming of City parks and greenspaces. Through this process, Santiago Services
was one of four companies to submit pricing. Not only were they the most cost-efficient option
through the bid process, they completed the work in 2025 at a satisfactory level. They did a great
job learning and maintaining our park system while working with staff to address needs in a
timely manner.
