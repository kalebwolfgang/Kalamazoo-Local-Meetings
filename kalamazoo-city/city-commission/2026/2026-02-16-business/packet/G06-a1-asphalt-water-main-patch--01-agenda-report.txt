RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a contract with A1 Asphalt, Inc. for the
2026 Water Main Break Patch Repair Project in the amount of $217,401.00.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water System experiences approximately 100 water main breaks annually.
About half of the Kalamazoo Water System water main resides within Road Commission of
Kalamazoo County (RCKC) right-of-way (ROW). Historically, Public Services has repaired
RCKC roads with temporary patches that need to be properly restored to RCKC standards.

This contract will allow road repairs to be completed to ROW owner specifications on initial
repair for both RCKC and City of Kalamazoo Streets. Public Services is too busy with other
work to be able to complete these road patches. Public Services went out to bid to have a
contractor assist with completing this work. Bids were opened on January 13, 2026. One
responsive bid was received from A1 Asphalt, Inc. in the amount of $217,401.00.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The funding for the project is available in the City’s 2026 Water O&M and Capital Improvement
Budgets. This purchase order is being requested as a blanket purchase order so that invoices can
be coded as they come in instead of having a pre-defined split, because this supplements demand
maintenance. The majority of the work will be coded to main maintenance (591-562-02.000-
801.000). However, this contract also supplements roadway repairs for City LSLRs
(wat0500400), valve replacements (wat0500002), service line maintenance (591-562-03.000-
801.00), and hydrant replacements (wat0500402).
