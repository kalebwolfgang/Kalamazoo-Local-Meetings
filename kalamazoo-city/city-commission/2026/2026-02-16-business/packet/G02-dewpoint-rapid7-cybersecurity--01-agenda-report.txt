RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a cooperative MIDEAL purchase for the
annual renewal agreement with Rapid7 for Vulnerability Management and Insight Intrusion
Detection and Response software services in the amount of $147,322.

BACKGROUND:

Rapid7's Vulnerability Management and IDR (Intrusion Detection and Response) solutions offer
a comprehensive approach to cybersecurity for city infrastructure. By combining InsightVM for
continuous vulnerability assessment and InsightIDR for real-time threat detection and response,
cities can proactively identify security risks, detect potential breaches, and swiftly respond to
cyber incidents, ensuring robust protection of critical municipal systems and data.
