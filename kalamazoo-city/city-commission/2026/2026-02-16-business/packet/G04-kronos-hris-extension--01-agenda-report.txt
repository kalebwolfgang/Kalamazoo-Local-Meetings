RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a one-year extension with Kronos
Workforce Readiness to continue with the Human Resources Information System (HRIS), in the
amount of $169,919.65.

BACKGROUND:

The Human Resources Information System (HRIS) system allows Human
Resources and Management Services Departments the ability to maintain a state-of-the-art
Human Resources/ Personnel system that supports integrated quality of service through
efficiency, real-time information, and accuracy. The HRIS reduces labor-intensive work by the
departments by automating time entry, attendance tracking, benefit administration, recruiting,
training, payroll, and compensation management.

The KRONOS system provides file feeds to vendors for transmission of data entries and secure
payments. Kronos supports online open enrollment for employees and creates a user-friendly
environment for benefit and payroll administrators. The Kronos system is currently used by the
City and staff are pleased with its performance.

Strategic Goal Impact:

implement the community's Strategic Vision in a way that is sustainable over the long term.
