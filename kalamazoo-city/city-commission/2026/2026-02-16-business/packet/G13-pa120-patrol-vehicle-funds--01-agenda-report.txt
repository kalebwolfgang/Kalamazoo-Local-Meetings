RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission accept $63,672.19 in reimbursement funding from
the State of Michigan under Public Act 120 of 2024 – Campus Safety Grant Program. These
funds will offset costs associated with the Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety’s (KDPS)
purchase and upfitting of a police patrol vehicle used to support campus safety operations.

BACKGROUND:

The State of Michigan established the Campus Safety Grant Program under Public Act 120 of
2024 to enhance safety and security measures in communities with significant student
populations. The City of Kalamazoo, home to multiple higher education institutions and a large
transient student population, experiences a concentrated service demand in neighborhoods
adjacent to Western Michigan University, Kalamazoo College, and other student-dense areas.
Calls for service in these zones routinely involve issues such as high-density housing, pedestrian
and vehicular congestion, and increased nighttime activity, all of which require sustained, visible
public safety presence.

In 2025, the Kalamazoo Department of Public Safety purchased and upfitted a fully equipped
police patrol vehicle to support proactive policing, rapid response, and high-visibility patrols
within campus-adjacent neighborhoods. Vehicle upfitting included installation of emergency
lighting, communication systems, in-car video, computer and mounting hardware, prisoner
transport equipment, and exterior markings consistent with KDPS fleet standards.

The State of Michigan has authorized reimbursement in the amount of $63,672.19, representing
eligible vehicle and upfitting expenses under Public Act 120 of 2024. Acceptance of these funds
will directly reduce previously incurred costs to the General Fund and support continued
deployment of this specialized patrol resource.
