RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended the City Commission approve an MDOT contract for transportation
enhancement activities under the Transportation Alternatives Program, specifically the Safe
Routes to Schools program. The project involves infrastructure improvements on Winchell
Avenue and Howard Street, leveraging federal funds to cover the majority of construction costs
in the amount of $47,367.00.

BACKGROUND:

The project encompasses the following improvements along Winchell Avenue (Rambling Road
to Oakland Drive) and Howard Street (Stearns Road to Merrill Street):
• Installation of concrete sidewalks, curb and gutter, and curb ramps.
• Tree removals, earthwork, and plantings.
• Installation of rectangular rapid flashing beacons and permanent pavement markings.
• Required audio-visual filming for project documentation.
Per the agreement, the City must provide the following at no cost to the project (excluded from
the federal cost-sharing):
• Project design and preparation of plans.
• Appointment of a project engineer in responsible charge.
• Construction engineering, materials testing, and inspection services.
• Future maintenance and operation of all facilities once construction is complete.
The following provisions require careful attention; however, they are standard items included in
all of our MDOT funding agreements:
• Hazardous Materials: If unknown hazardous substances are discovered, the City is
responsible for all remediation and contractor delay costs if they are not deemed eligible by the
FHWA. Refusal to pay can lead to project termination.

FISCAL IMPACT:

The funds required for this contract are $47,367.00. The funding available and estimated costs
are detailed below. The contract divides costs into two categories based on federal funding
eligibility: Part A (Federal Participation): Includes major construction items. Federal funds and
toll credits will cover up to $387,825 (capped at an 81.85% participation ratio). The City is
responsible for the remaining balance of Part A costs. Part B (No Federal Participation): Includes
audio-visual filming and specific plantings. These costs are 100% the responsibility of the City.
