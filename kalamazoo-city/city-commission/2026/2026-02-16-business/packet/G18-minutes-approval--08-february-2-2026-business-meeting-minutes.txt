Commissioner Questions:

   Following the presentation, Assistant City Planner Durkee responded to
   Commissioner questions regarding: the connection between the development
   of this NEZ and the housing corridor study presented during the Committee of
   the Whole meeting; how this development would further the City’s housing
   goals, including demographic goals and the desire to address “the missing
   middle”; whether there was a benefit to an NEZ being on a single parcel rather
   than multiple parcels; the potential duration of the tax exemption vis-à-vis
   conflicting information in the agenda report; and whether the developer had
   contracts in place with a specific daycare provider and grocer.

City Manager’s Report    Chief Boysen reported on the use of Flock cameras to read license plates and the
                         policies and practices around how the images were stored, accessed, and shared.
                         Chief Boysen spoke about the benefits of the license plate readers and how they
                         had helped KDPS solve various crimes.

Commissioner             Commissioner Praedel offered congratulations to Commissioner Hoffman and
Comments                 Neighborhood Activator Cee Maul for their recognition by Kalamazoo Valley
                         Community College. Commissioner Praedel thanked staff and community
                         members for serving the unhoused during the bitterly cold weather, and he
                         expressed appreciation to staff from the Community Planning and Economic
                         Development Department for their Imagine Kalamazoo 2035 public engagement
                         sessions.

                         Commissioner Hess thanked City staff who were working outside, and she
                         expressed gratitude to Pastor Millard Southern for organizing the recent concert
                         and community conversation with noted trumpet player Winton Marsalis.

                         Mayor Anderson offered remarks on the passing of community activist Jean
                         Baraka-Love and the funeral for a young person who lost their life to gun violence.
                         Mayor Anderson expressed gratitude to people and organizations who helped the
