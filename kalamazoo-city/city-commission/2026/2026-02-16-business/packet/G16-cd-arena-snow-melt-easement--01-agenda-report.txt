SUBJECT:       CD Arena, LLC Snow Melt System Easement

RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve and accept the snow melt system easement
with CD Arena, LLC for private snow melt installed as part of the Kalamazoo Event Center
Project; and authorize the City Manager to sign all related and necessary documents regarding
the acquisition of the easement.

BACKGROUND:

CD Arena, LLC is in the process of constructing the Kalamazoo Event Center – a state-of-the art
landmark development, designed to host collegiate and professional sporting events, concerts,
and community gatherings, together with associated facilities and amenities for students and
professional athletes, performers, spectators and the general public. The approved design
includes a private snow melt system to be installed in public right-of-way of West Kalamazoo
Avenue, West Water Street, North Park Street, and North Westnedge Avenue.

CD Arena, LLC has requested that the City grant a snow melt system easement for construction,
operation, maintenance, repair, and replacement of this system. CD Arena, LLC in collaboration
with City of Kalamazoo Attorney’s Office, Public Services, and Community Planning &
Economic Development have drafted this agreement for approval.
