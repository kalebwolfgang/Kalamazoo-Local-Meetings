RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a sole source purchase for a complete tear
down and rebuilding of two Faulk gear drives for our primary clarifiers with MiMotion, in the
amount of $124,914.09.

BACKGROUND:

The Kalamazoo Water Reclamation Plant (KWRP) utilizes six primary clarifiers for physical
settling of solids and floatation of fats, oils, and greases. The dive mechanism for removal of
these residuals is powered by existing Faulk gear drives. Currently we have two failed Faulk gear
drives. These Faulk gear drives have been routinely repaired and have now reached the end of
their service life and are recommended for rehabilitation.

Rehabilitation of these gear drives aligns with the Capital Improvement Project for our Primary
clarifiers which is scheduled to begin phasing with affected tanks in the Spring of 2026. It is my
recommendation to approve a purchase order to MiMotion, in the amount of $124,914.09.
