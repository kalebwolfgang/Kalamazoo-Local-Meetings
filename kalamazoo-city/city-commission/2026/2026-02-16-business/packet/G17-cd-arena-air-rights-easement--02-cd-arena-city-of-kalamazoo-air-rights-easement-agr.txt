                       EASEMENT AGREEMENT FOR AIR RIGHTS

This Easement Agreement for Air Rights (this “Agreement”) dated as of       ___________,
2026 is made by and between the CITY OF KALAMAZOO, a Michigan municipal corporation,
having an address of 241 West South Street, Kalamazoo, Michigan 49007 (the “City”) and CD
ARENA, LLC, having an address of 100 W. Michigan Avenue, Suite 300, Kalamazoo, Michigan
49007 (the “Company”).

                                        BACKGROUND:

       A.     The Company owns certain real property situated in downtown Kalamazoo lying
between West Kalamazoo Avenue to the north, West Water Street to the south, North Park Steet
to the east and North Westnedge Avenue to the west (the “Benefitted Property”), upon which
Company plans to construct a state-of-the-art landmark development, designed to host collegiate
and professional sporting events, concerts, and community gatherings, together with associated
facilities and amenities for students and professional athletes, performers, spectators and the
general public (the “Arena”). The legal description of the Benefitted Property is attached as
Exhibit A.

       B.    The Company developed detailed plans for the Arena project (the “Plans”), which
were considered and approved by action of the City’s planning authority.

        C.    The design of the Arena proposed by Company and approved by the City in the Plans
includes a portion of the building (the “Overhang”) that extends into the air above that portion of
the Park Street right-of-way as shown on Exhibit B (the “Easement Area”). Such Easement Area
shall only include the air rights up to forty-nine and one-half (49.5) feet above the elevation of the
surface of Park Street, measured at a point ten (10) feet along a line perpendicular to the centerline
of Park Street, but excluding such air rights below the bottom of the Overhang, which has an
approximate elevation of twenty-three and one-half (23.5) feet above the surface of Park Street.

       D.    Plans showing elevations of the Overhang in relation to the main massing of the
Arena structure and the Park Street right-of-way are attached as Exhibit C.

       E.     Pursuant to Michigan law and the City's Charter, the City has absolute control of the
Park Street right-of-way (“Park Street”). Therefore, Company has requested that the City grant

certain easements in connection with the Arena, including easements for air rights over Park Street
to permit the construction of the Overhang within the air rights.

       F.     The granting of an easement in support of the innovative and iconic design of the
Arena is aligned with the interests of the City and the general public.

                                        AGREEMENT:

      THEREFORE, in consideration of the above and the mutual covenants, conditions and
agreements set forth below, the parties agree as follows:

        1.     Recitals. The statements in the Background section above are true and correct and
are incorporated herein by this reference.

       2.      Grant of Easements. Subject to and in accordance with the terms of this
Agreement, the City grants to the Company an exclusive and perpetual easement appurtenant to
the Benefitted Property (the “Air Rights Easement”) to construct, operate, use, maintain, repair,
and replace the Overhang over, across, within and through the air rights in the Easement Area as
described with more specificity on Exhibit C. The Air Rights Easement also includes the right
for Company to allow its invitees and licensees to use and enjoy the Overhang in connection with
normal operations of the Arena after construction is complete.

       3.      Construction and Repair of Overhang.

               3.1.   Construction of Overhang. Company may use the Easement Area for the
       construction of the Overhang, including any related temporary improvements necessary
       for the construction of the Overhang within the Easement Area, pursuant to construction
       plans materially consistent with the Plans.

              3.2.    Entry on Park Street Right-of-Way and Sidewalks. The Air Rights
       Easement includes the right to enter upon the right-of-way of Park Street including the
       sidewalks of Park Street under and adjacent to the Easement Area in connection with the
       construction, operation, maintenance and repair of the Overhang. Upon prior notice to the
       City and coordination with the City, Company may close Park Street and/or the sidewalks
       of Park Street in the Easement Area and adjacent thereto during construction, repair or
       replacement of the Overhang. The costs for any right-of-way permits, street closures,
       detours, and other obstructions to right-of-way access are Company's responsibility.

                3.3.    ‘As-Built’ Adjustment to Easement Area. Upon completion of
       construction of the Arena and the Overhang, Company will promptly provide the City with
       'as built plans' detailing the actual dimensions and elevations of the Overhang as
       constructed; and if those plans alter any of the vertical or horizontal dimensions of the
       Easement Area from what is described in this Agreement, the parties agree to record an
       amendment to this Agreement to accurately reflect the Easement Area as established by
       the 'as built plans.'

        4.     Indemnification. The Company, and any party claiming rights by, through or
under Company with respect to any easements granted by this Agreement shall defend, indemnify
and hold the City and all its elected and appointed officers, agents, contractors and employees
harmless from (a) all claims by persons, firms or other entities for labor, services, materials or
supplies provided to the Company for the construction, installation and maintenance of any
improvement made in the Easement Area; and (b) all claims for injuries to or death of any and all
individuals using or accessing the Overhang or for loss of or damage to property from any
environmental damage, degradation, response and clean-up costs and all reasonable and actual
attorney fees and related costs which the City may incur arising out of or which may arise out of
any activity the Company or its agents and invitees engaged in or for which any of them are
responsible relating to any activity involving the use of the Easement Area. The Company's
obligations to defend, indemnify, and hold the City harmless as stated above shall survive the
termination of this Agreement for a period of six (6) years after the date of termination.

        5.     No Liens. The City is not responsible to pay or satisfy any obligations or liabilities
by contractors of any of the Company, their subcontractors or any other person not a party to this
Agreement. The Company shall not engage in any activity or conduct which arises either directly
or indirectly out of its obligations, responsibilities and duties under this Agreement, which may
result in a claim being asserted or a judgment being entered against the City relating to the
Easement Area, and Company shall promptly bond over or otherwise remove any claim of lien
against the Easement Area within ten days of receiving notice or demand from the City therefor.
If Company breaches any of the provisions of the preceding sentence, the City shall have the right
to seek and obtain any necessary relief or remedy including, but not limited to, a judgment for
money damages. However, the City shall not have the right, under any circumstances, to file a
claim requesting the termination or rescission of this Agreement.

        6.     Use of Easement Area. Company shall, in connection with the use of the Easement
Area:

                6.1.   Construct and maintain all Overhang within the Easement Area in
        compliance with the provisions of all applicable federal, state or City laws, ordinances and
        regulations over the construction and use of any improvements made within the Easement
        Area.

                6.2.    Maintain the Overhang within the Easement Area in good condition, both
        as to safety and appearance; and the maintenance of those improvements accomplished in
        a manner to not interfere with the use, maintenance or operation of the public right-of-way
        located below the bottom of the Overhang. However, during construction of the Arena and
        the Overhang (“Construction Period Use”), Company may temporarily obstruct,
        including the complete closure for vehicle travel and parking, Park Street as reasonably
        necessary to perform construction, but shall, to the extent reasonably possible, preserve
        pedestrian access to businesses located on Park Street. Company shall further obtain the
        approval or permit by the City for any closure of Park Street prior to and during the
        construction of the Arena and Overhang. Company and City shall for purposes of the
        approval or permitting process determine the minimum period that is reasonably necessary
        to completely close Park Street.

               6.3.    If, in the reasonable judgment of the City, Company's failure to discharge
       its maintenance obligations causes interference with or jeopardizes the use or operation of
       the public right-of-way for Park Street, then after written notice to the Company and failure
       of the Company to abate or eliminate such interference within thirty (30) days after such
       notice (provided that the thirty (30) day period shall be extended so long as Company began
       efforts to abate or eliminate such interference within thirty (30) days and is diligently
       pursuing the same), the City may, but is not obligated to, enter onto the Company's
       Benefitted Property and perform any work reasonably necessary in order to perform the
       Company's duties and obligations under this subparagraph. The Company shall, upon
       written request of the City, promptly reimburse the City for all reasonable expenses the
       City may incur in discharging any of the Company's duties and obligations under this
       subparagraph. The City shall provide the Company with reasonable documentation for all
       the reasonable expenses incurred.

               6.4.     Except for Construction Period Use and except for the Overhang, Company
       and all its officers, directors, members, managers, agents, contractors and employees shall
       not place any improvements or fixtures or conduct any activity within the Easement Area
       which will unreasonably interfere in any way with the use or enjoyment of the public right-
       of-way by the City, or the public within that area below the Overhang.

               6.5.   Company may not store, allow the storage of or discharge any radioactive,
       toxic, flammable, poisonous, explosive or other dangerous, hazardous materials or waste
       within the Easement Area.

        7.      No Rights in Public; No Implied Easements. Nothing herein shall be construed
as creating any rights in the general public or as dedicating for public use any portion of the
Benefitted Property or the Easement Area. No easements, except those expressly set forth in
Section 2, shall be implied by this Agreement.

       8.     Miscellaneous.

               8.1.    Authority. The City represents and warrants to Company that it has the
       authority to grant this easement and execute this Agreement.

             8.2.    Amendment. This Agreement may only be amended by a written
       agreement executed by the City and the Company.

               8.3.   No Waiver. The waiver of any breach of any provision of this Agreement
       by a party shall not operate as or be construed as a waiver by the other party of any
       subsequent breach. Each right, remedy and power granted to a party under this Agreement
       or allowed by law shall be cumulative and not exclusive of any other.

              8.4.    Separability. If any of the provisions of this Agreement or its application
       to any party under any circumstances is determined to be invalid or unenforceable, such
