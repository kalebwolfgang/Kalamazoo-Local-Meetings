RECOMMENDATION:

It is recommended that the City Commission approve a cooperative MIDEAL purchase of
Audio/Visual Equipment and Sound Masking for the 150 East Crosstown Facility Renovation
Project with Smart Homes & Smart Offices, Inc. in the amount of $313,430.18 for audio-visual
equipment and integration services and $57,664.60 for sound masking system installation, and
authorize the City Manager to sign all related documents.

BACKGROUND:

As part of the renovation of the 150 East Crosstown facility, the Community Room and
adjoining workspaces require modernization of technology systems to support public meetings,
staff training, community programming, and efficient internal operations.
The audio-visual (AV) system provides the core technology infrastructure needed for effective
communication and public engagement. AV components include presentation systems, displays,
audio reinforcement, cameras, microphones, switching hardware, and control equipment. These
systems are essential for City Commission meetings held in the Community Room, staff
development activities, and events utilizing hybrid or remote formats.
The project also includes a sound masking system to be installed in designated work areas within
the facility. Sound masking enhances speech privacy, reduces acoustic distractions, and supports
a more functional and productive workplace environment for employees and visitors.
Smart Homes & Smart Offices, Inc. was identified as the preferred vendor through the project’s
AV design and evaluation process based on system compatibility, integration capabilities, and
alignment with technology standards used throughout other City facilities. Procurement through
MiDeal #240000001076 ensures competitive pricing and compliance with the City’s purchasing
policies.
